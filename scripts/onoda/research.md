## "29 years" and "30 years" arithmetic

- War end 15 Aug 1945 → 9–11 Mar 1974 = **28 years 6.8 months** (28.57 yr). From 2 Sep 1945 → 28 y 6 m (28.5).
- Dec 1944 (26 Dec) → 9–11 Mar 1974 = **29 years 2.5 months** (about 29 y 3 m). This is his time on Lubang.
- Hence "สงครามจบไปแล้ว 29 ปี" overstates by ~5 months if counted strictly from 15 Aug 1945. Many outlets (e.g. S-05 headline) write "29 years after World War II" loosely, but for a fact-checked Short use **"เกือบ 29 ปี"** (safest hook, still fits within 2 s) or "ผ่านไป 28 ปีครึ่ง". Plain "29 ปี" is defensible only as a round number; tell the user if kept (rounding up 28.6). The book's "Thirty-Year War" and "30 years in the jungle" are loose: on Lubang 29 y 3 m, so say "เกือบ 30 ปี" or "29 ปีกว่า" if needed. "29 ปีในป่า" is accurate (29 y 3 m); "สงครามจบ 29 ปี" is the shaky one.

## Phrasing notes for Preaw (proposed, not final)

- Civilian deaths, non-graphic: "ระหว่างนั้น กลุ่มของเขาถูกรายงานว่าทำให้ชาวบ้านเสียชีวิตราว 30 คน" (hedged, group-level); or "ชาวบ้านหลายสิบคนเสียชีวิต". Add that he believed the war was still on, as reported cause, not excuse. Avoid "ฆ่า 30 คนด้วยมือเขา".
- Avoid "hero" and avoid "murderer": record both views (Japan hailed him; Lubang residents angry; pardon by Marcos).
- Leaflets: "เขาเชื่อว่าเป็นกลลวงของศัตรู" is supported (C-05/C-06). Avoid inventing his reasoning.
- Twist order: only his old commander could rescind it (C-03/C-11). Taniguchi had to fly to Lubang (reported).
- Companions: Akatsu left and surrendered (not killed); Shimada killed 1954; Kozuka killed 1972. Don't say "เพื่อนหายไปทีละคน" for Akatsu without noting he left alive; "ทีละคน" okay as general (left 1949/1950, died 1954, died 1972).

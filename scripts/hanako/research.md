# HANAKO research packet

- **Revision:** research r1 (Boat, 30 Oct 2026)
- **Deliverable:** `HANAKO-SHORT` (Thai Short, 45–60 s, mystery-educational, ending B_loop). Profile and tier: [delivery.md](delivery.md).
- **Mode:** real folklore / urban legend with documented spread. It is not a named real case and not fiction. The script presents the legend as a legend (folklore), never as a real event.
- **Central question:** Who is Hanako-san, and was she ever a real girl?
- **Learning takeaway (proposed):** No documented real girl lies behind Hanako-san. The legend grew out of children's oral school lore, which folklorists collected from the 1980s on and which books and films (1990s) spread nationally. Why the toilet: it is an isolated, in-between space, and older Japanese beliefs placed a god in the privy.
- **Period/context:** postwar Japan (earliest recalled telling 1948) → school-ghost boom of the late 1980s and 1990s.

## Unsupported or circulated-only (do not assert)

- Any real girl named Hanako who died in a school toilet. This covers the air-raid, murder, suicide and snow-collapse versions (F008).
- That the name or red skirt comes from toilet-god doll offerings (F010).
- That the bob and red skirt are the "original" look (F007).
- Exact 1948 wording, the village, and that "1948" is a publication date (F001–F002).
- Any death toll, date or place given as a real event.

## Sound implications (for later roles)

- An air-raid siren, explosions or a scream must not imply that the WWII origin is real. If the script uses them, label them as legend dramatization (F008).
- The knocking SFX and the 「はい」 reply are legend content and fictional sound.
- No real recordings are needed.

## Supported story angles (loopable, educational payoff)

1. **"She never existed, yet everyone knows her"** (premise → twist)
   - Hook: knock ×3, 「花子さん、いらっしゃいますか…」
   - Middle: the many death stories (labelled as rumours).
   - Twist: no record of any real girl exists; the oldest recorded version (about 1948, Iwate) has no girl at all, only a voice and a hand.
   - Payoff: children made her. A teacher-folklorist collected thousands of stories → a 1990 book → 1995 films → nationwide.
   - Loop: ending on the knock returns to the opening.
   - Needs: F001, F002, F004, F008 inspected.
2. **"Why always the toilet?"** (knowledge angle)
   - Hook: of all places in a school, why there?
   - Payoff: the space where children are alone and unseen (F011), plus an older belief that a god lived in the toilet (F009).
   - Keep F010 out or clearly as "some think".
   - Loop: "…so next time you're alone in the third stall…" → knock.
3. **"The rule of three"** (pattern angle)
   - Third floor, third stall, three knocks. The earliest version already says "the *third* Hanako".
   - Twist: "three" was in the story before the ritual was.
   - Rests on F002, the weakest lead, so it **requires inspection first**.

**Recommended:** angle 1 with one line of angle 2 as the educational payoff. It needs the fewest weak claims.

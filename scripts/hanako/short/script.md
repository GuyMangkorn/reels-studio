## Story core

- **Logline:** A Japanese school ghost has a precise ritual, a name and many death stories, yet when you look for the real girl behind her, there is no record of one; what exists is a story told around 1948 and the children who kept retelling it.
- **Central question:** Who was Hanako-san, and was she ever a real girl?
- **Intention / obstacle:** The viewer (stand-in investigator) wants to find the girl behind the ghost. Obstacle: the origin stories contradict each other, and none leaves a record. The oldest trace is itself a story.
- **Intended feeling:** creepy hook → curiosity → small chill of reversal ("she was made by the living") → loop back into the knock with new meaning.
- **Learning takeaway:** No record of a real girl behind Hanako-san has been found. The oldest recorded version is a story someone told around 1948 in Iwate, written into a folklore collection decades later. Children's school lore, a teacher's 1990 book and mid-90s films carried her across Japan.
- **Twist (fair):** The hook and B03 define Hanako only through what children do and tell. The reveal (B05–B06) shows that this is all she has ever been: a told story, not a dead girl. B08 makes the reversal explicit and hedged ("อาจไม่เคยมีตัวตนจริง").

## Truth and ending

- **What is true within the video:** Hanako-san is folklore. The ritual and the "ค่ะ" reply are legend content (F005). The origin stories are circulated-only (F008). No documentary evidence of a real girl was found in the research leads (F008). The oldest recorded telling is testimony attributed to about 1948 in Iwate, published in Matsutani's collection in the 1980s–90s (F001). Tsunemitsu's 1990 book and the 1995 film are documented (F004, F012).
- **Ending chosen before clues:** she may never have existed; she persists because she keeps being told; the final line hands the viewer the knock, which is the hook.
- **Remaining uncertainty (kept visible):** all sources lead-only; village of the 1948 telling (Kurosawajiri vs Sawauchi) unresolved, so not named; the vol. 7 publication year is unknown, so VO says only "หลายสิบปีต่อมา"; whether Hanako is central in Tsunemitsu's 1990 book is unconfirmed (open question 2), so VO says "เรื่องพวกนี้" (school ghost stories), not "her"; "the book made her famous" is a commentator attribution, so VO says "ช่วยพา".
- **Not used:** F002 (white hand, "third Hanako") is kept to the gated insert `B06-OPT`; F010 (toilet-god dolls) and F009 are not used; F007 (bob, red skirt) is not in VO.
- **Brief tension (flag):** the brief's twist wording "the oldest version has no girl at all" rests on F002, which the brief also says not to rely on. The main script therefore lands the twist on F008 + F001 (no record of a girl; the oldest trace is a told story). `B06-OPT` adds the "no girl, only a hand" line if Boat inspects SRC001/SRC003 and supports it.

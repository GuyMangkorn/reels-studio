## Story core

- **Logline:** A Japanese school ghost has a precise ritual, a name and many death stories, yet when you look for the real girl behind her, no evidence of one has been found; what exists is a ghost story someone told around 1948 and the children who kept retelling it.
- **Central question:** Who was Hanako-san, and was she ever a real girl?
- **Intention / obstacle:** The viewer (stand-in investigator) wants to find the girl behind the ghost. Obstacle: the origin stories contradict each other, and no evidence for any of them has been found. The oldest known trace is itself a told story.
- **Intended feeling:** creepy hook → curiosity → small chill of reversal ("she was made by the living") → loop back into the knock with new meaning.
- **Learning takeaway:** No evidence of a real girl behind Hanako-san has been found. The oldest known version is a ghost story someone told around 1948 in Iwate, written into a folklore collection decades later. Children's school lore, a teacher's 1990 book and a mid-90s film carried her across Japan.
- **Twist (fair):** The hook and B03 define Hanako only through what children do and tell. The reveal (B05–B06) shows that this is all she has ever been: the record itself is a told ghost story, not news of a dead girl. B08 makes the reversal explicit and hedged ("อาจไม่เคยมีตัวตนจริง").

## Truth and ending

- **What is true within the video:** Hanako-san is folklore. The ritual and the "ค่ะ" reply are commonly told legend content (F005). The origin stories are circulated-only (F008). No evidence of a real girl was found in the research leads (F008). The oldest known telling is testimony attributed to about 1948 in Iwate, published in Matsutani's collection (series 1985–96, Chikuma reissue 2003) (F001). Tsunemitsu's 1990 book and the 1995 film are documented on leads (F004, F012).
- **Ending chosen before clues:** she may never have existed; she has never disappeared because generations kept telling her; the final line hands the viewer the knock, which is the hook.
- **Remaining uncertainty (kept visible):** all sources lead-only; village of the 1948 telling (Kurosawajiri vs Sawauchi) unresolved, so not named; the vol. 7 publication year is unconfirmed, so VO says only "หลายสิบปีต่อมา"; SRC002 and SRC003 may share one origin (Asato 2019, Matsutani), so they are not independent; "the book made her famous" is a commentator attribution, so VO says "ช่วยพา"; present-day playground telling is not claimed (SRC011 is a contrary lead).
- **Not used:** F002 (white hand, "third Hanako"): `B06-OPT` **rejected** (FC08). F010 (toilet-god dolls) and F009 are not used; F007 (bob, red skirt) is not in VO. F011 only in the optional line below.

This deliberately overrides the sheet's older elbow y and x without editing the sheet; Mark/Film should reconcile it later. The camera looks along −z. RR-KNOCK has focal length `1200 × 0.78 = 936 px`, principal point `(540, 2336 − 1200 × 1.45) = (540, 596)`, and elbow depth `0.78 − 0.42 = 0.36 m`. Thus x = `540 + 936 × .25 / .36 = 1190`, y = `596 + 936 × (1.45 − 1.00) / .36 = 1766`. These are pre-camera plate pixels; the plate's authored scale still applies.

Its elbow is held at the same body-relative depth .36 m (world z = 1.20, not z = .42 measured from the door) and x/y, yielding `(1190, 1820)` screen pixels. The unchanged fist size .80 projects a .085 m palm at depth `.085 × 936 / (190 × .80) = .523 m`, consistent with the sheet's .53 m. The elbow is a chosen projected point, not a measured human joint; `sleeveFrom` `(1300,2400)` is an offscreen continuation chosen to steer the visible sleeve.

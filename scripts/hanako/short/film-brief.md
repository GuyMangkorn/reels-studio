## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge:** a ritual (B01), a name and a hedge (B02–B03), contradictory origin stories (B04), no trace of a real girl (B05), the oldest record is itself a told ghost story (B06), how a story went national (B07), she lives only in retelling (B08), the knock again (B09).
- **Mood:** cold, quiet, school-after-hours dread that turns into curiosity, then a warm "understanding" chill. Never gore, never a ghost jump-scare.
- **Value/colour key:** low-key, desaturated cool mint-teal and grey tile (`B01`–`B05`), one **warm amber lamp** that arrives at `B06` ("understanding, not fear") and stays until the `B09` hold. **Red is reserved** for the "how she is usually pictured" doodle (skirt, `B03`/`B07`) so it reads as the only saturated accent. Reference palette ([production requirements](../../../docs/production-requirements.md)) guides skin/clothing continuity only; the grade is set here.
- **Conceal/reveal:** Hanako is **never** shown as a body, face or shadow-figure in the world. She is a voice (`B02`), a doodle labelled as popular image (`B03`, `B07`) and nothing at all in `B06` and `B08`. The reveal (`B05`–`B06`) is the absence: empty folders, an illegible page.
- **Camera language:** observational; a slow drift or push on every shot (default); the viewer is the stand-in investigator (first-person hand, no narrator on camera). One fixed vantage returns on purpose: **door 3** (`B01`, `B02`, `B08`, `B09`) so the loop reads.
- **Caption zone:** captions sit in the band y ≈ 860–1248, x 120–888 ([vertical safe zone](../../../docs/production-requirements.md#vertical-caption-safe-zone)). All story objects (door number, hand, labels, map pin, cards) live **above y ≈ 860**; the lower third is floor, tile or paper texture only (dark and quiet, so outline captions stay legible). Chrome (channel label, progress bar) keeps its default slots.

## Sound direction

Library checked: `/Users/mangkornkatawong/Documents/ChatGPT/sound-library/INDEX.md` (31 Oct 2026). Candidates are **not listened to**; Editor audits before use. IDs below are candidates only.

- **Music family / arc / ending:** sparse mystery. None for `B01`–`B02` (the knock and the hush are the hook). `B03` a slow minor music-box or low felt-piano figure (low energy), `B04` thinned to a held note, `B05` near-silence, `B06` a warmer, slightly brighter bed after "ไม่ใช่ข่าว", `B07` highest energy (light rhythmic, playful-eerie, ≈90–100 bpm explainer pulse), `B08` back to the `B03` motif, slower. **Music cuts out on "ยกมือขึ้นเคาะ"; the hold is room tone only, no stinger, no goodbye.** Candidates: `tension_drone-sparse-piano_01` (B03–B05, narration-safe), `bed_mystery-investigation_01` (B07, 100 bpm), `bed_sad-aftermath-ambient_01` (B08), `tone_thin-unresolved_01` (B01 sub entry).
- **Ambience:** the restroom is the home tone: tiled room, faint drip, fluorescent hum. Candidate `room_dark-empty-building_01` (loopable) as the base; the **same bed** runs `B01` frame 0 and the `B09` hold so the seam is continuous. Other locations are picture-only inserts (cards, map, files) and take the music, not a new ambience.
- **SFX language:** dry, soft, close, storybook-muffled. Knock x3 (`impact_knuckle-rap_01` copies at 0.45 s, the library note describes exactly this use). `B02` reverse-swell stinger into "ค่ะ" (reply is a distant girl's voice: legend dramatization, Gemini TTS, not the narrator). `B04` muffled low rumble (war story) and one muffled door-slam (second story), under a storybook filter; **no real siren, air raid or scream** (would assert a real death). `B05` paper/drawer foley then `reveal_riser-tension_01` ending on "ต้นเรื่อง". `B06` one low soft impact on "ไม่ใช่ข่าว". `B07` page-flip whooshes per date card, tiny projector click on "หนังของตัวเอง". `B08` the `B03` whispers return under "เด็กรุ่นแล้วรุ่นเล่า".
- **Silence policy:** silence before "ค่ะ"; ≈0.4 s silence after "อยู่ไหมคะ?"; near-silence on "ต้นเรื่อง"; brief silence after "มีตัวตนจริง…". Silence is carried by dropping the bed, not by dead digital zero.
- **Generation gaps (library has no match):** children's unintelligible whispers (loop-safe); music-box/felt-piano motif for `B03`/`B08`; tiled-restroom room tone with a single drip (if `room_dark-empty-building_01` is judged too generic); muffled low rumble that is not a seafloor/quake (`quake_seafloor-rumble-muffled_01` is the only near match and is sea-specific); paper/drawer foley; page-flip whooshes; projector click. ## Loop seam `B09`→`B01` (`B_loop`, knock)

- **Contract:** `B09` hold final frame = `B01` frame 0: same plate, same close camera on door 3, same POV hand pose (raised, fist ready). `B01` begins with the knock contact on frames 0–1.5 s (before VO), then pulls back. So the viewer's last image is the hand about to knock and the first image after the seam is the knock, which completes "…ยกมือขึ้นเคาะ".
- **Hold behaviour:** the ≈3 s hold is not a freeze: only the ambient clock runs (dust, lamp flicker, drip), per [DF-20261030-painted-story-hold-ambient-clock](../../../docs/dev-findings/DF-20261030-painted-story-hold-ambient-clock.md). Caption stays (subtitles from frame 0).
- **Light:** the `B09` lamp is amber and `B01` frame 0 is cold. Seam solution to confirm in the look frame: `B01` starts in the amber state and cools within ≈1 s with the knocks, or `B09` cools in the last 0.5 s. Film recommends cooling in `B09`'s last 0.5 s (the hold stays one continuous take). Fallback: accept a hard light jump on the seam.
- **Chrome:** the progress bar resets at the seam by design; confirm the Editor shows no flash.
- **Sound seam:** room-tone bed identical at both ends; no music on the hold; the knock is the first sound.

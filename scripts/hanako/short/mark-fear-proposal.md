1. **The door disappears for 53 s.** Fear devices live only in `B01`–`B02` (F0–F443, 14.8 s) and `B09` (F2302–F2596). From F443 to F2046 (62 % of the runtime) the restroom is never seen or heard: notebook, crayon storybook, cabinet, map, book, classroom, map, poster.

The script asked for this ("light, rhythmic explainer pulse"); it is working as written.
4. **The fear release comes at 48 s and never re-arms.** The twist (`B06-SH02`, F1430) is scored as relief, so `B07`–`B08` play as a history lesson; `B08`'s silent dip (F2127–F2147) has nothing in it.

## 4. What I would not do

- **Eye in the seam (`VD21b`), any shadow, hand or figure behind the door, a knock back from inside:** breaks "Hanako is never a body/face/shadow" and the fact frame ("no evidence of a real girl"); a knock-back also makes the loop answer itself. The eye stays a user-only override (requested exception).
- **Stingers, screams, hits on cuts, heartbeat under the VO:** jump-scare grammar the brief rules out (`film-brief.md` mood: "never a ghost jump-scare"); a heartbeat fights the voice and is a cliché.
- **Door/restroom cutaways inside `B05`/`B06`:** juxtaposing the door with «ยังไม่พบหลักฐาน» or the Iwate record implies a real-world cause (EP05); it also breaks the user-accepted dead stop.
- **Text glitches, red flashes, hook text in the hold:** red is reserved for the doodle; glitches read cheap and fight the captions; hook text in the hold breaks the F0 plate match.
- **Darkening every shot:** darkness alone is not suspense (cinematography principle 6) and would hurt the caption-zone and value hierarchy already measured.
- **Rewriting narration for scarier wording:** the facts are lead-only and Boat-checked; I9 keeps the words and changes only delivery.

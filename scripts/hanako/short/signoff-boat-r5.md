Whether the user's physical flyer is an original 1998 release item is unverified (provenance only from the user; rights responsibility recorded as the user's in the Boss sidecar).

Mismatch. The VO says "mid-90s… her own film", which points to the 1995 film. The picture shows a specific real artifact of a different film from 1998 (late 90s, a sequel, not her first film). Blurring is a concealment, not a correction. Severity: low for most Thai viewers (title unreadable), but it is a checkable factual mismatch for anyone who recognises the art, and it would be a wrong attribution in a fact-focused channel.

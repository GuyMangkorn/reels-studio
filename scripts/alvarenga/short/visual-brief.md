## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge / mood:** awe at the improvised survival (S04) → quiet grief at the empty seat (S05) → unease at an allegation that nobody else can check (S07–S08). The ending stays open; no image says he did it.
- **World:** one small open boat on one calm sea, reused in every beat (user). Story time passes by sky and light, not by place. The only other place is the Marshall Islands islet and shore (S06, S08).
- **Look (user's words: "2D pastel cut-out"):** flat paper-cut shapes in a soft pastel key (dusty blue, sage, peach, cream, one warm accent), each shape a separate card with a hard clean edge and a faint paper-edge shadow on the layer below; sea and sky are flat bands with very slight tint steps. Few texture marks. Reads as layered coloured paper, not watercolour mess. See VD01 for the engine mapping and the look check this needs.
- **Colour / value key by time of day** (carries story time, script continuity line): dawn peach and pale blue (S01, S02) → storm grey-violet (S03, still water) → bright day (S04) → dusk apricot to blue-grey (S05) → bright reef light, aqua and cream (S06) → dusk (S07, S08) → S01 tone (S09, loop seam). Value stays high and soft; the storm is darker but never black.
- **Camera language:** observational, eye level, fixed side-on plane. Every shot is a slow drift or push (default); no handheld, no whip, no fast move (user). Wide and small-figure framing for loneliness; close inserts only for the engine (S03), the cup and hands (S04), the hat (S05, S07, S08) and the document (S07).
- **Conceal / reveal:** the lawsuit stays out of picture and text until S07. Ezequiel's death is never drawn as a body (R1.2, user): he slumps from exhaustion and goes down **behind the near gunwale**, out of sight, and the hat on the empty seat is the last image (S05). No frame shows a prone figure. The hat is the continuity device that carries CL03 (S02 worn → S05 on the empty seat → S07 in the boat → S08 drifting away). It is a visual device of the video, not a factual claim about a hat.
- **Must not be seen** (script, user, monetization): a body or anyone lying dead (no prone figure in any frame, R1.2), burial, butchery, any wound, gore or dripping or splashing blood (R1.2, user: a flat red or yellow liquid in the S04-SH03 cup is allowed, not a kids' clip; Film uses yellow-amber, see VD07), a court, judge or gavel, anything implying he ate his crewmate, big waves, fast motion. Also not seen: the lawyer, a real face, a map with a route line (distance was cut, F005).

## Sound direction

- **Music family:** sparse, warm, low-energy ambient: airy pad plus felt piano, no drums, no melody lead, narration-safe, calm-sad rather than horror. Tempo about 60–70 bpm if any pulse; mostly unmetered. Arc: none at S01's first line, a low pad enters under "14 เดือน" and stays low through S02 → music out for S03 → warmer low pad S04 → single sustained low note S05 → gentle lift S06 (relief, not triumph) → out at the S07 pause, low riser, muted thud → sparse unresolved figure S08 → resolves into the S01 ambience bed at S09. Energy never rises above medium-low; the twist is carried by silence, not volume.
- **Ending plan:** `B_loop` (profile default; Film confirms, Editor checks the seam): S09 hold (about 2.6 s) fades the music to the S01 ambience bed so frame 0 of S01 starts on the same sea bed; no end stinger. The S09 picture matches the S01 framing and tone (VD09).
- **Ambience per location:** calm open sea (soft lapping, light creak of the hull, faint far gull) in S01, S02, S04, S05, S09; storm (rain and low wind over a **calm** water bed; no breaking-wave roar) in S03; reef and shore (small surf on a reef, a few birds) in S06 and S08.
- **SFX language:** natural, sparse, soft, paper-like. Paper slide for name cards and the document (S02, S07), outboard sputter then stop (S03), rain drops into a container (S04), one soft wing flap (S04), muted low thud on "เพื่อนร่วมเรือ" (S07, FC08: no heartbeat, siren, crowd or horror cue). No gross-out sound, no drip or pour for the cup in S04.

## Technical handoff

**Projection contract:** spaces: `world` (metres, origin mid-hull at the waterline, +X right, +Y up) and `screen` (px, origin top-left, 1080×1920). Camera: one fixed orthographic-style side-on plane at eye level; horizon at about y 760 px; pixel↔world about 150 px per metre for wide shots (so a 6 m boat is about 900 px wide and Alvarenga about 270 px tall, which must be checked against the lead-size rule), about 300 px per metre for the S04 vignette, and about 450 px per metre for the S03 engine insert. All values are estimates, set by the scene sheets. No perspective depth; layers by painter's order (sky, sea band, far shore, boat, hull-front water edge, foreground silhouette). Shared projection owner: the episode module for sets and boat; the kit `Proj<S>` and `Camera` (`camera.tsx`) for the shot camera. Transform owner: the camera layer moves the picture; the boat has only a tiny sine bob (<1 degree) and no travel.

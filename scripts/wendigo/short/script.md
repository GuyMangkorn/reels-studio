## Story core

**Logline:** A man snowbound in a northern forest hears a voice outside his tent asking "หิวไหม…" every night — until he follows the footprints and finds the voice is his own. Then the video turns: this was fiction, but the fear comes from a real tradition, the windigo of Algonquian-speaking peoples, where an ordinary person can become the monster.

**Central question (hook):** Who is whispering "หิวไหม…" outside the tent? → after the turn: what is the Wendigo really, in the tradition it comes from?

**Intention / obstacle (fiction):** the man wants to survive the storm with his companion; the obstacle is hunger, which turns out to be inside him rather than outside the tent.

**Feeling:** isolation → dread (something circles the tent) → cold shock (it is him) → calm, serious curiosity in the fact section → a quiet chill at the end (the monster is a want that is never satisfied).

**Knowledge takeaway:** In the tradition of Algonquian-speaking peoples such as the Ojibwe and Cree, the windigo is a cannibal being of winter and hunger, said never to be satisfied; in many tellings a *person* can become one. The belief was serious enough to appear in a real court case around 1879 (Swift Runner). Some Indigenous writers today use it as a figure of greed.

**Plot function of the knowledge:** the fact section re-reads the cold open. "A person can become one" (F004) is exactly what happened to the fictional man; "ยิ่งกินยิ่งหิว" (F003) explains the voice. (R3: the F005 "no antlers" re-read was cut with S02-B04.)

## Truth and ending

- **Inside the fiction (S01):** there is no creature outside. Starving for days, the man has been walking around the tent at night himself (his own footprints) and whispering "หิวไหม…" himself. The last fiction image is him standing over his sleeping companion. Nothing further happens on screen; no attack, no blood, no eating is shown or said.
- **Real facts (S02–S03):** limited to what the ledger allows, hedged as it says (see [claim mapping](#claim-mapping)). Swift Runner's windigo explanation is stated as **his claim**; the court convicted him. No victim count, no execution detail, no hanging sound.
- **Remaining uncertainty (kept visible):** all sources are lead-only; F005 (weakest) is no longer used (R3); F003 "ยิ่งกินยิ่งหิว" is hedged as `เล่ากันว่า`; the mechanism of transformation varies by community (F004), so the script says `ในหลายเรื่องเล่า` and names no single rule.
- **Ending:** the last line returns the fiction's whisper as a metaphor (`ปีศาจตัวนี้… แค่มีเสียงเล็กๆ ในใจ ที่คอยถามว่า…`) and seams into the frame-0 whisper `หิวไหม…`. See [ending proposal](#ending-proposal).

**Twist logic (fair):** planted by CL01 (the voice comes "every night" and only he hears it), CL02 (footprints circle the tent every morning although nobody has come), CL03 (`เรา`: he is not alone; his companion sleeps), and CL04 (the whisper is cast in the man's own voice, whispered). Payoff in S01-B03: the footprints fit his boots and the whisper comes from his own mouth. No new information is introduced at the twist.

## Ending proposal

- **Selected: `C_seamless` only** (user choice on R1; `B_loop` and its appended whisper removed in R2). The last line and the hook are a pair: `…แค่มีเสียงเล็กๆ ในใจ ที่คอยถามว่า…` → (seam) → `หิวไหม…` → `เสียงนั้น… ถามผมทุกคืน`. On replay the opening whisper reads as the "small voice inside" the narrator just named, and the man's line becomes the answer to the narrator's metaphor. Last ~0.5 s speech-free; last picture = frame-0 tent composition; wind level matched at the seam.
- **FC04 seam check (`ในใจ` adopted):** the pair still closes grammatically (`ที่คอยถามว่า…` → `หิวไหม…`), and `เสียงนั้น` in the man's first line now refers back to a voice *inside him*. The frame-0 whisper is still placed as if outside the canvas, so on first viewing the hook is unchanged (no spoiler: `ในใจ` is only heard at the end); on replay the gap between "in the mind" and "outside the tent" restates the twist (CL04). It also removes the risk that the line reads as the internet "voice-mimicry" lore. Cost about +0.3 s, paid by trims.
- `A_fade` and `B_loop` are not used.

## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge/feeling:** isolation → dread (something circles the tent) → cold shock (it is him) → calm, serious curiosity (the real tradition) → a quiet chill (the monster is a want that is never satisfied). The fiction is one tent, one man, one night; the fact section is wide, quiet, daylight-pale, and never uses horror grammar on the real people or case.
- **Value/colour key (estimated hexes, not yet seen in a frame):** S01 low-key night: snow and canvas deep blue `#0f1c2e` / `#27405c`, **one warm lantern** `#e8a64d` (glow peak ≤ 0.3, kit `glow.tsx`), carrying the man and the canvas. S02–S03 documentary register: overcast pale blue-grey `#8fa4b5` / `#c9d3da`, desaturated, no warm light, so the register change is felt without sound. The last picture returns to S01 night (seam).
- **Camera language:** project default, eye level, a slow push or drift on every shot, no handheld. Fiction: tight and low (canvas, boots, lips) so the "creature" is never framed because nothing is outside. Fact section: wide, still, observational. Two vantages are returned on purpose: the tent-wall frame of `S01-B01-SH01` (frame 0 = last frame) and the silhouette in the tent flap (`S01-B03-SH05` → callback in `S02-B03-SH02`).
- **Conceal/reveal:** there is no creature in the fiction; the reveal is that the footprints fit his boot and the whisper comes from his own lips (`CL01`/`CL02`/`CL04`). The man stays human-looking; no transformation, blood, attack or eating (script rule). The windigo is never drawn clearly (S02-B03), no antlers in the traditional-belief imagery.
- **Marker and chrome (decision `VD10`):** `เรื่องแต่ง` from frame 0 through `S01-B03` (hook must not be covered); `ข้อมูลจริงจากตำนานและประวัติศาสตร์` from `S02-B01` to the end; frame 0 of the loop restores `เรื่องแต่ง` automatically. Chrome (progress bar) **off** except the marker, as for KSO `VD12` (a bar flashes at the seam); proposed, user not yet asked. Name card `Ojibwe: wiindigoo · Cree: wīhtikōw` on `S02-B02` (script, user choice on R1).
- **Caption zone:** outline Thai captions in x 120–888, bottom edge ≤ y 1248 ([vertical safe zone](../../../docs/production-requirements.md#vertical-caption-safe-zone)); band y ≈ 860–1248. All story art (faces, lips, boot, silhouette, map highlight, skull) sits above y ≈ 860; the lower third is dark snow, floor or canvas texture only. Marker at the top area; name card above the caption band and below the marker (estimated y ≈ 720–800), clear of the highlighted map region.
- **Cultural framing (delivery.md, script):** the real tradition belongs to Algonquian-speaking peoples (Ojibwe, Cree). No headdress, war paint, costume or "Indian" iconography; no tribal boundary lines without a Boat-sourced map; no portrait or depiction of Swift Runner, his family or the victims, no re-enactment, remains, gallows or rope. **The fictional man is deliberately not coded as Indigenous** (see `VD04`), the fiction's place is never named, so the video cannot read as "a Native man becomes the monster". The Cree man of the 1879 case is not drawn as a monster, nor drawn at all.

## Answers to Preaw's questions

- **Q2 (B04 "หมดแล้วไม่มีใครเติม"):** acceptable as written; the softer alternative is not required. The story has already said "แล้วไฟก็ดับทั้งตึก" (B03), so in the story the pumps are unpowered, and the general mechanism is supported (`SRC008`). In a real building whose generator does run the pumps, the tip still errs on the safe side: viewers store water they turn out not to need. The generator caveat (GAP01) is a known limit that doesn't need narrating. If time allows, "ไฟดับทั้งตึก ปั๊มก็หยุด" is a cheap optional tightening; it is not required.
- **Q3 (B05 "ขวดไว้ดื่ม ถังไว้ราดชักโครก"):** acceptable and `supported`. It closely mirrors `SRC013`: pitchers, bottles and jugs for drinking and cooking; bathtubs or large buckets for manual flushing. It does not compress anything misleadingly, because B04 already says to cover every container.
- **Q4 (B07 "คาร์บอนมอนอกไซด์ ฆ่าคุณได้ก่อนซอมบี้"):** does not overstate. "ได้" (can) matches `SRC005` ("If CO levels are high enough, you may become unconscious or die"). "ก่อนซอมบี้" compares the risk against the fictional threat, which is dramatic framing (`not-factual`), not a factual claim about speed. No change.
- **B06 "สองวัน":** `supported` for a packed freezer with the door kept closed (`SRC003`, "about 48 hours"). The "ราว" hedge and the "อัดแน่น" (full) condition are both present. Optional, non-blocking: the figure is best read as an upper bound in a hot unit with the air-conditioning off, so "อย่างมากราวสองวัน" would be more precise. The source gives no ambient-temperature condition, so this is not required. See `FC-TH-06b` for the separate, blocking issue in the next phrase.
- **B09 countdown and ZC-C22 (rationing):** no rationing is implied. "น้ำเหลือสองถัง / ปลากระป๋องสี่กระป๋อง / แบตเตอรี่สิบสองเปอร์เซ็นต์" counts fictional supplies, and no line tells the viewer to drink less. `not-factual`, and it passes. A clarity point, not a factual one: in R1, "ถัง" means a bucket (B04, B05, B09) and also the roof tank and the cistern (B04 "ถังบนดาดฟ้า", B05 "ถังพักน้ำ"). ## SRC022 handling

`SRC022` (CDC, "Four Steps to Food Safety", updated 24 Nov 2025) was opened and read on 28 Oct 2026, so it is `inspected`. It is cited by the R2 B06 claim rows, takeaway (3) and the B06 visual constraint.

- **What it supports:** raw meat, poultry and fish must be cooked to safe internal temperatures, and perishables should not sit out for more than 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F). That is why the "eat first" order must be limited to ready-to-eat food while B07 forbids cooking.
- **What it does not support:** it says nothing about power outages or the 4-hour fridge and 48-hour freezer figures, which still rest on `SRC003`. The script claims no new number from `SRC022`.
- **Registry status:** it is registered here and referenced by the scripts. It has **not** been added to [../research.md](../research.md), which stays `ZCONDO-RESEARCH-R1`. Boat adds `SRC022` to the registry the next time the research packet is revised, and nothing in either script depends on that edit. The scripts' header lines ("`SRC022` (added in fact-check.md)") are accurate.

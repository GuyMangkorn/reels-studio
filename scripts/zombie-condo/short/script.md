## Story core

- **Logline:** คุณเปิดประตูห้องไปทิ้งขยะ เจอซอมบี้ปลายทางเดิน ต้องขังตัวเองในห้องคอนโดตอนไฟดับ แล้วใช้ทุกอย่างในห้องให้อยู่ได้นานที่สุด จนวันที่สามมีเสียงเคาะประตูที่ไม่ควรมี
- **Central question:** ติดอยู่ในห้องคอนโดตอนไฟดับ คุณจะอยู่ได้นานแค่ไหน และต้องทำอะไรก่อน?
- **Intention / obstacle:** you want to last as long as possible; the obstacle is that the blackout quietly starts every clock at once (finite tank water, fridge cold, no safe way to cook) while the corridor is closed to you.
- **Intended feeling:** jolt (hook) → urgency (water clock) → small "aha" (the cistern) → grim control (food order, no stove) → isolation (balcony) → unease and choice (the knock).
- **Learning takeaway (4 points):** (1) after a blackout the tap may still run, but in many high-rises that is the finite remainder in the roof tank, so fill and cover containers now; (2) the toilet cistern, not the bowl, can be clean spare water if clear and untreated, and stored bucket water can flush the toilet; (3) a closed fridge stays cold about 4 hours and a full freezer about 2 days, so eat the ready-to-eat fridge food first and keep cans for last (no raw perishables eaten uncooked); (4) never light a camp gas stove indoors; eat no-cook food.

## Truth and ending

- **What happens in the story (fiction):** day one, morning: the viewer opens the door, sees an infected figure, retreats, barricades. The power then fails building-wide (story-world cause unstated). The viewer stores water, sets up the bathroom, sorts food, rejects the stove. Night one: balcony view of a dark, burning city. Day three: supplies counted down; a soft knock at the door.
- **Twist logic (fair, open):** the infected **pound** on the door (planted in B02, heard). On day three the sound is a **knock** (B09). The sound rule (CL04, “พวกมันตามเสียง”) is planted in B02, so calling out is a real risk; the pounding-vs-knock contrast was audible from beat 2: whoever is outside is not one of them, or is something new. The ending does not resolve who it is; it hands the viewer a three-way decision (open the removable barricade from B02, answer and risk the sound rule, or stay silent), with no peephole to check (CL05). User confirmed this unresolved-knock ending on 28 Oct 2026.
- **Verified facts used:** ZC-C01/C02/C03 (water mechanism and filling), ZC-C05/C21 (cistern, not bowl), ZC-C07/C08 (bucket flush, conditional on drains), ZC-C10 (fridge/freezer hold times + derived order), ZC-C13 (no camp stove indoors), ZC-C18 (removable barricade, fire caveat), ZC-C15 (cover stored water, without numbers).
- **Uncertainty kept visible:** no water duration is stated (GAP01); "หลายแห่ง" not "every condo"; hold times say "ราว"; the flush works "ถ้าท่อยังดี".
- **Cautions followed:** no indoor camp-stove cooking (stove appears only as a warning); "รองใส่ทุกอย่าง" with bucket/pot/bottles shown, rather than bathtub; no hour count for water; no weapons or violence instructions; no building-code rule about door swing; drinking water is not rationed (the countdown counts supplies, it never tells the viewer to drink less; ZC-C22).

## 2026-10-29 Boss: v2 eye shapes, lid modes and brow presets (research, presets, picks)

- **Research** (standard web search + two page fetches; sources are tutorials of mixed quality and mostly qualitative; they give no sizes, so every number below is my choice): (1) https://tips.clip-studio.com/ko-kr/articles/2632?org=1 : larger eyes and lower placement read younger, smaller eyes and higher placement read more mature; an upturned outer corner reads confident, a droopy one gentle or lazy; a smaller iris reads surprised. (2) https://tips.clip-studio.com/zh-tw/articles/6622?org=1 (search summary only, page not opened) : the upper lid does most of the mood work: a sharp downward angle reads angry, a high rounded arch surprised, a flat lid tired. (3) https://la.disneyresearch.com/wp-content/uploads/Designing-Animated-Characters-for-Children-of-Different-Ages-Paper.pdf (search summary): characters for younger children get larger and rounder eyes, so a calm adult wants smaller, less round eyes. (4) https://georgiestclair.substack.com/p/creative-snack-draw-expressive-cartoon and https://medibangpaint.com/en/use/2020/08/how-to-draw-character-like-cartoon-facial-expression (search summaries): a simple arc brow reads surprise, a reversed arc worry; straight brows read serious or angry; sad = brows lowered and close to the eyes; small changes of angle or thickness change the read. (5) https://polycount.com/discussion/comment/2692999 and a design article from the same search (summary): one main highlight placed on the light side, not a centred pupil, one-eye-width spacing as a baseline (closer = intense, wider = ethereal).

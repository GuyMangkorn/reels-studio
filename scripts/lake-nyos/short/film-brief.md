## Visual intent

**Audience knowledge.** The viewer starts with a death toll and a locked-room question (nothing broken, everything that breathes dead), is invited to guess a monster, watches the guesses fail the clue test, and learns the killer *did* come out of the lake: dissolved gas. Mood: quiet dread → imagined shiver (`B05`) → the click of `B09` → sober respect → open unease at `B13`.

**Colour and value key (VD02).** Real-event world: low-key night blue-green (moonlit, cool) with small warm lamp windows as the only warm points; dawn after is desaturated grey-green; day (`B12`) is clean, warm, higher key (the one lift). Rust red-brown is reserved for the lake colour clue (`B08`) so it is a fresh hue event. The CO₂ haze is a pale cool grey-white translucent layer, never green or toxic-looking. Imagination world (`B05`, card in `B07`, reprise in `B09`): monochrome charcoal/sketch on dark paper with white scratch highlights, deliberately a different material from the painted real look.

**Camera language.** Default WORLD FOG: eye level or slightly elevated, observational, a slow drift or push on every shot, repeated vantages only where repetition carries the story (here: the `B01` night village, which returns under the haze in `B11` and is the loop target). The lake is "present but unremarked" before `B08`. Map and diagram shots (`B02`, `B10`, `B11`) are flat, drawn, and stay readable first (they are the explainer beats).

**Conceal/reveal constraints (carried from script).** The word/idea CO₂, any haze or gas drawing: not before `B09` (`B10`–`B11` explain it; `B01`–`B08` show only lake, village, animals, houses). The lake is the cause only from `B08`. The creature exists only in sketch style inside the "จินตนาการ" window: `B05`, its cross-out card in `B07`, the ≈1 s dissolve in `B09`; never attacking, never near a person, never again after `B09`. No eruption, lava, explosion or flame-going-out. Trigger stays a question mark.

**Respect rules (sensitivity note, delivery.md).** No bodies shown at all (not even distant human shapes lying down); animals only as small distant silhouettes or fallen birds as tiny shapes; no faces of victims or survivors; no skin lesions, screams, panic crowds, named people; no flag/country in the rumour beat; spirits shown as a calm dusk lake. The creature is never comic. Cattle in `B03` are distant, still, small. A real-event frame never reuses the sketch material, and the sketch never appears on a real location's people.

### `B06` assessment (priority item)

1. **"ชาวบ้าน" (localising a Grassfields belief): fair.** Nyos lies in Cameroon's Northwest (Grassfields) region (`SRC002` p3 "northwest area of Cameroon"), which is the area Shanklin's Grassfields lake lore (`SRC009`) covers. `SRC010` also reports Nyos-area residents giving supernatural explanations (the late "Lake Chief"). In Thai, "ชาวบ้านเชื่อว่า" is the normal frame for a traditional or collective belief; it does not assert that every villager held it. The "ความเชื่อท้องถิ่น" tag keeps it a reported belief, not the narrator's claim.
2. **Dropped "บางแห่ง": no overstatement in context.** Coming after `B05`, the spoken "ทะเลสาบ" reads as *this* lake, and `SRC010` (residents' supernatural attributions) plus `SRC022` (lead) point to Nyos-specific spirit belief. Read generically ("lakes have spirits"), the line matches `SRC009`'s regional category of lakes as spirit homes. Either reading is a fair report of a belief. "บางแห่ง" (+2 syllables, at or over the cap) is **not required**.
3. **Does `F023`/`SRC010` also support it?** Yes, partly. `SRC010`'s supernatural attributions (spirits, ancestors, the Lake Chief) are reports from after the event, and they back a belief that the lake holds spirits. The line makes no claim that villagers *blamed* the deaths on the spirit, so it needs no more than that.
4. **Limits kept by the script (required to stay):** no named Nyos legend, spirit, chief or ritual; no claim that the lore records past eruptions (low confidence, `F024`); no spirit image. Confidence stays **medium**: `SRC009` and `SRC010` are known only from abstracts and snippets, and `SRC022` repeats them rather than corroborating them independently.

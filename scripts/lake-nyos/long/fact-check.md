### Non-blocking

- **`LR01` (recommendation, the smell):** the VO is accurate, but after `B03` ("ได้กลิ่นเหมือนไข่เน่า หรือดินปืน") and the `B10` reveal (CO2), a viewer may conclude *the CO2 smelled of gunpowder*. This answers open question 1 with evidence. If Preaw adds a line, supported wording (≈+3.5–4 s est., needs recheck): "ส่วนกลิ่นนั้น… อาจไม่มีอยู่จริง คนที่สูดก๊าซนี้เข้าไปมาก ๆ มักรู้สึกว่าได้กลิ่นแบบนั้น". Keep "อาจ": it is the 1987 team's medical interpretation from one primary source (medium), not a measurement.

Accurate update: *the USGS team attributes the reported smell (and warmth) to CO2 exposure itself (olfactory/sensory hallucination); lake waters had low sulfur and there was no toxicologic evidence for H2S (`Q10`, PDF p13, p22).* This is also contrary evidence to `research.md` `F016`'s "may come from trace sulfur" lead.

## Story core

**Logline:** A small lift in an old building stalls at 3 p.m. and the lights die for three seconds. Five strangers are inside, each in a hurry for a reason of their own. Something from outside Earth, hiding in the mirror wall, takes the place of one of them. It can copy anything, but it copies from the reflection, so its left and right come out swapped. The viewer hears the same facts as the narrator and must name the copy by shirt colour before the reveal.

**Central question (hook, fixed by the user):** `1 ใน 5 คนในห้องนี้ไม่ใช่มนุษย์` — which one?

**Intention / obstacle:** the viewer is the detective. Intention: name the copy. Obstacle: after the lights come back, all four humans do something that looks wrong (one has moved, one whispers into a corner, one digs in a bag, one cannot stop staring at the mirror), while the copy is the calm, kind one who helps. The decisive details are said once each, flatly, in the middle of the clip.

**Characters (one stake each, all stated before the blackout):**
- `เสื้อแดง` — his mother has just gone into hospital; he is on the phone with her.
- `เสื้อเหลือง` — afraid of small spaces; took the lift only because she is late for a job interview.
- `เสื้อเขียว` — married yesterday; new ring on his left ring finger; analog watch on his left wrist.
- `เสื้อฟ้า` — student, must hand in work by 4 p.m.
- `เสื้อชมพู` — first date tonight; touching up lipstick at the mirror; hair clip on the right.

**Feeling:** cold hook → a small, ordinary room full of people in a hurry → the rule (a fair challenge) → claustrophobic unease when the lights return and everyone looks guilty → the itch of "I heard something wrong" → recap, then a pause to commit a guess → click of recognition as the reveal walks the clues → a last chill about what the copy will do next (optional `L39`).

**Knowledge takeaway:** none required by the profile. Side value (not a lesson line): a mirror swaps left and right, so a clock face in a mirror reads as a different time (3 o'clock looks like 9 o'clock). The reveal uses this knowledge, so it has a plot function.

**Twist logic:** the narrator never hides a clue. It states the flaw rule by about 18 s and restates it in the recap. It plants Green's watch **and** ring on his left side and the time (`บ่ายสามโมงตรง`), and Blue's 4 p.m. deadline keeps the afternoon in the viewer's ear. After the lights, it says in flat action lines that Green raised his **right** wrist and said it was "nearly nine", and later that his wedding ring is on his **right** ring finger while he comforts Yellow. Each line has an innocent first reading (an answer to a question; a kind gesture). On rewatch they are the whole solution. The reveal names the one mistake (`L25`: left/right swapped) and walks its three symptoms in story order (wrist → clock → ring), so "only one mistake" (`L02`) holds (N01).

## Truth and ending

- **What actually happens (in-story):** at 15:00 the lift stalls between floors; the lights go out for 3 s. A mirror-skinned alien that was hiding in the mirror back wall takes the place of the man in the green shirt, copying him from his reflection. Everything about the copy is the human Green with left and right swapped: his analog watch (index marks only, no numerals, human and copy alike) is now on his right wrist and its face is mirrored; his plain wedding band is now on his right ring finger. When he reads the watch by hand position a couple of minutes after 15:00 he reads about 8:58, "เกือบเก้าโมง". The mirrored watch runs counterclockwise, so the reading stays at 12:00 − t. What happened to the real Green is not stated or shown (implication only, CS04).
- **The four humans:**
  - Yellow is claustrophobic (stated before the blackout). In the dark she groped her way to the door panel; when the lights return she is no longer where she stood, and is jamming the emergency button. She does not force the doors (F002).
  - Pink was at the mirror putting on lipstick and, **just as the lights were going out**, saw her reflection move before she did (the alien on the glass), so she cannot stop staring at the mirror. After the lights return the mirror is normal; she speaks in the past tense (`ตอนไฟจะดับ`). Her hair clip is on her own right before and after.
  - Red is on a call with his mother (in hospital) through a single earphone in his left ear. The signal drops in the stall; he turns into the corner, whispering, ignoring everyone. The earphone is still in his left ear.
  - Blue must hand in work by 4 p.m.; his phone battery is dead; he digs in the backpack on his right shoulder (still the right shoulder) and asks the time.
- **Why nobody reacts to "nearly nine":** Pink's whisper about the mirror follows at once and pulls every head toward the glass (`L19` says so: `ทุกคนหันไปทางเสื้อชมพู`, FC10); Yellow's alarm is still ringing. Nobody in the lift follows up, and Blue gets no reaction (story beat, see edge case 9).
- **Ending (E1):** NARR restates the rule and tells the viewer how to check it (compare left/right before the blackout and after the lights), then challenges the viewer to type the shirt colour, a 2.0 s speech-free pause, then the reveal names Green, names the one mistake (left/right swapped) and walks the three symptoms in order. `L39` (kept, user decision 2026-11-02) closes on the real Green's stake (his wife). The clip ends on Green's calm face and a held stinger tail. No loop seam, no "ตอนหน้า", no second-copy hint.
- **Remaining uncertainty:** none in the fiction. F004 is sourced in the fact-check (SRC018 chirality, SRC019 no-numeral mirrored clock). The ring clue uses the same chirality fact (F004a) and needs Boat's check of the wording only.

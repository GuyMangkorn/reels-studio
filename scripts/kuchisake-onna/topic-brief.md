# KSO-BRIEF-R1 — "ฉันสวยไหม?"

- Deliverable: `KSO-EP01-SHORT`; see [delivery](delivery.md). Short, about 50 s.
- Language: English technical material; Thai spoken narration/dialogue/captions.
- Genre: fictional horror, tense and frightening ("ระทึก สยอขวัญ"). Story built on the Japanese urban legend Kuchisake-onna (the slit-mouthed woman).

## Legend material (folklore, commonly told)

- A woman in a surgical mask, often in a long coat, approaches someone alone at night and asks "Am I pretty?" (わたし、きれい?).
- "No" → she kills them. "Yes" → she removes the mask, revealing a mouth slit ear to ear, and asks "Even now?" / "How about now?"; "No" → killed; "Yes" → she makes their mouth like hers.
- Commonly told escapes: an ambiguous answer ("so-so", "average"); throwing candy (bekkō-ame) or money to distract her; saying "pomade" three times. Use at most one or two; the script need not explain all.
- Japanese setting is fine; a Thai-set retelling is also allowed if it stays recognisably this legend. Preaw chooses and records the reason.

## Requirements for the script

- Hook in the first 1–2 seconds; a first-person or close second-person POV is welcome.
- Build dread with fair, specific sensory detail (footsteps, mask, streetlight, the question repeated). One genuine scare/reveal beat, not gore for its own sake: implication over explicit mutilation; no on-screen killing.
- Loop-friendly or unresolved horror ending (e.g., the question asked again, now to the viewer). Hold the final beat about 3 s.
- Mark lines needing acted delivery (whisper, breath, trembling) for Gemini TTS; mark the woman's voice separately from the narrator.
- Present the legend as legend; make no claim that events happened.
- Sound and visual handoff notes per beat (Light tier: Film writes the brief later, not authorized now).

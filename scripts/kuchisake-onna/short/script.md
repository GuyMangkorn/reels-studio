## Setting choice

Japan, a narrow residential street at night. The narrator is a young Thai woman working a part-time job in Japan (`ฉัน`, female particles). **Reason:** the legend stays recognisably Japanese (street, mask, the Japanese question first), while a Thai narrator makes first-person Thai speech natural and lets the woman's switch from Japanese to Thai become a fair, specific scare (S02-B02).

## Story core and underlying truth

**Logline:** On the night a coworker tells her the Kuchisake-onna legend, a Thai woman in Japan meets the masked woman, survives with the ambiguous answer, and realises why her coworker looked relieved after telling the story — so she tells it to you.

**Central question (hook):** Who is asking "ฉันสวยไหม?" and why must the viewer not answer yet? **Intention / obstacle:** the narrator wants to get home; the woman blocks the street and demands an answer where both "yes" and "no" are fatal. **Feeling:** unease → creeping dread → shock at the reveal → brief relief → cold realisation that the viewer is next. **Knowledge takeaway:** none required by the profile; the only "rule" spoken is the legend's commonly told one (answer neither "pretty" nor "not pretty"), presented as what the coworker told her.

**Truth within the story:** The masked woman is real inside the fiction. The narrator survives by answering `ก็… ธรรมดา` (so-so). The woman does not vanish: in this story she moves on to whoever hears the story next. The coworker passed her on to the narrator by telling it; the narrator passes her on to the viewer. Nobody is killed or mutilated on screen.

**Ending:** Unresolved. The narrator apologises to the viewer; the woman's voice, now close to the viewer, says `ถึงตาเธอแล้ว…` (your turn). Paired with the opening question for the loop ([loop-friendly writing](../../../docs/scriptwriting-principles.md#loop-friendly-writing)).

**Twist logic (fair):** planted by (CL01) the coworker's relieved smile after telling the story, (CL02) the narrator's opening order to the viewer `อย่าเพิ่งตอบ… ฟังให้จบก่อน`, which only makes full sense once we learn she is passing the woman on and giving the viewer the rule first. Payoff in S03-B01/B02. No new information is introduced at the twist that the viewer has not already heard.

## Full spoken script and required story events

**Only the Thai lines labeled VO/dialogue below are spoken.** English notes are production directions. Captions follow the spoken Thai verbatim, except the one separately recorded case in S02-B02 (Japanese line). From frame 0 show a discreet marker `เรื่องแต่ง • จากตำนานญี่ปุ่น`; it is not spoken.

**Voices**

- `NARR` — the narrator, a young Thai woman (`ฉัน`). Hushed, close storytelling, as if telling a secret late at night. Per the [one-narrator rule](../../../.agents/skills/gemini-tts/SKILL.md#narration-one-narrator) keep one voice and one base style for all NARR lines (suggested base style: `hushed, tense late-night storytelling, close to the mic`); the per-line notes below are **inline tags or alternative takes** for the Editor to try, not a different style per beat. Where a line needs a clearly different read (the slip in S02-B03), it may be a separate short take with the same voice.
- `WOMAN` — Kuchisake-onna. A different voice from NARR, clearly distinguishable: lower, slower, breathy, very close, with an unnerving softness (never shouting, never a monster growl). Generate per line (acted dialogue). Voice choice and approval by ear belong to the Editor and the user.

**Event:** Black-blue night frame; extreme close on a white surgical mask edge and a strand of long black hair, too close to the lens, as if at the viewer's ear. The mouth stays covered.

**WOMAN** — style: `slow, intimate breathy whisper right at the ear`; tags: `<whispering>`

> ฉัน… สวยไหม?

**NARR** — tags: `<short pause>` before; urgent, low, not loud

> อย่าเพิ่งตอบ… ฟังให้จบก่อน

### S01 / B02 — The story at work, 3.5–13 s estimated

**Event:** Warm-but-dim back room of a small shop or izakaya near closing. A Japanese coworker (young woman, apron) tells the story with a flashlight-under-the-chin feel, no props needed. On `ยิ้ม` the coworker's smile reads as relief, not teasing (CL01). Narrator seen from behind or over the shoulder.

**NARR**

> คืนนั้นก่อนเลิกงานที่ญี่ปุ่น เพื่อนที่ร้านเล่าตำนานให้ฉันฟัง… คุจิซาเกะอนนะ ผู้หญิงใส่หน้ากาก ที่ถามแค่คำถามเดียว

> ห้ามตอบว่าสวย… ห้ามตอบว่าไม่สวย เล่าจบ เพื่อนก็ยิ้ม… เหมือนคนที่เพิ่งโล่งใจ

### S02 / B01 — Walking home, 13–24 s estimated

**Event:** Narrow residential street, vending machine glow far behind, wires overhead. Narrator walks alone. Footsteps behind her; she stops; the second set stops one step late. She keeps going; the following steps fade out. Ahead, a streetlight flickers; under it stands a tall woman in a long beige coat and a white mask, still, facing her. The woman never moves fast on screen; she is simply *there* (the fair dread: the steps were behind, she is ahead).

**NARR** — tags: `<short pause>` at each `…`; breathing slightly quicker

> ฉันเดินกลับห้องคนเดียว มีเสียงรองเท้าเดินตามหลังมา… ฉันหยุด มันก็หยุด

> แล้วเสียงนั้นก็หายไป… ใต้เสาไฟข้างหน้า มีผู้หญิงใส่โค้ทยาว ใส่หน้ากากสีขาว… ยืนรอฉันอยู่

### S02 / B02 — The question, 24–31 s estimated

**Event:** Medium shot, the woman under the flickering light; her eyes above the mask are calm and fixed. On the second question she has taken one step closer (one heel click), framing tighter. Narrator's face in fear, lips pressed shut.

**WOMAN** — Japanese; style: `soft, polite, slow, eerie calm`

> わたし… きれい?

**NARR** — tags: `<whispering>`

**WOMAN** — style: `closer, breathy whisper, patient and cold`; tags: `<whispering>`

> ฉัน… สวยไหม?

**NARR** — tags: `<short pause>` before; trembling whisper (`<gasp>` may open the line)

### S02 / B03 — The mask, 31–41 s estimated

**Event:** Panic: narrator's mouth opens, a half-word escapes. The woman's fingers hook the mask's ear loop and lower it slowly. **Implication only:** the stinger frame shows the corners of a too-wide smile running back into the shadow of her hair toward the ears, lit from above by the streetlight; no blood, no open wound detail, no gore. Cut to the narrator's eyes for her line about the mouth. The woman tilts her head on `ยังสวยอยู่ไหม`. The narrator forces out the answer; the woman goes perfectly still.

**NARR** — separate short take allowed; style addition for that take: `panicked, involuntary, cut off`; tags: `<gasp>` before

> ส… สวย—

**NARR** — trembling whisper

> เธอดึงหน้ากากลง… ปากของเธอ ฉีกยาว… ไปถึงหู

**WOMAN** — style: `smiling while speaking, wet slow whisper, almost tender`; tags: `<whispering>`

> แล้วตอนนี้ล่ะ… ยังสวยอยู่ไหม?

**NARR** — tags: `<short pause>` before; breathless, shaking but deliberate

### S03 / B01 — Escape and realisation, 41–46.5 s estimated

**Event:** The woman stays frozen, head tilted, as if the answer does not fit. Narrator runs (quick cuts: feet, wires overhead, apartment door). Door shut, lock turned, narrator slides down against the door in the dark. She looks up, toward the lens.

**NARR** — breath still uneven at the start, then quieter and colder

> เธอนิ่งไป… ฉันวิ่งไม่หยุดจนถึงห้อง ฉันรอด… แต่ตอนนี้ฉันเข้าใจแล้ว ว่าทำไมเพื่อนถึงโล่งใจ

### S03 / B02 — Your turn, 46.5–50 s estimated (+ ~3 s hold for `B_loop`)

**Event:** Narrator, in her dark room, speaks straight to the viewer, guilty. On the woman's line, cut to the same extreme close as S01-B01: mask edge and hair at the lens, now unmistakably at *the viewer's* ear. Final visual state = the S01-B01 opening frame (for `C_seamless`, the last frame can match frame 0 exactly).

**NARR** — tags: `<whispering>`; guilty, soft

**WOMAN** — same voice/style as S01-B01: `slow, intimate breathy whisper right at the ear`; tags: `<whispering>`

**Then:** ~0.5 s of silence (hum only) before the seam (`C_seamless`), or ~3 s hold on the close frame with hum (`B_loop`).

## Ending proposal

- **Proposed primary: `C_seamless`.** The last line and the hook are written as a pair: `ถึงตาเธอแล้ว…` → (loop) → `ฉัน… สวยไหม?` → `อย่าเพิ่งตอบ… ฟังให้จบก่อน`. On replay the question is now asked *to the viewer*, and the narrator's warning reads as her giving the viewer the rule before passing the woman on. Last ~0.5 s speech-free; last picture is the opening extreme close; hum level matched at the seam.
- **Also deliverable: `B_loop`** (profile default pair). The final line still stands alone as an unresolved ending; hold ~3 s on the close frame with only streetlight hum. `A_fade` is not proposed (a fade softens the threat).
- Final pick is the coordinator's/user's (see open questions).

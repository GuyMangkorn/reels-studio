## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge/feeling:** a legend told (B02) → a walk (dread) → a question → one reveal → relief → "you are next". Dread comes from **stillness, an empty street and a woman who is where she should not be**, never from motion or gore.
- **Value/colour key:** low-key night. Street: deep blue-black `#0f1a2b` / `#1b2a3d` (estimated hexes, not yet seen in a frame), **one warm streetlight cone** `#e8c079` (glow peak ≤ 0.3, kit `glow.tsx`) carrying the woman; far vending-machine glow cool `#9fd0d8` small and low. Woman: coat beige `#c9b79a`, mask the brightest value `#ecebe6`, hair near-black `#16161a`. Izakaya (B02): the only warm interior, one table paper lantern, warm orange `#ff9a3c` (`LF04` lamp colour; glow peak ≤ 0.3). Narrator's room (S03): dark, one cool blind-stripe of streetlight `#5b7fa6`. **No red anywhere** (no blood): the only saturated colour is the lamp amber.
- **Camera language:** eye level, observational, a slow push or drift on every shot (project default), no handheld. Two fixed vantages return on purpose: the lens-level extreme close (`CAM-CLOSE`, frame 0 and the last shot) and the street from the narrator's side. The woman never walks, runs or teleports on camera (`D`: script handoff note 3): her "step closer" is a cut to a tighter frame with a heel click.
- **Conceal/reveal:** woman's mouth hidden by the mask until `S02-B03-SH03`. **R1.1 (user, 1 Nov 2026), look-locked in round 3:** the reveal is an **open slit grin**: the mouth slashed ear to ear, open, dark interior, uneven teeth and fangs, torn lip edges and rips into the cheeks painted as ink and wash, lit from above (approved `LF03`, `src/painted/kuchisake/`, frozen for the episode). It is painted in the style's flat shapes: **no red, no blood spray, no dripping blood, no photographic flesh or wound texture, no scissors, no attack, no killing**. Coworker never harmed. The face state from `S02-B03-SH03` on is `REVEAL_FACE` (wide eyes with small pupils and a fixed stare, angry brows); before that the woman keeps `CALM_FACE`. **Face state (R2.1, user decision 1 Nov 2026):** the mouth **tears open gradually once, in `S02-B03-SH03`** (closed mouth to the open slit grin, see [acceptance](#mask-reveal-shot-s02-b03-sh02sh05-observable-acceptance) item 1); after that the mouth never moves again (no jaw, no lip-sync, `SH05` static), only the head tilts.
- **Caption zone:** outline Thai captions in x 120–888, bottom edge ≤ y 1248 ([vertical safe zone](../../../docs/production-requirements.md#vertical-caption-safe-zone)); band y 860–1248. All story art (eyes, mask, mouth line, coworker smile, hands) sits **above y ≈ 860**; the lower third is dark road, floor or hair/coat texture only. Fiction marker `เรื่องแต่ง • จากตำนานญี่ปุ่น` from frame 0 via `eyebrow` with `eyebrowStyle {fade: false}` (the POOLDEPTH `ฉากสมมติ` precedent, [toolkit 10a](../../../docs/remotion-toolkit.md#10a-footage-stills-music-entry-transitions-and-caption-styles)), top slot, clear of the mask/eyes in `CAM-CLOSE`.
- **Chrome (default on in painted scenes):** Film recommends **off** for this deliverable except the marker: a progress bar kills dread and flashes at the loop seam. Decision `VD12` **confirmed by the user, 1 Nov 2026** (chrome off except the fiction marker).

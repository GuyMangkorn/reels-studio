## Story core

**Logline:** You walk your usual morning route through a Bangkok with no one in it: Yaowarat, Victory Monument, the river pier. Every place has a fresh garland, smoking incense and the same white sheet of paper. Back in your soi, the wind turns that sheet around: it says "ตามหา", and the person in the picture wears your favourite hoodie.

**Central question (hook):** Where did everyone go, and why only you? It turns into: who keeps walking this route just before you?

**Intention / obstacle:** You walk the route you always walk, unhurried, to understand the empty city. The obstacle: the city gives you no one to ask, only small fresh traces of someone who keeps arriving just ahead of you.

**Feeling:** hushed calm (a city that is yours alone) → gentle curiosity (traces that are minutes old) → a held breath at the lamp post → slow ache as the frames fill with people who walk past → tenderness at the mother → stillness on the hold.

**Knowledge takeaway:** N/A for this profile (photo-story drama, no fact beat; Boat FIC006 default: no checkable statistics or times). The **emotional takeaway** is the reveal itself: the city was never empty, and one person has not stopped looking for you.

**Plot function of the clues:** the garland, the incense and the white sheets are the "someone was just here" signs, and they are also the mother's own trail (she walks your route every morning, posts the notices and makes merit along the way). The reveal re-reads them; nothing new is invented at the end.

## Truth and ending

- **What actually happens (fiction):** The city is full and ordinary every morning. You are absent from it in a way the story never names (user decision OPN201: no "dead", no "missing" as a stated status, no ghost framing; SEN202). You perceive the city as empty; people pass you without noticing. Your mother walks your old morning route every day: a garland and incense at a shrine, a fresh "ตามหา" sheet at each stop, and she ends at the pier, waiting for the boat you used to take.
- **The twist:** the paper everywhere is a notice looking for **you**, and the person who walked "this route just before you" every morning is your mother. The reveal lands by matched frames (each empty plate crossfades to the same plate full of people), not by a shock cut.
- **Verified facts used:** none spoken. Real places (Yaowarat, Victory Monument, a Chao Phraya pier) are painted as settings only (FIC002). No pier name, no time, no schedule, no statistic, no organisation (OPN204, FIC006, POS203).
- **Remaining uncertainty (story-level, deliberate):** what the protagonist is stays open. The last line uses `ยังไม่ได้ยินคุณ` ("does not hear you yet") instead of the concept's `ไม่ได้ยินคุณอีกแล้ว` ("no longer hears you") so the ending does not lean toward a death reading (recorded reason below).

## Ending

- **Selected: `A_fade`** (concept and brief): the last spoken word `คุณ` is followed by a 3 s hold on the mother at the pier, then a fade to black. No loop, no goodbye, no outro, no help resource (OPN203).
- **Pairing with the hook (not a loop):** L01 says nobody is left `ยกเว้นคุณ`; L17 answers that someone has been here all along, every morning: `คือแม่`. The hook's "except you" is turned around: you were the one the city lost track of.
- **Final line changed from the concept (recorded reason):** concept `เมืองนี้ไม่เคยเงียบ… มันแค่ไม่ได้ยินคุณอีกแล้ว` → R1 `เมืองนี้ไม่เคยเงียบเลย… มันแค่ยังไม่ได้ยินคุณ`. `อีกแล้ว` ("any more") reads as permanent and pushes toward a death or ghost reading (SEN202), which the user ruled out (OPN201). `ยัง` ("yet") keeps the state open and matches a mother who is still looking. The concept wording is offered back as an alternative at W1.4 (open question 1).

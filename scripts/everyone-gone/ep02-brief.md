## Visual intent

**Audience knowledge and mood.** The viewer is `คุณ` and knows nothing until `L13`. Arc: hushed calm → gentle curiosity → a held breath → slow ache as the frames fill → tenderness → stillness. Nothing is wrong with the picture except absence, then presence nobody answers.

**Look.** WORLD FOG hand (p5.brush plates, rotring ink, flat decal heads, kit `Grade`/`Grain`), new key per state:
- **Empty plates (S01–S03):** blue hour into first light: cool desaturated blue-grey and sand, streetlights and shop signs still lit as the only warm points, milky pale sky. Value key mid-low. Warm accents only where "someone was just here": the white jasmine garland, the incense glow and smoke, the white sheet (the one bright, clean white object in every plate; it must be findable, not shouting).
- **Crowded plates (S04–S05):** the same plates in **warmer morning light**, shutters up, shops open, ordinary people. Same composition, same props in the same pixels (PAIR201); only crowd, light warmth, shutters and signage lights change (PAIR202).
- Real places are painted generic: Yaowarat (tall vertical signs as coloured rectangles with unreadable glyph-like strokes, **no readable text**, Chinese-style street shrine), Victory Monument across the traffic circle (obelisk silhouette plus the BTS viaduct; no banners, nothing that reads as a rally, LOC202), an unnamed Chao Phraya pier (covered hall, bench row, river, pigeons; no name board, no clock), the soi (cables, concrete lamp post, motorbikes, spirit house at a gate).

**Camera language** (project default): eye level about 1.6 m, observational, a slow drift or push on every shot. Matched pairs are **locked-off**: crowd changes, camera does not (drift identical in both states of a pair, < 1% scale). `คุณ` is always back view and small (10–35% of frame height), never facing the lens.

**Conceal / reveal.** No person, silhouette or hand until `S04-B01` except `คุณ` (back, small) from `S01-B01-SH02`; no readable text on any sheet until the flip; the poster's printed side is hidden until `L12`.

**Caption style.** Verbatim Thai from the script including `…`. `captionStyle.preset: 'outline'`, position by the portrait [caption-safe box](../../docs/production-requirements.md#vertical-caption-safe-zone) (x 120–888, y 288–1248). Plates are mid-low key (blue hour) so the default low band (bottom edge 1150–1248) is the start; the first caption stills decide (`scripts/caption-zone.py`). Keep the poster header, the garland/incense/sheet details and the mother's face direction above y 1000. Fallback `box`.

**Ending.** `A_fade` (`video.ending`, [remotion-toolkit](../../docs/remotion-toolkit.md#10a-footage-stills-music-entry-transitions-and-caption-styles)): after `L19` a 3 s hold on the mother with the plate's ambient clock still ticking (river, crowd drift), then fade to black. No text card, no CTA, no help line. Not a loop.

## Sound direction

Source: script "Sound intent" (Preaw, library checked 2026-11-03). Sound never replaces a clue.
- **Music family and arc:** sparse solo piano with long reverb, 1–2 notes at 60 bpm free (energy 1/10 at S01), a slow three-note figure (S01-B02), warm felt pad enters under `L09` (2/10), thins to one held note at S03 and near silence after `L11`, main melody returns after `L13` swelling with each crossfade (peak 3–4/10, still soft), drops back to the opening figure under `L17`–`L19`. **Ending plan:** the last piano note rings into the 3 s hold, fades with the picture, river ambience under the fade, then silence. No resolving stinger (`stinger_outro-resolve_01` avoided).
- **Ambience per location:** dawn hush with a distant bird (S01-B01); Yaowarat shutter-street room tone; Victory Monument open-air wind, no traffic; pier water lapping, pigeons, distant river-boat engine; soi cable hum and a far motorbike. **Crowd murmur bed is present from frame 0 at a level below naming**, rises a hair under `L05`, and fades up with each crossfade until each plate sounds as busy as it looks.

- **Silence policy:** long pauses after `L05`, `L09`, `L11`, `L14` are near silence; true silence for the last 3 s of the hold apart from the river.
- **Library:** candidates `bed_sad-aftermath-ambient_01`, `bed_warm-pad-felt-third_01`, `whoosh_page-flip-paper_01` or `foley_notebook-paper-rustle_01` (poster flip; may be too indoor). **NEEDS-GEN:** sparse solo-piano bed, pigeons, river-boat engine, water at a pier, dawn city tone, Bangkok street crowd murmur (a looped bed), outdoor wind on paper, hood rustle, incense crackle.

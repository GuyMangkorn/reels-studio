## R1 — Preaw pitch (2026-11-02)

- **Topic (user):** "ตื่นมาในโลกที่ทุกคนหายไป — ประมาณว่า 'คุณตื่นมา กรุงเทพเงียบสนิท ไม่มีใครเหลือเลย' — หักมุม: คุณต่างหากที่หายไป".
- **Fixed by the user:** Short; second-person narration ("คุณ"); Bangkok; the core twist is that the viewer is the one who vanished.
- **What varies:** genre, medium and *how the twist lands*: fair clues (C1), a comic reversal in a chat (C2), a quiet photo reveal (C3), a news ticker (C4), a tutorial that loops (C5), found-footage horror (C6).
- **Spread check:** genres: mystery-educational, comedy, slideshow/photo story, news/recap, explainer/tutorial, horror/found footage (an added genre) = 6. Mediums: painted 2D, phone UI / screen recording, still photographs, mixed painted + broadcast graphics, kinetic typography, photoreal CCTV footage = 6. Channel default (mystery-educational in the WORLD FOG look): C1 only. Wild card: C6.
- **Mode:** all concepts are fiction. C1 is labelled fiction-with-factual-knowledge (one real-world takeaway). These are pitches, not research. Nothing here counts as checked until Boat's research and fact-check.
- **Duration:** every concept is estimated at 40–60 s (the Short default). Timing is not measured.
- **Sensitivity (C2, C3, C4):** these use a fictional missing-person poster or report. Keep it clearly fictional: no real case, name or photo.

### C1 — ประตูที่ไม่เปิดให้คุณ

- **Genre:** mystery-educational (channel default), fiction-with-factual-knowledge.
- **Medium:** illustrated, painted 2D in the WORLD FOG look (project defaults).
- **Logline:** Bangkok is empty, yet everything is still running. A 7-Eleven automatic door tells you the truth: it opens for people you can't see, and it won't open for you.
- **Hook (Thai):** "คุณตื่นมาเจ็ดโมงเช้า… กรุงเทพฯ เงียบจนได้ยินเสียงตู้เย็น"
- **Beats:**
  1. You walk down an empty Sukhumvit soi. A BTS train passes on schedule with no one on board. Planted clue: across the street a shop's automatic door slides open by itself, and you read it as a glitch.
  2. More life without people: a street stall's rice pot still steaming, an iced coffee on a table still sweating. "ทุกคนหายไป… เมื่อกี้นี้เอง?"
  3. You step up to the 7-Eleven door. It doesn't move. You wave, you jump, nothing. A second later it opens on its own and "ติ๊งต่อง" sounds for nobody.
  4. Takeaway: an automatic door "sees" you by bouncing radar-like waves off a moving body or by sensing a change in infrared heat. If it opens, something with a body is there. If it won't open, there is nothing there for it to detect.
  5. Twist: "ทุกคนไม่ได้หายไปไหน… คนที่ไม่มีอยู่ในโลกนี้แล้ว คือคุณ". The door chime in beat 1 now plays back as someone walking past you.
- **How the twist lands:** fair clues. Beat 1 and beat 3 mirror each other, and the knowledge beat is the key that unlocks them.
- **Ending:** `B_loop`. Last line, paired with the hook: "…แล้วพรุ่งนี้เจ็ดโมงเช้า คุณก็จะตื่นมาอีก" leads back into "คุณตื่นมาเจ็ดโมงเช้า…".
- **Voice and tone:** calm and curious, cooling to an even, quiet tone at the reveal. Default Gemini narrator.
- **Why it holds:** a concrete everyday object becomes the clue. The viewer gets a small "so that's how doors work" fact, and the rewatch rewards them with the beat-1 door. Shared as "ไม่กล้าเดินผ่านประตูเซเว่นแล้ว".
- **Research load:** general knowledge (how automatic-door sensors work: microwave/radar motion versus passive or active infrared).
- **Sound idea:** near-silence with a fridge hum and distant BTS rails; the "ติ๊งต่อง" chime is the key stinger (beat 1 soft, beat 3 dry and close); a low drone rises under the takeaway and cuts to silence on "คือคุณ".
- **Film annotation (W0.2, 2026-11-02, checkout `ff7dcfa`):**
  - *Visual approach:* painted 2D (p5.brush) in the WORLD FOG look per project defaults: morning daylight Bangkok exteriors, eye-level observational camera with slow drift; repeated fixed vantage on the shop door (beat 1 and beat 3 mirror). The sensor takeaway is a crisp Remotion SVG insert (diagram card). Store is a generic convenience store, no 7-Eleven logo or livery (brand rule).
  - *Buildability:* `new assets: Bangkok soi street set (day), BTS viaduct + passing empty train, generic convenience-store frontage with automatic sliding door (closed/open states, synced chime), street stall with rice pot + steam, iced-coffee glass with condensation (kit has no glass prop), sensor diagram SVG insert`. Cast `ready` (one `PEOPLE` adult, `MOTIONS` walk, poses P01–P45 incl. wave/jump checked at brief). No 3/4 turn needed.
  - *Size:* `L` (every set is a new daylight city exterior; the kit and existing episode modules hold only night/fog, condo and shop interiors).

### C2 — อ่านแล้วไม่ตอบ ทั้งกรุงเทพฯ

- **Genre:** comedy.
- **Medium:** phone UI / screen-recording look (LINE-style chat, lock screen, delivery app), all on screen.
- **Logline:** You wake up to a silent city and spam everyone you know. Nobody reads anything. You enjoy the empty city, until the family group chat shows who is really missing.
- **Hook (Thai):** "คุณตื่นมา ไลน์หาทุกคนที่รู้จัก… ไม่มีใครอ่านเลยสักคน"
- **Beats:**
  1. Planted clue: the lock screen says Thursday, but you went to bed on Monday. "นอนเพลินไปหน่อย". Then come messages to work, best friend, crush and mom, all unread.
  2. Joy montage in photos you send: an empty Asok intersection, a selfie lying across a BTS bench, "ฟรีทั้งเมือง!!". The delivery app answers "ไม่พบไรเดอร์ในพื้นที่ของคุณ".
  3. A notification: "คุณ ถูกนำออกจากกลุ่ม 'ทีมการตลาด'". Then the family group opens, three days of messages: mom posted a missing poster with your worst graduation photo, an auntie replied with a "สวัสดีวันพุธ" flower sticker, dad wrote "แชร์ไปแล้ว 200 กลุ่ม".
  4. Twist: "กรุงเทพฯ ไม่ได้ว่าง… คนที่หายไปสามวัน คือคุณ"
  5. Button: one message from you finally changes to "อ่านแล้ว". It's mom.
- **How the twist lands:** a comic reversal inside the chat UI. The date clue in beat 1 is fair, and the punchline lands as both a gag and a sting.
- **Ending:** hard cut on the "อ่านแล้ว" ping.
- **Voice and tone:** bright and cheeky, a little smug, dropping into one soft line at the reveal and one beat of hope at the end.
- **Why it holds:** everyone knows the pain of "ยังไม่อ่าน", and the Thai family-group gags are instantly recognisable. The mood swings from laugh to lump-in-throat inside 50 s. Shared by tagging the friend who never reads.
- **Research load:** none (UI must be generic, not a copy of LINE's trademarked design).
- **Sound idea:** upbeat plucky ukulele/marimba loop and send "whoosh" taps; the music stops dead on the removed-from-group notification; the family-group scroll plays over room tone only; a single soft "อ่านแล้ว" ping as the closing stinger.
- **Film annotation (W0.2, 2026-11-02, checkout `ff7dcfa`):**
  - *Visual approach:* full-screen crisp Remotion SVG/React phone UI (generic chat app, lock screen, notification banners, delivery-app screen), scripted scroll and typing; the "sent photos" are painted 2D stills (kit cast in a few Bangkok frames) or licensed stock. No LINE look-alike.
  - *Buildability:* `missing capability: shared PhoneChat/phone-UI component (only a design in docs/planned-visual-components.md; the existing src/painted/worldfog/phone.tsx is an episode-only headline insert), read-receipt state change, notification banner, chat scroll timing`; `new assets: generic chat skin, sticker art (flower "สวัสดีวันพุธ"), fictional missing poster, 3–4 sent-photo stills`.
  - *Size:* `M` (one reusable UI component does most shots; few painted plates).

### C3 — เมืองที่ไม่รอคุณ

- **Genre:** slideshow / photo story (melancholy drama).
- **Medium:** still photographs of real Bangkok locations (licensed stock or user-shot), slow pans, crossfades between matched empty and crowded frames.
- **Logline:** A slow walk through a dawn-empty Bangkok, one photograph at a time. Each frame has a small sign that someone was just there. At the end every photo fills with people, and the paper on the lamp post in your soi is about you.
- **Hook (Thai):** "กรุงเทพฯ เช้านี้ ไม่มีใครเหลือเลย… ยกเว้นคุณ"
- **Beats:**
  1. Empty frames: Yaowarat, Victory Monument, a Chao Phraya pier. "คุณไม่รีบ… เพราะไม่มีที่ไหนต้องไปแล้ว"
  2. Small details: a fresh garland at a shrine, a school bag on a bench, a lit candle. Someone has been here every morning.
  3. Your soi: a sheet of paper taped to a lamp post. Its face is turned away.
  4. Reversal: each earlier photo crossfades to the same frame full of people walking past. The paper is a missing-person poster, and the garland and candle were left by your family.
  5. Last frame: the empty pier from beat 1, now with your mother standing where the camera looks. "เมืองนี้ไม่เคยเงียบ… มันแค่ไม่ได้ยินคุณอีกแล้ว"
- **How the twist lands:** melancholy reveal by image matching, with no shock cut. The clues in beat 2 are visible from the start.
- **Ending:** `A_fade`, a 3 s hold on the mother at the pier.
- **Voice and tone:** soft, slow, intimate, close to a whisper. A warm female narrator suits this best.
- **Why it holds:** the matched before/after frames are deeply satisfying to watch, and the ending is a gut punch about the people who keep looking. It is shared as "อย่าลืมโทรหาแม่".
- **Research load:** none for facts. Image licensing or user shoot needed, and the matched empty/crowded pairs need a shoot plan.
- **Sound idea:** a sparse solo piano with long reverb; pigeons and distant river-boat engine as the only ambience; under beat 4, the crowd murmur fades in as the photos fill, and it was faintly there from the start, too low to name.
- **Film annotation (W0.2, 2026-11-02, checkout `ff7dcfa`):**
  - *Visual approach:* real still photographs via `scene.media[]` images (existing-integrated, Remotion toolkit 10a) with slow pans and matched empty/crowded crossfades through `transitionIn: fade`; warm graded, no painted layer.
  - *Buildability:* `missing capability: animated media transform (pan/zoom over time; media.ts has only static box/opacity), in-scene crossfade between two stills (only between-scene fades exist)`; `new assets: 10–14 matched empty/crowded photo pairs shot locked-off at the same Bangkok locations (or licensed), poster prop, a model-released "mother" photo`. Kit not used.
  - *Size:* `L` (external shoot or licensing with matched pairs and a model release is the critical path; code side is small).

### C4 — ข่าวเช้า: ผู้สูญหาย 1 ราย

- **Genre:** news/recap (fictional broadcast format, deadpan into chill).
- **Medium:** mixed. A painted condo room and window view, with a TV broadcast built in crisp Remotion SVG graphics (anchor desk, lower third, scrolling ticker).
- **Logline:** In an empty Bangkok the only thing still on is your TV. The ticker is reporting one missing person, and the description keeps getting closer to you.
- **Hook (Thai):** "เช้านี้ ทั้งกรุงเทพฯ มีอยู่สิ่งเดียวที่ยังทำงาน… ทีวีในห้องคุณ"
- **Beats:**
  1. You wake in your condo in On Nut. The street below is empty. The TV shows a news studio with an empty anchor desk, but the ticker is still scrolling.
  2. Ticker: "ด่วน… ตำรวจเร่งค้นหาผู้สูญหาย ย่านอ่อนนุช". You read it as the whole city.
  3. Ticker: "…ผู้สูญหาย 1 ราย สวมเสื้อยืดสีเหลือง". You look down at your yellow T-shirt.
  4. Ticker: "…ครอบครัวเผย ในห้องยังเปิดทีวีทิ้งไว้". Twist: the anchor desk isn't empty. It is only empty to you.
  5. Final ticker line, read aloud: "ถ้าคุณกำลังดูอยู่… กลับบ้านเถอะ"
- **How the twist lands:** reversal through text the viewer reads along with you. Each ticker line narrows from "everyone" to "one" to "you".
- **Ending:** hard cut to TV static, then black.
- **Voice and tone:** the narrator is cool and observational, set against the ticker's flat news voice (a second TTS voice for the ticker read).
- **Why it holds:** the viewer races the ticker and wants to read the next line before the narrator does, and the yellow-shirt beat is a clean "oh no" moment. Short and clear enough to watch twice.
- **Research load:** none (generic, non-branded broadcast design; no real channel's logo).
- **Sound idea:** a looping news-sting bed, tinny through the TV speaker; city silence outside; a riser under ticker line 3; static burst as the closing stinger.
- **Film annotation (W0.2, 2026-11-02, checkout `ff7dcfa`):**
  - *Visual approach:* mixed: painted 2D condo room and window view of an empty On Nut street (WORLD FOG look, repeated fixed vantage on the TV), with the broadcast as a crisp SVG screen layer on the painted plate (pattern proven by the WORLD FOG phone insert); static/black ending as SVG noise.
  - *Buildability:* `new assets: condo room set with window view (zcpaint interiors are episode-only, not kit), TV set prop, generic broadcast graphics (empty studio + anchor desk, lower third, scrolling Thai ticker), TV static card`. Cast `ready` (`PEOPLE` adult with a yellow T-shirt via outfit override, `WARDROBE`/`dress`). Second ticker voice is a voice choice, not a capability.
  - *Size:* `M` (one interior set reused across most shots plus one SVG broadcast component).

### C5 — 5 ข้อ ถ้าตื่นมาแล้วทุกคนหายไป

- **Genre:** explainer/tutorial (fictional survival guide that turns on itself).
- **Medium:** typography / kinetic text on minimal Bangkok iconography (BTS line colours, road-sign shapes, numbered cards).
- **Logline:** A calm survival guide for waking up in an empty Bangkok. Each step is practical, until step 4 asks you to check a mirror and step 5 sends you back to step 1.
- **Hook (Thai):** "ข้อแรก: ถ้าตื่นมาแล้วกรุงเทพฯ เงียบ… อย่าเพิ่งออกจากห้อง"
- **Beats:**
  1. Step 1, the hook. Stay put and listen.
  2. Step 2: "เช็กว่าตู้เย็นยังมีไฟไหม ถ้ามี… แปลว่าเมืองยังทำงานอยู่"
  3. Step 3: "ดูแก้วกาแฟบนโต๊ะคนอื่น ถ้ายังอุ่น… แปลว่าเขาเพิ่งนั่งตรงนั้น เมื่อกี้นี้เอง"
  4. Step 4: "ส่องกระจก… ถ้าไม่เห็นตัวเอง ให้ข้ามไปข้อห้า"
  5. Step 5: "ทุกคนไม่ได้หายไป อย่าออกไปตามหาเขา… เพราะเขากำลังตามหาคุณอยู่ และนั่นคือเหตุผลของ—"
- **How the twist lands:** the rules contain it. Steps 2–3 are evidence that others still exist, step 4 is your test, and step 5 turns the guide back on its reader.
- **Ending:** `C_seamless`. Step 5's cut-off "…และนั่นคือเหตุผลของ—" completes as "ข้อแรก:" on the loop (written as a pair with the hook).
- **Voice and tone:** a flat, reassuring instructional voice, like an airline safety card, with dread seeping in from step 3.
- **Why it holds:** the numbered list promises a quick payoff, so viewers stay to step 5, and the loop makes them rewatch to reread step 1 in its new meaning. It is also the cheapest concept to produce.
- **Research load:** none.
- **Sound idea:** a clean minimal ticking/UI pulse per step card; a soft sub-bass swell enters at step 3; one reversed-reverb swell into step 5; the seam has no hard stinger, so the loop stays invisible.
- **Film annotation (W0.2, 2026-11-02, checkout `ff7dcfa`):**
  - *Visual approach:* kinetic typography on flat SVG Bangkok iconography (BTS line colours, road-sign shapes, numbered step cards), `bg: 'none'`, crisp Remotion only; airline-safety-card graphic style.
  - *Buildability:* `missing capability: shared kinetic text-card component (only static captions and quadTexts exist)`; `new assets: step-card layout, icon set (fridge, coffee cup, mirror, BTS/road-sign shapes)`. `C_seamless` loop ending is `existing-integrated` (toolkit 10a).
  - *Size:* `S` (no painted plates, one card component, short shot count).

### C6 — กล้องวงจรปิดไม่เห็นคุณ (wild card)

- **Genre:** horror / found footage (an added genre).
- **Medium:** photoreal CCTV footage: a multi-camera grid, fisheye, timecode burn-in, grain and dropouts. The painted kit and project defaults cannot make it. It needs licensed stock CCTV-style clips, a user shoot, or generated photoreal video, plus a new CCTV overlay component.
- **Logline:** Alone in an empty mall, you find the security room. Every monitor reads NO SIGNAL, until one by one they come on and show the same rooms you just walked through, full of people.
- **Hook (Thai):** "กล้องวงจรปิดทุกตัวในกรุงเทพฯ ยืนยันแล้ว… คุณเหลืออยู่คนเดียว"
- **Beats:**
  1. Your own handheld view through an empty Siam mall. CCTV domes everywhere, little red lights watching nothing.
  2. The security room: a wall of monitors, all reading "NO SIGNAL".
  3. One monitor flickers on. It shows a live feed of the mall atrium, today's timecode, crowded. The people are walking through the exact spot you passed a minute ago.
  4. The monitor for this room comes on. A guard sits in the chair you are standing behind. On screen he shivers, turns and looks straight at the empty space behind him.
  5. Twist: "เมืองไม่ได้ว่างเลย… คุณแค่ไม่อยู่ในภาพแล้ว". The feed cuts out.
- **How the twist lands:** found-footage horror reveal. The world is full on camera and empty to your eyes, and the guard reacting to you is the final proof.
- **Ending:** hard cut to a NO SIGNAL card, held for 3 s.
- **Voice and tone:** low, tense and close-miked. Sparse lines, letting the picture do the scaring.
- **Why it holds:** CCTV horror is a proven Shorts format, and the moment the monitor shows the crowd is a strong freeze-frame. Viewers comment on the guard's turn. It's a deliberate break from the channel's painted look.
- **Research load:** none for facts. Footage licensing or shoot needed, plus a new capability (CCTV grid/overlay).
- **Sound idea:** electrical hum and fluorescent buzz; dead silence on the NO SIGNAL wall; when the crowded feed comes on, a muffled crowd murmur is heard only "through" the monitor speaker; a sharp low hit on the guard's turn; signal-loss crackle to end.
- **Film annotation (W0.2, 2026-11-02, checkout `ff7dcfa`):**
  - *Visual approach:* photoreal CCTV footage through `scene.media[]` video (existing-integrated) under a new CCTV overlay (multi-camera grid, fisheye, timecode burn-in, grain, dropouts, NO SIGNAL card); handheld POV for beat 1. Deliberate break from the painted look.
  - *Buildability:* `missing capability: CCTV overlay component (grid, fisheye warp, burn-in, dropout), a photoreal footage source (no video generator in the pipeline)`; `new assets: licensed or shot mall footage in matched empty/crowded pairs, staged security-room shot with a guard actor turning (stock is unlikely to match), handheld mall POV`. Kit not used.
  - *Size:* `L` (footage acquisition with actors and a mall location is the critical path, plus a new component).

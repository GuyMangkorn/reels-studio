## 3. Sensitivity notes

| ID | Note | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SEN201 | Real families of missing people exist; the poster and the mother must not be mistaken for a real case. | Fictional poster rule FIC003; no real names, faces, case numbers, dates tied to known cases. |
| SEN202 | The ending implies "you are the one being looked for". Whether the protagonist is dead, missing, runaway or unseen changes the weight. If death is implied, the episode risks reading as a suicide or ghost story. The concept's "ไม่ได้ยินคุณอีกแล้ว" ("no longer hears you") is open to a death reading. | **Premise/reveal impact, flagged to Preaw and user, not rewritten.** Recommend leaving the protagonist's state unstated and never showing a method, a hospital, a body, a funeral or an accident. |
| SEN203 | Avoid language that blames the person (e.g. "หนีออกจากบ้าน", "ทิ้งแม่ไป") or the family (e.g. "ไม่ดูแลลูก"). The concept's share line "อย่าลืมโทรหาแม่" (do not forget to call mum) pushes toward guilt; that is a tone choice, fine for fiction, but it should not read as an instruction to the audience in the narration. | Wording check at fact-check. |
| SEN204 | Avoid hope-promising or outcome-promising claims about real cases ("ถ้าคุณกลับมา แม่จะรอ" is fiction, ok; "คนหายส่วนใหญ่กลับบ้าน" is a statistic, banned without a source). | FIC006. |
| SEN205 | Avoid real-world call-to-action unless verified. If the video wants an outro line pointing viewers to help, that needs a verified current Thai resource (hotline, police, NGO) and a source. Default: none. | Open item OPN203. |
| SEN206 | An unattended bag in a public place reads as a threat in many contexts. | Paint a school bag in daylight, near a bench, with no wires or tape; no siren or alarm sound. |
| SEN207 | Religious imagery: garland, candle and alms are ordinary merit-making. Do not make a joke of it, do not stage monks as part of the "gone" theme, and avoid the monk-woman touch issue (MER203). A Chinese shrine and a Thai spirit house are different traditions; do not mix labels in narration. | Painter note. |
| SEN208 | Do not use real emergency, police or news branding (no real broadcaster style, no "BREAKING"). | Applies to poster and any on-screen text. |
| SEN209 | Mother figure and grief: depict a quiet, specific act (standing, holding a flyer or a garland), not wailing or collapse. Tone fits the concept's whisper; also avoids exploiting grief. | Direction note to Film. |

Wording to avoid in narration: real organisation names; "ตำรวจ" claims about procedure (e.g. a waiting period before reporting; Boat could not verify any such rule); numbers or percentages about missing persons; "ศพ", "ตาย", "วิญญาณ" unless Preaw deliberately chooses a ghost frame (FIC005); instructions to the viewer to act.

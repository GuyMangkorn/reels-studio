# EG-EP02-SHORT cover A: layout and taste research (4 Nov 2026)

Follow-up to [cover-type-research.md](cover-type-research.md): the A-TYPE round only changed the font. This round changes where the text and subject sit, how the plate is cropped and graded, and what graphic elements are added. Cover A copy unchanged (`คนทั้งเมืองไปไหน?` / `เหลือแค่คุณ`, split or stacked in some variants), plate `EG2-S01-B01-SH01` local frame 100, no twist shown (CV04). Variants L1..L5 are `EG2_A_LAYOUT` in `src/videos/covers/everyone-gone-ep02.ts`; the layout fields are in `src/videos/cover-overlay.tsx`. Palette stays cream `#f4efe6`, accent `#ff7a52`, dark `#0b0d16`. Font: Noto Sans Thai only.

## Findings and limits

- Search quality was low: most results were creator-marketing blogs, freelancer ads and templates. No Thai-language source gave cover-layout guidance (one Thai article covered only the Shorts cover feature status, and it is itself unofficial). Thai channel behaviour is therefore not evidenced here; the recipes are judgement built from the international sources below. Nothing here is measured CTR.
- Shared signals: one strong image, few words, negative space and dark gradients for horror; imply something is off rather than show it; a circle or arrow pointing at an ambiguous detail creates a curiosity gap; analog/found-footage cues (CRT scanlines, tracking, timestamp, security-cam framing) mark "mundane space made wrong"; liminal-space imagery is desaturated with a sickly cast, grain, mild compression and vignette; Swiss/minimal posters use a grid, restricted palette, strong size hierarchy and one reduced visual idea; Japanese posters use vertical type columns.
- Platform caveat: Shorts cover handling is unsettled (one unofficial report of 3:2 custom covers from July 2026). We keep 1080x1920 with CV06 zones, which also survives a centre crop.
- Capability limits found while implementing: the plate cannot be zoomed or shifted beyond the scene `pictureMove` validator (|x|,|y| <= 0.05, scale 1-1.2, `src/scenes/timeline.ts`), so crop is 1.2x at most; "small subject in vast negative space" is done with an opaque frame/window (L1) instead of a zoom-out. The overlay sits above the chrome layer, so an opaque frame hides the `@TamMaiLa` mark; L1 leaves the top 200 px of the plate open for it.

## The five tastes (all 1080x1920; text inside y 240-1680, last baseline <= 1500, nothing in the right 120 px)

**L1 framed title card.** Mood: quiet, composed, A24-like film title; the question is a title, the street an exhibit. Recipe: dark paper frame from y 200 down with a window x 90-990, y 660-1400 onto the plate (plate 1.2x, y +0.02); thin cream inner border (3 px, 70%) 16 px inside the window; accent rule 120x4 at (480, 330); headline `คนทั้งเมืองไปไหน?` accent 90 px weight 600 tracking +0.02 em, no outline, baseline y 470, centred; sub-line cream 62 px, y 580. No grade (the paper frame is the contrast). Sources: [Swiss-style minimal movie posters](https://www.creativebloq.com/posters/swiss-style-41411292), [Trendhunter minimal movie posters](https://www.trendhunter.com/trends/minimal-movie-poster), [Dark gradients and negative space in horror templates](https://packapop.com/collections/horror-crime-youtube-thumbnail-templates).

**L2 vertical side column.** Mood: a Japanese-poster or tombstone feel; the text stands on the edge like a signboard and leaves the vanishing point free. Recipe: headline rotated 90 degrees (reads top to bottom, glyph tops to the right), baseline x 170, start y 330, accent 118 px weight 800 with dark stroke 0.1; sub-line rotated the same way at x 450, cream 82 px; left-edge dark fade 520 px deep at 85% plus vignette 35%; plate 1.2x shifted right (+0.05) so the street's far end sits right of the column. Risk: rotated Thai is slower to read than horizontal; check at 120 px wide. Sources: [Japanese posters use vertical type (FilmArt Gallery)](https://filmartgallery.com/blogs/news/a-guide-to-collecting-vintage-japanese-movie-posters), [vertical Japanese type balancing the image, with horizontal title below](https://japanposter.co.uk/products/stephen-king-s-graveyard-shift-%e5%9c%b0%e4%b8%8b%e5%ae%a4%e3%81%ae%e6%82%aa%e5%a4%a2-original-japanese-b2-movie-poster-1990-b2-size-51-73cm-za672) (tategaki columns run top to bottom, not verified for Thai).

**L3 CCTV found footage.** Mood: surveillance of an empty city; the viewer is the one watching. Recipe: plate 1.15x, desaturate 80%, green-grey tint `#7fa890` multiply 50%, vignette 80%, grain 35% (overlay), scanlines 16%, bottom dark fade 700 px at 75%; viewfinder corner brackets cream 6 px at x 100/980, y 290/1540; accent REC dot r 14 at (140, 360) + `REC` 44 px; `CAM 02` and timestamp `05:48:21` right-aligned at x 960 (40 px, cream); headline split and stacked flush-left: `คนทั้งเมือง` / `ไปไหน?` accent 150 px (y 1150, 1300), `เหลือแค่คุณ` cream 84 px y 1420, all weight 900 stroke 0.1. The timestamp is a decorative graphic, not a story claim (no date, no place). Sources: [analog horror: scanlines, tracking, timestamps](https://filmdaft.com/what-is-analog-horror-definition-history-examples/), [found-footage security-cam language with timestamp UI](https://skills.sh/arjun988/blender-skills/analog-found-footage-horror), [desaturated, grainy, found-footage liminal look](https://nofilmschool.com/six-design-tricks-backrooms).

**L4 evidence board (red-circle annotation).** Mood: someone has marked a detail in a photo; the circled spot is the empty end of the street, the question made visual. Recipe: plate unscrimmed at 1.0x, desaturate 20%, vignette 35%; cream paper slip (790x170, rotated -3 degrees) with two translucent tape pieces holding the headline in dark ink 92 px weight 900 at y 448; double hand-drawn accent ellipse (172x128, 12 px, gapped dash, rotated -8; second 182x136, 6 px, 75%) at (548, 905); accent arrow curve from (330, 1230) to (430, 1012) with a head; dark slip (490x110, rotated 2 degrees) with `เหลือแค่คุณ` cream 76 px at y 1316. Sources: [red circle and arrow as "look here" pointers (People's Pharmacy, descriptive)](https://library.peoplespharmacy.com/big-red-arrow-circle), [curiosity gap: point at an ambiguous detail (Descript)](https://descript.com/blog/article/the-youtubers-guide-to-the-curiosity-gap-how-to-keep-your-audiences-interest), [useful pattern: imply something is off, minimal text](https://touhfa.pages.dev/blog/thumbnails/youtube-thumbnail-tips-2026/). The circle/arrow trope is called cheap by some; here it points at emptiness, not at a prop that implies a reveal.

**L5 Swiss poster, one huge word.** Mood: blunt and personal; the whole city is gone and the cover addresses the viewer. Recipe: plate 1.2x (y +0.05), desaturate 85%, night-blue tint `#2a3f86` multiply 80% (duotone-like), vignette 60%, grain 25%; flush-left grid at x 90: cream rule 900x3 at y 340, small question `คนทั้งเมืองไปไหน?` 66 px weight 500 at y 410, `เหลือแค่` cream 110 px weight 600 at y 1010, then `คุณ` accent 560 px weight 900 at x 60, baseline y 1440, spanning about 600 px. Sources: [Swiss-style hierarchy, grid, restricted palette](https://www.creativebloq.com/posters/swiss-style-41411292), [negative space and one reduced idea](https://www.shutterstock.com/blog/minimalist-typography-layout-tutorial), [desaturated cold grade with grain and vignette](https://nofilmschool.com/six-design-tricks-backrooms).

## Not chosen (for a later round)

Split-screen or before/after (needs a second plate; a second EP02 frame with a crowd would break CV04), zine/collage with cut-out shapes (needs a painted cut-out, CV07), diagonal text (L2 covers the rotated idea; a 12-degree diagonal is a one-line `rotate` change).

## Rules check

CV02 same copy, at most two lines (L3 and L5 split it into three blocks). CV04 plates and elements show no twist. CV05 text in cream, accent, dark only; graphics add tape beige (L4) and the night-blue grade (L5). CV06 text and subjects inside y 240-1680; lowest baseline 1500 (L1 title is at the top; L3 timestamp 1500); right 120 px clear in all five (L3 right-aligned items end at x 960). CV07 all type and graphics are overlay. CV08 series identity relaxed for exploration. Frame check: viewed a 5-up sheet at 360 px wide per cover (`out/EG-EP02-SHORT-A-LAYOUT/covers/layout-sheet.png`) and L1/L2 at 540 px; lead-height and caption-zone measurements and a 120 px phone-grid check were not run.

## Backward compatibility check (4 Nov 2026)

Covers of EG-EP02-SHORT, EG-EP02-SHORT-A-TYPE, EG-EP01-SHORT and WDG-EP01-SHORT were rendered with `node scripts/render.mjs <ID> --covers` before and after the `cover-overlay.tsx` change. Bytes differ in most PNGs, but the PSNR is 76-116 dB (at most one level on a few pixels), the same as re-rendering the unchanged code twice (77.7, 82.0, 108.3, 101.2, 82.1 dB for EG-EP02-SHORT A-E), so the difference is p5.brush render noise, not the change; EG-EP01-SHORT D and E are byte-identical. Existing covers have no `layout`, per-line fields or `look` changes, so their SVG attributes are unchanged by construction.

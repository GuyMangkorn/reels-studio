## Visual intent

**Audience knowledge and mood.** The viewer is "คุณ". They know nothing on first watch; by the end they must feel the cold click that every "nobody" was their own perception. Mood arc: hushed unease (S01) → curiosity at signs of life (S01-B03) → a chill of failed test (S02) → calm clarity (S03) → cold stillness (S04).

**Look.** Same hand and texture family as WORLD FOG (p5.brush plates, rotring ink, flat decal heads, kit `Grade`/`Grain`) but a new key: **clean pale morning daylight**, the opposite of the fog nights. Nothing is wrong with the image; the wrongness is only absence.
- Value key: high-key, mid-low contrast; sky milky pale (no sun disc), low raking sun from camera-left at about 07:00 with long soft shadows on the soi.
- Color: cool desaturated blue-greys and sand for concrete, buildings and viaduct; **warm accents only where "someone just left"**: steam from the pot, the coffee glass, the lit shop interior. The warm spots are the planted clues, so colour guides the eye to them.
- Everything sharp and orderly (swept pavement, parked motorbikes neat, shop lights on): "functioning city, no people". No litter, no decay, no apocalypse cues. Nothing about death is shown at any point.
- Generic convenience store: invented colour scheme (not a chain palette), no logo, no livery, no readable brand text; BTS train generic shape, no readable line map or timetable.

**Camera language** (project default, with this story's rule). Eye level (about 1.6 m), observational, a slow drift or push on every shot. **Station A shots are "the world's view" and never contain you (one exception: the first frame of the closing pull-back, VD11 proposal); station B and G shots are your own eyes (FPOV, R1.3, VD03): no body, your right hand only when you wave or reach.** Two fixed vantages carry the mirror clue (VD04): the same door centre and the same door timing. Locked-off holds are tiny drifts (< 1% scale over the shot), never frozen, so the ambient clock keeps ticking (steam, ice, light) under holds ([DF-20261030-painted-story-hold-ambient-clock](../../../docs/dev-findings/DF-20261030-painted-story-hold-ambient-clock.md)).

**Conceal / reveal constraints (from script).** No person other than you (FPOV hand only in S02/S04; the small figure only in the closing pull-back), no silhouette, ghost, injury or accident is visible; the figure shows no face, no wound, no pallor. The no-reflection clue (CL04), the missing shadow (CL07) and the second door opening (CL05) must read on rewatch and not shout on first watch: no zoom, no hold, no sound accent on either absence. The passer-by in S04 is never drawn.

**Caption style.** `captionStyle.preset: 'outline'` (white-on-dark outline, the channel look), verbatim Thai from the script including `…`. Position follows the portrait caption-safe-box rule (`position: 'middle'`, y range fixed with `scripts/caption-zone.py` at the scene-sheet pass; [remotion-toolkit 10a](../../../docs/remotion-toolkit.md#10a-footage-stills-music-entry-transitions-and-caption-styles)). Doors, pot, glass and diagram sit above or beside the band. Reason: outline survives both the pale daylight plates and the white SVG card without a box; `box` would add a heavy shape over the quiet image.

**Chrome: OFF** (`chrome={{channelLabel: false, progressBar: false}}`, [DF-20261030-scenerenderer-default-chrome](../../../docs/dev-findings/DF-20261030-scenerenderer-default-chrome.md)). Reason: the default progress bar would tell a first-time viewer the video is nearly over before the twist, and both chrome items clutter a deliberately empty frame; there is no loop, so no bar is needed to cue a seam. Reversible by the user.

**Ending (user override: no loop; form decided in R1.2, user 2026-11-03).** Last spoken line `L20b` `คนที่หายไป… คือคุณ`. Closing picture (R1.3, VD11 proposal, `Q2`): from the FPOV reach at the glass (station G), a cut at the start of `L20a` to the station A view now showing you at the glass, then the camera **pulls back** (dolly back, about 3 s, eased) to the wide soi (station C): the street, the store, and a **small back-view figure of you** still standing at the door with no shadow and no reflection. On `คือคุณ` the music is already gone and the picture **holds** (ambient clock running, drift < 1%) through the about 1.2 s of true silence, then **fades to black over about 1.0 s on that picture** with a 0.5 s black tail (`endHoldSec` / `endFadeSec`, [DF-20261030-ending-hold-option](../../../docs/dev-findings/DF-20261030-ending-hold-option.md), status fixed uncommitted: Boss confirms it is merged in the target checkout; fallback is the default `A_fade`, which freezes the last 3 s and is acceptable because the hold is a near-still frame). Reason: the viewer leaves you on the street, in the busy city nobody sees you in, so the cold fact is on screen while the narration says it; the pull-back is the "disappearing into the world" without a cut; fade, not cut, because the story does not resolve. No end card, no text, no tag. No loop: frame 0 (empty station A wide) and the end (station C with the figure) need not match.

## Sound direction

Source: script "Sound intent" (Preaw, library checked 2026-11-03). Silence is a character; sound never replaces a clue.
- **Music family:** almost none. A single very soft sustained pad (S01-B01), then sparse single piano or mallet notes (S01-B02/B03, energy 2/10), clean light pulse for S03 (3–4/10), a low drone rising under L16–L17 and holding to L19, **cut to true silence on `คือคุณ`**. Tempo: free/slow (about 60 bpm if a pulse is needed). Arc: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 (S03) → silence. Ending plan: silence about 1.2 s, then only the faint fridge-hum room tone under the fade (continuity with the video's first sound, no music hit). No loop seam is needed any more.
- **Ambience per location:** soi, near-silence with close fridge/compressor hum as the loudest thing and faint birds (the hook's sound, S01-B01); BTS viaduct hum and rail pass-by overhead (S01-B02); stall, steam hiss and ice settle tick (S01-B03); storefront closer hum, your own footsteps and jump scuff (S02, you can hear yourself); SVG card, ambience ducked.
- **SFX language:** one generic two-tone entry chime used twice as a mirror: chime 1 soft/distant/slightly reverberant (S01-B02), chime 2 dry/close/louder (S02-B01); door motor glide; wave "tick/sweep" per animation on the card; siren pass-by for L16 (short, generic). No stinger on the reveal.
- **Silence policy:** pause after `L09` (music to almost nothing), the two SFX gaps (0.6 s and 0.8 s of no speech), and the 1.2 s silence after `L20b`.
- **Library IDs (script check 2026-11-03):** candidates `reveal_riser-tension_01` (S03 to S04 drone rise), `tension_heartbeat-slow_01` (optional under S02), `foley_glass-door-push_01` (manual door; probably not the motor glide). **NEEDS-GEN:** automatic-door glide, generic entry chime, fridge hum, BTS pass-by, morning-soi room tone, pot steam hiss, ice tick, siren pass-by, wave tick. Label reconstructed/synthesised (research F104/F106/F107). Sound-library recheck by Film at the scene-sheet pass for per-shot cue frames.

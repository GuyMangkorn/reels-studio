# Viewer check: EG-EP01-SHORT Final cut (first finished-grade cut)

- **Input:** `out/EG-EP01-SHORT/clips/_final_cut.full.mp4` (1080x1920, 1945 f, 30 fps); the 540x960 `_final_cut.mp4` was used only for the frame-difference scan. Rows: `review-rows.json` `SS-R6`; shot ranges from `index.md`; `sc04.md` for the S04 step-in and ending.
- **Checker:** Film viewer check (fresh invocation, did not paint the plates), 3 Nov 2026. Stills only; no audio (the cut has no mixed sound).
- **Method:** frames pulled with system `ffmpeg`. QA sheets are in `out/EG-EP01-SHORT/viewer-final/` (`s1`–`s6` are start/mid/end per shot plus both sides of every cut; `d1`–`d5` are detail sheets; `crop/` holds caption-band, train, artefact and flyer crops). A frame-to-frame luma difference (`tblend` + `signalstats` YAVG at 540p) over the whole cut is in `out/logs/vc-diff.txt`. Caption-band background was measured with `scripts/caption-zone.py --band 360:560 --max-mean 0.80 --max-p95 0.92` (VD14 thresholds).
- **Result:** 11 pass, 5 minor (findings for the user), 1 fail (goes back to Boss once).

## Whole-cut checks

- **Story reads without narration:** yes. The cut moves from an empty shop and street, to an empty stall, to "I" walking up and waving at a door that stays shut until I stand still, then to a sensor diagram, then to the reveal of a small figure at the glass with no shadow, and ends on black. The reveal at 1776 works as a picture.
- **Painted finish:** consistent across all painted shots: the same flat washes, grain and palette. The S03 card is SVG, as the brief specifies. Static holds do not flicker: mean frame difference is 0.07–0.09 on S01-B02-SH03, S02-B01-SH03, S04-B01-SH01 and S04-B01-SH02. No banding is visible in the sky or road gradients on the sheets. There is one paint artefact, a one-frame white streak at frame 190 (S01-B02-SH01, below).
- **Captions:** all captions are legible on the crops (`crop/caps.png`) and sit in the VD14 high band. The caption-free background passes VD14 on every painted shot (mean 0.59–0.76, p95 0.75–0.905; e.g. f0000 0.595/0.886, f0845 0.729/0.903, f1776 0.595/0.878). On the A view (f0080, f0452, f1683) the caption lies across the red/cream fascia stripe. It is readable, but the stripe runs behind the lower half of the glyphs.
- **Cuts:** all 16 cuts are clean. The 227→228 cut is a near-match (difference 6.1 against 28–36 for a hard cut) and reads as continuous. The 1395→1396 cut has a band blink (S03-B01-SH03, below).
- **User taste:** UT01 and UT03 hold on every shot except a soft UT01 on the stall close-ups. UT02 fails on the train in S01-B02-SH02. UT06 holds on both FPOV hands. Details are in the table.

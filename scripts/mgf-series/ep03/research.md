- Researcher: Boat. Research date: 2026-11-15. Packet revision: R1.

- Central question carried from C4: did วันทอง ever actually choose, or was every choice made for her?
- Learning takeaway candidate (fact-safe): the label "วันทองสองใจ" comes from a text in which she is forced, abandoned, abducted and finally condemned by a king; the phrase itself appears in her own mouth as a self-reproach (F017).

## Story angles (supported by the evidence above; not a plot)

1. **Choice ledger from the text.** The record shows both forced steps (the lie, the mother, ขุนช้าง's bed) and acts of her own: refusing to believe the news, the hanging attempt, "ยอมไปกับขุนแผน", pleading for ขุนช้าง's life, "ทูลเป็นกลาง". A count of "who decided" is possible, but "every choice was made for her" is not literally true; "none of her choices were free of cost" is.
2. **Where the label lives.** The phrase appears in the text as her own bitter line about herself ("ประหนึ่งว่า…") — before the sentence; the king's speech charges the cause-of-quarrel, two husbands and lust. The label "สองใจ" attaches later in reception (F040) and in Damrong's guess about an older tale (F033). That split is a clean, fact-safe reframe.
3. **The text's own cruelty.** ขุนแผน abandons her (ตอน ๑๓) and the mother beats her into the marriage bed; the king's pardon is real and late (ตอน ๓๖). The tragedy has an in-text twist (reprieve too late) for Shorts that end before the verdict.
4. **Edition angle.** The story most people know is a 1917–18 composite (Damrong's committee choosing among poets' versions, with older and later layers). Good for a short "who wrote วันทอง?" beat, with hedges.

## Sensitivity and sound notes

- The king is a fictional character in a literary work; Damrong's identification with สมเด็จพระรามาธิบดีที่ ๒ is his hypothesis (F031). Do not name a real reign as the executioner without "ตามที่สมเด็จฯ สันนิษฐาน".
- Violence: the text describes beheading by the executioner (เพชฌฆาต) and, in the king's order, an axe ("ขวาน") (p. ๒๗๗, image). Keep depiction minimal; coercion in ตอน ๑๓ (ศรีประจัน beating her, ขุนช้าง's forced consummation) must be implied, not shown.
- Sound: a drum/crowd cue at the ตะแลงแกง is fiction (the text shows a crowd, ตอน ๓๖ OCR); label as dramatised. No real recording exists; invented sound stays unmarked as real.

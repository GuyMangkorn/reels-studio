## Story core

- **Logline:** Three days into a fog where something copies whatever it sees and hears, Fon gets a text from Mom: "if I knock, don't open". Mom knocks. Every test Fon tries proves nothing. Then the texts say Mom is behind her.
- **Central questions:** Q1 — is the voice at the door really Mom? Q2 — if Mom is outside, who is texting as Mom, and where are they?
- **Intention / obstacle:** Fon wants Mom safe inside. Obstacle: the only warning she has comes from Mom's own number, and every test she can think of can be passed by something that listens.
- **Intended feeling:** cold dread at the door → warmth and loss in the backstory → hope, then doubt, as each test collapses → the floor drops out at the rehook (the danger is behind her) → mounting dread as the clues close in and Mom weakens → the "it was there the whole time" chill → a fragile, sad relief in the fog, and a rewatch to find the glow.
- **Twist (fair):** Mom never had her phone. The thing slipped in when the door was left open, picked up Mom's pink-cased phone in the dark kitchen and has been texting from it. It was never keeping Mom out; it was keeping Fon in (`L44`). Every plant is on screen or in the narration before `L40`.

## Truth and ending

Chosen before the plants.

- **What actually happens (story world):** Three days ago the fog came; the radio says something in it copies everything it sees and hears, and tells people to open the door to no one. Night two: the old man next door opened his door to his wife's voice; by morning that house was empty. The same night, by the fogged window, Mom told Fon stories about their first cat, ขนมปัง; something in the fog heard. Mom has coughed for two days. An hour ago she grabbed only her wallet and went out for cough medicine; her phone (pink case, always on silent) stayed on the kitchen counter; the front door stood open for a moment and the thing got in. It picked up the phone and stands in the dark kitchen holding it at hand height. Each text it types is preceded by a soft tapping on the glass screen. It types the word ลูก correctly; Mom never does. The voice at the door is the real Mom, sick, cold and short of breath; she refuses to say the spare-key place aloud because she knows the thing listens.
- **Ending:** Fon sees, in the black TV screen, the pink glow floating at hand height in the kitchen each time a text arrives; she understands, unlocks without turning, and pulls Mom into the fog. Mom whispers that she left her phone in the kitchen; Fon already knows. Nobody goes back to close the door. Fon's phone buzzes; last shot through the open door: the pink glow in the dark house, a new text: "กลับมาสิลูก".
- **Left open on purpose:** what the thing is, why it wants Fon to turn around, and what happened to the neighbours. Nothing is explained beyond `P1`.

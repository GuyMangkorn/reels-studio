## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** `S1`: the viewer gets the same two facts as Fon (a warning text from Mom, then Mom's voice at the door) and one fact she does not have: a small pink light in the kitchen behind her lit at the same frame as the text. `S2`: the backstory is memory, warm where Mom is (the shopfront, the window night) and cold where the fog is (day 1, night 2); the present tests are tight, quiet and close to the door. `S3`: the floor drops; the frame holds Fon still and lets the black kitchen doorway behind her do the work. `S4`: the camera refuses to look behind her, as she does; the only view back is the black TV glass. `S5`: the reveal re-shows the plants (recalls), then the picture breaks out into white fog and ends on the one thing left in the house: the pink light.
- **Colour and value key.** Present house: low key, ≈ 85 % of the frame below 20 % value; cool blue-black (`#0d1118`–`#1c2430`), phone-screen key cool white (`#dfe8f2`) on Fon's face, fog light under the door and through the shutter slats cool grey (`#8e9aa6`), the fridge LED a tiny green. Cold flashback (fog days): desaturated grey-blue, mid values, soft. Warm flashback (before the fog, the window night): cream-amber (`#f0d9a8` key, `#c98f4a` lamp), mid-high values. Aftermath exterior: near-white fog (`#d8dde2`), figures as soft mid-grey silhouettes, the only saturated colour left is the final pink.
- **Camera language.** Observational, eye level, slow. Every shot moves inside the set (dolly, truck, arc ≤ ±40°, push 0.1–0.6 m), per the [painted 3D camera](../../../docs/film-visual-brief-workflow.md#painted-3d-camera); recalls reuse their source set with a new move. Three fixed vantages repeat because the repetition carries the story: **V-FRONT** (frontal on Fon with the kitchen doorway deep behind her; the only vantage where the glow is seen in the house), **V-DOOR-CU** (the door as Mom's face for every Mom line until `L46`), **TV-REFLECTION** (the only way back in `S4`). One fast move in the whole film: the door burst at `L45`.
- **Conceal and reveal.** Until `L40` the present-time glow is a soft round light, never a rectangle, never a case colour on a surface, never near a hand or figure. The thing is never shown, at any point, including the neighbour flashback and the last shot. Mom's face is not seen in present time until `L46a` (soft, in fog). Mom's phone in flashbacks shows only its screen side, so the case colour stays unseen until `L40`.

<a id="long-vs-short"></a>
### How the 16:9 Long differs from the Short framing

- **Side-by-side instead of stacked.** The vertical Short stacked Fon over the depth; 16:9 puts Fon on one third and the kitchen doorway on the other, so the glow and her face share a frame with ≥ 250 px of clear space between them (V-FRONT numbers below). Fon sits screen-right in every V-FRONT shot; the doorway glow lands at x ≈ 800–830.
- **SMS in two modes.** (a) **SMS-INSERT**, first-person: Fon's phone screen fills ≈ 80 % of the frame height on the right half (screen centre x ≈ 1240), with her painted hand and the dark door or room on the left; used where the words carry a clue (`L07e`, `L16f`, `L23/L26`, `L31`, `L33`, `L34a–b` scroll, `L35d`). (b) **SMS-FLOAT**, a notification bubble over a painted shot at the right third (x 1180–1780, y 240–520), used where the picture must stay on Fon and the glow (`L02`, `L16d`, `L44c`, `L47`). The Short used inserts only.
- **Glow at hand height.** The vertical axis is short in 16:9, so height is read against references: whenever the glow is in frame, the kitchen doorway (2.10 m) and the back-counter edge (0.90 m) are in frame too, and the glow sits visibly above the counter line (≈ 30–40 px at V-FRONT distance).
- **Fon at the door (`L11`–`L45`).** The door and Fon's profile fit side by side: V-SIDE-DOOR (door fills screen-right), V-BACK-DOOR (Fon's back, door ahead) and tight frontal MCUs with the front wall as a wild wall. In `S4` frontal shots keep the kitchen out of frame or black, so the reflection stays the only view back.
- **Flashback locations get width.** The soi and the shopfront are lateral trucks (16:9 shows a street, not a slot); the neighbour's door is a high three-quarter from Fon's upstairs window.
- **Caption band and mark.** Captions sit bottom-centre (est. band y ≈ 900–1040). Faces, the glow, bubbles and the ลูก / ลุก words stay above y 860; nothing important in the mark zone (x < 420, y < 160).

### Pink-glow continuity rule (Requirement for this deliverable)

- **PG01 — One pink.** The pink-magenta hue band (hue ≈ 300–350°, saturation > 35 %) appears only on Mom's phone: the glow G (present), its case at the reveal (`L40`, the recall `L41` push, `L47`). No pink or magenta anywhere else: costumes (Fon `hoodie` grey-blue; Mom sage, cream, teal; the kit `home-lounge` rose tee and the `woman-curly` plum skirt are excluded for this reason), props, set paint, grade, grain, fog tint, UI accents (SMS bubbles: incoming `#e9eaee` with dark text, outgoing `#2f6fd6` with white text).
- **PG02 — Hand height, never on a surface in present time.** Centre y 1.18–1.24 m; never on the counter after `L09`. Positions: **G-A** (−0.55, 1.20, −10.40) from `L01` to `L29`; **G-B** (−0.45, 1.20, −9.90) after the creak `L29`; **G-C** (−0.35, 1.22, −9.15) after the second creak `L35c`, held to `L47`. It only ever moves toward the door between creaks, off screen.
- **PG03 — Lights only on events.** Dark (invisible) except when a text is sent from it or a call/text reaches it: rise 4 frames, hold while "awake", decay over 90 frames. Events: `L01`, each call in `L10` (3 visible pulses ≈ 1.0 s on / 0.3 s off for the ten calls), `L16d` (on delivery), `L21`, each S4 text (seen only via the reflection at `L38`), the tap-flicker in `L43a`, `L44b` (off screen), `L47`.
- **PG04 — Must be seen** at `L01`, `L10`, `L16d`, `L21` (V-FRONT) and in the reflection at `L38–L39` in sync with Fon's phone (same frame), per the script notes.
- **PG05 — Shape.** Before `L40`: a soft core ≈ 0.06 × 0.10 m (the lit rim around a screen seen from behind) with a halo radius ≈ 0.35 m; no rectangle, no edge, no hand. At `L40`: the halo drops and the phone back with a pink case rim resolves. Why pink: the screen faces the holder, so from Fon's side only the case rim, lit by its own screen, is seen.
- **PG06 — Mom's phone in flashback** is screen-up (`L07f`, `L08a`) or seen over Mom's shoulder from the screen side (`L07d`); the case colour is never readable before `L40`. The screen light in flashback is white, not pink.
- **PG07 — Sound pairs with it.** Mom's phone never makes a sound (it is on silent, `P11`); only the taps (fingernail on glass) come from that position. Every buzz is Fon's phone.

## Sound direction

Designed from the story first ([fit beats reuse](../../../docs/sound-generation-workflow.md#fit-beats-reuse)); every cue below is `NEEDS-GEN` until Ton finds a library file that fits the description. The script's *Sound intent* table binds; this adds the families and rules.

- **Music family and arc.** No score in `S1`. One original motif, **Mom's motif** (4 soft felt-piano notes, slow, ≈ 60 bpm feel, no pulse), heard warm under `L07c–L07f` and `L07i`, never in present time until two notes, very low, under `L46e`. Cold bed for the fog memories: a sustained low pad, very low energy, thinning to near silence at `L07h`. Tests (`L16a`–`L16h`): a faint slow low pulse (≈ 50 bpm, felt more than heard). `S4`: a low drone that rises slowly from `L28` to `L39` and tightens through `L44`; **hard cut at the door opening** (`L45`). Aftermath: optional thin sad pad; the ending returns to the house's room tone, one soft buzz, then silence with the fade.
- **Ambience per location.** Present house: fridge hum (low, steady), fog hush outside (a filtered, airless wind), old timber floor ticks. Door side: Mom's voice muffled through solid wood (Editor's door filter on MOM until `L46`, then dry and close in fog). Soi day 1: dusk street fading to fog hush; night 2 and morning: fog hush through glass, nothing else alive. Warm home: soft daytime soi murmur, a ladle on a pot, a wok. Upstairs window night: lamp-room quiet, fog breathing at the glass. Aftermath: open white fog, wide, damp, cold, no traffic, footsteps on a wet road.
- **SFX language.** Fon's phone buzz = a dry haptic in her hand (soft at `L01`, loud and close at `L21`, short at `L25`, muffled in a pocket at `L46f`). The taps = a fingernail on phone glass, small, a few metres behind, in a dead room; always just before a text. Knocks = knuckles on solid wood, dry, close, no stinger. Banging (`L30`) = palm thumps that rattle the bolt. Creaks = old timber floorboards behind her, the second nearer. Bolt = a steel slide bolt (shut in `L07b`, gripped in `L20`, scraped open in `L44a`, clack in `L45`). Door burst = the door hitting its stop and a rush of fog air.
- **Silence policy.** ≈ 1 s dead silence before "เจ้าขนมปัง" (`L15`); ≈ 1.5 s near-silence for the unanswered key (`L16k`); **≈ 1.5 s full silence after "แม่อยู่ข้างหลังหนู"** (`L27`, not even room tone); `L32b` drops everything but the three taps; the last ≥ 0.5 s of the film has no speech, and the final fade carries only room tone into black.

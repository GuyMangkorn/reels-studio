## R1 — Preaw pitch (2026-11-11)

- **User request (verbatim, 11 Nov 2026):** "เราทำ fiction กันดีกว่า แพรวหาเรื่องให้หน่อยเป็นเรื่องยาวได้โดย ความยาว short ยาวได้ถึง 60-90 วิเลย หรือหยิบ world fog มา remake ทีโดย ใช้ flow แบบ hook => question + delay #1 => rehook => question +delay#2 => answer ให้หน่อย remake wordfog ได้เริ่มใหม่หมดเลย"
- **Fixed by the user:** Short, 60–90 s; every concept follows **HOOK → QUESTION + DELAY #1 → REHOOK → QUESTION + DELAY #2 → ANSWER**; two concepts may remake WORLD FOG from scratch.
- **Channel:** [`fiction`](../../channels/fiction/channel.md). Invented stories told as stories; no concept is presented as a real case. Edu rules ER01–ER10 do not apply. Mode: pure fiction (channel direction), so no knowledge takeaway is required; C4 shows one real-world safety action, flagged for a light check.
- **Structure rules used here:** the rehook flips direction or raises stakes (never repeats the hook); each delay names what the viewer is made to wait for; the answer pays off both questions from plants that are on screen or in the narration earlier (listed per concept as `P1…`).
- **Spread check:** sub-genres: fog thriller (C1), survival perception horror (C2), thriller into heartfelt drama (C3), quiet horror into heartfelt (C4), comedy thriller (C5), sci-fi mystery (C6) = 6 angles. Mediums (proposed; Film decides in W0.2): painted 2D for C1, C2, C3, C6; a photo-sequence for C4; phone UI + CCTV clips for C5. **Wild card: C5** (a full chat app and CCTV look; `PhoneChat` is only [planned](../../docs/planned-visual-components.md), not in the kit or project defaults).
- **Remakes:** C1 and C2 are `remake: WORLD FOG`. Both start over: new characters, new premise twist and new ending. C2 deliberately inverts the old EP1 reveal (we are the "monster" seen through the glass); C1 drops it and keeps only the fog and a voice-copying threat. Old plot reference: [topic brief](../world-fog/topic-brief.md), [series bible](../world-fog/series-bible.md).
- **Duration:** all estimates are rough seconds from the beat plan. Timing is `estimated`, not read aloud or audio-tested.
- **Voices:** narrator/character voices follow the [project default](../../docs/delivery-workflow.md#project-defaults) (Gemini TTS); the user picks from samples of at most 10 s before any full read (channel rule).
- These are pitches, not research. Film annotation (W0.2) is not done in this pass.

### C1 — ข้อความจากแม่ (Mom's Texts)

- `remake: WORLD FOG`
- **Sub-genre:** fog thriller, home-invasion twist. **Medium (proposed):** painted 2D, project defaults (night, fog, one house).
- **Logline:** Three days into a fog where something copies voices, a girl gets a text from Mom: "if I knock, don't open". Then Mom knocks, and the texts keep coming from inside the house.
- **Hook line (Thai):** "แม่ส่งข้อความมาว่า ถ้าแม่มาเคาะประตู… ห้ามเปิดเด็ดขาด"

| Stage | ~s | Beat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HOOK | 0–6 | Phone lights up in a dark living room: message from `แม่`. VO reads the hook line. Three knocks on the front door. A voice outside: "ฝน… แม่เอง เปิดให้แม่หน่อย" |
| QUESTION + DELAY #1 | 6–32 | Context in two lines: the fog came three days ago; the radio said "ในหมอก… มีบางอย่างเลียนแบบได้ทุกอย่างที่มันเห็น" (P1). Mom went out for medicine "ประตูเปิดค้างไว้แป๊บเดียว" (P2); Fon called her ten times, no answer, while behind her on the kitchen counter a pink-cased phone lights up silently with each call (P3, never mentioned). Fon tests the voice: "แมวตัวแรกของเราชื่ออะไร" "…เจ้าขนมปัง" Correct. Outside, the voice coughs hard and wet (P4). Her hand goes to the lock. |
| REHOOK | 32–42 | Phone buzzes. New text from `แม่`: "แม่ไม่ได้อยู่หน้าบ้าน" … then: "แม่อยู่ข้างหลังหนู" Direction flips: the danger may be inside, not outside. |
| QUESTION + DELAY #2 | 42–70 | Fon freezes, can't turn. A floorboard creaks in the kitchen. The door voice: "ฝน! เปิดเร็ว แม่หายใจไม่ออก" More texts: "อย่าเปิด" "หันมาหาแม่สิลูก" She looks into the black TV screen for a reflection: only the dark kitchen… and the pink phone lighting up as each text arrives. |
| ANSWER | 70–84 | VO: "โทรศัพท์แม่… อยู่ในบ้านมาตลอด" Mom never had it; whoever texts is standing beside it. Fon unlocks, runs out into the fog and grabs a cold, shaking hand that coughs (real). Last shot through the open door: the pink phone lights in the empty room: "กลับมาสิลูก" |

- **Question #1:** is the voice at the door really Mom? **Delay #1 holds by** a test the viewer wants to see answered (the cat's name), then a hand on the lock: will she open?
- **Question #2:** who is sending the texts as Mom, and where are they? **Delay #2 holds by** her not turning around: the viewer waits for her (and the camera) to see what is behind.
- **Fair payoff:** Q1 (door = real Mom): P3 her phone was home all along, so the warning was never hers; P4 the cough, a body that breathes. Q2 (sender is inside): P1 it copies what it sees, including texting; P2 the door left open lets it in before the fog; P3 the phone lights in sync on every call.
- **Ending / loop:** `B_loop`. Last text "กลับมาสิลูก" pairs with the hook's "ห้ามเปิดเด็ดขาด": the rewatch sees the pink phone light up in the first shot.
- **Voices:** NARR Fon (young woman, first person); MOM (outside, coughing). The thing never speaks aloud; it only texts. Cast 2 + one never-seen presence. **Sets:** 1 (townhouse living room with kitchen counter and front door; fog exterior for the last shot). **Est. length:** ~84 s.
- **Why it holds:** two everyday objects (a text, a door) turned against the viewer; the clue is visible from second 10 and pays off on rewatch. Shared as "ใครเห็นมือถือสีชมพูตั้งแต่แรกบ้าง".
- **Research load:** none.
- **Sound idea:** fog hush and fridge hum; knocks dry, no stinger; phone buzz is the stinger (soft in the hook, loud and close at the rehook); silence under "แม่อยู่ข้างหลังหนู"; a low rising drone through delay #2 that cuts at the door opening.
- **Film (W0.2, 2026-11-11):**
  - **Visual approach:** painted 2D, [painted 3D camera](../../docs/film-visual-brief-workflow.md#painted-3d-camera) (p5 plates + crisp SVG phone screens): one townhouse living room/kitchen/front door built in world metres so the yaw camera can hold the pink phone (P3) in the background of the hook and every later wide; night lamp-and-screen light, WORLD FOG palette as look reference (old `worldfog/phone.tsx` insert pattern copied, not imported). The TV-screen reflection is the same set re-projected from a mirrored camera, darkened, inside the TV rectangle; glass sheen kept thin ([DF-20261030-painted-glass-sheen-veil](../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-painted-glass-sheen-veil)).
  - **Buildability:** `new assets: townhouse set (living room, kitchen counter, front door, fog doorway), pink-case phone prop with lit-screen states, SMS screen insert (Thai bubbles), TV-reflection layer`. Kit-ready: cast `woman-ponytail + home-lounge, P01, scared` (Fon), `woman-bob` (Mom, mostly hand/voice), ENV-FOG, hand macro (lock, cold-hand grab), `Grade`/`Grain`.
  - **Size:** `M` (one set, two figures; the reflection layer is the only new technique).

### C2 — คนข้างนอก (The One Outside)

- `remake: WORLD FOG`
- **Sub-genre:** survival perception horror, with an open sting. **Medium (proposed):** painted 2D, project defaults; the petrol-station shop seen from outside this time.
- **Logline:** A mother walking through the fog reaches a lit shop and knocks for help. Everyone inside screams at her, because from in there she is the monster.
- **Hook line (Thai):** "ฉันเคาะกระจกร้านนั้นสามครั้ง… แล้วทุกคนข้างในก็กรีดร้อง"

| Stage | ~s | Beat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HOOK | 0–5 | Tight on Nok's soaked face at the glass, fog behind. Three knocks. Inside, a shop girl and an old man scream and back away. |
| QUESTION + DELAY #1 | 5–32 | "ช่วยด้วยค่ะ มีเด็กอยู่ด้วย" The old man switches off the shop light. Nok's torch is dead; her hands stay raised at her chest, holding something under her raincoat (P1). Earlier a small voice: "แม่… ผมหนาว" (P2), then quiet. In the dark glass her reflection is a tall shape whose head sits higher than hers and moves a beat after she does (P3). "ทำไม… พวกเขามองฉันเหมือนฉันเป็นปีศาจ" |
| REHOOK | 32–40 | The old man lifts a fire extinguisher, but he's pointing past her, mouthing "ข้างหลัง!" Direction flips: they are not looking at her. Behind her: metal scraping, coming closer. |
| QUESTION + DELAY #2 | 40–68 | She can't run with the weight she carries. The scrape stops and starts, closer each time; a long arm-like shadow reaches over her shoulder onto the glass; a slow squeak (P4). "อย่าหันนะ… อย่าหัน" Breath, close behind (P5). |
| ANSWER | 68–85 | A torch beam from behind. A delivery rider pushing a cart with a crooked wheel: "พี่ครับ ไฟฉายผมยังใช้ได้" In the light the glass shows the truth: a mother with her sleeping son on her shoulders under one raincoat, a 2.3 m figure with a second head that moves on its own. The door opens. Coda: the shop girl, "ขอโทษนะคะ เมื่อกี้เราเห็นข้างนอก… สามคน" Nok: "ฉัน ลูก กับน้องไรเดอร์ไง" Girl: "ไม่ค่ะ… ตอนนั้นน้องเขายังไม่มา" |

- **Question #1:** why are they afraid of her? **Delay #1 holds by** hiding the child's position (tight framing on her face, the boy only heard once): the viewer waits to see what the shop sees.
- **Question #2:** what is behind her? **Delay #2 holds by** a sound and a shadow that keep approaching while she cannot turn or run.
- **Fair payoff:** Q1: P1 raised hands holding his ankles, P2 the child is with her, P3 the reflection is taller and its head moves late. Q2: P4 the squeak is a cart wheel, P5 breath means a person. The coda opens one new question for EP2 without breaking either answer.
- **Ending / loop:** `A_fade` on "ยังไม่มา" (series cliffhanger), or `B_loop` if the coda is cut: the final three knocks from the rider pair with the hook's three knocks.
- **Voices:** NARR Nok (mother, first person); RIDER (young man); SHOP girl; BOY (one whispered line). Cast 3 + a child (the child is a small extra; drop his line to stay at 3 voices). **Sets:** 1 (shop frontage and forecourt in fog, seen from outside; one reverse from inside for the reveal). **Est. length:** ~85 s.
- **Why it holds:** the viewer is placed inside the monster without knowing it; the reveal flips who was afraid of whom, and the sting ends on a fresh chill.
- **Research load:** none.
- **Sound idea:** wind-in-fog bed, close breath; the knocks bare knuckles on glass; scrape and squeak carry delay #2 (squeak must also be seen: the cart wheel in the reveal); silence on the glass reveal; a single low note under "ยังไม่มา".
- **Film (W0.2, 2026-11-11):**
  - **Visual approach:** painted 2D, painted 3D camera: a new petrol-station shop frontage and forecourt in fog seen from outside, one reverse from inside for the reveal ([PL-WFOG-SHOP-INSIDE](../../src/painted/kit/plates.ts), `PL-WFOG-SHOP-DOORS`, reference only). The whole trick is light and glass: tight face framing with the boy off-frame, a dark-glass reflection that is a taller two-headed silhouette moving late, then the torch beam that turns the reflection into the truth. Glass-sheen limits per [DF-20261030-painted-glass-sheen-veil](../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-painted-glass-sheen-veil).
  - **Buildability:** `new assets: shop-frontage exterior set, mother-with-child-on-shoulders under one raincoat (combined card, child's head on its own late-motion channel), glass reflection layer with lag, cart with crooked wheel + its long shadow on glass, fire extinguisher prop, torch beam through fog`; `missing capability: shoulder-carry pose (no P-sheet entry carries a second figure)`. Kit-ready: `man-spiky + rider` (rider), `man-elder` (old man), `woman-ponytail + vendor` (shop girl), `boy-school` (scaled down, `headScale`), ENV-FOG `paintFogCone`, `drawTorch`.
  - **Size:** `L` (the reveal rests on a new composite silhouette and a lagging reflection that must read at Short size).

### C3 — ออเดอร์เดิมทุกคืน (Same Order Every Night)

- **Sub-genre:** thriller misdirection into heartfelt twist drama. **Medium (proposed):** painted 2D, with crisp SVG inserts of the rider app (order card, note).
- **Logline:** A food-delivery rider gets an order with a note: "help, don't ring the bell". The man who opens the door says it's nothing, until a second note arrives: "that man is not my husband".
- **Hook line (Thai):** "ออเดอร์นี้มีโน้ตเขียนมาว่า… ช่วยด้วย อย่ากดกริ่ง"

| Stage | ~s | Beat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HOOK | 0–5 | App insert: order card, note in shaky Thai (P1). Rider VO reads the hook line. Rain on his visor. |
| QUESTION + DELAY #1 | 5–32 | Order: "กะเพราหมูกรอบ 2 กล่อง กล่องนึงไม่เผ็ด ไม่ใส่ถั่วฝักยาว"; app badge "ลูกค้าประจำ สั่ง 41 ครั้ง" (P2). An old wooden house at 9 p.m. He doesn't ring; he knocks. A tired man in his forties opens a crack and takes the bag fast: "ไม่มีอะไรครับ แกชอบเขียนเล่น" (`แก` stays ambiguous). Past him: a woman's slippers, and on the wall a framed photo of a young man on an old postman's motorbike (P3). Upstairs, a curtain moves. Door shuts. |
| REHOOK | 32–40 | As he rides off, the app pings: new order, same address, note: "ผู้ชายคนนั้น ไม่ใช่สามีฉัน" Stakes rise: someone may be trapped with a stranger. He turns the bike around. |
| QUESTION + DELAY #2 | 40–68 | Who is that man? He knocks again with an excuse: "ลืมให้ช้อนครับ" The man blocks the door, hands shaking, "ไปเถอะครับ" The rider pushes: "ขอเจอคุณป้าหน่อยได้ไหมครับ" Footsteps on the stairs, slow. |
| ANSWER | 68–86 | An old woman looks at the rider in his helmet and jacket and lights up: "มาแล้วเหรอพ่อ" The man, quietly: "แม่ผมจำใครไม่ได้แล้วครับ ทุกคืนแม่สั่งกะเพราให้พ่อ… พ่อเสียไปสิบปีแล้ว" To her he's a stranger in her house; the rider, in a uniform, is her husband come home. Last beat, next night: the same order pops up; the rider taps accept and writes on the bag "กล่องนี้ไม่เผ็ดนะ" |

- **Question #1:** is someone in that house in danger? **Delay #1 holds by** a closed door and half-seen clues (slippers, curtain) while the man gives an answer that explains nothing.
- **Question #2:** who is the man, and what is he hiding? **Delay #2 holds by** the man blocking the rider and the slow footsteps of the person who wrote the notes.
- **Fair payoff:** Q1 (no crime, a woman lost in time): P1 the shaky hand, P2 the same order 41 nights for someone who eats it mild. Q2 (her son): P3 the postman photo explains why a uniformed rider reads as her husband; "ไม่ใช่สามีฉัน" was literally true.
- **Ending / loop:** `B_loop`. "ออเดอร์เดิมเด้งมาอีกแล้ว" pairs with the hook's order card.
- **Voices:** NARR RIDER (young man, first person); SON (man, 40s); MOTHER (elderly woman, one line). Cast 3. **Sets:** 2 (house front door at night; street/bike for the rehook) + app inserts. **Est. length:** ~86 s.
- **Why it holds:** a suspicious note every viewer has imagined receiving, then a reversal that turns fear into tenderness; the shareable last line is the rider choosing to play along.
- **Research load:** none; portray memory loss with care and without medical claims (the condition is not named).
- **Sound idea:** rain and bike idle; app ping is the stinger (hook and rehook, same sound); a held, near-silent room tone through delay #2 with footsteps only; a soft warm music entry on "มาแล้วเหรอพ่อ".
- **Film (W0.2, 2026-11-11):**
  - **Visual approach:** painted 2D, painted 3D camera for two night sets (old wooden house doorway seen through a door crack, a soi street for the U-turn) under ENV-FALL rain, with crisp SVG rider-app inserts (order card, handwritten note, "41 orders" badge) that stay clear of the caption band. Look reference: warm porch bulb against blue rain, `PL-KSO-DOORWAY` and `PL-KSO-STREET-LAMP` as reference only.
  - **Buildability:** `new assets: old wooden house front + door-crack interior glimpse (slippers, framed postman photo, upstairs curtain), soi street, delivery motorbike (kit cycling kit is a road bicycle), rider-app UI inserts, food bag prop`. Kit-ready: `man-spiky + rider, P01, worried` (rider), `man-parted + home-lounge, scared` (son), `woman-elder, happy` (mother), ENV-FALL:rain, `walk` motion, `Grade`.
  - **Size:** `M` (two sets, three ready people; the motorbike and app UI are the new work).

### C4 — เด็กในรูปถ่าย (The Boy in Our Photos)

- **Sub-genre:** quiet horror into heartfelt twist. **Medium (proposed):** photo-sequence (a phone gallery of "photos" swiped and zoomed, painted as photographs), with painted 2D for the night kitchen.
- **Logline:** After moving back into the family's old house, a teenager finds the same boy in every photo she takes, closer each day and always pointing the same way.
- **Hook line (Thai):** "ตั้งแต่ย้ายกลับมาบ้านหลังเก่า… ทุกรูปที่ฉันถ่าย มีเด็กผู้ชายคนนี้"

| Stage | ~s | Beat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HOOK | 0–5 | A selfie in the living room; zoom to a small boy in the doorway behind her. VO reads the hook line. |
| QUESTION + DELAY #1 | 5–30 | Swipe through the week: each day he stands nearer the lens, and in every photo his arm points off-frame to the left (P1). Photo 2 shows the old stove in the background (P2). "บ้านนี้มีกลิ่นแปลก ๆ แม่บอกว่าบ้านปิดไว้นาน" (P3). She deletes, retakes; he's still there. |
| REHOOK | 30–40 | She shows Mom. Mom drops the phone and starts crying: "นี่… พี่ชายหนู" A brother who died at eight, in this house, before she was born. Direction flips: not a stranger, family. But why now? |
| QUESTION + DELAY #2 | 40–64 | Tonight's photo: the boy right at the lens, mouth open as if shouting, finger pointing left. She follows the direction in the dark: hallway… kitchen… a faint hiss. |
| ANSWER | 64–80 | Behind the stove the old gas hose is cracked. She doesn't touch a switch: closes the tank valve, opens every window (P1–P3 pay off). Mom, in the doorway: "พี่เขา… ก็ชี้ไปทางนี้ทั้งชีวิต" Next morning's photo: the boy far back in the garden, waving. |

- **Question #1:** who is the boy? **Delay #1 holds by** the photo-by-photo approach: the viewer waits to see how close he gets.
- **Question #2:** what does he want? **Delay #2 holds by** following his pointing hand through a dark house toward an unseen sound.
- **Fair payoff:** Q1 (her brother): Mom's line is the answer, and the old house is the link. Q2 (a gas leak): P1 the same pointing direction in every photo, P2 the stove in shot, P3 the smell explained away early. Mom's last line ties his death to the same danger without stating it.
- **Ending / loop:** `A_fade` on the waving boy, or `B_loop`: last line "นั่นเป็นรูปสุดท้าย… ที่มีเขา" against the hook's "ทุกรูปที่ฉันถ่าย".
- **Voices:** NARR Mew (teen girl, first person); MOM. The boy is silent. Cast 3. **Sets:** 2 (living room/doorway; kitchen at night) + garden in the last photo. **Est. length:** ~80 s.
- **Why it holds:** the "find the ghost" game in each photo keeps eyes on screen; the scare turns into protection.
- **Research load:** general knowledge for one safety action (no switches, valve off, ventilate): flag for a light check by Boat even though the channel's fact-check is otherwise `N/A`.
- **Sound idea:** shutter click per photo as the rhythm; a low room tone that drops a step at each closer photo; silence on "พี่ชายหนู"; a thin gas hiss carries delay #2 (the hose is also shown); soft piano on the wave.
- **Film (W0.2, 2026-11-11):**
  - **Visual approach:** painted 2D in two registers. The "photos" are painted 3D camera stills of one old-house set from different camera positions (phone-wide lens, flash or flat daylight, slight grain), shown in a crisp SVG gallery with swipe and zoom; the night search (hallway, kitchen, stove) is live painted 3D camera with a torch-less dim grade. The boy is painted plainly, no ghost effect: the fear comes from distance to lens per photo. Look reference: `PL-EDU-KITCHEN` and `PL-EDU-LIVING-ROOM-SUN` reference only, aged and dusty.
  - **Buildability:** `new assets: old-house living room + doorway, hallway, kitchen with old stove, LPG tank and cracked hose, garden (last photo), phone gallery UI (swipe, pinch-zoom), selfie framing`; `missing capability: selfie arm-out pose (front, phone toward lens) not in P01-P46`. Kit-ready: `girl-school + home-lounge, scared` (Mew), `woman-bob, sad` (Mom), `boy-school` (scaled to age 8, `headScale`) with the pointing arm from the pose sheet, hand macro (valve turn, window), gas hiss sound only.
  - **Size:** `M` (many stills but little animation; three small sets of one house).

### C5 — กลุ่มไลน์บ้านเรา (The Family Chat) — wild card

- **Hook line (Thai):** "ตีสองสี่สิบเจ็ด… พ่อส่งคลิปนี้เข้ากลุ่ม: มีคนเข้าบ้านเรา"

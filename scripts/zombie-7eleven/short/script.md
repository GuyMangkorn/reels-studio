## Story core

- **Logline / question:** you are the night-shift clerk, alone in a convenience store at 02:00 with 50 zombies outside and the front glass 40 seconds from breaking: what do you do first?
- **Intention / obstacle:** get out alive; every obvious move (lock up, run out back, hide and wait) makes a sound and calls the horde, and the only way out, the back door, rings when it opens.
- **Intended feeling:** trapped dread → "wait, what repeats?" → the click of understanding → a held-breath escape → relief.
- **Truth within the story:** the zombies hunt by sound, not sight; the horde itself is silent, so in the dead street every small sound carries. Nobody opened the door at `B00`: a passing zombie brushed it, almost entirely outside the camera frame (only its shoulder crosses the very edge for 6 frames), and the moving door rang "ติ๊ง-ต่อง". The back door rings the same way, and a second group stands in the back alley. The last customer saw the first zombies at 01:00, slipped into the stockroom and has kept silent there ever since; he is alive. How the doors make their sound is never shown or explained.
- **Twist / payoff:** all three obvious choices fail by sound. Main twist (`B07`): the door never opened by itself and they never saw you: "มันไม่ได้ดู... มันฟัง". The answer inverts the threat: sound is the weapon. The phone that betrayed you in choice 3 becomes the lure at the far front corner; you go the opposite way; the 40 s deadline (the glass) becomes your cover; the mop keeps the back door from ringing behind you. Second reveal (`B09`): the customer who "never came out" is alive, because he worked out the rule an hour before you did.
- **Ending:** clear escape, no loop. Clerk and customer walk away down the empty alley; behind them the horde stands silent in the broken shop around the crushed phone; on-screen `ซอมบี้ 50 | รอด 2`. Last line pairs with the hook's first claim (50 zombies) and pays off the rule: "ห้าสิบตัว ไม่มีตัวไหนเคยเห็นคุณ... มันได้ยินแค่เสียงที่คุณเลือกให้ได้ยิน".
- **Knowledge takeaway:** none claimed (fiction only). Story-internal logic: find what the threat follows, then make it follow something else and go the other way.

## Story core

- **Question:** you are alone in a convenience store at 02:00 with 50 zombies outside: what do you do first?
- **Twist / payoff:** all three obvious choices fail. The door sensor chime "ติ๊ง-ต่อง" is what draws the zombies; the first move is to kill the sound. The chime is the hook and the answer.
- **Fair clue (planted before the reveal):** in `B01`, `B03`, `B04` and `B05` the front zombies turn their heads exactly on each chime, never on the actor's movement. `B07` replays these four shots.
- **Loop twist:** after the chime box is ripped off the chime sounds once more from inside the shop, which returns to the hook frame (`B00`).
- **Engagement:** the closing line asks viewers to comment 1, 2 or 3.

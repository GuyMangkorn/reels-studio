## Rulings on Preaw's questions

1. **"ฮ่องเต้องค์สุดท้าย" for Puyi (L02): pass.** He is the last Qing emperor and the last emperor of China (reigned 1908-1912; search results from several reference pages agree: born 7 Feb 1906, abdicated 12 Feb 1912). The Japanese-sponsored Manchukuo "emperor" title (1934-45) is a separate, non-Chinese-state title; "องค์สุดท้าย" in a Qing story is standard and not misleading. No need for "ฮ่องเต้เด็ก". Optional: L02's "เลย" is safe because the memoir says he never touched those dishes ("向来不动它一下").
2. **"ฝั่งไทเฮา" for the dowager, later the consorts (L04): pass as a simplification (optional note).** The memoir says the dowager and the consorts each had their own kitchen and sent the dishes ("太后或太妃们都有各自的膳房"). In 1909-1912 the sender was the dowager Longyu (ไทเฮา); after her death four dowager consorts (太妃, not ไทเฮา) took over. "ฝั่งไทเฮา" is true for the child years Film will show; "the dowagers' side" in the broad sense covers the later years. No audience-visible error, so optional. If Preaw wants it exact, "ฝั่งไทเฮา" is already the shortest true form.
3. **"ปูยีบอกเองว่า สวยแต่จืด... ทำไว้โชว์" (L15): must-fix, see F01.** The "สวยแต่จืด" verdict is not about the long table. The ER10 question is therefore moot: the whole lead-in goes, and the replacement carries no speaker phrase.
4. **L05-L07 order (does it suggest the eunuch tasted dishes nobody ate)? Pass.** The memoir ties the silver tags, the tasting and the lid call to the imperial-kitchen dishes brought to his table, and then says, of those same dishes, "这些菜肴经过种种手续摆上来之后，除了表示排场之外，并无任何用处。我是向来不动它一下的" (after all the procedure the dishes serve only display; I never touched them). So a viewer who reads "the ritual was done on the long table he never ate" gets what the memoir says. This is stronger than the packet's C06 grade (hedged): the memoir does link the procedure and the uneaten table. What stays unsupported, and the script does not say: that the dowager's or consorts' dishes were tasted or untasted (the passage is silent). Keep Film's rule not to show eunuchs tasting or skipping the dowager's tray. No change to L05-L07.
5. **L16 wordplay: pass; reads as "this table was for display", not "the tasting was fake".** The checks (silver, tasting) are shown as real in L06-L07 and L13 doubts only the silver. L16 pairs the refrain "ดูปลอดภัย" with "โต๊ะนี้ก็มีไว้ให้ดู", which says the table was for show (memoir: "做个样子"). The residual risk is a viewer hearing "all the ritual was theatre". That is a reading, not a false statement, so optional. I was not given the text of Preaw's fallback ending (it is not in R1); any fallback that keeps "โต๊ะนี้มีไว้ให้ดู" and does not say the tasting was pointless or the food was thrown away passes by this ruling. Re-send it if a recheck is wanted.
6. **Printed-edition check: not possible from here.** Library or print editions were not reachable. Two independent web reposts agree on every number and procedure the script narrates. The script narrates no quoted wording and no page-level detail, so the gap does not hold readiness. Recommended but not gating: whoever has the 定本 or 全本 at hand confirms the "二十来样", "三十种上下", "不动它一下" sentences and the F01 paragraph before the video is posted.

## Findings

### F01 (must-fix, L15; severity rule 4: misrepresents a real person's words and takes a false line into the takeaway)

**Problem.** The memoir's four-phrase verdict "华而不实，费而不惠，营而不养，淡而无味" (pretty but useless, costly but not kind, many but not nourishing, bland) is about the dishes Puyi's own kitchen made and sent to the consorts at New Year and on their "千秋" birthdays (S01b: "每逢年节或太妃的生日...我的膳房也要做出一批菜肴送给太妃。这些菜肴可用这四句话给以鉴定"). It is not about the long table served to him. The Paper's repost does not contain the verdict at all in the passage I read. What Puyi says about the long table is: "不过做个样子而已" (only for show), "除了表示排场之外，并无任何用处。我是向来不动它一下的" (apart from displaying pomp, no use; I never touched it), and that the kitchen made the food half a day to a day early and kept it warm over a fire, so it was overcooked ("早已过了火候"). "สวยแต่จืด" for the long table is therefore a wrong quote, and the "pretty but bland" label in the script's takeaway is wrong.

**Replacement L15** (drops the speaker phrase and the "bland" claim; 10 syllables against R1's 11):

> เพราะมันทำไว้โชว์... ไม่ได้ทำไว้กิน

Fallback if Preaw wants the speaker named: "ปูยีเล่าว่า ทำไว้แค่ให้ดูอลังการ" has the same meaning, but ER10 prefers the flat claim, and L02 already carries the memoir as the only source.

- B05 description: "Puyi's own verdict (pretty but bland, made for show)" becomes "Puyi's own verdict (only for show)".

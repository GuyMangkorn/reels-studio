## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Viewers "know" the drama-series picture: a eunuch tastes, silver warns, the emperor eats. Feelings by beat (script story core): role-play curiosity (you are the taster) → surprise (the little emperor does not touch that table) → comic relief (he still eats about twenty dishes) → doubt (the silver tag) → the long table reread as a display, the eye moved to the dishes he actually ate. The picture carries one clean spatial idea from frame 0 to the last frame: **two tables in one hall. Left: the long silver-lidded table, for looking at. Right: the child's own table, where the food he eats arrives.** Every beat is staged on that axis, so L16's "จานตรงหน้าเขา" needs no explanation.
- **Story first (designed before the kit was opened).**
  - **CHILD (Puyi, staged reconstruction).** A boy of about five (the 1909–1912 years; F06 range 3–6), small for the huge carved seat he sits on, feet dangling above a footstool. Curious, a little bored by ceremony, quick to brighten at food. Bright imperial-yellow padded robe to the ankles with a few simplified round dragon roundels (no drama-costume detail), a dark stone-blue short over-jacket, a black satin skullcap with a red knot, shaved front and a small queue at the back. Round cheeks, a child's face; never an adult Puyi.
  - **TASTER (T).** A calm older eunuch, about fifty, beardless, unhurried: practised, not nervous. Slate-blue long robe, darker sleeveless over-jacket, black court cap. Uses a small porcelain spoon; tastes a little, sets the lid back, steps back. Never chokes, winces or collapses.
  - **CALLER (Y).** A young eunuch, about fifteen, slimmer and shorter, same uniform in a lighter blue; stands by the long table and calls the lid call (the narrator voices "เปิดฝา!"; he lifts his chin and opens his mouth on the word).
  - **LIFTERS (E1–E3) and CARRIERS (K1–K4).** Adult eunuchs in the same uniform, slightly varied tints and seeds; lifters stand in a line along the far side of the long table, arms lowered at their sides (standing in attendance); carriers walk in from the right side door, each carrying one lacquer tray of five dishes at chest height.
  - **Not shown:** the dowager (her side is a warm doorway and the carriers; showing her adds a person and claims the memoir does not support visually), any tasting or pointed skipping of the child's dishes (script Film constraint, F06), any sick, poisoned or fallen person.
  - **SET.** One Qing palace hall interior, a reconstruction, **not a named hall**: grey square floor bricks, red lacquered columns along the walls, white paper lattice windows glowing with late-morning light, a carved wooden screen behind the child's seat. Two tables: the **long table** (dark red lacquer with a yellow silk runner, 28 lidded bowls under domed silver covers, each with a small silver tag inside) stands far to screen-left, about 3.6 m from the child; the **child's table** (smaller, same lacquer) stands in front of his seat and is empty at frame 0 until the four trays arrive (about twenty dishes, open, steaming lightly, home-style mixed porcelain: warm colours, food visible). A warm doorway on the right wall is "the dowager's side".
  - **DEMO tabletop (B04).** A placeless demonstration space: a plain cool-grey paper surface under one soft top light, a small white dish with a peeled boiled egg cut in half, two plain silver strips and a small clear cup. Its plainness marks it as an illustration, not a scene from Puyi's table (the egg is a prop, not a dish from his menu; script ledger, F06).
- **Medium (VD01).** Painted 2D p5.brush through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`, `cam-moves.ts`) in world metres for the hall and the demo top; people are 4-head kit rig cards with new Qing wardrobe; dishes, lids and trays are new painted kit props. Crisp Remotion SVG only where it serves a crisp, deterministic change: the **checkpoint glints** (world-anchored, kit `glow.tsx` pattern) and the **tarnish ramp** on the demo strip (an SVG gradient mask locked to the painted strip quad). Everything the viewer reads as an object stays painted ([UT11](../../../../docs/user-taste.md#medium)); the SVG layers are effects on painted objects. Captions and the mark are screen layers above.

- **Light (colour marks the chapters, retention camera).** B01–B03: late-morning daylight through the right-wall paper windows (key from screen right, elevation about 35°, soft and warm), cooler bounce from the open hall doors behind the camera; the long table's silver covers carry the brightest cool glints in the frame (the eye goes there in B01 and B03). B04 (doubt): the placeless demo top in a cool neutral top light; no warm colour except the yolk. B05 (payoff): back in the hall, the same daylight a little warmer and lower contrast; a warm pool on the child's table from the right windows; the long table stays in its even, cooler light far to the left. No candle or lantern light (midday; avoids a "night banquet" read).
- **Camera language.** Every shot moves inside its set (retention camera: an eased yaw arc of 6–10° and a slow push of 5–15 % of the camera distance). Large moves only at three places: the frame-0 hook (arc and push off the child onto the row of lids), the lid ripple (B03, a truck along the long table), and the L15 reveal (a crane up and back so both tables and the line of eunuchs read as a display). **One action axis:** the camera stays on the open-hall side (+z) of the two tables; the long table is always screen-left of the child, the dowager's door always screen-right. Cut-ins (dish close, taster, caller) keep that orientation. Camera height defaults to the child's seated eye (about 1.0–1.1 m) so the hall reads big to him; standing-eunuch shots go to 1.5 m.
- **Conceal and reveal.**
  - CL22-01 (long table, wider than the frame): planted at frame 0 as the obvious "imperial meal"; nothing on screen calls it fake or wasted before L15. Paid at L15–L16 by the crane reveal (display) and the truck back to the child.
  - CL22-03 (the spread arriving): glimpsed before L03 as the first tray crosses the right edge of frame in B01's last shot; paid at L04 (a spread of about twenty dishes, F06/O1: never one tiny tray) and at L16 "จานตรงหน้าเขา" (the camera settles on them).
  - CL22-02 (silver tag in each dish): planted as a close in B03 on L06; paid in B04 by the same strip shape in the demo.
  - CL22-04 (taster's spoon): shown plainly and kept true; its checkpoint glint never dims (L13 limits the doubt to the silver, "ด่านนี้").
- **Truth limits on picture (binding on every shot).**
  1. **Two facts side by side, never merged (C06):** tasting (L01, L07) is shown only at the long table, where the memoir places it; the picture never says or implies "the taster tasted food nobody ate" (no shot of the taster looking at the child and shrugging, no "for nobody" gag, no food thrown away, no untouched-dust or wilting imagery).
  2. **The child's dishes are never shown tasted, skipped, checked, unchecked or unsafe** (F06): no eunuch lifts a lid or spoon at the child's table, no glint or tag close-up on his dishes, no shot that compares "tag versus no tag".
  3. **The long table is "made for show, not for eating" only:** no "bland" imagery (F01), no grimace at its food, no grey or wilted dishes; it looks rich and handsome, because it is a display.
  4. **The child never touches the long table** (L02 "ไม่แตะ"): he does not take its tags, lids or food, including in B04 (the demo strip is the demo's own).
  5. **The egg demo reads as an illustration** (placeless top, plain props, cool light), not as an event at Puyi's table; its tarnish is time-compressed (real silver in a hard-boiled egg darkens within minutes, S18) and is not labelled as real time.
  6. **No poisoning event:** no sick taster, no skull, no "poison" label, no dark smoke; the L12 cup is plain clear liquid with no label (an illustration of "a poison without sulfur").
  7. **Qing only:** no Ming, Tang or Korean imagery, no named hall, no Forbidden City exterior, no costume lifted from palace dramas; dress is simplified and labelled `est` (Boat question Q-B1).
  8. **Sound never carries a clue or a claim** (script).

## Sound direction

Built on the script's [beat and sound handoff](script.md#beat-and-sound-handoff) (B01–B05 rows are the base). Every cue is designed from its shot first; a library ID is named only where its description fits ([fit beats reuse](../../../../docs/sound-generation-workflow.md#fit-beats-reuse)); otherwise NEEDS-GEN for Ton. Shot-local frames come in the W4 sheets.

- **Music family and arc.** One light court-chamber family in one key. **M1** NEEDS-GEN: playful, curious explainer bed: pizzicato strings and a plucked zither colour carrying a short four-note motif, soft wood block on the off-beats, low plucked bass, about 104 bpm, clean 4-bar phrase ends / medium-low energy, warm, a little mischievous / no gong hits, no erhu melody, no "oriental" cliché pentatonic run (the story is about a real court, not a drama). Arc: medium-low under L01 → cut to near-silence on "ไม่แตะ" (L02) → returns bouncy on "ราว ๆ ยี่สิบจาน" (L04) → brisk ceremonial variant (M1b: wood block on every beat, light zither tremolo) for B03, settling warm and confident under L08 → drops to **M2** NEEDS-GEN, a single low held bowed-bass note with a faint cool shimmer, for L09–L12 → sparse M1 fragment (two plucks) under L13 → soft riser into a resolved warm chord on "ไม่ได้ทำไว้กิน" (L15) → thins under L16, near-silence at its ellipsis → **END1**.
- **Ending (UT15, Film's choice): END1, "the dish in front of him".** NEEDS-GEN: the M1 motif's first two notes played slowly on the plucked zither, the second left ringing, with one warm low pizzicato root under it / one phrase / zither, pizzicato / small warm room / about 2.5 s / content, settled, a quiet smile, no fanfare and no wry tag. Starts on "จานตรงหน้าเขา" (L16) and rings through the ~3 s hold over the hall room tone. (`stinger_pizz-tag-ending_01` is brighter and resolves on a full chord: too "ta-da" for this ending.)
- **Ambience per location.**
  - SET-HALL22: **A1** NEEDS-GEN: a large quiet timber-and-brick palace hall at late morning: soft air in a high room, faint birdsong outside the paper windows, very occasional silk-robe rustle and a soft cloth-shoe step on stone, far courtyard quiet / steady, sparse events / timber, stone, paper / large reverberant room, close-to-mid / 12 s loop / hushed, formal, safe.
  - DEMO (D): **A2** NEEDS-GEN: near-silent small-room tone with a faint cool hum / steady / dry, close / 6 s loop / focused, a little clinical. The cut from A1 to A2 is part of the chapter change.
- **SFX language.** Porcelain and silver, small and precise: everything in the hall is a soft, expensive, careful sound; nothing clatters. The demo is tiny and close.
  - S1 NEEDS-GEN dry porcelain tick: one small dry tick of a porcelain spoon resting against a bowl rim / one / porcelain / hall, close, dry / 0.3 s / a tiny full stop (script: on "ไม่แตะ" in L02 as music cuts).
  - S2 NEEDS-GEN silver cover lift: a domed silver cover lifted off a porcelain bowl, a soft metal-on-glaze scrape and a light ring / one / thin silver, porcelain / hall, close / 0.6 s / careful. Used for the taster's lid (L01 POV, L06 reveal of the tag, L07 tasting).
  - S3 NEEDS-GEN lid ripple ("เปิดฝา!" cascade): eight to twelve light silver-cover lifts in quick succession, each a soft clink and ring, travelling left to right across the stereo field, tails overlapping / one cascade / silver, porcelain / hall, mid / 1.5 s / ceremony in motion. **Reconstructed** (research open item 5); label it so in the cue sheet.
  - S4 NEEDS-GEN spoon taste: a porcelain spoon dipping into a thick sauce and lifting, a tiny drip back into the bowl / one / porcelain, sauce / close / 0.6 s / calm, routine.
  - S5 NEEDS-GEN tray procession: soft cloth-shoe steps on stone bricks for four walkers and the light rattle of porcelain dishes on lacquer trays / 6–8 steps / cloth, stone, porcelain / hall, mid / 2 s / food is coming. Then S6.
  - S6 NEEDS-GEN tray set-down: a lacquer tray set on a wooden table, five porcelain dishes settling with small clinks, ×4 slightly varied / one each / lacquer, wood, porcelain / close / 0.5 s each / plenty.
  - S7 library candidate `comedy_pluck-question-bend_01` ("pizzicato pluck whose tail bends upward like a question", 0.4 s) under the child's non-verbal "huh?" before L03 (fits the comic question; Ton checks key against M1). The "huh?" itself is a voice cue (V01 below).

  - S9 NEEDS-GEN checkpoint chimes (L08): three tiny warm chime ticks rising in pitch, one per glint (tag, spoon, lid) / three / small bell, glass / dry, close / 0.25 s each / reassurance stacking up.
  - S10 NEEDS-GEN strip into egg: a silver strip pressed into a soft boiled yolk, a tiny damp squish / one / metal, egg / very close, dry / 0.4 s / careful experiment.
  - S11 NEEDS-GEN tarnish shimmer (L10): a small dull downward shimmer, like light going out of metal, ending flat / one / soft metallic, low-passed / close / 1.2 s / "huh, it went dark".

## The opening contract

**Approach: role-play, then contradiction.** Put the viewer in the taster's seat, then pull the table out from under them.

> L01 ถ้าคุณเป็นขันที ชิมอาหารให้ฮ่องเต้จีน... คุณต้องชิมก่อนทุกจาน
> L02 แต่ฮ่องเต้องค์สุดท้าย... ไม่แตะโต๊ะเกือบสามสิบจานนั้นเลยนะ

L01 carries the title promise (ขันทีชิม) and speaks to "คุณ" (ER01). The contradiction "ไม่แตะ" lands at about 5–6 s (estimated). The sharp viewer's objection, "so what did he eat?", is spoken at L03 (~7 s) and answered by L04 (~12.4 s, about 28 % of runtime) with a comic release: about twenty dishes of his own. No technical term in the first 7 s (ER05).

Frame 0 must show the child emperor and the long silver-lidded table together, so "โต๊ะนั้น" in L02 needs no explanation.

## Protect these boundaries

- Keep L01 (eunuch tastes before serving) and L02 (Puyi does not eat that table) as two facts side by side. Never merge them into "the eunuch tasted dishes nobody ate" or "tasted only the uneaten table" (C06).
- Qing court only. No Tang, Ming or Korean scene; no "eunuch tasters through Chinese history".
- The long table is "made for show, not for eating". Never "pretty but bland" ("สวยแต่จืด"): that memoir verdict is about other dishes (FACT-R1 F01).
- No "Li Wei", no three-spoon rule, no "so the eunuch dies first", no poisoning event.
- The refrain's doubt applies to the silver check (L13 "ด่านนี้"), not to the tasting.
- L16's "จานตรงหน้าเขา" says only where the food he ate was set (memoir "放在我面前"). Never call those dishes untasted, unchecked, unsafe, hidden or "ที่ไม่มีใครมอง", and never say the tasting was fake.
- Silver: the boiled egg is the only false-alarm example; no claim that silver never caught anything.

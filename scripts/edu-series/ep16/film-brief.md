## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** A Thai viewer has seen crows on wires but never thought one could know a face. The first frame must say *a crow on a wire, close, watching a strange masked person walk below* with no words (UT01): outdoors, morning, a tree-lined path. Feelings by beat: a small chill at the stare and the chick's red open mouth → amused surprise at the mob → a "huh" when the bare-faced walk gets nothing → a laugh at the woman's protest, then the click of the key image (she is mobbed, he strolls behind untouched) → a small chill as ringless crows join → a cold turn at the young copying their parents → a quiet dip at the half-empty wire at dusk → the stare into the lens.
- **Story first (designed before the kit was opened).**
  - **Place.** A university campus path in the American Pacific Northwest (the study's region, never named on screen): a concrete footpath 2.4 m wide between lawns, a row of big-leaf maples on the far side, two tall Douglas firs and a three-storey red-brick hall with white window frames and a slate roof behind, black campus lamp posts, a wooden bench and a trash can, a bike rack, and a line of wooden utility poles carrying one overhead wire along the path. The wire is the crows' stage. The hero maple holds a stick nest (K03) in a fork on the path side. Nothing Thai: no soi, no Thai signs, no tropical trees (UT05, CL13).
  - **MAN.** A staff member in his forties in a navy blazer, walking to work with a takeaway coffee. In the mask he is the "villain" the crows remember; bare-faced he is a mild, slightly rumpled office man. He is gentle with the birds.
  - **WOMAN.** A student, early twenties, black bob, cardigan, a canvas tote on her shoulder; game, then indignant.
  - **WALKER (R1.1, Q-F3).** Years later the mask goes out on someone else: a young lab volunteer in his twenties, shorter than MAN, deep brown skin, black hair, a mustard hoodie with the hood down, jeans, white sneakers, hands free. Never seen bare-faced. He exists so "หน้ากากเดิม" means the same face on a different person, and so MAN never seems to come back (the real walkers were researchers and volunteers, [research](research.md) SRC007).
  - **MASK-A (the face, K02).** One distinct invented rubber pull-over mask: pale yellow-beige latex, one heavy bushy unibrow, a big red bulb nose, a wide flat grin with a gap in the teeth, sticking-out ears, a bald dome with a few painted wrinkles. Creepy-funny, cartoonish, readable as a silhouette at 40 px, not any real person's likeness and not a copy of the study's mask. It covers the whole head; the wearer's hair shows only at the nape.
  - **MASK-B (the other mask).** A white paper-plate mask with a simple ink smile and pink cheeks on an elastic string: plainly different in shape, colour and texture.
  - **Crows.** American crows: all glossy black, heavy straight bill, black legs, dark eye, about 0.45 m long. Painted as blue-black with a violet sheen and a warm rim from the sun, never a flat black hole (UT03). **Caught crows wear one bright orange leg ring** (K01). **Young crows** are scruffier and browner, with blue-grey eyes and a red-pink gape at the bill corners (real fledgling traits; Boat picture check B-P2). A mob is six or seven crows (SRC002 mobs averaged 6.4).
  - **Time.** Catch day and the mob weeks: spring morning, fresh leaves. The spread: seasons wash over the trees. Years later: spring again, the hero maple's nest rebuilt. The fade and the end: dusk, long after.
- **Medium.** Painted 2D p5.brush through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`, `cam-moves.ts`) in world metres for both sets (the path and the aerial neighbourhood). People are 4-head kit rig cards squared to the lens; the crow is a new painted animal card with views and states, written by Film at W3 on the method of the EP13 flea card (`kit/flea.ts`) and the EP15 tokay (`edu15/tokay.ts`); the masks are head overlays on the kit head. Screen layers (the species tag on the L02 line, captions, channel mark) are Remotion DOM above the canvas. No footage, no photos, no Three.js (Blender deactivated).

- **Light.** Morning: one low warm sun from screen left (east) through the maples, soft cool sky fill; dappled light on the path; every crow gets a warm rim on its sun side and its violet sheen on the sky side so black birds read (UT03). Seasons: the sun's colour and the sky shift with the leaves (summer high and white, autumn low gold, winter flat grey, spring fresh). Dusk: the sky is the key (bright gradient behind the wire, so crows read as rim-lit silhouettes), the lamp posts on with a warm halo; never a large black area.
- **Camera language.** B01 opens on a close stare from the wire (the crows' point of view of the face), then a nest close, then the catch at ground level (low, beside the bird: we are with the crow), then three identical side-on walk framings that act as the **test rig** (SH05 masked, B02-SH02 bare-faced, B02-SH03 other mask: same camera, same path, only the face changes), and the mob's point of view from the wire looking down at him 10+ m away. - **Conceal and reveal.** K01: the orange ring is clicked on in close-up (B01-SH04) and visible on the wire crows from then on; paid when bare legs land (B04-SH01) and when the ringed spots are empty (B05-SH03). K02: MASK-A is in the first frame; paid when the woman wears it (B03-SH03 push). K03: the nest is in shot 2 with one red gape; paid by the young beside a ringed parent (B05-SH02) and the ringless young crow at the end. Held back: no ringless crow is singled out before L08; no fledgling scolds before L11 except the cold-open tease.
- <a id="truth-limits"></a>**Truth limits on picture (binding on every shot).** The tag "อีกาอเมริกัน · ภาพจำลอง" once on the L02 line, no per-shot label (VD25, user Q-F2, 2026-11-10); no number, year, name or distance on screen; no Thai place cue. The catch: the crow calm, wings folded in two mitten hands, eye open, feathers smooth, no tangle close-up, no wing pinned, no blood or plucking; release is an open-hand take-off. Mobs: mostly perched, scolding (open beak, body pump, wing flick, tail flare) and following tree to tree; **the following mob stays up and behind the masked walker at ≥ 10 m** (straight line from the world positions in the sheet), leapfrogging forward only to perches that are still ≥ 10 m behind; crows that merely sit on the wire overhead and watch or call before the mob forms are staging Boat accepted (R2-O1); **one** low pass per walker (V01, V02) passes about 0.6 m above the head from behind and touches nothing; no pecking, no contact, no barrage. The other-mask pass draws one glance, nothing more. The spread (B04-SH03) changes season across the pull-out and bridges into the B05 season wash; the young (B05-SH02) are shown with the masked walker present below and the parents scolding, after **visible repeated mask visits** across seasons (B05-SH01), never as the first-ever pass. L12 is absence only: a spot on the wire is occupied in one season state and empty in the next; nothing falls, no feathers, no body. The ending claims no present: the stare is a mood, not "it still remembers".

## Sound direction

Following the script's [sound intent](script.md#sound-intent-english-notes-narration-unchanged) (its B01–B05 rows are the base); each cue was designed from the shot first, and a library ID is named only where its description fits ([fit beats reuse](../../../docs/sound-generation-workflow.md#fit-beats-reuse)); Ton makes every NEEDS-GEN item; Editor sets levels and places exactly the cue sheet at W5.3. **All crow calls are designed sounds, never study recordings** (fact-check Sound); sound never carries a clue (K01–K03 are visual). Prompt fields: source / action / material / space / length / feeling.

- **Music family and arc.**
  - **NEEDS-GEN M1** sly-curious campus bed: plucked low strings and muted kalimba with a dry woodblock on the off-beats and a soft bass-clarinet note under each phrase end, playful with a slightly crooked edge, no drums, 96 bpm, about 30 s with clean phrase ends every 4 bars so Editor can cut it dead on "แต่…". (Library `bed_pizzicato-tiptoe-curious_01` is close in colour but is EP15's theme and its tiptoe feel is sneaky, not sly: fallback only.)
  - **NEEDS-GEN M0** uneasy pulse: a soft, low muted double thump like a slow heartbeat with a faint high string harmonic above, 2 s, one rise, no hit; under "ลูกมันก็จะร้องด่า" in the cold open.
  - **NEEDS-GEN M2** time pad: a slow, low, cool pad (bowed string harmonics over a soft sub hum), still, minor-leaning but not horror, about 20 s, able to drop lower and quieter in its second half and lift a hair at the end. (Library `tone_soft-sustain-reflective_01` "single soft sustained warm pad tone" is warm, not cool: fallback only.)
  - Energy (of 5): cold open 1 (no music for the first second) → M0 1.5 → L02 M1 enters at 2 → mob 3 (the chorus carries it; M1 ducks) → cut dead on L04 → 1 (silence carries the test) → L05 M1 softly 1.5 → L06 M1 a step faster 2 → V02 3 → L07 2.5 with the low hit → L08 thins to 1.5 → L09 2.5 as the chorus rolls away → B05 M2 at 1.5 → L11 2 (young caws join) → L12 drops to its lowest, 0.5 → L13 near silence, pad lifts very slightly on "ส่งต่อ" → V03 in the clear → breeze only.
- **Ending (UT15, Film's choice): the young crow's single caw (V03) is the closing sound.** M2 thins to a held low note under L13 and stops before V03; the caw sounds dry and close; the dusk breeze carries the 2 s hold and fades to silence on the last frame. No music tag (the EP02 tag `stinger_pizz-tag-ending_01` would turn the cold stare playful; unused).
- **Ambience per location.**
  - SET-PATH16 morning (B01–B03, B04-SH01–SH02): **A1** `outdoor_park-birds-breeze_01` ("park morning songbirds with leaf breeze, 13 s loop": fits a campus morning path) — Ton confirms it holds **no crow calls** (the only crows must be ours); if it does, NEEDS-GEN A1 with the same description minus corvids. Plus **NEEDS-GEN A1b** one distant crow caw, far across the lawns, single, light reverb, 1 s (L01 open only).
  - Aerial (B04-SH03): A1 low-passed and thinned by Editor, plus **NEEDS-GEN A3** high open air over a town: soft steady wind, a faint far traffic wash, no birds / steady / air / open, high / 6 s / lifted and far.
  - Seasons (B05-SH01, SH03): A1 under a season wind swell (S15).
  - Dusk (B05-SH03–SH04): **NEEDS-GEN A2** dusk on a quiet campus path: low breeze through leaves, a very distant traffic hum, one far dog bark once, **no songbirds** / steady, sparse / outdoor air, leaves / open lawn, dry / 12 s loop / emptier, the day ending. (Library `city_still-night-air_01` is a night street without leaves: does not fit.)
- **Crow voices (all NEEDS-GEN; designed, not study recordings).**
  - **C1** scolding chorus: five to seven American crows mobbing, harsh rapid "caw-caw-caw" bursts overlapping, some birds closer and some farther in the trees / continuous with rising and falling bursts / harsh, raspy, nasal crow voices / outdoors among trees, light reflections off a building / 8 s loop, plus two stems (near left, near right) for panning with the walker / pestering, outraged, comic-menacing.
  - **C2** single adult scold caws: one harsh "caw" with a short burst after it, three variations / one call each / raspy, loud / close, outdoors / 0.5–0.8 s / an accent on a scolding phrase.
  - **C3** young crow calls: higher, rougher, nasal fledgling begging-scold "aah-aah", wobbly, three variations / one call each / soft throat, less controlled / close in a tree / 0.4–0.7 s / small and rude, copying the grown-ups.
  - **C4** bored single caw: one flat, low, short adult caw with no follow-up / one call / dry / medium distance / 0.4 s / "meh".
  - **V03** closing caw: one sharp single caw from a young crow, close and dry, a slight rasp at the end, no echo / one call / 0.5 s / it looks at you.
- **SFX language.** Feathers and air for the birds (flaps, whooshes, ruffles, tiny talon ticks on a wire), rubbery and papery for the masks, small and domestic for the people (coffee, footsteps, tote). Nothing violent, no impact on a person.
  - S1 **NEEDS-GEN** take-off flaps: a crow springing up, three or four strong wingbeats, feathers and air, close, 0.6 s, lift and freedom.
  - S2 **NEEDS-GEN** low pass: one large bird passing about half a metre over a head from behind, a feathery air whoosh with a slight doppler, no call, outdoors, 0.5 s, a start.
  - S3 **NEEDS-GEN** landing: wings braking in a short flutter, then a tiny grip creak on a taut wire, close, 0.4 s, four variations.
  - S4 **NEEDS-GEN** ring click: a small plastic leg band snapping shut, one tiny crisp click, close, dry, 0.15 s, precise and gentle. (Library `ui_click-tick_01` is a UI tick, too synthetic: does not fit.)
  - S5 **NEEDS-GEN** net swish: a soft nylon mesh net on a wooden pole lowering through air onto grass, a hushed swish and a soft settle, outdoors, 0.7 s, calm.
  - S6 **NEEDS-GEN** mask peel: a rubber latex mask pulled up and off a head, a stretchy squeak and a soft slap as it comes free, close, dry, 0.7 s, comic relief.
  - S7 **NEEDS-GEN** mask on: a rubber latex mask pulled down over a head, a stretchy rub and a snap at the neck, close, dry, 0.6 s.
  - S8 **NEEDS-GEN** paper mask: a stiff paper plate mask lifted to a face, a light papery rustle and an elastic twang, close, 0.4 s.
  - S9 **NEEDS-GEN** coffee slosh: hot coffee sloshing inside a lidded paper cup when the holder ducks, a small splash out of the lid, close, 0.4 s, a fluster.
  - S10 footsteps: MAN leather office shoes on a dry concrete path, unhurried, **NEEDS-GEN** (close, outdoors, 4 s); WOMAN white sneakers: `foley_walk-sneakers-asphalt_01` ("unhurried walking sneakers on dry asphalt, close and dry": fits).
  - S11 **NEEDS-GEN** preen ruffle: a perched crow fluffing its feathers and running its bill through them, soft feather rustle, close, 0.6 s, calm indifference.

  - S14 `whoosh_parachute-ease-out_01` ("soft airy whoosh that swells and eases out, then settles": fits a gentle time jump) at the B05 start.
  - S15 **NEEDS-GEN** season swell: a gentle wind swell rising through full leaves, then thinner through bare branches, then soft again, 3 s, time passing.
  - S16 **NEEDS-GEN** twig placing: a crow pushing a dry twig into a stick nest, a small woody crackle and rustle, close in a tree, 0.4 s.

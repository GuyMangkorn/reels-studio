## Visual intent

A whole Isan village wakes up in the dinosaur world. The fun is everyday village life meeting an alien forest; the lesson is survival logic told by one family, ยาย and เก่ง. Dinosaurs are scenery and hazards, never the subject, never named, never a film likeness. The world is **lived-in and warm**: wood, rice, hens, fire; the alien part is the **green wall** beyond the fence (ferns, cycads, 30 m conifers, no flowers). EP16 answers "what do we eat?": the forest is green but not food; the answer has been in the coop all along.

Truth limits: no flower or fruit in the Mesozoic forest; the long-neck eats the plant but people must not; raw fish (ก้อย) never eaten on screen; the hen's dinosaur link is shown by the head-tilt and the three-toed foot only.

## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Frame 0 must say, without words: *night, a rooftop over a big city, a girl alone with a home-made dish, and a screen that just spiked* (UT01, retention camera). Feelings by beat (script "Feeling"): thrill at the ping → unease at "อย่าเพิ่งตอบนะ" → stuck (she ages, "ไม่รู้ใจกัน") → a cold jolt when the voice turns out to be decades old and stops → slow dread through "กล้า" and the imagined standoff → the cold turn at "เหมือนคุณเมื่อกี้" → quiet awe at the still sky.
- **Look in one paragraph.** A hushed, mysterious painted night: a lived-in Bangkok condo rooftop at 2 a.m. where the only warm thing is her clip lamp and the only moving thing is a green blip, under a low, hazy, orange-bellied city sky; every time she thinks, the camera falls through her eyes into a cold black thought-space painted inside a ragged bubble frame, where lights are civilisations and the fear has a shape: two identical long-armed shadow monsters, colour-swapped mirrors of each other, frozen mid-lunge with their claws a hand's width apart, and later one of them rising into a giant over her crawling message and bringing its arm down on her own small light: one amber flash, then black (R2, user decision). Dread comes from silhouette, stillness, the gap, silence and that single strike on a light, never from blood, gore, fire, debris, a manufactured weapon or a person being hit. The real world stays warm-to-cold and quiet; the imagination is where it gets scary; the ending takes all warmth away and leaves the real sky saying nothing.
- **Story first (designed before the kit was opened).** **WOMAN** is about 21, a quiet, practical hobbyist who solders her own gear: the dish is hand-bent mesh on a camera tripod, the receiver is a scavenged box with a green phosphor trace, the reply box is an aluminium project case with one big lit button she made herself. She has been coming up here for years (the logbook: rows of dates, each "เงียบ"). It is a warm Bangkok night but late enough for a cardigan; headphones on, a thermos and a mug, a red plastic stool. She is excited, then quiet; she never panics; her acting is small (grin, finger stop, smile thinning, a swallow, a hand drawn back, a lamp clicked off). The **rooftop** belongs to an older 8-storey condo: patched tar waterproofing, a water tank on a rusty stand, two AC condensers, a laundry line, a puddle that holds the sky, a low parapet with the city's sodium haze rising behind it. The **imagined other side** is never shown as aliens: only a light, and the monster her fear (and theirs) paints over it.
- **Casting (kit, after the design).** `woman-bob, P31 (seated, low stool), happy` → `worried` → `scared` (held breath) → `neutral`. `woman-bob`'s base outfit already is the designed one (coral cardigan over a white top, slate trousers, white sneakers): used as it is, no outfit change (VD03). New: over-ear headphones (gear), the aged states (VD07), the seated reach and withdraw poses (VD04).
- **Medium.** Painted 2D p5.brush through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`, `cam-moves.ts`) in world metres for every space: the roof (`R`), the thought-space (`TH`, symbolic metres) and the forest (`F`). WOMAN is the 4-head rig card squared to the lens. The monsters are painted silhouette cards (not a creature rig). The finger-hover close-ups use the kit hand macro (`paintHandMacro`). Screen layers ("ภาพจำลอง", captions, channel mark) are Remotion DOM above the painted canvas, as in the series. No footage, photos or photographic texture anywhere (the inserts must never read as real footage). No Three.js; Blender deactivated.

## Sound direction

- **Music family.** Dark ambient minimal: sub drone + a sparse, cold minor pad (bowed glass / felt piano single notes at the far end of the space), no beat, no melody hook. Tempo free (no pulse); the only regular rhythm in the film is the blip (about 84 per minute, one every 0.71 s, est) so its stopping is felt. Energy arc: cosy-quiet (B01 no music) → drone enters at L02 → pad under the delay (B02) → stripped to blip at the rehook (B03) → silence at W-STOP → drone lower, swells in the imagination (B04: the low mirrored swell pair, then the giant's riser) → **W-STRIKE (R2):** the riser chokes on the downstroke into one muffled in-head thud, then ~6 f of dead silence → L08 surfaces on the faint city hum and her swallow, **no** refrain hit (script R3 dropped it) → forest stillness (L11) → drone drops a semitone (L13) → single low sustained tone to the end.
- **Ending (UT15, Film's choice).** No tag, no strings, no bright sting. **END-TONE:** a single low sustained tone (soft sine with a faint bowed-metal overtone) begins under L15, holds through L16 and fades over the 2 s hold together with the faint city hum, the last thing heard is the hum alone for ~0.5 s, then black-silent end. NEEDS-GEN.
- **Ambience per location.** ROOF: far Bangkok night (traffic wash far below, one far motorbike, a dog far off, a few insects, faint AC hum from the condensers); library candidate `city_still-night-air_01` (no insects, steady) as the base layer if it fits on listening, insects and the motorbike NEEDS-GEN. THOUGHT-SPACE: no world sound at all; a close, slightly reverberant "inside the head" air (very quiet, wide). FOREST: still night air, wind high in leaves only at the start, insects **stop** at the cut in (the silence of animals that have gone quiet), then nothing; NEEDS-GEN. The real sky (L15–L16): the roof ambience thinned to the city hum only.
- **SFX language.** Diegetic and small on the roof (ping, blip, headphone creak, stool scrape, swallow, held breath, lamp switch click), all close and dry. Imagination sounds are tonal, breathy, never creature voices (no growl, roar or scream): bubble blooms as soft airy pops with a glassy tail; the monsters as two low breathy swells panned left and right; the giant as a slow pressure rise. **One impact in the film (R2):** the W-STRIKE thud, a deep sub thump with a short air-pressure whoomp heard as if through water (inside her head), ~0.4 s with a fast-decaying low tail; no crunch, glass, explosion, debris, scream or anything that sounds like a real event; never the blip or any receiver sound (W-STOP and W-STRIKE must sound unrelated, script sound intent). **Loudness cap (Boat P8, for Ton and Editor):** the thud's momentary loudness ≤ the narration's average momentary loudness + 3 LU, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, no limiter-pumped tail; the riser into it is choked, not swelled to a peak, so the contrast against the near-silence does the work. No other impact lands on anything, ever. Transitions into thought: a short airy inhale-whoosh; out: a reverse of it ending on the roof ambience.
- **Silence policy.** Three designed silences: L02 (everything but the blip drops out), W-STOP (~1 s, faint city hum only; the silence is the stinger) and the W-STRIKE black (~6 f dead silence after the thud, R2; the city hum returns first at the L08 iris-out, like coming up for air). Narration always sits in clear air; the drone ducks 4–6 dB under narration lines and comes back in the gaps.
- **Cue design** follows the script's sound intent table beat by beat (it is the sound intent this brief adopts, with the additions above); each cue's shot-local frame, description and library fit are written in the timed scene sheets. Library candidates to audition (fit, not reuse, decides): `tension_airy-swell-stop-dead_01` (L14 riser that must stop dead at W-STOP), `impact_sub-hit-short_01` (R2: now auditioned for the W-STRIKE thud, fits only if it can take the underwater muffle and the air whoomp; otherwise NEEDS-GEN; the L08 refrain hit is dropped), `foley_flashlight-click_01` (lamp click; the clip lamp is a small plastic rocker, so it fits only if its spring tick is small), `bed_sub-drone-fade-in_01` (drone base). Everything else NEEDS-GEN, notably the PING (bright, clean, slightly old-electronic, not a phone ding: `ui_notification-ding_01` is too glassy-friendly), the BLIP loop (soft version of the ping), the mirrored swells, the bubble blooms, the W-STRIKE thud (unless the candidate above fits), the giant pressure rise, the travel tone, the clock-tick bed, the forest bed and END-TONE. Ton makes the sounds.

## Imagination scenes (IM1–IM4)

**One device for all of them (VD09).** Every insert enters through her: the camera pushes on her eyes, three small dotted circles rise from her head (edu14 bubble language, darker), and a ragged-edged circle opens from the last dot to fill the frame (in-canvas dissolve, 10–14 f, `painted.shots[].dissolveSec`, DF-20261029-painted-timeline-dissolve). Inside, the frame keeps a painted bubble rim (ragged pale wash 30–60 px wide, slowly breathing ±4 px), the cold thought palette, light grain, and "ภาพจำลอง" in LBL. Exit reverses into her face. Sound goes "inside the head" (VD14). Anything inside a bubble rim is her imagination; nothing outside the roof set is ever shown without the rim. Space `TH`: a black void with dim star dust at infinity (ENV-STARS, low alpha), lights and cards placed in symbolic metres so the camera can move with parallax (painted 3D camera); TH distances are not physical claims.

### IM1 — The long letter (L04–L05; SH06–SH07)

- **Read in 2 s:** a word goes up to a far star, comes back, and she is old.
- **L04 "แต่… ดาวดวงนั้นอยู่ไกลหลายสิบปีแสง":** enter from her eye. Vertical frame: bottom third, the dark city as a curved silhouette with **her one warm light** (amber point with a small halo); top, the **far star**, cold `#bfeaff`, unblinking, with a long thin halo: an eye that does not look away. Between them black and faint dust. Camera tilts up from her light to the star (pitch +35° over the line, eased), so the distance is travelled by the viewer. No star name; no number by default (VD12).
- **L05 "ทักไปหนึ่งคำ… กว่าจะได้คำตอบ คุณก็ผมหงอกแล้ว":** her bust moves into the lower foreground (front three-quarter, close, about 1.0 m from the lens; lead ≥ 480 px), the faint thread rising from behind her shoulder to the star. The returning bead touches her light as the last step lands on "ผมหงอกแล้ว". Eerie and lonely, not violent: the cold star is the only menace.
- **Clock-tick** bed in sound; the bead carries a thin high travel tone.

### IM2 — The standoff (L07; SH13)

- **Read in 2 s:** two lights, each imagining the other as the same monster; their claws almost touch.
- **Composition (VD08).** Diagonal mirror (180° rotation about frame centre) so both bubbles get room in 9:16: **her light** lower-left (TH (−0.9, −1.4, 0)), amber; **their light** upper-right (TH (0.9, 1.4, 0)), cold. Each light sends up its three dots and blooms a large bubble toward the other. Inside **her** bubble: *them as she imagines* — a tall, hunched shadow figure, too-long arms with mitten-claw hands (three long hooked points, no fingers, UT14 spirit), a crown of **cold pinpoint eyes** (their colour), rimmed in her amber; it leans out of the bubble toward her light. Inside **their** bubble: the **same silhouette mirrored**, with **amber eyes** and a cold rim — what she looks like to them. The two reaching claws meet at frame centre with a gap of about 40 px (a hand's width) that never closes: "about to kill each other", frozen. Pure opaque black bodies (DF-20261031-painted-opaque-cast), no inner detail beyond eyes and rim, no mouth or teeth needed; dread is the shape, the lean and the gap.
- **Beat within L07 "ฝั่งเขาก็เหมือนกัน… ต่อให้คุณใจดี เขาก็ไม่รู้":** at "ฝั่งเขา" her bubble blooms (we see her fear first); at "เหมือนกัน" their bubble blooms as its mirror (the reveal: identical). At "ต่อให้คุณใจดี" inside their bubble, for ~0.6 s, the monster recedes and a **small kind figure of her** stands there in warm light (the 4-head `woman-bob` card, P07 wave, front, small); at "เขาก็ไม่รู้" a shadow rises up behind the little figure and swallows her into the monster again, amber eyes opening. Camera: slow push toward the gap (r −12 %) with a 8° yaw arc; the bubbles sit at different TH depths (her bubble z −0.3, theirs z +0.3) so they slide apart slightly as the camera arcs.
- ~~L08 return (SH16): the same standoff, frozen~~ **Superseded in R2** (script R3): IM2 does not return. L08 is the iris-out onto her face ([VD11](#scene-decisions)); the gap is paid by W-STRIKE.
- **Monster look:** **A, long-arm shadow** (user pick, 2026-11-14), exactly as above. The same silhouette, rim and eye crown are used for the IM3 giant and the W-STRIKE arm (Boat P7: plainly a thought-shape, never lit or detailed).

### IM3 — The giant wakes (L09–L10, W-STRIKE; SH14–SH15, SH15S)

- **Read in 2 s:** her word crawls toward a light that grows into a giant far bigger than her, and it strikes first.
- **L09 "แต่… ระหว่างรอคำตอบหลายสิบปี":** IM1's thread and bead again, but laid along IM2's diagonal (her light lower-left, theirs upper-right): the warm bead crawls, slow (one-third of IM1's speed). Their light is **smaller than hers** (radius 0.5 of hers), visibly. **R2 (Boat P7):** the far light here is IM2's "their light" (a round cold point with a short soft halo), not IM1's star with its long thin "eye" halo; nothing in IM3 is cut, matched or dissolved from the W-PING/L17 star or the real-sky star, so the giant reads as her fear of "someone bigger", not as the sender.

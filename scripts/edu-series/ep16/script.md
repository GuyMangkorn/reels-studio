## Story core

- **Logline:** your village drops into the dinosaur world, the forest is green to the horizon, and almost none of it is food: the fruit and vegetables you know come from flowering plants that most of the age did not have, the plants you do not know can poison you, and rice takes about four to five months. What carries the village is fish, cooked, and eggs from the only dinosaurs you know well: your hens.
- **Central question:** in a forest this green, what will the village eat?
- **Intention / obstacle:** เก่ง goes foraging with a basket; the forest itself is the obstacle. Each guess is a viewer's guess.
- **Feeling:** "huh?" at the hook; a jolt at ยาย's "อย่า!"; a laugh at the long-neck eating what he may not; a shrug at "ต้มแล้วก็ยังอยู่"; a sag at "สี่ห้าเดือน"; a laugh at the stolen fish; a grin at the hens, and at the hen answering him.
- **Takeaway:** most fruit and vegetables come from flowering plants; unknown plants are never tested by tasting, "an animal eats it" proves nothing, and boiling does not take many plant poisons out; the first rice takes about four to five months, and the rice you have is also next season's seed; raw freshwater fish carries parasites; and chickens are dinosaurs.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "ไม่รู้จัก ห้ามกิน". L05 says it as a loss (the forest is off limits); L10 flips it (the dinosaur you know best is food).

## Truth and ending

- **Spoken as settled, plain:** fruit and vegetables come almost all from flowering plants, and most of the dinosaur age had none (R2-F01); some plants poison in a small taste (R2-F03, M01); an animal eating a plant does not make it safe for people (R2-F03, M04); boiling leaves many plant poisons in (R2-F03, M03; R2.2); the first rice harvest is about four to five months away (R2-F07); stored rice has to be kept back as seed (R2-F07 storage point, `plausible`; R2.2); raw freshwater fish carries parasites (R2-F05, as today's fact); hens are dinosaurs (R2-F06).
- **Hedged:** "แทบ" (L02, L03), "ส่วนใหญ่" (L04), "บาง" (L05), "หลายชนิด" (L06b), "ราว ๆ" (L07), "น่าจะ" (L09, fish is the easiest catch, R2-F04 `plausible`). One per line.
- **Fiction:** the wormhole and the village's trip ("สมมติ" once, L01); เก่ง's basket, the cycad seed, the long-neck, the pterosaur, the eggs, the full granary.
- **Knowledge twist (rule flip):** "ไม่รู้จัก ห้ามกิน" first closes the forest, then opens the coop: the dinosaurs you know best were in your yard. Fair clue: the hens in their coop, on screen in B01 (K01).
- **Ending (closed):** L10–L11 over ยาย walking out of the coop with eggs in her apron; เก่ง's double-take "ไก่… เป็นไดโนเสาร์เหรอ?" (V10, R2.2) and the hen answers with one "กุ๊ก" (V06); hold ~2 s. The last spoken words are the payoff restated as his question, answered by the hen: no call to action, no tip, no next-episode line.

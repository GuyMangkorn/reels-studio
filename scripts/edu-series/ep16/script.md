## Story core

- **Logline:** at 2 a.m. on a Bangkok rooftop, the home-made dish of a woman who has logged years of silent nights finally pings. She reaches for "reply"; the narrator says don't. Why not, if they're kind? Because you can't know: the star is tens of light-years away, so one word and you are grey before the answer. Then the jolt: this voice, too, left decades ago; is the sender still there? The ping stops. So who would dare make a sound at all? From their side, even if you are kind they can't know it; and while you wait decades, the smaller side may outgrow you. Not knowing anyone's heart, you keep quiet; if everyone does, the universe is a forest at night where nobody dares make a sound. The one who sent this was someone who didn't know that yet, like her a minute ago. She takes her finger off the button and switches off her lamp.
- **Central question:** why would the smartest move be to stay silent, and what does that say about the silent sky?
- **Two waiting questions (user's shape):** Q1 "ทำไมไม่ควรตอบ… ถ้าเขาใจดีล่ะ" (V01, B02) and Q2 "แล้วใครกัน… ที่กล้าส่งเสียงออกมา" (L18, B04). Both are paid in the answer beat B05: Q1 by L08 + L11 (the rule, then the forest), Q2 by L13 (the naive sender, the twist), then L15–L16 flip the rule onto the real sky.
- **Intention / obstacle:** she wants to reply; each problem removes a reason to trust the reply would be safe, and the rehook removes the person she would be replying to.
- **Feeling:** a thrill at the ping; unease at "อย่าเพิ่งตอบนะ"; a stuck feeling at the end of the first delay (ageing, "ไม่รู้ใจกัน"); a cold jolt mid-video when the voice turns out to be old and the ping stops; slow dread through "กล้า" and the two pieces; a hit of fear when, in her imagination, the grown monster strikes her light out (W-STRIKE), then relief that she is still on the roof with her lamp on; the cold turn at "เหมือนคุณเมื่อกี้"; quiet awe at the last line.
- **Takeaway:** a silent sky is not proof that nobody is there. Under one simple "what if" (you cannot know anyone's intent, so you keep quiet), an empty universe and a hiding one would sound the same. It is one answer among many, not a known fact.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "ไม่รู้ใจกัน… ก็ต้องเงียบไว้ก่อน". L06 plants half of it at the end of the first delay, L08 says it whole as the first line of the answer, L11 scales it to everyone, L13 turns it on the sender ("ยังไม่รู้เรื่องนี้"), L15–L16 flip it onto the sky: the rule that makes her afraid is the same rule that would explain the silence she had been logging (K01).

## Truth and ending

- **Premise tension resolution (Boat research fix 3): naive sender.** A signal arriving means someone transmitted, which seems to contradict "everyone stays silent". R2 makes this the second waiting question: L18 asks it out loud right after the ping stops ("แล้วใครกัน… ที่กล้าส่งเสียงออกมา"; "กล้า" hints there is something to fear before the pieces explain it), the delay B04 shows why sending is risky, and L13 answers "someone who doesn't know this yet, like you a minute ago" (she was about to transmit too, K04). The "is the sender still there" dread (L14) now comes earlier as the rehook, before Q2, and stays unanswered on purpose. The ping stopping (K03, W-STOP) is picture only; no line says anything happened to them. The bait/test reading stays rejected (it needs a hostile actor).
- <a id="strike-guard"></a>**The imagined strike (R3, user decision 2026-11-14).** The "about to kill each other" imagination shows the strike, once, as the wordless beat **W-STRIKE** right after L10, at the end of IM3 (the standoff breaks because one side has grown). What is shown: inside her bubble rim only, the grown cold monster (look A) brings one long clawed arm down the diagonal onto **her own amber light** (lower-left); the impact reads as silhouette and light only (the claw's shadow covers the light, one short amber flash and a soft shock ring, then her light is gone and her word-bead dies mid-way); hard cut to black inside the rim for ~6 frames. Not shown: gore, blood, fire, explosion, debris, a weapon, a body or person hit (the struck thing is a light, not a figure), any light outside a bubble dimming. **Guards against the two wrong reads:** (1) *Not the real sender:* the light that goes out is hers, never the cold far light, the IM1 star or anything on the receiver; the far light stays lit (it is the giant); W-STOP, ~13 s earlier, stays a neutral flat line with no cause; between them sit a clean cut back to the real roof (L18) and a fresh iris-in from her eyes (L07), and no receiver shot is cut directly after the strike. (2) *Hers, not the world's:* the strike happens only inside the bubble rim with "ภาพจำลอง" on, reached by the iris-in at L07 and left by the iris-out at L08 onto her face, alive, lamp still on, finger still hovering; her lamp being on in the very next roof shot shows the strike was a fear, not an event. No spoken or on-screen text says the danger is real, says what anyone does, or names the strike; the narration is unchanged.
- **Spoken as settled, plain:** a star tens of light-years away means a reply takes decades (CL06–CL08, L04, L05, L09); a signal from it left decades ago (CL07, L17); the real sky has given no confirmed sign of anyone yet (CL02, L15 "ฟ้าจริง ๆ ยังเงียบ").
- **Model steps, hedged (ER10, one hedge per line):** L10 "อาจ" (the smaller side may outgrow you); L11 "ถ้า" (if everyone thinks this way); L13 "อาจ" (the sender); L15 "แค่คำตอบหนึ่ง… แต่ถ้ามันใช่" carries the condition for L16, which therefore needs no hedge of its own. L07 and L08 are logic, not facts about the world. L18 is a story question, not a claim.
- **Distance for the picture (Boat fix 2, CL08; O1 applied):** the far star is **unnamed and fictional, about 30 light-years away** (round trip at light speed about 60 years; a woman about 21 would be about 81 at the reply: grey, and plausibly alive to see it). "หลายสิบปีแสง" (L04), "ผมหงอก" (L05), "หลายสิบปี" (L09) and "หลายสิบปีก่อน" (L17) all hold at 30 ly. "ไปกลับก็หมดชีวิต" is not used (CL08: false below about 40 ly). Film shows no star name and preferably no number; if a number is shown it reads "ราว 30 ปีแสง". Never Proxima, Barnard's or Tau Ceti.
- **Real sky vs story sky (O2 applied):** "ภาพจำลอง" stays on screen through every roof shot with the receiver in frame until W-OFF; L15 says "ฟ้าจริง ๆ" and the label comes off as the camera tilts to the sky, so the final pull-back reads as the real sky.
- **Dramatization only:** the woman, rooftop, dish, logbook, the signal and its readout ("ภาพจำลอง"), the light pulse crawling between stars, the ageing insert, the mirrored monster thought shapes (her imagination, framed as thought bubbles), the swelling far light and the giant, the imagined strike on her light (W-STRIKE, inside the bubble only), the forest-at-night insert, the ping stopping.

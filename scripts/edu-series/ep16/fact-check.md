## Answers to Preaw's questions

**Q2: can the L05 picture show the same man bare-faced?**

- **Short answer: safe to show as staging; no inspected source states it for the same person.** The design makes it plausible but I could not confirm it in a read text.
- **Evidence.** SRC002 (full text) says controls were "people wearing other masks not used for trapping or no mask" and that at UW the neutral stimulus was an "unmasked face"; crows "respond similarly to neutral people whether they are masked or not". It does not say that a trapper walked unmasked. SRC001's abstract (via research.md, second hand) says trappers with no mask were rarely scolded; that sentence is what supports the man himself, and I could not open the 2010 methods to confirm it.
- **Decision.** The narration names no walker, so L05 passes either way. The picture is a dramatization labelled "ภาพจำลอง", and the L07 image (woman masked and mobbed, a bare-faced man untouched behind her) matches the finding "different people in the same mask were scolded, unmasked people were not", but a simultaneous two-walker trial is only in a secondary summary of SRC001 (search summary, `lead-only`). No change needed. Film fallback if Preaw wants zero ambiguity: make the bare-faced passer a different man or leave the same actor and add nothing in narration.

**Q3a: L09 "ไกลเป็นกิโล" vs at least 1.2 km.**

- Fine: at least 1.2 km ≥ one kilometre. "เป็นกิโล" is colloquial distance and reads as kilometres.
- One caveat for Film, not the words: the spread took years (SRC002: over a 5-year period at one site), and the picture pull-out should not read as a single day. L09 sits before L10 "หลายปีผ่านไป", which keeps it honest. Optional O4.

**Q3b: L11 "เกิดหลังวันนั้น" vs timing of young crows scolding.**

- The words are true: SRC002 says the young hatched after trapping concluded and joined their parents' scolding. The young were tested at UW in 2007 (24 young, 10 families, 25–35 days after fledging) and in 2008 at the other four sites, so within about one to two years of the 2006 UW trapping, not "years later".
- **Caveat that matters for the picture (O2):** the researchers deliberately primed the young. They visited each territory 10–24 times for 5–20 min in the mask "to initiate learning" while parents scolded. Without parental scolding, fledglings "rarely" scolded a mask or unmasked person (3 % typical). The authors infer learning from parents, not a spontaneous memory. The study does not show young crows scolding with no researcher exposure years later; the 5-year rise at UW is attributed to social learning in general.
- Verdict: the L11 wording stands; L10 + L11 together imply "years later the young scold", which the long-term data does not isolate. Optional hedge in O2.

**Q3c: working title (N5), "อีกาจำหน้าคนที่จับมันได้ แล้วลูกมันก็ด่าตาม".**

- **T1 (must-fix, title only): bare "อีกา" asserts it for crows in general.** The study is American crows; Thai crows are unchecked (CL13), and Thai viewers will read "อีกา" as the crows they know. The hook and L01 carry "อีกาบางชนิด" precisely for this reason; the title must carry the same hedge (or name the species).
- Corrections, any one works (title is not read aloud; no ER09 impact):
  1. "อีกาอเมริกันจำหน้าคนที่จับมันได้ แล้วลูกมันก็ด่าตาม"
  2. "อีกาบางชนิดจำหน้าคนที่จับมันได้… ลูกมันก็ด่าตาม"
  3. Short cover form: "อีกาอเมริกันจำหน้าคน… ลูกมันก็ด่าตาม"
- "จำหน้า" in the title: acceptable (O3), but the cover picture should show the mask so the thumbnail does not promise a bare human face.
- "ด่าตาม" and "ลูกมัน": supported (L01 and L11 rows).

**Fade line (Q1 option B): does L12 stay true, and the wording.**

- **L12 stays true with or without the fade line.** It claims spread ("ส่งต่อถึงตัวที่ไม่เคยเห็นวันนั้นเลย"), which SRC002 supports, not present memory.
- **Fade support:** SRC005 (KUOW, Marzluff interview, 2024): the latest year's reaction was the lowest recorded; the originally captured birds "are all dead, as are most, if not all, the birds who witnessed the initial capture". SRC011 (UW, `lead-only`): calls peaked in 2013, then declined; Sept 2023 walk with no scolding. So the fade now has two secondary sources (a news interview and a UW page), still no peer-reviewed one. The cause (witnesses died) is Marzluff's interpretation in one interview.
- **The proposed line as written needs softening before use:** "จนอีกาที่เห็นวันนั้นตายไปหมด… เสียงด่าถึงค่อยเบาลง" says (a) all witnesses died ("หมด": the source says most, if not all) and (b) the fade happened because of it ("จน…ถึง"). Required text if option B is chosen (must-fix only if adopted as written):
  - **"นานหลังจากนั้น… อีกาที่เห็นวันนั้นตายไปเกือบหมด เสียงด่าก็เบาลง"**
  - Parallel, not causal; "เกือบหมด" matches "most, if not all"; no year count (16 years is news-only, CL11).
- **Reading risk (optional):** placed directly before L12 "แต่ความจำนี้ ส่งต่อ…", the pair can read as a contradiction ("it faded, but it was passed on"). It is true (it spread to crows that never saw the day, and the reaction later faded), but the story ends on a fade-then-spread order that blurs the takeaway. Preaw's Option A recommendation holds; if B is taken, close on the L12 wording unchanged.

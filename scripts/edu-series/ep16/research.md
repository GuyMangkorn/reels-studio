- Short answer:
  1. **Population `settled`:** UN WPP 2024 gives about **8.2 billion** (2024; about 8.23 billion for 1 July 2025 by the same dataset). Say "กว่าแปดพันล้านคน", not "แปดพันล้านคน" as a flat figure (SRC001, SRC002).
  2. **The hook holds only at standing densities of about 3.7 people per m² or more.** Bangkok 1,568.7 km² plus Nonthaburi about 622.3 km² = **2,191 km²**. At 4 people per m² (0.25 m² each, the pitch's number) 8.2 billion need **2,050 km²**: it fits with about 6 % to spare. At 5 per m² it needs 1,640 km² (a bit more than Bangkok alone). At the 0.3 m² "standing ellipse" a transport study recommends for design (3.3 per m²), it needs 2,460 km² and **does not fit**. At 2 per m² it needs 4,100 km². The hook needs a stated density on screen (SRC003–SRC006, own arithmetic).
  3. **The map fill must not "stop at the Nonthaburi border"** (concept beat 1). At 4 per m² the crowd fills Bangkok and then **about 77 % of Nonthaburi** (481 of 622 km²). Film and Preaw should show it ending inside Nonthaburi.
  4. **Jump: `model`, true as worded.** Humanity is about 4 × 10¹¹ kg (range 3–5 × 10¹¹) against Earth 5.9722 × 10²⁴ kg: ratio about **7 × 10⁻¹⁴** (range 5–9 × 10⁻¹⁴). If all push off in one place (the packed crowd), the Earth's centre shifts opposite to the people's rise by about **2–3 × 10⁻¹⁴ m** for a 0.3–0.5 m jump. A hydrogen atom is about 10⁻¹⁰ m across, so "less than the width of an atom" is true, with room to spare: about 1/3,000 of an atom (own arithmetic; assumptions in CL08).
  5. **Crowd quakes `settled` as events, magnitude loosely.** Seattle, 8 Jan 2011 (Seahawks v Saints playoff, Lumen Field then Qwest Field) on PNSN strong-motion station KDK, "a block or so away" (SRC008, SRC009). Taylor Swift Eras Tour, Lumen Field, 22–23 Jul 2023, same station KDK "across the street" (SRC008). PNSN itself says neither "caused" nor "triggered" an earthquake and that any magnitude is "at best, very approximate". The "magnitude 2.0 / 2.3" figures are press and one professor's equivalents (SRC007, SRC010). The distance range of the two Seattle events is **not given in what I inspected** beyond "next to the stadium"; the only documented range is Edinburgh 2024 (up to about 6 km; search excerpts only, SRC011).
  6. **A premise correction for the pitch:** "a football crowd in Seattle" is American football (NFL, ฟุตบอลอเมริกัน). Not a false claim, but a Thai viewer hears association football.

## Computation behind CL07–CL09 (own, reproducible)

- Mass of humanity: 8.2 × 10⁹ people. Adults 62 kg (SRC013), children lighter. Upper bound all at 62 kg: 5.1 × 10¹¹ kg. Lower bound average 40 kg: 3.3 × 10¹¹ kg. Central 4 × 10¹¹ kg. Ratio to 5.9722 × 10²⁴ kg: 5–8.5 × 10⁻¹⁴, central 6.7 × 10⁻¹⁴.
- Earth's shift by centre-of-mass: Δ_Earth = (m_humanity / M_Earth) × Δ_people. With Δ_people = 0.3 to 0.5 m: 2.0 × 10⁻¹⁴ to 3.3 × 10⁻¹⁴ m. At the upper mass bound (5.1 × 10¹¹ kg, 0.5 m): 4.3 × 10⁻¹⁴ m.
- Recoil speed: 6.7 × 10⁻¹⁴ × 2.4–3.1 m/s (rise 0.3–0.5 m) = 1.6–2.1 × 10⁻¹³ m/s for about half a second in the air. Same order.
- Reading: the shift is **about 1/2,000 to 1/5,000 of a hydrogen atom's width** (2–4 × 10⁻¹⁴ m against 10⁻¹⁰ m). "Less than an atom" is therefore true with a wide margin.

## 5. Top 10 facts for hooks (surprise and certainty)

1. Stegosaurus and T. rex lived more than 80 million years apart, longer than T. rex is from us (F02, checked, USGS). Hedge none.
2. Real Velociraptor: about turkey-sized and feathered, not the film's size (F20, likely; weights vary).
3. A mammal ate baby dinosaurs: Repenomamus, from a stomach-content fossil (F23, likely; Nature 2005).
4. Chickens in the village are living dinosaurs; the hook is the word "ไก่" (F41, checked for birds).
5. A Jurassic drop has no flowers; a Cretaceous one has few, and grass almost none (F03, F04, likely).
6. A day was about 30 minutes shorter near the end of the Cretaceous (F30, checked source; one shell).
7. Constellations were unrecognisable (F32, likely; popular source, only astronomy-textbook logic).
8. The Moon was only slightly closer: the film image of a huge Moon is wrong (F31, likely).
9. A crocodile relative up to roughly 10 m killed dinosaurs (F22, likely).
10. A wormhole is allowed on paper, but would need negative-energy matter not known to exist (F51, checked textbook-level).

Cut per ER07 (do not use, answer is unclear): oxygen level (F11), compass and magnetic field (F33), solar brightness (F34), sea level (F16), germs and immunity (F44 specifics), Quetzalcoatlus attacks on people, whole-body "which plants are edible" claims beyond "บางชนิดมีพิษ", wormhole time-travel physics (F52).

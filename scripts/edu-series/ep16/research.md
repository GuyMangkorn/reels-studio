# EDU-EP16-SHORT — research packet (W1.1, Boat)

- Deliverable: `EDU-EP16-SHORT` (Short, Thai, educational, concept C45 "อีกาจำหน้าคุณได้ และบอกลูกมันด้วย", `scripts/edu-series/concepts.md` `### C45`). Research date: 2026-11-10. Non-fiction explainer with acted-out beats. No script is written here.
- Short answer:
  1. **The study is real and the core is `settled`.** American crows (Corvus brachyrhynchos), Seattle area. Researchers in a mask trapped, banded and released crows; afterward crows scolded and mobbed that mask on sight, for people of different sizes, ages, sexes and gaits; no-mask and other-mask controls drew little scolding (SRC001 abstract, SRC003).
  2. **Never-caught crows joined in and later-hatched young scolded: `settled`.** Unbanded crows that watched or heard others scolded, spreading at least 1.2 km from the site; young crows whose parents had been conditioned scolded independently (SRC002, SRC004).
  3. **Numbers differ by site and by source; keep them rounded.** Campus site: 7 crows caught, trapping began 2006. Four other sites: 41 more (SRC004, press summary, so 48 in all and 7–15 per site, SRC001). Scolding at the campus site rose from about 20 % of crows met to about 60 % after five years (SRC004, SRC002).
  4. **"Years" is safe, "17 years" is not as stated in C45.** Peer-reviewed span: at least 2.7 years (SRC001); campus follow-up in press: five years (SRC004). Later non-peer-reviewed reports (SRC005, SRC006) say walks continued twice a year for about 16 years, then reactions faded. The original crows are dead. So "ยังจำได้" as a present-tense last line overstates the latest report. See Wording fixes.
  5. **Thai crows: no study found.** Hedge wording below.
- Tooling limits: web search excerpts plus fetched pages. Animal Behaviour 2010 and Proc R Soc B 2012 full texts were not opened (403 or wrong page); I read abstracts via catalogue/search summaries and a PMC review. Several fetched pages were summarised by a small model, so exact numbers outside SRC002/SRC004 are `lead-only`. No Thai source consulted.

## Wording fixes for Preaw (C45 and the hook)

1. **Last line (must-fix):** "คุณอาจลืมไปแล้ว… แต่อีกาแถวบ้านคุณยังจำได้" asserts present-tense memory on a Thai street and, worse, the latest report says the Seattle reaction faded as the original birds died. Documented fact: for years the scolding continued and passed to crows that never saw the capture. Suggested: "นักวิจัยเดินทดสอบอยู่หลายปี… อีกายังร้องใส่หน้ากากนั้น ทั้งที่หลายตัวไม่เคยเห็นวันนั้น" or the hedge "ความจำนี้ส่งต่อได้ทั้งฝูง". If Preaw wants the twist of fading, it is true and short: "จนอีกาที่เห็นวันนั้นตายไปหมด เสียงด่าถึงค่อยเบาลง". That sentence is `settled` per SRC005 (news), flag as one news source.
2. **Hook (must-fix, species):** "อีกาจำหน้าคุณได้… และมันจะสอนลูกมันให้เกลียดหน้าคุณด้วย" is documented for American crows only, and "สอนลูก" is parent-to-young learning shown in the study; "เกลียด" is interpretation. Safe Thai hook with narrow hedge (ER10): "อีกาบางชนิด จำหน้าคนที่ทำร้ายมันได้ นานหลายปี… แล้วยังบอกต่อให้ตัวอื่นด้วย". If the hook keeps "อีกา", add the species early: "ที่ซีแอตเทิล อีกาอเมริกัน…". Thai-specific: "ยังไม่พบงานวิจัยแบบเดียวกันกับอีกาบ้านหรืออีกาปากหนา" is true for what I searched; use "ไม่รู้ว่าอีกาบ้านเราทำแบบนี้ไหม" only if the Thai hedge beat is kept (optional).
3. **"เกลียด" (optional):** swap for "ร้องด่าและโฉบใส่" (what was measured).
4. **Total caught (optional):** narrate "ไม่กี่ตัว"; do not give a total unless the full text is checked.
5. **"ปล่อยโดยไม่เจ็บ" (optional):** the studies say trapped, banded, released; do not narrate "ไม่เจ็บ".
6. **No-mask control (optional):** keep the bare-faced beat but narrate "ไม่ใส่หน้ากาก" without claiming the same individual in every trial.

## Premise, reveal and learning impact

- Premise and mid-reveals (mask is tracked, face not person, never-caught join in) are supported, so the story holds.
- The final beat needs a rewrite (Wording fix 1); it changes the last line, not the plot.
- The flip "the face outlives the event" is true in a stronger form than the pitch: the memory spread to crows that never met the original event and young born later.
- Thai relevance is zero as a claim; the viewer-address ending is a framing only. Mark the hook "อีกาบางชนิด" or name the species.

## Uncertainties and access gaps

- 16 and 17 year figures: news only (SRC005) or tertiary (SRC006); no Urban@UW or journal page reached. A later peer-reviewed long-term paper may exist; I did not find one.
- Crow count (7 campus, 41 elsewhere) is from one press summary of SRC002; 7–15 per site comes from SRC001 abstract. A reader could add them wrong (the 7–15 range gives 35–75), so give no total.
- Year 2006 comes from secondary sources. SRC001 was published online 2009.

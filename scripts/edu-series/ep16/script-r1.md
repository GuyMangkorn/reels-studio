## Story core

- **Logline:** your village drops into the dinosaur age and the one dinosaur everybody expects is the one you will surely not meet: T. rex is not born yet. The dinosaur age lasted about 165 million years, and the T. rex and Stegosaurus in the same toy box lived more than 80 million years apart, longer than T. rex is from you. The track by the gate belongs to a real meat-eater of ancient Isan; and T. rex's relatives were in your village all along: the hens.
- **Central question:** in the dinosaur age, why would you never meet a T. rex?
- **Intention / obstacle:** เก่ง wants to see a T. rex (he carries a toy one); the age itself is the obstacle. His guess (the track is a T. rex) is wrong, which opens "then whose?"
- **Feeling:** the "huh?" of the hook; deflation at "ยังไม่เกิด"; the "wait, what" of the toy box; a cold second under the shadow; a smile at the hen.
- **Takeaway:** "the dinosaur age" is not one time: it lasted about 165 million years, and its famous dinosaurs were often tens of millions of years apart; where you land decides which ones exist. Birds, the village hens included, are dinosaur relatives that are still here.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "ยังไม่เกิด". L03 says it as the reason he cannot meet T. rex (a loss); L09 flips it (T. rex is not born yet, but its relatives came with you).

## Truth and ending

- **Spoken as settled, plain:** the dinosaur age lasted about 165 million years and T. rex came only near its end (F01, F02); the real T. rex and Stegosaurus lived more than 80 million years apart, longer than T. rex is from you (F02, USGS); at about 130 million years ago, T. rex was not born yet (F02 date 68–66 Ma vs the fiction drop at ~130 Ma; F24); Isan then had meat-eating dinosaurs, and their bones are still dug up there (F24, SRC16, no species name); hens are relatives of T. rex (F41, "ญาติ" only).
- **Fiction:** the wormhole and the drop (F53, "สมมติ" once in L01); the village date "ราว 130 ล้านปี" as a story choice inside Isan's fossil age (F24, SRC16); the track in the mud, the shadow, the hen's print beside the big print.
- **One narrow hedge per line (ER10):** L03 "ราว", L04 "ราว". No source, method or evidence phrase.
- **Knowledge twist (rule flip):** "ยังไม่เกิด" is first a disappointment (no T. rex for you), then the frame of the payoff (not born yet, yet its relatives walked into the past with your village). Fair clues: the hens in their coop and the three-toed track, both on screen in B01 (K01, K02).
- **Ending (closed):** L09 over the two prints side by side; the hen clucks; hold ~2 s. No call to action, no tip, no next-episode line.

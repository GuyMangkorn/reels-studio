## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Everyone "knows" rubber tyres save you from lightning in a car. Frame 0 shows the threat and the vehicle at once: a red hard-top sedan stopped on a rain-lashed rural road shoulder at dusk, windows shut, a young woman inside, a fork of lightning already flickering on the horizon; the near strike lands within the first half second of L01. Feeling arc (script story core): surprise (B01) → apparent contradiction "metal conducts?" (B02) → "so the car is fine?" deflated by a damaged tyre (B03) → small eerie silence when the dash stays dead (B04) → a calm, sober rereading of the same metal shell (B05). The payoff is quiet, never a grin.
- **Story first (designed before the kit was opened).** **DRIVER** (the occupant; the narration's "คุณ"): a Thai office worker of about 25 on her drive home, sensible and a little tense, who has pulled onto the shoulder to wait out a storm. Ponytail, mustard short-sleeve blouse, navy trousers; seated at the wheel (right-hand drive). She reacts, she never demonstrates: startle at the strike, a doubtful glance at the door frame on L04, a breath of relief on L06, a puzzled press of the dark radio screen on L08, a sober face on L11. Hands lift off the wheel to her lap after the strike and stay clear of the door frame (conservative staging, F06; not advice). **CAR21**: a generic compact four-door hard-top sedan, painted metallic red, every window closed, no brand or badge. **Place**: a two-lane rural highway in central Thailand, flat paddy on both sides, a few sugar palms far off, no poles or towers near the car (so no taller conductor confuses the picture), dusk under a thunderstorm.
- **Medium (VD01).** Painted 2D p5.brush through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`, `cam-moves.ts`) in world metres; DRIVER is a four-head kit card; CAR21 is a new painted world-metre build (exterior shell, glass, wheels, cabin interior, dashboard) in the same projection; rain via kit `paintRain`/`fall.ts`; storm sky from kit `paintStormSky` as a base; the near bolt is a new painted element; the strike flash is the kit `Flash` screen layer (`glow.tsx`, top-heavy, fades to ≤ 0.1 by the caption band). The explanatory route glow is painted **in the same canvas** on CAR21's shell geometry (shares the camera, no overlay registration); its label chip and captions are Remotion screen layers.

- **Light.** One storm look with two states. Before the strike: cool diffuse sky key from above, a faint warm-grey dusk residue low behind the car (−x), the cyan dash glow on her face (CL21-03 plant). Strike: one white flash from above, top-heavy. After: same cool key, no dash glow (the cabin is a shade darker; fair-play clue, never pointed at). Explanatory shots (SH06, SH10, the SH13 echo) desaturate and cool the world so the warm glow and the warm blouse are the only warm things. Payoff (SH13 + hold): the rain eases and the horizon band lifts warm (`#b8a48a`) as the storm moves off (retention camera: warm again on the payoff).
- **Camera language.** Every shot moves inside the set (eased yaw arc 6–10° and a push of 5–15 % of the distance). Larger moves only where the beat turns: SH03 crane-up to read the whole enclosure (L02), SH07 window-to-tyre descent (L06 "ดูยางก่อน"), SH13 pull-back from the wet roof edge to the whole car (L12, the ending). One action axis: exterior cameras stay on the driver/verge side (+z, yaw −10…+90); interior cameras stay inside the cabin. The car never moves.
- **Conceal and reveal.** CL21-01: the front tyre is large in frame 0 (tyre-height camera) and named by L01; it is paid in B03 with the same tyre damaged. CL21-02: the closed windows, roof and continuous body are legible in SH01 and declared in SH03. CL21-03: the dash glow lights her face before the strike; after it the dash is dark but small and unremarked (SH04) until SH09.
- **Truth limits on picture (binding on every shot).**
  1. **The vehicle is always the enclosed hard-top metal car with every window closed** (CK21-02). No open window, sunroof, convertible, motorcycle or person outside.
  2. **No exact roof entry, no single exclusive line, no four-tyre exit, no sealed bubble** (script Film constraints, which bind B02's route graphic). **R5 (user request after Draft v1, 2026-11-17):** the live strikes (B01-SH01 and the two B05-SH13 hold strikes) are seen landing on the car's outer metal, each inside a small white contact flare, at two different places across the video (near rear roof edge twice, bonnet once), so no single entry point is implied; no live strike shows light travelling over the body (only a 2-frame cold rim flash), and every route graphic stays labelled and never starts from a strike point. Earlier (R1–R4): the strike's contact was hidden inside the flash bloom. The route glow is a broad band over the whole outer skin, flowing around the cabin and into the wet road as a diffuse spread under the whole car, not at four wheel points.
  3. **Every route graphic carries the label chip** (proposed Thai text "ภาพอธิบายแบบง่าย", Preaw/Boat check, Q1) and appears only in SH06, SH10 and the SH13 echo.
  4. **SH10 shows a thin, dim inward branch to the dash wiring** so the picture never claims zero current inside (CK21-09); SH06 must not contradict it (no "nothing gets in" emphasis).
  5. **Selected possible consequences only:** one damaged front tyre (scorched split sidewall, sagging; a wisp of steam in the rain) and a dead dashboard/radio screen. No fire, no shattered glass, no smoke from the bonnet, no engine failure attempt, no injury.
  6. **No proof by grin:** she never smiles at camera or celebrates; L11 plays on a sober face. No caption or graphic promising safety.
  7. **No real place, brand or incident;** no named road or sign, no car badge.
  8. **Sound never carries a claim:** no high-voltage hum as "evidence" (script sound intent).

## Sound direction

Built on the script's [beat and sound handoff](script.md#beat-and-sound-handoff). Each cue is designed from its shot; a library ID is named only as a candidate whose description fits, and Ton confirms by ear ([fit beats reuse](../../../../docs/sound-generation-workflow.md#fit-beats-reuse)).

- **Music family and arc.** **M1** NEEDS-GEN: a quiet, curious explainer bed, muted felt piano ostinato over a soft warm synth pad and a low pulsing sub, light brushed shaker, 92 bpm, about 46 s, phrase ends every 4 bars, no drum kit / tense but intelligent, never scary. Arc: enters under L01 after the strike accent at about −14 dB under VO; **drops out under L04's question** (rain carries it), re-enters with the pulse on L05; lifts a little lighter in B03; **drops to silence for the B04 cutout** (about 0.5 s) then returns sparse under L09; resolves warm in B05 (pad opens to a major colour, pulse stops on L12) and hands over to the ending sound. Library check: none of the `public/audio/bed` files was auditioned here; Ton may offer one only if it matches this description.
- **Ending (UT15, Film's choice): END1 "storm passing".** NEEDS-GEN: one warm open felt-piano chord (major ninth) laid over the last rain and a far, receding thunder roll that fades left; a single high soft glass-like harmonic tone rings out over it / one gesture / felt piano, distant thunder, rain / wide, outdoor, gentle tail / 2.8 s / relief and understanding, calm, not triumphant. Starts with the hold after L12; Editor fades the rain under it (`A_fade`).
- **Ambience per location (the inside/outside switch is part of the story: the shell is audible).**
  - **A1 exterior storm** NEEDS-GEN: heavy tropical rain hissing on wet asphalt and paddy water, rain drumming on a car's sheet-metal roof heard from two metres outside, wind gusts, far thunder rumbles now and then / steady with sparse events / water, steel, asphalt / open, wide, wet / 14 s loop / exposed, loud, threatening. Library candidate for the far rumbles only: `weather_thunder-roll-distant_01` (fits "far thunder" if Ton confirms it sits behind rain). `weather_rain-thatch-tin-heavy_01` does **not** fit (thatch and tin, not a car).
  - **A2 cabin** NEEDS-GEN: the same rain heard from inside a closed car: dense muffled drumming on the roof right overhead, water sheeting on glass, low cabin resonance, no wind / steady / steel roof, glass, upholstery / small closed space, close / 12 s loop / enclosed, muffled, oddly sheltered.
  - **A3 explanatory state** (SH06, SH10, SH13 echo): A1 or A2 low-passed and pulled back −8 dB, so the glow cue is clear; no new bed.
- **SFX language.** Restrained and physical; one reconstructed strike, small aftermath sounds, one tiny electronic death. No sirens, no explosion, no zaps on the route glow.
  - **S1 strike** NEEDS-GEN (SH01): an extremely close lightning crack, a sharp tearing whip with a bright sizzle edge, then a low thudding pressure pulse under it and a short rolling tail / one event / air, electric discharge / open, very close then wide / 1.6 s / startle, not horror. A tail into far thunder may borrow the library roll above.
  - **S2 startle breath** (SH02): her sharp inhale, cut by the cabin; generate with the driver voice only if Editor casts one; else omit (no dialogue in the script).
  - **S3 glint ticks** NEEDS-GEN (SH03): three tiny soft glassy ticks, one per highlight sweep (roof, body, window) / soft, close / 0.15 s each / "look here".
  - **S4 route glow** NEEDS-GEN (SH06, SH10, SH13): a soft airy rising shimmer like a breath through fine wind chimes, warm, no electric buzz, travelling left to right / 2.5 s, fades / curious, clear.
  - **S5 tyre** NEEDS-GEN (SH08): a slow thin air hiss from a split tyre, rubber settling with a soft creak onto wet asphalt, a faint steam tick in rain / small, close, dry / 2.5 s / "oh, it did get hurt".
  - **S6 radio press + cutout** NEEDS-GEN (SH09): a soft plastic button click inside a quiet car, then a tiny electronic relay tick and a falling two-note blip that dies mid-note / close, small, intimate / 0.6 s / eerie. Library candidate for the blip only: `electric_glitch-blip-soft_01` (Ton checks it dies cleanly rather than glitches). Then **silence of music for about 0.5 s**; cabin rain stays.
- **Silence policy.** Narration is intelligible from frame 0: S1 peaks under the first syllable but is ducked so "ไม่ใช่ยางนะ" reads (script: no long thunder prelude). Music rests under L04 and for the B04 beat. The rain never stops, only changes room.
- **Ducking.** Music −8 dB under narration; A1 −6 dB, A2 −8 dB under narration; S1 allowed 4 dB above the music but the VO stays on top after its first 6 frames.

## What the chip must say

1. The glow is a drawing that explains, not a photo or footage of a real strike.
2. The glow is simplified: a typical route, not a measured or exclusive path. SH10 shows a thin branch reaching the dash, so the label must not suggest that "all current stays outside".
3. It must read in under about 1 s at chip size (est 360×56 px, top-left under `@TamMaiLa`).
4. It uses plain words at the hook level (ER05). It names no source, method or evidence quality. ER10 binds spoken lines only, but the chip follows its spirit too, so it avoids words like "ตามแบบจำลอง" or "จากงานวิจัย".
5. Tone: the narrator speaks plainly and directly ("คุณ"). A neutral TV-style tag fits that tone better than a formal disclaimer.

## Recommendation

**CHIP-A "ภาพจำลองแบบง่าย".** It is the only option that marks both boundaries the script needs (not footage, not a measured path), in a tag Thai viewers already know from news re-enactments. It stays about as short as the placeholder.

Notes for Boat's check:

- "จำลอง" here means "illustration or re-enactment", as in TV usage. It does not mean a computed simulation. If Boat reads it as implying a physics model, fall back to CHIP-B.
- No option claims that current stays only on the outside. The SH10 inner branch stays compatible with L09.
- Write it without ไม้ยมก ("แบบง่าย ๆ") to save width and keep the tag neutral.

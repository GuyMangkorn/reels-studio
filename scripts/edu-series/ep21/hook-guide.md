## The opening contract

**Approach: result/myth reversal.** State immediately that rubber is not the explanation, then make the conducting metal shell the apparent contradiction. This is a declarative opening; do not convert it into EP20's directional guessing game.

Proposed spoken opening, L01:

> ไม่ใช่ยางนะ...ที่ช่วยป้องกันคนในรถถูกฟ้าผ่า

The opening “ไม่ใช่ยางนะ” puts the reversal inside approximately the first 2 s, subject to actual voice measurement. Frame 0 must already communicate a lightning threat and a visibly enclosed metal hard-top car with closed windows. It must not show an occupant triumphantly proving survival. L02 immediately speaks the conditions:

> ต้องเป็นรถปิดมิดชิด หลังคากับตัวถังโลหะ ปิดกระจกด้วย

During approximately the first 7 s, the viewer should understand **which vehicle** is meant and wonder what helps if the tires do not. The word “โลหะ” is ordinary language here; no Faraday-cage label, voltage figure or ideal electrical bubble belongs in the hook.

## Protect these boundaries

- Keep **enclosed, hard-top metal vehicle, closed windows**, in the spoken scope and opening picture. A metal roof alone, open convertible, motorcycle or arbitrary vehicle with metal is not equivalent.
- “Helps protect” is not guaranteed survival. Do not use an uninjured fictional passenger as proof. No actual named case is claimed.
- No claim that rubber blocks lightning, all current follows one drawn line or no discharge reaches components. Label route graphics explanatory, not measured. No exact roof entry or four-tire exit pattern.
- Tire damage and dashboard failure are **possible selected outcomes** in the hypothetical scene. Do not imply the same sequence always happens or add an explosion for spectacle.
- Keep the passenger inside and clear of exposed conductive parts. Do not show safe exterior contact or supply an unsupported interior-injury mechanism. Metal conduction does not mean metal attracts lightning.

## Story core

- **Logline:** your village drops into the dinosaur age; the forest is green everywhere, yet you will hardly find a single flower, because about 130 million years ago flowers have only just begun. Stranger still, there is no grass under your feet: grass is a plant that flowers too, so it has not arrived. Rice is a grass, so the paddy at your village's edge is a field this age almost never had, and to the long-necked dinosaur that comes to sniff it, the strange things in this world are your rice and your flowers, not it.
- **Central question:** why can't you find a flower (and then grass) in a dinosaur forest?
- **Intention / obstacle:** ยาย goes to hang a fresh marigold garland at the spirit house and wants flowers for the next one; the forest has none. เก่ง's guess (a cone is a flower) is wrong.
- **Feeling:** wonder at an all-green world; a laugh at the cone; ยาย's small worry for her garland; surprise at "grass flowers too"; awe and a laugh when the long neck sniffs the rice; a warm flip at the end.
- **Takeaway:** flowering plants appeared late in the dinosaur age; around 130 million years ago they had only just begun, and grass, itself a flowering plant, was almost absent. The plants we think of as "nature" are newer than many dinosaurs.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "ดอกไม้เพิ่งเริ่ม". L04 says it (why no flowers); L06 repeats it as the cause of the next problem (no grass); L09 flips it: what is rare here is what *you* brought, so the strange thing in this world is you.

## Truth and ending

- **Spoken as settled, plain:** at about 130 million years ago flowering plants had only just begun (F03, earliest credible ~130–125 Ma); grass was almost absent (F04: oldest evidence ~113–101 Ma); rice is a grass (F42); the cone is a conifer cone, not a flower (F13 note: conifers, ferns, cycads, ginkgoes, horsetails; textbook-level). Grass being a flowering plant is textbook botany, **not in the research ledger: `unchecked`** (L06).
- **Hedged (one word):** L02 "แทบ"; L04 "ราว"; L06 "แทบ"; L07 "แทบ"; L08 "อาจ".
- **Fiction:** the wormhole and the drop; the long-necked dinosaur by the paddy and its sniff (a long-neck of ancient Isan, F24/SRC16, no species name); "ในสายตามัน" is the narrator's playful reading of the animal, not a claim about what it thought.
- **Knowledge twist (rule flip):** "ดอกไม้เพิ่งเริ่ม" first explains a lack (no flowers, then no grass), then turns the picture around: the rarest things in this world are the village's own rice and marigolds. Fair clues: the garland (K01), the green paddy (K02) and the grass that stops at the fence (K03), all on screen in B01.
- **Ending (closed):** L09 over the long-neck snorting at the garland, petals lifting; hold ~2 s. No call to action, no tip.

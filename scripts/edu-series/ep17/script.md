## Story core

- **Logline:** in the dinosaur world the most valuable thing in your village is not gold or a phone but the woodpile: the forest is full of wood, but fresh wood is wet and has to dry before it burns well; clear stream water still has to be boiled, boiling for a whole village burns tens of kilos of wood a day, one rain makes the wood hard to light (and the free rain water still has to be boiled), and the evening smoke is what keeps the mosquitoes (which already existed in the dinosaur age) off. Fire helps with almost everything, except the one thing you feared most.
- **Central question:** why is the woodpile worth more than gold?
- **Intention / obstacle:** ยาย runs the village's fire; เก่ง thinks it is a chore. Each rule shows him why.
- **Feeling:** "huh?" at the hook; "but the forest is full of wood!"; "but it's clear!"; the village scramble in the rain; a slap and a laugh at the mosquito; ยาย's tired "ฟืนอีกแล้ว…"; a frozen held breath at the end.
- **Takeaway:** fresh-cut wood must dry before it is good firewood; clear water still has germs and must be boiled, rain water too; boiling for many people takes a lot of fuel; wet wood is hard to light; mosquitoes are older than you think, and smoke helps keep them off; a fire is not armour.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "ฟืน" (every rule costs firewood; V04 "ฟืนอีกแล้ว…"). The last rule flips it: fire helps "แทบทุกเรื่อง… ยกเว้นเรื่องนี้".

## Truth and ending

- **Spoken as settled, plain:** fresh wood from the forest is still damp and has to dry before it is firewood (R2-F19; R2.2); clear stream water must be boiled (brought to the boil) before drinking (R2-F11, M02); rain water caught from a roof still has to be boiled (R2-F14; R2.2); germs are too small to see (M02 reason); wet wood is hard to light (R2-F19); mosquitoes existed in the dinosaur age (R2-F28).

- **Preaw's framing (not a fact claim):** L02 "ของที่มีค่าที่สุด… คือกองฟืน" is the episode's argument, paid by the rules.
- **Fiction:** the wormhole, the rain, the mosquito swarm at dusk, the predator walking past the fire.

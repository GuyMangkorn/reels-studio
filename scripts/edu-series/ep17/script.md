## Story core

- **Logline:** everyone alive, more than eight billion people, packed at about four to a square metre (half a metre by half a metre each), fits in Bangkok and spills into Nonthaburi, running out of people before Nonthaburi is full. If that whole packed crowd jumped at once, the Earth would move, by less than the width of one atom, and settle back when they land, because the Earth is so much heavier than all of us. A single stadium crowd does make the ground shake, a little and only near the stadium. Pull back to space: the whole of humanity is one dot.
- **Central question:** how big are "all of us", really, next to the Earth?
- **Intention / obstacle:** `man-spiky` is stuck in the packed crowd (he wants room: "แน่นจริง"); the viewer's sense that humanity is huge and heavy is the obstacle each beat tests (map, jump, stadium).
- **Feeling:** disbelief at the hook; "it really fits" as the map fills and runs out; fun at the countdown; a small laugh at "น้อยกว่าความกว้างอะตอมเดียว"; surprise that one stadium does shake the ground; a quiet, small feeling at the last line.
- **Takeaway:** humanity takes up very little room and weighs almost nothing next to the Earth: packed tight we fit in about two thousand square kilometres, and all of us jumping together moves the planet less than an atom's width.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "เราว่าเรา…แล้ว… โลก…กว่า" (we think we are a lot; the Earth is more). L06 says it for size ("เยอะ… ใหญ่กว่า"), L09 for weight ("หนัก… หนักกว่ามหาศาล"). L11 tests it ("แต่…" one stadium shakes the ground) and L13 keeps it (only near the stadium). L15 flips it from comfort ("the Earth is fine") to smallness ("we are one dot").

## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Every viewer has made a video call abroad and assumed it goes "up" to a satellite. Frame 0 shows that call by the sea, with no words: a phone on a seaside rail, a woman waving on its screen, the green hose spraying the pot beside it, a bay of fishing boats behind (UT01, retention camera). Feelings by beat: surprise (the call's line dives into the sea) → "huh, a helicopter versus a motorway" → disbelief at the hose → unease when he looks at the boats in his own bay → a quiet, serious turn at the Red Sea case, then "oh, the anchor again" → calm wonder as the pulse takes another cable and he keeps laughing on the call.
- **Story first (designed before the kit was opened).** **MAN**: a Thai man in his mid-twenties at home on a weekday afternoon (about 16:30) in a fishing-village house on the Gulf coast: mustard tee, denim knee shorts, bare shins, rubber flip-flops, messy hair. Relaxed, curious, a little slow to worry; he waters with his thumb half over the hose end (the Thai way, a fan of spray), so the hose and his hand are always in play. **SISTER**: his older sister working abroad, seen only on the phone screen: long black hair, cream knit sweater, a warm lamp-lit room with a window behind her showing a snowy blue evening and far city lights (the "abroad" cue; no country named, no claim); she waves, laughs, talks. **The yard**: packed sand and patchy grass in front of a wooden house on low stilts with blue shutters; at the sea edge a low whitewashed wall with a wooden plank on top (the script's "porch rail"), crowded with pots (basil, chilli, a tin orchid), a big glazed pot of magenta bougainvillea and an aloe at its foot, an earthen water jar (โอ่ง), a tap post with the green hose looping across the sand; the phone stands on a mini tripod on the plank. Beyond: a short sand beach, two leaning coconut palms, a calm bay with two longtail boats and a small cabin fishing boat at anchor (anchor lines visible at their bows) and, further out, a trawler working with its outrigger booms up (K01); a green headland on the left; the open sea a deeper blue band at the horizon. **Under the sea**: one continuous, stylized slope from the bright sandy shallows (ripples, seagrass, hull bottoms and anchor chains overhead, the cable buried with a faint ridge and a few bared stretches) down a darker slope to an empty, still, near-black deep floor where the thin cable lies on the ooze. **The Red Sea flashback**: a grey, overcast open sea; a generic cargo ship already damaged, listing, low by the stern, a thin trail of grey smoke; nobody, no name, no flag; below, a plain, mid-grey sandy floor with three cables. **The globe**: a simple painted Earth with sand-coloured continents, thin glowing lines across the oceans and light pulses running on them.
- **Medium (VD01).** Painted 2D p5.brush through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`, `cam-moves.ts`) in world metres for the yard, the seabed, the Red Sea and its seabed; the globe as a painted sphere in its own unit space through the same camera; people are 4-head kit rig cards. Crisp Remotion SVG (project default PHONE-SVG) only for the phone screen content (the sister's call window). Screen layers (the thought bubble outline, "ภาพจำลอง" label, "2024 · ทะเลแดง" card, captions, channel mark) are DOM/screen layers above the painted canvas, as in the series. No footage, no photos, no Three.js, no Blender (deactivated).

- **Light.** Yard: low afternoon sun from screen right in the sea-facing shots (azimuth from +x, elevation about 28°, slightly from the sea side), warm key `#ffd08a`, cool sky fill; a glitter path on the bay right of centre; long soft shadows toward the camera-left. The reverse shots (facing the house) take the sun on the man's front-right face side. Underwater: shafts and caustics in the shallows, falling off with depth; the deep is lit only by the cable's own soft signal glow and a faint blue ambient (UT03: the cable and the anchor never sink into black). Globe: even soft light, no terminator (it is a diagram, not a time of day). Red Sea: flat overcast, no sun, no fire glow. Flashback seabed: flat diffuse mid-value light (neither bright shallow nor dark deep, F023 note: no depth is claimed). End: the same sun, a touch warmer (payoff warmth, retention camera colour rule: cool B04–B05, warm B06).
- **Camera language.** Every shot moves inside its set (retention camera: a small eased yaw arc of 6–10° and a slow push of 5–15 % of the camera distance; large moves only for the hook (B01-SH01 to SH04), the twist climb (B04-SH02) and the anchor run-down (B05-SH04)). The yard keeps one action axis, the line from the man to the phone: every yard camera stays on its camera-left side (the OTS behind his right shoulder and the reverse from his front-left beyond the wall). Under the sea, the shore is always toward −z: in descending moves the camera faces the open sea, in the climb it faces the shore; the cutaway (B04-SH05) puts the shore on screen-left and the deep on screen-right, the same as the yard's bay at screen-left-to-centre. The globe returns twice (B01-SH04, B06-SH01) with the same framing family so the repeat reads as "back to the big picture".
- **Conceal and reveal.** K01 (boats at anchor, trawler) and K02 (the green hose) are both in frame 0 and in every yard wide, never pointed at until L07 (hose) and L10/V03 (boats). The deep floor is shown empty in L09 before the climb finds the danger in the shallows (L10). The anchor appears first as a hanging chain under a hull (B04-SH02) before it drags (B04-SH04). In B05 the cause (the anchor) is held back until "แต่"; L14 shows only the result (three cables going dark).
- **Truth limits on picture (binding on every shot).**
  1. **Red Sea flashback (F023):** "ภาพจำลอง" in the label slot on every frame from the first frame of L14 to the last frame of L15, including the yard reaction cut inside L15 (the script's continuous range); "2024 · ทะเลแดง" card on L14 only. No missile in flight, no explosion, no fire, no attacker, no flag, no weapon, no face, no crew, no ship name or livery, nothing that makes the attack exciting; the ship is shown already damaged (a dark scorch patch on the aft hull side and a thin smoke trail only). The ship is **abandoned and drifting** (R1.2.1): no wake, no lights, no lifeboat action; the anchor chain runs taut forward from the bow. The anchor drag is a depiction (label on). The flashback seabed is **depth-neutral**: never the bright shallow bay palette, never the near-black deep.
  2. **Cable look (F007, F008, F026; Boat L02 note):** in deep water the cable lies on the floor, the same 20 mm as the hose; near shore it is shown thicker and mostly buried (a ridge with a few bared stretches), so no shot claims a bare near-shore cable. No armour, burial depth, repair ship or number is narrated or labelled.
  3. **Globe (F001, F014, F016, F027):** the lines are illustrative, not real routes; nothing on the globe is labelled; no number on screen except the subtitle "ราว 99%" (L03). L17 sparks sit mostly on near-coast segments (F016). L18 reroutes one pulse onto a neighbouring line; nothing implies the reroute is instant or lossless.
  4. **Satellite and capacity (F005, F006):** the helicopter carries one crate, the motorway many lanes: direction only; no lane count, no Gbps, no "slow" cue (the helicopter flies briskly, it is just small).
  5. **Breaks (F015, F016):** the L12 snag is an anchor hooking and parting the cable and a trawl net scraping past; no sharks, quakes or natural causes (ER07). The L13 cutaway shows the deep cable calm and the shallows busy; no fault statistics drawn.
  6. **Sound never carries a clue**; no siren, alarm, explosion, missile whoosh or news audio anywhere in B05 (Boat W1.3 sound note).

## Technical handoff

- **Composition (painted 2D).** One painted canvas per shot through `ycam.ts`; figures depth-sorted in the set (`paintSorted`); the signal line is a world-space polyline projected per frame and sorted with the set (it passes behind the palm CP1 and in front of the sky; it enters the water at the waterline); PHONE-SVG screen is a DOM layer locked to the projected screen quad of the painted phone (Boss: a 4-corner screen-quad export from the phone draw, the WORLD FOG VD09 method); bubble outline, LBL, CARD, captions and mark are screen layers ([DF-20261030-painted-screen-layers-overlay](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-painted-screen-layers-overlay), [DF-20261030-painted-label-slots](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-painted-label-slots): LBL and CARD keep their slots while on).
- **Scale / projection.** One pinhole per space (`ycam`, f 900–1400 as written per shot). W is compressed staging (recorded above). D is a 3D model block with heavy recorded vertical exaggeration and miniature boats, one pinhole like every other space. The B03-SH02 equal-scale rule binds both halves.
- **Coordinates / camera.** Camera lines use `camMove(at, yaw, r, eye, {f, hy})` (kit `cam-moves.ts`); positions in the sheet are the resulting camera points (est). The yard keeps one axis (man ↔ phone). Near plane 0.05 m for B01-SH01's phone close (`YCam.near`, the EDU-EP02 counter-level method).
- **Contact / identity.** Hose: tap anchor fixed at the post; grip anchor = MAN right-hand mitten; slack anchor = left hand (when not on the brow or hips); the hose end opening is the spray emitter. Anchor drag: chain from the boat (B04) or the hawse (B05) to the anchor shank; the anchor's fluke tip carries the furrow and, at K, the cable lift. Cable identity: the same cable from the beach to the deep in every W shot; the break at K exists only after B04-SH04.
- **Depth / alpha.** Water surface seen from below is a painted bright band with hull silhouettes; caustics are additive glazes ≤ 0.3 ([DF-20261030-painted-glaze-pickup](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-painted-glaze-pickup) if pickup appears); large flat areas (sky, deep water, globe ocean) stay wash-flat ([DF-20261030-p5brush-large-fill-mottle](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-p5brush-large-fill-mottle)).
- **Lighting / colour.** Painted light only (no runtime light). Grades: the reconstruction grade (EP14/15 grade method; `edu14/grade.tsx` pattern) on B05-SH01, SH02, SH04; never on the yard cut B05-SH03.
- **Motion / clock.** Event clocks shot-local; ambient clocks (boat bob, trawler drift, palm sway, spray droplets, caustics, globe pulses) run through holds ([DF-20261030-painted-story-hold-ambient-clock](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-painted-story-hold-ambient-clock)); every value a pure function of frame + seed (seek parity). The splash cut (B01-SH02 → SH03) and the pulse match (SH03 → SH04) are in-canvas dissolves ([DF-20261029-painted-timeline-dissolve](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261029-painted-timeline-dissolve)).
- **Loading / resources.** Natural Earth land rings loaded once (static JSON import); no per-frame allocation of land polygons.
- **Contract / delivery.** Episode module `src/painted/edu17/` (sets, cast, globe, shots) and a composition registered per [README](../../../README.md#videos-and-parallel-work) (never `src/Root.tsx` per episode). No new runtime timeline fields; no shared renderer change.
- **Feasibility / cost.** Hardest: the world-space signal line in the hook; the continuous sink-and-climb (B04-SH02); the over-under split. Each has a fallback in the scene sheet. R1.1 adds the 3D cutaway block (N12, medium-high effort: about 1.5–2× the flat diagram; face ordering of the cut faces under a moving camera and the front-face glaze are the risks; cut-face slivers at the block edges are a known section risk, [DF-20261029-zcondo-sp02-section-spike](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261029-zcondo-sp02-section-spike), different stack, read as a caution only).
- **Dev findings consulted (index read 2026-11-14 at `64291e8`; none revalidated here):** DF-20261029-painted-timeline-dissolve (splash and pulse transitions), DF-20261030-painted-label-slots (LBL + CARD at once), DF-20261030-painted-screen-layers-overlay (bubble/label/caption layers), DF-20261030-painted-story-hold-ambient-clock (ambient motion in holds), DF-20261030-p5brush-large-fill-mottle (sky, deep water, ocean washes), DF-20261030-map-land-clip-closure (globe land rings), DF-20261030-painted-glaze-pickup (caustics), DF-20261030-ending-hold-option (closed hold: the hold is plate-owned, not a frozen frame); R1.1 adds DF-20261030-painted-layer-order (the block's front-face glaze over its contents) and DF-20261029-zcondo-sp02-section-spike (cut-face caution, Blender-era scope, not revalidated).

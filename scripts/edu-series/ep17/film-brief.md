## Visual intent

Wood is the village's gold: every survival rule (boil water, keep wood dry, smoke for mosquitoes) burns it, and the episode counts the rules on wooden signs. The woodpile under the stilt house is the visual clock: full → shrinking → covered → feeding the fire. The last rule turns: fire helps with almost everything, except a huge meat-eater that sniffs the smoke and walks on. The family is safe up on the veranda (K02); the animal never looks up and harms no one (user, 13 Nov 2026).

## Sound direction

Silence policy: from shot 20 the hens and then the crickets stop; only the fire is left under the predator's footfalls and sniffs (no roar). Ending **E-FIRE**: one low muted wooden note + a soft pad fading over 2 s with the fire crackle continuing. Rain A-RN, dusk A-DU with a rising mosquito whine.

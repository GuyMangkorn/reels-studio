## What the research settled

1. **C82 direction:** Stony Brook's enclosed-box demonstration supports the forward helium tendency. The archived physics explanation supports the role of surrounding air; UAF's firsthand car account reports forward motion under acceleration and rearward motion under braking. The hanging-object comparison and settled straight-speed reset are explicit physical deductions, not inspected motion-trace measurements.
2. **C83 mechanism and consequence:** NWS identifies the enclosed metal shell as protective and distinguishes it from the tire-insulation misconception. The same article documents possible electronics, glass and tire damage, including possible fire. A chosen damage detail is a possible consequence, not an inevitable sequence.
3. **Both premises can proceed:** omitted precision is not needed to tell either story. Exact transient motion, a specific vehicle's current map, and interior-contact injury mechanisms remain outside the evidence; do not invent them for spectacle.

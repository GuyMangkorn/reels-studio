## Hook-safe and presentation boundaries

- **Must-fix if introduced:** an unconditional survival guarantee, rubber stopping lightning, protection for every vehicle, or a person outside safely touching the struck shell. These would change the learning takeaway or create a safety risk.
- **Must-fix if introduced:** claiming the shell is an ideal Faraday cage, that no discharge can reach the interior/components, or that all lightning takes the illustrated route. Cut the absolute claim rather than adding a physics lecture.
- **Optional:** naming a Faraday cage at all. Plain exterior-route language is sufficient for ER05; any explanation stays inside ER09's approximately 10 s answer budget.

## Story core

- **Logline:** on a sunny Bangkok afternoon the Sun vanishes (hypothetically). For about eight minutes nobody notices: the light already on its way keeps arriving, and, per Einstein's theory, Earth keeps curving because the change in gravity also travels at light speed. Then the sky darkens, Earth goes straight, the Moon goes dark seconds later, and Jupiter keeps shining for an hour or more. Twist: that delay is happening to you right now.
- **Central question:** if the Sun vanished, when would we find out, and how?
- **Intention / obstacle:** the viewer wants to know when the catastrophe "arrives"; the obstacle is the speed of light itself, which makes every piece of news late.
- **Feeling:** a chill at the hook, dramatic-irony comedy at the stall under the countdown, awe at the 2017 measurement, a hard silence at the blackout, wonder at Jupiter still lit, and a small shiver at the twist: the warm sunlight on your skin is already eight minutes old.
- **Takeaway (knowledge):** light, and changes in gravity, take time to travel. Sunlight needs about 8 min 20 s to reach Earth, so what we see in the sky is always the past: the Sun 8 minutes ago, Jupiter's reflected light even later, every star older still.

## Truth and ending

- **Spoken as settled:** C01 sunlight ~8 min 20 s (L03, SRC001; the clock's 08:19 is the computed mean, a rounding difference only). C10 every star is a picture of the past (L13, no numbers).
- **Spoken as theory, hedged:** C03/C11 Earth keeps orbiting until the gravity change arrives, "ตามทฤษฎีของไอน์สไตน์" (L05); never "proven for the Sun". L09 "พุ่งไปตรง ๆ" = Earth leaves along the tangent at its orbital speed, not falling outward (picture must show the tangent, N3).
- **Spoken as an observation, corrected:** C05 GW170817 (L06–L07): waves and light from a neutron-star collision, ~130 million years of travel, arriving less than 2 s apart (gamma rays after the waves), therefore the **waves** travel at light speed almost exactly. L07 does not say the light came "together" and does not claim the 2017 event tested the Sun's pull; L05 → L07 reads as theory, then the best evidence for it.
- **Timings, hedged:** C06 sky "มืดลง" (L08); C07 Moon "ไม่กี่วินาที" (L10, a first-quarter Moon in the afternoon sky, ~1.3 s in the research table); C08 Jupiter "ราวชั่วโมงกว่า" after the sky (L11; research table 66–86 min).
- **Premise:** C04 hypothetical, said once (L02). The narration never implies the Sun could vanish.
- **Dramatization only:** the stall, VEN/CUS small talk, the time-compressed clock (8 minutes of story pass in about 22 s of video; drawn so it clearly runs fast), the vanishing pop, the dog bark, Jupiter's drawn size (stylised, not to scale).
- **Not used:** C09's optional ~170,000-years-inside-the-Sun fact (search excerpt only; needs a recheck before any use); exact Moon/Jupiter numbers.
- **Knowledge twist (reframe):** the hook frames the 8-minute delay as a future disaster. L13–L14 turn it on the viewer: the delay is not hypothetical, it is happening now; the sunlight on your skin left the Sun about 8 minutes ago. Fair: the rule is fully planted (L03 light needs time; L11–L12 Jupiter shines after its source is gone because its light is still in transit), so the twist is the same rule applied to the present, with no new fact.
- **Ending `B_loop` (pair):** L14 "…ออกจากดวงอาทิตย์มาตั้งแต่ 8 นาทีที่แล้ว" hands straight into L01 "ถ้าดวงอาทิตย์หายไปตอนนี้…": the light "from 8 minutes ago" → "if it vanished now… you would not know for 8 minutes". The last picture is the same sunny soi as the opening frame (back in the real present), so the seam returns to frame 0 without a jump. Final ~0.5 s speech-free for the blend window; no goodbye or call to action.

Nothing says the Sun's pull was tested. "แปลว่าคลื่นนี้เร็วเท่าแสงแทบเป๊ะ" is fair: the gap (<2 s over ~130 million years) bounds the speed difference to about 1 part in 10^15 (SRC007), i.e. "almost exactly". Keep. Do not add "พิสูจน์แล้ว" or "ยืนยันแรงโน้มถ่วงของดวงอาทิตย์". Limit: the step from waves to the Sun's changing pull is GR theory, which L05 already owns.

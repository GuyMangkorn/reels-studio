## Does a replacement beat the baseline?

Honestly, only a little on retention. The baseline already removes the drop and keeps a new problem roughly every 7 s. What a replacement adds is a **spoken rule** (A, B) that the ending reuses, or a laugh (C), at the cost of 4–10 s of runtime, new TTS and a new picture. B costs nearly as much as the cut saves, so I would not pick it. C is cheap but adds no knowledge.

## Recommendation

**Option A plus Add-on R** (B03 ≈ 6.2 + 7.2 = ~13.8 s instead of 19.3 s; video ~55 s). It is the only option where the character meets the problem on screen (the phone with no warning on it) before the narrator explains it, it uses zero new terms, and it gives the episode its ER03 refrain, "ไม่มีอะไรเร็วกว่าแสง", which L12 (Jupiter's light still on its way) and L14 (your sunlight is 8 minutes old) then cash in without a spoiler. R puts the gravity line into the same "เดินทางมา" words as L03, so the beat has one idea, said twice, instead of four terms.

Fallback if the user wants no new voice work: **baseline with L05 unchanged** (video ~47 s), which fixes the measured problem by itself.

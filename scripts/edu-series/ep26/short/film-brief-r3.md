## Visual intent

A comic day in a Roman laundry seen from beside its victim. Marcus is on screen in every shot of B01–B05: his face carries the laughs (pride, disgust, despair, bluff, relief). The story is told by contact with three objects: the **jar** (the smell arrives), the **tub** (his promised hands become feet) and the **coin** (he sniffs it). B06 switches to a second, warmer and grander room so the viewer feels "this part is real", then the last frame returns to Marcus, still smelling.

Light marks the chapters ([retention camera](../../../../docs/cinematography-principles.md#retention-camera)): morning apricot in the approved workshop (B01–B02), a cooler grey-green cast in the tread corner for the setback (B03), low gold evening sun through the doorway for the payoff (B04–B05), Pompeian red and lamp gold for the emperor (B06-SH01–SH04), dusk blue with a last orange slit for the final frame (B06-SH05).

## Sound direction

- **Music family.** Light comic pluck (pizzicato, marimba, soft woodblock), about 112 BPM estimated, medium-high energy in B01; stops dead on the lid in B02; returns in B03 as a plodding squelch-stomp groove at half time locked to the treads; warm hopeful pad and pluck in B04; near-silence in B05; mock-grand imperial brass (comic, not solemn) for B06-SH01–SH03; silence before "แต่มาร์คุสยังมี".
- **Ending plan (UT15, Film's choice).** The imperial brass motif's last note collapses into a muted-trombone "wah-wah" droop as the smell ribbons rise off Marcus, about 1.8 s, then the room tail. `NEEDS-GEN`. Reason: it reuses the B06 brass so the joke lands on the emperor's own music, and the droop is the smell. The EP02 pizz tag is not used.
- **Ambience per location.** Workshop: stone-room tone, slow water drip and slosh, distant street murmur through the doorway (morning busier, evening thinner). Tread corner: wet room tone, closer slosh. Imperial room: hushed large room, faint lamp flutter, no crowd.
- **SFX language.** Wet, squelchy and close for anything touching the liquid; dry and small for cloth, coin and ceramic. Comic stings are short and tonal (pluck, wobble, droop), never cartoon voices. All sounds are reconstruction sound; none claims to be a period recording (script sound note).
- **Silence policy.** The B02 smell gag plays in silence from the lid pop to "พี่…"; a short near-silence before "แม่ครับ…"; a half beat after "ใช้อะไรซักเนี่ย"; the coin sniff in near-silence; full drop before L20's second clause. Narration is never ducked under a sting; stings sit in the line gaps.
- Per-shot cues with descriptions are in the [storyboard](storyboard.md#shot-staging); shot-local frames follow at the timed pass. Every cue is `NEEDS-GEN` pending Ton's fit check unless a library ID is named there as a candidate.

### VD26-11 — The smell cue (CL04), answering the Film question

**Decision.** Three painted wavy ribbons, pale olive-yellow (about `#CFC67E` at 55–65 % opacity, soft dry-brush edge), each 0.03–0.05 m wide in world terms, rising and drifting along a curve. Comic convention, no text, no icon.

- **B02-SH02 (plant, without words).** Lid lifts; the three ribbons rise from the jar mouth, bend across the bench and reach Marcus's nose in one unbroken path before he speaks. The tip of the lead ribbon "taps" the nose; his face flips `happy` → `disgusted` on contact. Music is silent here, so the picture alone carries it.
- **B03.** Faint ribbons (opacity about 30 %) rise from the tub. In B03-SH03 the ribbons on the shirt thin and vanish as it is rinsed, while two thin ribbons stay on Marcus's legs and tunic hem: the plant that the smell soaked into him (L11).
- **B04–B05.** The shirt carries none. One very faint ribbon behind Marcus in B04-SH05 (the customer does not notice); none on the coin in B05.
- **Final frame B06-SH05 (payoff).** The same three ribbons, same colour and wave, rise from Marcus's shoulders and tunic as he stands in the doorway; one curls back past his own nose and he doesn't notice. This is the last image under the hold.
- **Rule.** The ribbons never touch Vespasian, Titus or their coin (the money has no smell). They are a screen-facing layer in the projected set (positioned by world anchors, drawn after the figure they leave), so they scale with distance.

**Acceptance.** At 270 px the ribbons read as smell, not steam or wind; the jar-to-nose path is unbroken; the colour differs from the liquid and the walls; the final-frame ribbons match B02's in colour and wave.

## Visual intent

Keep the audience at the apprentice's working eye height. The apprentice is present through the hands and sleeves. The customer and coworker look toward that eye line. A single visible seam near the hem gives the viewer a fixed place to look on the same ivory garment. Give the customer's fingertip and the oil mark a readable opening silhouette from frame one, with **เรื่องสมมุติ** in a separate screen-space position. Use three distinct actions during L01 in narration order. The customer presses the cloth into the viewer's grip, the viewer scrubs and lifts it to find the mark still there, and the coworker's hand brings the jar into the vacated foreground. The change in ownership and the failed lift carry the opening before the jar appears.

The fictional shop is matte chalk plaster, dark stone at the basins and warm terracotta. Let soft daylight enter through the door on the same side each time we see the cloth. A subdued cool bounce above the basins keeps fingers readable. Use wash texture for the room, solid silhouettes for the hands and garment, and one thin seam line that survives a phone-size preview. The drying ellipsis changes the cloth and time, with the room and light direction as anchors. At pickup, show the customer turn the dry seam into the doorway light and check the spot before the smile. Keep the stain unreadable on the wet shirt and while the apprentice approaches the doorway.

F001 plays over the master's offered jar and the apprentice's hesitation. The shot stays with the characters and the unfinished job. A plain terracotta jar and basins are reconstruction choices for the unnamed shop. The wash, rinse and dry precede the fictional payoff. Use the research packet's staging bounds for props and period cues. [Research and staging boundaries](../research.md#story-and-staging-boundaries)

## Sound direction

Keep a sparse muted pluck and quiet wood pulse under the room tone. Begin with cloth settling, water slapping the stone basin and one hollow terracotta clink when the jar is placed within reach. Let the pulse stop on the recoil, leave room tone under L04 and resume quietly on the decision. Use separate damp-cloth friction, broad rinse water and a light dry hem flap to distinguish the work states. Maintain one small plaster room around all effects. A single footstep at the doorway interrupts the apprentice, then leave a short pause through the customer's fingertip check. Close with dry cloth folding and a soft single plucked note under the smile, with the room tail carrying the final hold.

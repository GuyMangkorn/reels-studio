# EDU-EP26-SHORT — Hook options

- Revision `EDU-EP26-HOOKS-R1`, Preaw, 2026-11-18. Target 50–60 s, estimated.
- Inputs [C95 R17](../../concepts/C95.md), [delivery record](../delivery.md), [EDU-EP26-RESEARCH-R1](../research.md) and the [hook catalogue](../../../../docs/research/2026-11-16-shorts-hook-types.md#2-hook-catalogue).
- Shared premise. You are the new worker in an unnamed Roman laundry. You take the same garment from the customer's hand at intake to the customer's hand at pickup. F001 explains why the jar belongs in the shop. The result on this garment belongs to the invented shift. Its clean patch is revealed after the wash, rinse and drying ellipsis.

## A — C95 pitch opening (Recommended)

- Hook ID `new: workshop choice`.
- Spoken opening **“ถ้าคุณต้องซักเสื้อขาวให้สะอาด แต่คนในร้านยื่นไหฉี่ให้ล่ะ?”**
- Beat shape. Take the stained shirt on your first shift → try water and reach for the coworker's jar → learn why Roman laundry workers used it, then finish the wash → watch the customer touch the spot you worked on.
- Channel default. ER01 opens on a choice question. ER02 pays off why the jar is in the laundry by about 30%, with the customer's verdict at the end.
- Recommended because the shirt and jar make the contradiction visible immediately, and the next beat can put the decision in the viewer's hands.

## B — The customer's deadline

- Hook ID `HK04` (rehook chain).
- Spoken opening **“สมมุติว่าวันนี้คุณเริ่มงานร้านซักผ้าในสมัยโรมัน ลูกค้าชี้คราบมันบนเสื้อขาวแล้วบอกว่า ‘เดี๋ยวกลับมาเอานะ’”**
- Beat shape. Promise the pickup while the oily spot is in view → see the stain survive your first wash with water → ask what is in the jar the coworker hands you and hear F001 → check the spot again when the customer returns.
- Channel defaults. ER01 leads with a job and a deadline. ER02 delays the jar reveal beyond the initial setup and saves the answer to the opening question, whether the job comes out clean, for pickup. Each intermediate beat visibly advances the job.

## C — The jar in your hands

- Hook ID `HK07` (flash-forward).
- Spoken opening **“ลองนึกภาพว่าคุณถือไหฉี่อยู่เหนืออ่างซักผ้า แล้วได้ยินคนข้าง ๆ บอกว่า ‘ใช่ ใช้ไหนี้แหละ’”**
- Beat shape. Hold the jar over the basin → rewind to the moment the customer hands you the stained shirt → return to the same hesitation with F001 in context → finish the wash, then find out what the customer sees.
- Channel defaults. ER01 answers why the jar is there after the flash-forward and reset. ER02 aims to answer that question near 30% while keeping the wash result for the handover.

## Story core and ending

On your first shift at an unnamed Roman laundry, you take a white shirt with an oily spot. The customer is coming back for it. The spot survives your first attempt, and the coworker puts a jar in your reach. Its smell makes you hesitate. You decide to finish the work and let the customer inspect the same spot. The story lets the viewer feel the pressure of a first job, the surprise of the offer and the relief of having the work accepted.

The workshop, customer, garment and result are fictional. In this shift the spot is gone after the wash, rinse and drying ellipsis. F001 gives the one historical takeaway in the moment of choice. Roman-era cloth workers used urine as one aid in treating cloth. The treatment of this particular stain and the contents or supply of any excavated workshop remain outside the historical claim.

Does a viewer read it as a general claim that anyone who plays always ends above 100 (which CL-F05 supports only as "มัก")? 4. **Q4 — L12 "คุณดูแค่ตาตรงหน้า".** Is this an acceptable term-free statement of CL-F04 ("each bid compares only this step's loss")?
5. **Q5 — ER11 ratio.** The arithmetic lines are true but told as story; please confirm the fact share is acceptable for the story-led mix.

## O1 — HK02 variant: you are the bidder (recommended)

- **Hook ID:** HK02 "What if you…" (variant: the change is a seat in the game, not an impossible transformation).
- **Opening lines (Thai):**
  - L01: "ถ้าคุณได้ประมูลแบงก์ร้อยใบนี้ เริ่มที่ห้าบาท… คุณเสนอมั้ย?"
  - L02: "เสนอสิ… แต่สุดท้าย คุณจะจ่ายเกินร้อย ทั้งที่ทุกก้าว คุ้มหมด"
- **Beat shape:** 0–3 s the offer and the question to "คุณ" (character = you raises a hand at 5) → 3–7 s the broken certainty "you will pay over 100" plus the refrain → B02 second-place rule felt by you at 90 vs 95 → B03 you go past 100 → B04 fact (1971, "มัก") → B05 flip: the host holds the coin pile; ~2 s hold.
- **Bends:** HK02 normally needs an impossible change; here it is only a seat, so the counter-intuitive load sits on L02 (ER01 still met by ~5 s). Changes C90's cast: one bidder becomes "คุณ" and the narrator is no longer the host "ผม" (Film re-casts: you vs one rival + host). No ER02/ER03 conflict.
- **Why recommended:** the story is a chain of choices, and putting the viewer in the bidder's seat makes every "แต่…" their own decision, so the refrain "ทุกก้าวคุ้ม" and the final flip land on the viewer, not on a stranger.

## O2 — HK01 broken certainty (channel default, the pitch line)

  - L02: "ฟังดูบ้าใช่มั้ย? แต่ทุกก้าวที่เขาเสนอ… คุ้มนะ"
- **Beat shape:** 0–5 s claim over the note and bid board → 5–7 s the sharp objection ("who would?") answered by the refrain → B02–B05 as in C90 (two invented bidders, host narrates as "ผม").
- **Bends:** none; this is ER01. Risk: the viewer watches two strangers instead of deciding, so the middle (B02–B03 arithmetic) leans on following numbers rather than feeling them.

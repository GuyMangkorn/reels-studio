## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Everyone "knows" nobody pays over 100 for a 100-baht note. The viewer is put in the bidder's seat: frame 0 is the host offering the red note **toward the lens** (toward "คุณ"), so the opening line lands on the viewer; the reverse shot then shows "you" raising a hand. Feeling arc (script story core): greed and fun (B01, warm, bouncy) → alarm at the second rule (B02, cooler, tighter) → trapped by your own good sums (B03, cold, tightening two-shot and push-ins) → a step outside the story (B04, the room pauses in sepia) → the cold reveal (B05: the warm light now belongs to the host only).
- **Story first (designed before the kit was opened).**
  - **YOU** (the viewer's stand-in, addressed as "คุณ"): a **young man** of about 24 (user Q2, R2), the new guy at a lunchtime office game, eager, quick to raise a hand, readable faces (happy → surprised gasp → worried freeze → a short groan → tired). Slim build, messy spiky black hair, a warm rust jacket over a cream tee so the eye finds "you" first in every wide.
  - **RIVAL** (one other bidder, "เขา"): a calm, broad-shouldered man in his late thirties with a buzz cut and a full beard, in a cool grey-blue short-sleeve work shirt: the visual opposite of YOU in silhouette (round buzz head and broad shoulders vs spiky hair and slim shoulders, readable even from behind in the wides), face (beard vs clean-shaven), age and colour (cool vs warm); he is not a villain and never sneers. He bids calmly (smug-ish at first), tightens, and at B05 gives up with a long sigh.
  - **HOST** (holds the note, never narrates, never bids): a smooth man in his forties in a navy blazer and dark red tie; the tie's red rhymes with the red note (he and the note belong together). He offers the note at L01, flicks the rule card's flap at L05, and otherwise stands **arms folded** with a small grin that grows wide by wide (CL03). Never cheating, never sinister (script: no host-as-cheat, no stupid bidders).
  - **Place:** a small office meeting room after lunch turned into a game: back wall with a whiteboard-style **bid board** with flip-number windows, a bar-height side table at the host's side with the rule tent card and a small wooden tray, two office chairs facing the board, a window on the left wall letting in warm afternoon sun. Carpet, cream walls, one pot plant; no logos, no real company.
  - **The note:** a clearly stylised red bill with a large "100", a cream oval with a simple wave pattern, no portrait, no real security design (C90 W0.2 note stands; fact-check confirms).
- **Medium (VD01).** Painted 2D p5.brush through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`, `cam-moves.ts`) in world metres; kit four-head cards; new set SET-ROOM24; board digits, the rule card text and the "รวม" tally as **camera-mapped DOM text** on the board/card quads (QuadText homography, EDU-EP16 logbook precedent) so Thai and digits stay crisp while the camera moves; B04 inserts (the "1971" stamp, the "ทะลุ" arrow) as crisp Remotion SVG over the paused, sepia-graded painted room.

- **Light (retention camera: light marks the chapters).** B01 warm: window sun from −x across the floor and the bidders, soft overhead fill. B02–B03 cool: the sun goes behind a cloud on "แต่" (L05), overhead fluorescent dominates, faces flatter and cooler; YOU's rust jacket stays the only warm accent. B04 sepia pause. B05: the sun returns but as a low warm patch landing **on the host and the tray only** (the bidders stay in the cool), so the warmth of the payoff belongs to him: the light carries the ER03 flip.
- **Camera language.** Every shot moves inside the set (eased yaw arc 6–10° and a push of 5–15 % of distance). Families: (1) **the wide from behind the chairs** (yaw ≈ 0): both bidders' backs low in frame, the board and the host centred; it is where CL02 (tally) and CL03 (host's folded arms) are seen without being pointed at, and the ER03 hold frame lives here; (2) **reverse from the board side** (yaw ≈ 180): both bidders' faces, for reactions; (3) **singles and the YOU-over-RIVAL two-shot** that tighten as bids climb (B03 push-ins); (4) **inserts**: the rule card (B02), the board flip windows (B01 riffle, B03 flips). One action axis: bidders sit side by side facing −z, YOU screen-left and RIVAL screen-right in families 1 and 3; family 2 mirrors them by design (reverse across the room, never mid-scene axis jumps between 1 and 3). Large moves only for the hook (frame 0 push toward the note), the B04 pause (a slow dolly through the frozen room) and the L18 reveal (pull back to the wide hold).
- **Conceal and reveal.**
  - **CL01 (rule card):** on the host's side table from the first wide; line 1 "ใครเสนอสูงสุด ได้แบงก์" readable in the inserts, line 2 hidden under a folded flap; on "แต่ กติกามีอีกข้อ…" (L05) the host's finger flicks the flap down and line 2 "ที่สองก็ต้องจ่าย" appears. Not readable earlier in any shot.
  - **CL02 ("รวม" tally):** a small gold strip under the two bid columns, always the sum of the two current bids; visible in every wide, never framed alone, never lit, never mentioned until L18, when it lights gold with the ka-ching.
  - **CL03 (host never bids):** host arms folded (P03) in every wide from L03 on; his grin grows across the wides (est smile width +10 % per wide; Boss confirms how, see N10). His only hand actions are the L01 offer, the L05 flap flick and the L18 lift of the note; none is above shoulder height, so nothing reads as a bid.
  - **R01 refrain "ทุกก้าวคุ้ม":** each spoken "คุ้ม" lands on YOU's small satisfied nod (L02 end, L08, L12); at L19 the same nod beat is answered by the host's grin in the warm patch (the flip in picture).
- **Truth limits on picture (binding on every shot).**
  1. The auction is visibly a hypothetical story (a staged room, painted cast); B04 shows **no real person, no real photo, no real classroom, no dollar figure and no amount reached in real games** (script Film notes; FC24-12).
  2. The "1971" stamp is a typographic card on the paused story room, never a document, newspaper, photo or archive look; the gavel tap is a stylised sound on the stamp, not a "recording" (FC24-12).
  3. The "ทะลุ" arrow in L15 carries **no number and no axis values**: one dashed line labelled "ค่าแบงก์" and an arrow rising through it; it is an illustration of "often passes the note's value", not data.
  4. The board shows only the story's baht numbers (BS ledger below) and only in B01–B03 and B05; it never appears with numbers during L15.
  5. Nobody pays before the auction ends (CL-F02): no money changes hands until B05 L16–L18; the tray is empty until then.
  6. Host is never shown cheating (no hidden hand, no wink at the camera); bidders never look stupid (no facepalm at the board, no cartoon sweat). Each bid is a calm, reasoned raise.
  7. The note prop never reproduces the real 100-baht design (no portrait, no Garuda, no security thread drawing).

## Projection contract and scale baseline

- **Space M (SET-ROOM24), metres, estimated.** Origin on the floor at the room centre line, under the midpoint between the two chairs' front edge and the back wall: +x to screen right at camera yaw 0, +y up, +z toward the main camera (away from the board). Camera yaw as `camMove` azimuth: 0 = camera on +z looking −z (behind the chairs toward the board), 180 = camera at the board side looking +z (bidders' faces). Perspective pinhole (kit `YCam`), picture 1080×1920, `f` 900–1300, `hy` 600–760.
- **One projection owner:** every painted element projects through the shot's single `YCam`. The board digits, the "รวม" strip and the rule-card text are DOM text placed by the same camera: Boss projects each quad's four world corners through that shot's `YCam` per frame and feeds the QuadText homography, so text and paint cannot drift. Captions, the mark and the B04 SVG inserts are picture-space layers with no world position.
- **Occlusion rule for DOM text (VD03).** DOM text draws above the painted canvas, so **no painted element may cross a text quad**: in every shot the host, the bidders' heads and raised hands, and the side-table props stay clear of the board windows and the rule-card face (est margin ≥ 24 px). Where a raised hand would cross the board (B01 bids from the wide), the shot is framed so the hand stays below the "รวม" strip or the bid is shown from the reverse. If a shot needs an occluder in front of text, that shot's text is painted in-canvas instead (Boss decision per shot, recorded).

## Findings

All `optional`. Preaw may decline any of them without a reply.

- **F-1 (optional; L02, user-picked hook line).** "คุณจะจ่ายเกินร้อย" is a flat future claim. Inside "ถ้าคุณ…" it is true for this story (110), but it holds only because the rival keeps raising, and a viewer may carry it out as "anyone who plays pays over the note's value", while L15 correctly says only "มัก". It does not make the hook false (the hook is the story's outcome) and does not meet a must-fix case, so **no change is required**. If Preaw wants the narrowest fix, "คุณ**อาจ**จ่ายเกินร้อย" in L02 (one word); this changes the user-picked opening and **needs the user's OK**, and it softens the hook, so my recommendation is to leave L02 as is and let L15 carry the real-world hedge.
- **F-2 (optional; L14).** "ตั้งแต่ปี 1971" can be heard as "since 1971" (invented then, in use since). The true statement is "first published in 1971". Preferred wording: "นักเศรษฐศาสตร์คิดไว้ ปี 1971" (drop "ตั้งแต่"), or leave; the direction is true.
- **F-3 (optional note; L08).** "คุณเสนอร้อย คุ้ม": bidding 100 only breaks even if you win, and if you lose you pay 100, more than the 90 you faced. The line is saved by L07's "แล้วชนะ" and by L12 redefining the refrain. No change needed; do not add more explanation (ER09).
- **F-4 (optional; L16).** Story consistency, not a fact error. By the script's own logic the rival at 105 (facing a 110 bid) should also raise (115: lose 10 if he wins vs lose 105 if he stops). A viewer who followed the sums may ask why he stops, and may leave thinking the chain ends near 110. The refrain "ทุกก้าวคุ้ม" is about each step, so the end of the chain needs a reason. A word fix inside the line would do: "เขายอมแพ้ที่ร้อยห้า" instead of "เขาแพ้ร้อยห้า" (he chose to quit). No timing change.
- **F-5 (optional note; ER11 ratio).** See Q5.

## Answers to Q1–Q5

1. **Q1 (L02):** read in context, a viewer may take it as general, but it is an in-story outcome, so it is not a hook-false or wrong-takeaway case. Optional only. Narrowest fix if wanted: "อาจ" before "จ่ายเกินร้อย" (needs the user's OK, F-1). I recommend leaving it.
2. **Q2 (L15 "ค่าแบงก์"):** accurate. The original prize is a $1 bill and S02 says escalation past the prize "usually happens" in party play; versions with a $20 bill also exceed the prize (secondary class report, $54 for $20; not narrated). "ค่าแบงก์" = the note's face value, which is the prize's value in every version. "มัก" stays; no figure.
3. **Q3 (L14):** "คิดไว้ … ปี 1971" is safe. "ตั้งแต่ปี" is a small ambiguity (F-2, optional). "เขียนถึงเกมนี้ปี 1971" is more literal but longer and no better for a viewer; not needed.
4. **Q4 (L12):** acceptable and term-free. It states CL-F04 correctly (each bid is judged by this step's loss). "ตา" as "turn" is clear in context.
5. **Q5 (ER11 ratio):** acceptable. Hard fact lines are L03, L05 and L13–L15 (about 12 s of ~58 s speech, ~21%); the arithmetic is true and told as story. It is under the stated ~30% and no story line claims a real event. The ER11 range is a Default, so no action; Preaw/Editor can record the actual ratio in channel performance after the read.

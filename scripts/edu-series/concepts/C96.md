## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Workplace mystery in a fictional Inka storehouse.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 3D camera with close-ups of two parallel sets of hanging cords.
- **Logline** — สมมุติว่าคุณเฝ้าโกดังข้าวโพดของอินคา อีกเดี๋ยวจะต้องส่งของให้คนที่รออยู่หน้าประตู แต่ยอดที่คุณนับได้กับยอดของคนกะก่อนดันไม่ตรงกัน
- **Opening line** — “ถ้าข้าวโพดในโกดังหายไปหนึ่งกระสอบ แล้วหลักฐานอย่างเดียวคือปมเชือกล่ะ?”
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — A cord can carry a numerical inventory well enough to challenge a hurried count.

## Beats

1. **B01** — Your hand stops on the last sack. The group at the door is waiting for its allotment, and the outgoing count is short by one.
2. **B02** — You can close the doors to recount every sack or compare the hanging cords left by the last keeper. You pick the cords, but two rows look the same at first.
3. **B03** — You trace each cord from its attachment and compare the warehouse rows. In-story fact line explains how knot position can carry a number. The one unmatched count points you to a stack you already checked from the front.
4. **B04** — You go around the stack to find one sack set behind it. You lift it onto the outgoing pile and open the door yourself.

- **Ending** — Closed clue payoff. Hold on the final sack aligned with the matching cord.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore with quiet urgency and a small laugh of relief.
- **Retention and share hypothesis** — The viewer can inspect the same clue and guess which stack to check. The tactile inventory invites someone else to try.

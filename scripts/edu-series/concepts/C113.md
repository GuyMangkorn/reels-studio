## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Heist-movie parody that collapses. Pittsburgh, 1995: a man robs two banks in daylight with no mask, sure he cannot be seen on camera because he rubbed lemon juice on his face.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 3D camera for the "heist movie" in his head, cut against grainy 1990s CCTV frames and a local TV news bumper (the reality).
- **Logline** — ผู้ชายคนหนึ่งเชื่อสุดใจว่าน้ำมะนาวทำให้กล้องมองไม่เห็นหน้า เลยเดินเข้าธนาคารหน้าเปล่า ยิ้มให้กล้อง แล้วได้ออกข่าวสองทุ่มคืนนั้นเลย
- **Opening line** — “ผู้ชายคนนี้ปล้นธนาคารโดยไม่ใส่หน้ากาก… เพราะเขาทาน้ำมะนาวไว้ที่หน้าแล้ว”
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — The robber was not careless: he was confident, and he had even "tested" it.

## Beats

1. **B01** — The logic: lemon juice is invisible ink on paper, so on a face it must make you invisible to cameras (lead: the reasoning sources report). Deadpan: “ตรรกะแน่นมาก ผิดแค่ทุกขั้น”.
2. **B02** — The test: he takes a photo of himself and his face is not in it (lead: reported Polaroid test; the camera likely pointed the wrong way). Irony: the viewer sees the camera aimed at the ceiling.
3. **B03** — Bank one, bank two: no mask, no hat, and a look straight into the CCTV (lead). Today comparison: “คนทั่วไปเปิดกล้องหน้ายังจัดมุมก่อน คนนี้ให้กล้องธนาคารถ่ายแบบเต็มหน้าเลย”.
4. **B04** — The footage runs on the evening news; he is arrested within hours (lead). Rule of three: invisible to the camera, invisible to the bank, invisible to the TV… all three fail.
5. **B05** — Police show him the tape. His reported line: “But I wore the juice” (lead; Thai subtitle “แต่ผมทาน้ำมะนาวแล้วนะ”).

- **Ending** — Callback and irony: two psychologists read about him and ran the study that named the Dunning–Kruger effect (lead). “เขาอยากล่องหน… สุดท้ายกลายเป็นโจรที่คนทั้งโลกเห็นหน้ามากที่สุด อยู่ในตำราจิตวิทยาไปตลอดกาล”.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore as a friend who cannot believe the story she is telling, half pity, half awe; no character voice for him beyond the documented quote, read flat by a male voice.
- **Why it holds** — HK01 states a plan everyone knows is wrong but cannot guess the reason for. The test scene makes the viewer shout at the screen. Share trigger: “ส่งให้เพื่อนที่มั่นใจเกินเหตุ”.
- **Research load for Boat** — Named real case (McArthur Wheeler, Pittsburgh, 1995). Leads: date and the two banks; the lemon-juice belief and where he got it; the Polaroid test and why the photo was blank; how fast the news aired and the arrest followed; the exact wording and source of “But I wore the juice”; whether a weapon was involved and whether anyone was hurt; his sentence; the 1999 Kruger and Dunning paper and whether it cites this case; whether he is alive (sets the care level).
- **Sound intent (proposed)** — Slick heist-movie bass groove that drops to a tape hiss and a TV-news sting at B04; silence before the quote in B05; the groove returns as a sad kazoo-like version at the ending.

## ER08 pitch check — 6/6

1. **Hook** — Breaks "robbers hide their faces"; a man robs barefaced on purpose. Clear in about 3 s.
2. **Victim** — The robber; wants to be invisible, and every step makes him more visible.

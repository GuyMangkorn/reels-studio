## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — True-story comedy. 5 May 1961, the first American space flight (Freedom 7). Alan Shepard (died 1998) is strapped into a tiny capsule for a flight planned to last about 15 minutes, so nobody planned a toilet. Then the launch keeps getting delayed.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 2D on the project defaults, with a 1961 TV-broadcast insert (grainy black-and-white, countdown clock) for the control room.
- **Logline** — คนอเมริกันคนแรกที่จะได้ไปอวกาศ อยากได้แค่สองอย่าง: ขึ้นฟ้า กับเข้าห้องน้ำ และ NASA ให้ได้แค่อย่างเดียว
- **Opening line** — “เที่ยวบินนี้ยาวแค่ 15 นาที… แต่นักบินต้องนั่งรอบนจรวดเกือบ 4 ชั่วโมง แล้วเขาก็ปวดฉี่”
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — The first American astronaut went to space in a suit he had already wet, with mission control's permission.

## Beats

1. **B01** — Shepard climbs in, proud. Nobody packed a toilet: "บินแค่ 15 นาที จะฉี่ทำไม" (deadpan aside).
2. **B02** — But the countdown holds. Then holds again. Hours pass with him lying on his back on top of a rocket (rule of three: weather, a part, a computer).
3. **B03** — He radios: "ผมขอออกไปเข้าห้องน้ำได้ไหม". Control: no, getting him out takes too long (character lines).
4. **B04** — The answer from the ground: go in the suit. Worry: the wet suit could short the body sensors, so they switch the sensors off. The cotton undergarment soaks it up; the suit's oxygen flow dries it on the way up (wow fact as punchline).
5. **B05** — Launch. Fifteen minutes, history made, hero on the front page. Callback ending below.

- **Ending** — Callback and irony: “สิบปีต่อมา เขาได้ไปเดินบนดวงจันทร์… คราวนี้ชุดมีที่ฉี่มาให้แล้ว” (pending Boat on the Apollo 14 suit's urine device; if not supportable, end on the front-page hero shot with the suit drying in the corner).
- **Voice and tone** — Kore gossiping and barely holding a laugh; Shepard's lines tight and polite, control flat and bureaucratic.
- **Why it holds** — HK08 puts two numbers against each other in 3 s (15 min vs 4 h) and every viewer knows the feeling of needing a toilet you cannot reach. Escalation is time itself. Share trigger: "นักบินอวกาศคนแรกของอเมริกาฉี่ราดชุด" is a line people repeat.
- **Research load for Boat** — Named real case (historical, person deceased). Check: planned flight length, time on the pad before launch, the exchange asking to leave the capsule, the permission, the sensor power-off, how the suit dried, the Apollo 14 suit's urine collection. Character lines are played as jokes and labelled `แต่งเติมจากเรื่องจริง`.

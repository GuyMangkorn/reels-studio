## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre:** myth-buster survival ("who is actually dangerous").
- **Our cast:** a high-school girl (16) who knows dinosaurs only from movies, dropped with her street into the Late Cretaceous. She plans her survival around movie monsters, and each plan is wrong.
- **Logline:** Everything movies taught you to fear is the wrong size, in the wrong place, or the wrong animal. Each episode replaces one movie fear with the real one.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** the movie raptor is knee-high, the tallest hunter on the plain is a flying animal walking on foot, and a furry mammal like you was eating baby dinosaurs.
- **Structure: 1 (rules opening).** Why: "ห้ามกลัวตัวนี้" is a rule that fights a fear the viewer already holds, so the hook breaks a certainty in its first words; each episode swaps one fear for another.

### EP1 — ห้ามวิ่งหนีแรปเตอร์
- **Single question / answer:** Why is the movie raptor not the one to run from? The real Velociraptor was about turkey-sized and feathered.
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าคุณหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์ กฎข้อแรก… ไม่ต้องวิ่งหนีแรปเตอร์ เพราะตัวจริงสูงแค่เข่าคุณ"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: she hides behind a bin, hearing claws on the road, ready for a man-sized hunter.
  2. "แต่… ที่โผล่มา ตัวเท่าไก่งวง แถมมีขน" — it sniffs her shoe.
  3. Answer (one cause): the movie made it bigger than the real animal. `unchecked` (Boat: size, feathers; say only what is solid, ER10: no "according to…" in narration)
  4. "แต่ ตัวเล็กไม่ได้แปลว่าน่ารัก" — it snaps at her shoelace and runs off with it. `unchecked` tone check
  5. Payoff: "แรปเตอร์ตัวจริง… ขโมยได้แค่เชือกรองเท้าคุณ"
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold on the shoelace disappearing into ferns; `_A_fade`.

### EP2 — ห้ามคิดว่าตัวที่บินได้ อยู่บนฟ้า
- **Single question / answer:** What is the tallest hunter on the plain? A giant pterosaur, about giraffe-height, that hunted small animals on foot (Quetzalcoatlus as the candidate).
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าคุณหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์ ตัวที่สูงที่สุดในทุ่ง ไม่ใช่ไดโนเสาร์… แต่มันบินได้"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: she climbs onto a car roof to look over the grass-less plain; a head the height of a second-floor window rises behind her.
  2. Question: a dinosaur? "แต่ มันพับปีกเดิน ใช้สี่ขา" — it is a flier, walking.
  3. Answer (one cause): it was so big it hunted small prey on the ground like a stork. `unchecked` (Boat: height, ground-stalking idea; ER07 if contested)
  4. "แต่ มันไม่ใช่ไดโนเสาร์ด้วยซ้ำ" (pterosaurs are a separate group). `unchecked`; cut if it needs a long explanation (ER09).
  5. Payoff: "ตัวที่บินได้ตัวใหญ่ที่สุด… ชอบเดินมากกว่าบิน"
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold as it walks past the car; `_A_fade`.

### EP3 — ห้ามคิดว่าตัวขนปุย คือเหยื่อ
- **Single question / answer:** Did mammals only hide from dinosaurs? A badger-sized mammal ate baby dinosaurs; a fossil has the baby's bones in its belly (Repenomamus and a young Psittacosaurus, named-find candidate).
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าคุณหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์ ระวังตัวขนปุยที่ดูเหมือนเหยื่อ… เพราะบางตัว กินไดโนเสาร์"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: a furry animal like a badger slips past her ankle; she feels sorry for it ("ตัวนิดเดียว").
  2. "แต่ มันไม่ได้หนี มันกำลังล่า" — it heads for a nest of baby dinosaurs.
  3. Answer (one cause): a fossil of this mammal was found with a baby dinosaur's bones inside its belly. `unchecked` (period/region: Early Cretaceous, China; Boat confirms and sets the girl's period to match, or the series moves her to that time)
  4. Payoff flip: "สัตว์เลี้ยงลูกด้วยนม… แบบเดียวกับคุณ ไม่ได้มีแต่วิ่งหนีไดโนเสาร์ บางตัวกินมัน"
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold; `_A_fade`. Series ending: the three fears swapped, the last one points at "you".

- **Voice / tone:** monster-trailer energy that deflates into a grin: a dramatic, excitable narrator who builds each threat and then cheerfully undercuts it (Gemini **Fenrir**, male); the girl's small sounds ("ฮะ?", a squeak, a relieved laugh). One read per episode.
- **Why it holds:** the viewer's own fear (from movies) is the thing being corrected, a soft-landing myth-buster; each payoff is a comic image (shoelace thief, a flier that walks, a fluffy predator); share trigger: tagging the friend who loves dinosaur movies.
- **Research load:** general knowledge (Velociraptor size and feathers, giant pterosaur size and feeding) plus one named fossil find (Repenomamus with Psittacosaurus remains); film premise check. Period consistency: EP1–EP2 are Late Cretaceous animals, EP3 is Early Cretaceous; Boat flags whether the series needs two drop dates.
- **Sound intent:** EP1 horror-movie riser and claw clicks, cut to silence, then a turkey-like chirp; comic pizzicato for the shoelace theft. EP2 a deep air-shift whoosh when the head rises, giant slow footsteps (four, uneven), cello drone, a single wing-fold leather flap as the visual clue. EP3 soft cute bed (music box) that turns minor as it reaches the nest, a tiny growl, silence on the fossil line, low pluck tag.

## ER08 pitch check

1. **Hook:** breaks a certainty taught by movies ("raptors are huge", "fliers stay in the sky", "mammals were prey"); clear in ~3 s; no term in the hook (the species names come after the picture). Pass.
2. **Who:** everyone who has seen a dinosaur movie, which is nearly everyone. Pass.
3. **Beats:** each episode is a fear → "แต่…" → real animal chain, one cause each. Risk: EP2 beat 4 ("not a dinosaur") is a classification beat; keep it only if it plays as a surprise in under 5 s, otherwise cut (ER09). Pass with risk.
4. **Ending:** flips, not tips. Pass.

**Score: 4/4** (risks: EP2 classification beat; period consistency across episodes).

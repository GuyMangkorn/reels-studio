## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre:** survival "what if", rules list.
- **Our cast:** a Thai food-delivery rider (male, 20s) whose whole soi, motorbike included, wakes up in a fern-and-conifer forest. He is hungry and thirsty, and everything looks wild and clean.
- **Logline:** Untouched nature looks like a free buffet and a clean spring. Each episode gives one rule that sounds wrong (don't eat the greens, don't drink the clearest water) and shows why it keeps you alive.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** a world with no people is not clean or safe to eat from; its plants and water are older and better armed than you think.
- **Structure: 1 (rules opening).** Why: a survival rule is a promise the viewer wants broken and explained, and "ห้าม…" lands in one breath; each episode is one rule, so the series title "กฎข้อ 1 / กฎข้อ 2" carries the link.

### EP1 — กฎข้อ 1: ห้ามเก็บผักแถวนั้นกิน
- **Single question / answer:** Why can't you eat the plants in a dinosaur forest? The palm-like trees and ferns guard themselves with poison (one cause: the plant makes its own poison).
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าซอยคุณหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์ กฎข้อแรก… ห้ามเก็บผักแถวนั้นกิน ต่อให้ทั้งป่าเขียวไปหมด"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook over the rider's stomach growling in an all-green forest.
  2. He reaches for big seeds on a palm-like tree. "แต่… ต้นที่หน้าตาเหมือนมะพร้าวนี่ คือปรง" (cycad); its seeds carry poison. `unchecked`
  3. He turns to a fern that looks like ผักกูด. "แต่ เฟินบางชนิด ก็มีพิษ" — no grass field, no rice, no fruit he knows. `unchecked` (Boat: which Mesozoic food plants were toxic, and how close to modern ones; ER07 risk if the answer is "unclear")
  4. Payoff: a big plant-eater chews the same cycad. One-line flip to be chosen from what Boat supports, e.g. "ป่านี้ไม่ได้ปลูกไว้ให้คุณกิน… มันปลูกไว้สู้กับตัวที่กินมัน". `unchecked`
- **Ending:** closed on the flip, ~2 s hold; `_A_fade`.

### EP2 — กฎข้อ 2: ห้ามดื่มน้ำ ต่อให้มันใสที่สุดที่คุณเคยเห็น
- **Single question / answer:** Why is clear stream water dangerous in a world with no people? Germs did not arrive with humans; they are older than the dinosaurs.
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าซอยคุณหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์ กฎข้อสอง… ห้ามดื่มน้ำ ต่อให้มันใสที่สุดที่คุณเคยเห็น"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: a crystal stream, no factory, no plastic, no people. The rider cups his hands.
  2. "แต่ ไม่มีคน ก็ไม่ได้แปลว่าไม่มีเชื้อโรค" — a painted zoom into the drop: tiny living things swimming.
  3. Answer (one cause): germs lived on Earth long before any dinosaur, for billions of years. `unchecked`
  4. "แต่ ไดโนเสาร์ก็ดื่มน้ำนี้ได้?" — they also carried parasites; a dinosaur-era fossil can show it (named-find candidate for Boat, e.g. parasites in amber or dung fossils). `unchecked`; cut the beat if the evidence is thin (ER07).
  5. Payoff flip: "ในโลกที่ไม่มีมนุษย์สักคน… สิ่งที่แก่ที่สุดในลำธารนี้ ไม่ใช่ไดโนเสาร์ แต่คือเชื้อโรค"
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold on the drop; `_A_fade`.

- **Voice / tone:** firm, deadpan survival-briefing narrator, like a calm safety announcement that knows something you don't (Gemini **Orus**, male); the rider reacts with small sounds (stomach growl, "หือ?", a gulp held back). One read per episode; pace picked from the ≤ 10 s sample.
- **Why it holds:** "ห้าม…" is a rule the viewer instantly argues with; the payoff flips "nature is pure" into "nature is armed"; "ไม่มีคน ก็มีเชื้อโรค" is quotable and shareable to the friend who drinks from mountain streams.
- **Research load:** general knowledge (Mesozoic plant groups and their toxins, age of microbes, fossil evidence of dinosaur-era parasites); film premise check.
- **Sound intent:** EP1 jungle bed with insect drone, stomach growl as the hook's stinger, a wooden "tok" when he grabs the seed, music drops to silence on "มีพิษ", low brass sting under the flip. EP2 a bright, tempting water trickle and birdless calm, then a micro "swim" squelch layer on the zoom-in, one held silence before the last line, then a low hum tag. Sound never replaces the visual clue (the cycad, the swimming germs).

## ER08 pitch check

1. **Hook:** breaks a certainty ("untouched nature is safe to eat and drink"), clear in ~3 s, no term (ปรง is named only after the viewer sees the tree). Pass.
2. **Who:** everyone; every viewer has eaten vegetables and drunk water. Pass.
3. **Beats:** chain of "แต่…" problems (seed → fern → the dinosaur eats it; clear → germs → older than dinosaurs). No tips list: the rules are the hook, not a closing tip. Risk: EP1 beat 3–4 may hit ER07 if toxicity in Mesozoic plants is "unclear"; Boat decides before scripting. Pass with risk.
4. **Ending:** flip ("nature is armed", "the oldest thing is the germ"), not a tip. Pass.

**Score: 4/4** (risk: EP1 evidence, ER07).

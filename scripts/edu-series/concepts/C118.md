# C118 — พระราชาที่แตะอะไรก็เป็นทอง แล้วเกือบอดตาย (wild card) (CAT06)

- Status **pitched**, round **R20** (Preaw, 2026-11-21). Category **CAT06 Mythology (HIGH)**, named in the user's prompt.
- Channel `edu`. One Thai Short, about 50–60 s estimated. Profile: the myth is the story, the fact is what the real world says about it ([ER11](../../../channels/edu/script.md#er11) source 1); on-screen label “ตำนานกรีก”. Hook type **HK01** broken certainty.
- Not checked: every fact below is a pitch lead for Boat, not research.
- **Wild card:** told as an animated ancient Greek black-figure vase (black silhouettes on terracotta orange, figures in profile, the vase turning to move between scenes). The painted 3D camera and the four-head cast cannot make this look; it needs a new flat silhouette treatment.

## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Myth comedy. King Midas does a god a favour and is granted one wish: everything he touches turns to gold. It is wonderful for about a minute.
- **Proposed medium** — Black-figure vase animation (wild card); fallback is the painted 3D camera with a gold palette.
- **Logline** — พระราชาโลภขอพรให้แตะอะไรก็เป็นทอง ก้อนหินเป็นทอง ดีใจ แอปเปิลเป็นทอง ยังดีใจ ขนมปังเป็นทอง… เริ่มไม่ดีใจแล้ว
- **Opening line** — “พระราชาคนนี้ขอพรให้แตะอะไรก็เป็นทอง… แล้วเกือบอดข้าวตาย”
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — Unlimited gold is a death sentence when you are hungry.

## Beats

1. **B01** — The wish (lead: Ovid; the god is Dionysus, the favour is returning Silenus). Midas: “ขออะไรก็ได้ใช่ไหม… ขอแตะอะไรก็เป็นทอง”. Deadpan: “ไม่ได้ขอสุขภาพ ไม่ได้ขอความสุข ขอทองล้วน ๆ”.
2. **B02** — Rule of three: a stone, gold (yay); an apple, gold (yay); bread, gold, with a tooth-cracking clink (uh oh). Character line: “แล้ว… ข้าจะกินอะไร”.
3. **B03** — But worse: the wine turns to liquid gold in his mouth (lead: Ovid). Today comparison: “เหมือนรวยที่สุดในโลก แต่สั่งข้าวทุกแอปแล้วได้ทองแท่งมาส่ง”.
4. **B04** — The wow fact as a punchline: the famous scene where his daughter turns to gold is not in the Greek myth at all; an American writer added it in the 1800s (lead: Nathaniel Hawthorne, 1852). Irony: “ฉากที่ทุกคนจำได้ที่สุด… คือฉากที่ไม่มีในต้นฉบับ”.
5. **B05** — The cure: wash in the river Pactolus, and the gold flows into the water (lead). Real-world fact: that river really carried gold, and the kingdom beside it was among the first to strike coins (lead: Lydia, electrum).

- **Ending** — Callback: did he learn? No. Later he judged a music contest, picked the wrong god, and Apollo gave him donkey ears (lead: Ovid). “ได้ทองไปก็คืน ได้หูลาไป… อันนี้ได้ถาวร” Hold 2 s on the vase turning to show the ears.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore as a friend telling a myth like gossip about a greedy boss; Midas pompous, then whiny.
- **Why it holds** — Everyone knows the outline, so the hook lands instantly, and the "that scene was made up" fact rewards people who think they know it. Share trigger: “ส่งให้เพื่อนที่อยากรวย”.
- **Research load for Boat** — Myth plus named real-world facts. Leads: Ovid's Metamorphoses Book 11 (the wish, food and wine, the Pactolus cure, the donkey ears); the daughter's first appearance (Hawthorne, A Wonder-Book, 1852) and whether any ancient source has it; gold in the Pactolus; Lydia and the earliest coins; whether a real Phrygian king Midas existed (Assyrian records, Gordion). Myth events are presented as myth, never as history.
- **Sound intent (proposed)** — Lyre-and-frame-drum comic march; a bright metallic "ting" for every gold transformation, getting heavier each time, with a crunch on the bread; water rush at B05; a donkey-like bray played on a horn as the ending stinger.

## Film annotation (W0.2)

- **Visual approach (proposed)** — Wild card as pitched: an animated black-figure vase. Flat black silhouettes in strict profile with incised (scratched-through) orange detail lines on terracotta, meander borders top and bottom, and the vase body slowly turning (a horizontal scroll of the frieze with a cylindrical squeeze at the edges) to move between scenes. Gold is the one added colour: each touched object turns from black to burnished gold, which keeps the gag readable on the two-tone ground. Fallback as Preaw named: the painted 3D camera with a warm gold palette. Built on flat fills and flat ink ribbons, not p5.brush washes ([DF-20261120-opaque-fill-composite](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261120-opaque-fill-composite), [DF-20261121-flat-ink-lines](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261121-flat-ink-lines)).

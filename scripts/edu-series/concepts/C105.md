## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Sports-psychology comedy. Jo, a nervous amateur goalkeeper, reads one study before the cup-final penalty shoot-out: keepers who stay in the middle save more, yet almost nobody does it. He decides to follow the numbers. The crowd decides otherwise.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 2D on the project defaults, with a stadium crowd and a broadcast-style slow-motion replay frame for the saves.
- **Logline** — โกลคนหนึ่งรู้ความลับว่ายืนเฉย ๆ เซฟจุดโทษได้มากกว่า แต่ทุกครั้งที่ยืนเฉย ทั้งสนามมองว่าเขากลัวจนตัวแข็ง
- **Opening line** — “ถ้าคุณเป็นผู้รักษาประตูตอนจุดโทษ กฎข้อที่หนึ่งคือ… อย่าพุ่ง”
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — Keepers dive almost every time even though staying central stops more penalties, because a miss while standing still looks worse than a miss after a dive (action bias).

## Beats

1. **B01** — Kick one: Jo stands still as planned. Ball into the corner. Crowd: "ยืนดูเฉยเลยเหรอ!" (character line from the stands).

4. **B04** — Last kick decides the cup. Jo plants his feet. The striker chips it straight down the middle.
5. **B05** — Jo catches it without moving a step. Punchline below.

- **Ending** — Irony: the match-winning save, and next morning's headline “โกลตัวแข็งด้วยความกลัว… แต่ดวงดี”.

- **Why it holds** — HK05: a rule that sounds wrong, then three tests where obeying it costs him socially. Share trigger: every football fan has screamed at a keeper; this flips who was right. The rule also reaches beyond sport (doing something to look busy).

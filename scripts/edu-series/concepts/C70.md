## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre:** animal surprise with a twist ending (how dogs talk to us with their faces).
- **Medium:** painted 3D camera (project default): a Thai condo kitchen at dinner; one painted inside-view of the brow muscle (a see-through overlay on the dog's face, the EP09 forearm method) and a wolf beside the dog for comparison.
- **Our cast:** a Thai woman in her late twenties (four-head cast) eating moo ping at the counter, trying hard not to share; her mixed-breed dog. She meets each problem on screen (ER02).
- **Logline:** She swears she won't give the dog any moo ping, then it lifts its inner eyebrows and she caves. That face needs a small muscle that wolves barely have; people picked it, dog after dog. And the dog makes it for the person looking at it, not for the food.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** the "sad puppy eyes" are not just a cute accident: dogs have a small eyebrow muscle their wolf cousins mostly lack, and they move it more when a person is looking at them (`unchecked`: Kaminski et al. 2019 anatomy; Kaminski et al. 2017 attention study).
- **Rule that flips (ER03):** "หน้านี้ เกิดมาเพื่อคน". At first it means we shaped the dog: humans kept the dogs with that face. The flip: the face now chooses its humans; at shelters, dogs that make it more find a home sooner. We thought we picked the dog.
- **Hook (Thai):** "หน้าอ้อน ๆ ที่หมาทำใส่คุณ… หมาป่าทำไม่ได้นะ มันไม่มีกล้ามเนื้อคิ้วอันนี้"
- **Objection by ~7 s:** viewer: "หมาก็มาจากหมาป่านี่?" → narrator: "ญาติกันแท้ ๆ เลย… แต่คิ้วนี้ หมาป่าแทบไม่มี"
- **Twist clue planted:** CL01 a "ADOPT ME" shelter photo of this same dog stuck on her fridge, seen in beat 1 as background; it is where she found it. It returns in the flip.
- **Beats (each opens the next with "แต่…"; times estimated):**
  1. **Hook (0–7 s):** she raises a skewer, says "ไม่ให้นะ"; the dog lifts its inner eyebrows, eyes suddenly huge. A painted wolf beside it tries the same and its brow stays flat. Fridge photo in frame (CL01).
  2. **Problem 1 (~7–17 s):** "แต่ หมาไปได้คิ้วนี้มาจากไหน?" Inside-view: a thin muscle above the eye lights up on the dog, absent on the wolf. Answer (one cause): over thousands of years people kept and fed the dogs whose faces moved them most, so the face got passed on. Rule stated: "หน้านี้ เกิดมาเพื่อคน". She caves and gives one piece.
  3. **Problem 2 (~17–28 s):** hook payoff at ~30 %: "คิ้วที่ทำให้คุณใจอ่อน… คนเราเลือกมาเองกับมือ". "แต่ มันทำหน้านี้ เพราะอยากกินรึเปล่า?" She turns her back holding the skewer: the dog's face goes flat. She turns to face it with empty hands: the eyebrows go up. Answer: dogs move their faces more when a person is looking at them, food or no food (`unchecked`).
  4. **Problem 3 (~28–40 s):** "แต่ ทำไมหน้าแบบนี้ ถึงได้ผลกับเรานัก?" Answer (one cause): lifting the inner brow makes the eyes look bigger and sad, like a child about to cry, and we want to look after it (`unchecked`: Boat sets the true one-line wording). She melts again, sighing.
  5. **Problem 4 (~40–48 s):** "แต่ ได้ผลจริงแค่ไหน?" Cut to the fridge photo (CL01) coming alive: a shelter row of dogs; the one lifting its brows gets the visitor. Answer: at shelters, dogs that lift their brows more often get adopted sooner (`unchecked`: Waller et al. 2013).
  6. **Payoff flip (~48–55 s):** back in the kitchen, she looks from the photo to her dog, which lifts its brows at her once more. "เราคิดว่าเราเป็นคนเลือกหมา… จริง ๆ หน้านี้ เลือกเรามาตั้งแต่แรก" ~2 s hold on her face giving in.
- **Ending:** closed on the flip, ~2 s hold; `_A_fade`. No tip, no call to action.
- **Voice / tone:** Kore, warm smiling storyteller, teasing her weakness ("ไม่ให้จริงเหรอ…", "นะ", "หรอก"). She reacts with "ไม่ได้!", a long sigh, a soft "โอ๊ย…"; the dog only with a tiny whine.
- **Why it holds:** everyone has seen this face, owner or not; the hook breaks the idea that it is innocent cuteness; the turn-your-back test is something the viewer can try on their own dog tonight; the flip turns the viewer from chooser into the chosen, which is quotable and shareable among owners ("หมาฉันเลือกฉันเอง").

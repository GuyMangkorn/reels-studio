- **Genre and setup** — War-movie parody. Western Australia, late 1932: about 20,000 emus are eating the wheat. The army sends Major Meredith, two soldiers and two machine guns. The emus do not cooperate.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 3D camera on a flat, sunburnt wheat plain, graded like a scratched 1930s newsreel; war-film framing (low heroic angles on the soldiers, then the emus in wide shots, unbothered).
- **Logline** — นายทหารคนหนึ่งได้ภารกิจง่าย ๆ ไปยิงนกที่กินข้าวสาลีชาวไร่ แต่นกวิ่งเร็วกว่ารถ แยกฝูงเก่งกว่าทหาร และปืนก็ติดในวันสำคัญที่สุด

- **Counter-intuitive premise** — A modern army fought a bird with machine guns and pulled out.

1. **B01** — Farmers complain; the army answers with machine guns. Meredith, chest out: “สองสัปดาห์ก็จบ” (character line, played as a joke; planted for the ending).
2. **B02** — First attack: the emus split into small groups and scatter out of range. Deadpan: “นกไม่ได้อ่านแผนรบมาด้วย”.
3. **B03** — The ambush at a dam: a big flock walks right up, the gun fires a few rounds… and jams (lead).
4. **B04** — But worse: they mount the gun on a truck. The emus outrun it on rough ground and the gunner cannot aim (lead). Rule of three: plan A, scatter; plan B, jam; plan C, the truck loses a race to a bird.
5. **B05** — The army withdraws. Meredith's own report praises the enemy: they could face machine guns "with the invulnerability of tanks" (lead; the documented quote as the wow line).

## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Sensory workplace comedy in a fictional Roman fullery.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 2D with close views of wet wool and the worker’s hands and feet.
- **Logline** — สมมุติว่าคุณเป็นเด็กใหม่ในร้านซักผ้าโรมัน ผ้าขาวเปื้อนมันกองอยู่ตรงหน้า แต่คนคุมงานยื่นไหฉี่ให้คุณ
- **Opening line** — “ถ้าคุณต้องซักเสื้อขาวให้สะอาด แต่คนในร้านยื่นไหฉี่ให้ล่ะ?”
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — Dirty-smelling material can help remove an oily stain.

## Beats

1. **B01** — Take the garment. The customer says they will come back for it and points at the shiny oil mark.
2. **B02** — You rub the spot with water. The rest of the cloth is wet, but that patch still shines. You can hand it back with the mark or reach for the jar the other worker set down.
3. **B03** — You choose the jar in this imagined shift. The workshop fact lands in one line when the oil lets go. But you still have a cloth you would not want to put on.
4. **B04** — You rinse it in the larger basin and hand it back after a drying ellipsis. The customer looks for the spot where you had kept your thumb. Their fingers find clean cloth.

- **Ending** — Closed sensory reversal. Hold on the unstained cloth in the customer’s hand.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore with an amused wince, then relief.
- **Retention and share hypothesis** — A familiar stain creates the test. The jar next to the clean garment is the image worth sharing.
- **Research load for Boat** — General historical practice. Check where and how Roman fullers used urine in textile treatment and the scope of any claim about oil removal.

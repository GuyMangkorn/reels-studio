- Status **pitched**, round **R21** (Preaw, 2026-11-21). Category **CAT01 Science (HIGH)**; topic named by the user, so no category pick. Exotic pet: tarantula.
- Channel `edu`. One Thai Short, about 50–60 s estimated. Profile: absurd first-time-owner story built on general knowledge ([ER11](../../../channels/edu/script.md#er11) source 2); on-screen label "เรื่องสมมุติ". Hook type **HK07** start at the climax (the funeral).
- Not checked: every fact below is a pitch lead for Boat, not research.
- **Wild card:** told as claymation stop-motion: plasticine figures with fingerprint texture, stepped motion on twos (about 12 drawings per second), a tabletop set with visible felt and cardboard. The painted 3D camera and the four-head cast cannot make this look; it needs a new clay surface treatment and stepped-timing motion.
- **Care:** no handling advice and no "how to help a moult" step on screen (owners are commonly told not to touch a moulting tarantula; lead for Boat, used only if it lands as a bit).

## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Funeral farce. Nut (นัท) buys his first pet, a tarantula, because the shop said "เลี้ยงง่าย ไม่ต้องดูแลเยอะ". It stops eating for weeks, then lies on its back with its legs curled. He holds a funeral. Next morning there are two tarantulas.
- **Proposed medium** — Claymation stop-motion (wild card); fallback is the painted 3D camera in a small condo room.
- **Logline** — นัทซื้อทารันทูล่าเพราะคนขายบอกว่าเลี้ยงง่าย มันไม่กินข้าว นอนหงาย ขาหงิก นัทจัดงานศพให้ด้วยน้ำตา เช้ามาเจอสองตัว
- **Opening line** — "นัทจัดงานศพให้ทารันทูล่า… เช้าวันต่อมา มันเดินมาดูงานศพตัวเอง"
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — The "dead" tarantula on its back is the healthiest moment of its year.

## Beats

1. **B01** — Cold open at the climax: a shoebox coffin, a single flower, Nut sobbing. Then rewind: "สองเดือนก่อน". The shop: "เลี้ยงง่ายครับ ไม่ต้องดูแลเยอะ". Deadpan: "ประโยคนี้แหละ จำไว้".
2. **B02** — It refuses food for weeks (lead: tarantulas often fast before a moult). Rule of three: a cricket, a bigger cricket, a cricket with a ribbon; ignored. Nut: "ไม่กินข้าวเลย หรือมันไม่ชอบผม".
3. **B03** — But worse: it flips onto its back, legs curled, and lies still for hours (lead: many tarantulas moult on their backs). Nut lays it in the shoebox. Visual gag: a tiny clay candle and a framed portrait.
4. **B04** — Morning: the shoebox holds a tarantula, and the tank holds another one, bigger and shinier. The wow fact as a punchline: the one in the box is its old skin, shed in one piece, including the lining of its fangs and parts of its insides (lead: exuviae include the linings of mouthparts, foregut and book lungs). Character line: "แล้ว… ผมไว้อาลัยให้เสื้อมันเหรอ".
5. **B05** — Second fact (lead): a lost leg can grow back over the next moults. Today comparison: "เหมือนเปลี่ยนเคสโทรศัพท์ แต่ได้ขามาฟรีอีกข้าง".

- **Ending** — Callback to "เลี้ยงง่าย ไม่ต้องดูแลเยอะ": the wow fact as the final punchline: some female tarantulas live twenty to thirty years (lead). Nut reads the care sheet and looks at his rental contract, then at the spider. Narrator: "เลี้ยงง่ายจริง… แค่ต้องเลี้ยงไปอีกยี่สิบปี" Hold 2 s on the tarantula slowly raising one leg, like a wave.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore as a friend who cannot stop laughing at Nut; Nut dramatic and tearful; the shopkeeper breezy.
- **Why it holds** — A cold open on a funeral, a reveal people do not see coming, and a gross-amazing fact. Exotic-pet owners will share it as "this happened to me". Share trigger: "ส่งให้เพื่อนที่อยากเลี้ยงแมงมุม".
- **Research load for Boat** — General knowledge (exotic-pet care and arachnid biology). Leads: pre-moult fasting; moulting on the back; what the shed skin contains (fang and mouthpart linings, foregut lining, book lung linings); leg regeneration across moults; female lifespan by species (male lifespan much shorter, possibly a better twist); how often owners mistake a moult for death.
- **Sound intent (proposed)** — Sad solo violin funeral theme played slightly too seriously; clay squish foley on every step; a held breath of silence before the second tarantula is revealed, then a comic harp gliss; a squeaky-shoe "wave" sting on the last hold; the series closing sound.

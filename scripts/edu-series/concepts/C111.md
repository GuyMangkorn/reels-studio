## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — School cringe comedy. Laundry day: the only clean shirt James owns is his mum's gift, a huge winking face of a cheesy singer (invented, no real person). He is late for class and wants to get through it unseen.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 3D camera in a university lecture room; James's fear shown as a literal stage spotlight that follows him, then the camera cuts into thought bubbles above the other students.
- **Logline** — หนุ่มมหาลัยต้องใส่เสื้อที่น่าอายที่สุดในชีวิตเข้าห้อง เขามั่นใจว่าทุกคนจ้องเสื้อเขาอยู่ ยิ่งพยายามซ่อนก็ยิ่งพัง ทั้งที่ความจริงแทบไม่มีใครสังเกตเลย
- **Opening line** — “ใส่เสื้อตัวนี้เข้าห้อง คุณคิดว่าครึ่งห้องเห็นแน่ ๆ… คุณเดาเกินไปเท่าตัว”
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — People who wear an embarrassing shirt guess about twice as many people noticed as actually did.

## Beats

1. **B01** — James opens the door late; it creaks; a stage spotlight snaps on him. Visual plant: the girl in the next seat wears the very same shirt, and James never looks at her.
2. **B02** — The teacher calls his name. He stands with arms crossed over the singer's face. James (whisper): “อย่ามองเสื้อ อย่ามองเสื้อ อย่ามองเสื้อ” (character line).
3. **B03** — But worse: hiding the shirt, he knocks over his water. Now he is sure. Cut into heads, rule of three: “ผมวันนี้แบนไหมวะ”, “ปิดแก๊สยังนะ”, “หิว”.
4. **B04** — The real experiment, one cause under 10 s (lead): at Cornell in 2000, students sent into a room in a Barry Manilow T-shirt guessed about half the room noticed; about a quarter actually did. Psychologists call it the spotlight effect: the only spotlight is in your own head. Deadpan: “สปอตไลต์ดวงนั้น คุณเปิดเองปิดเองมาตลอด”.
5. **B05** — Punchline below.

- **Ending** — Irony planted in B01: class ends, the camera finally pans to the next seat: the girl in the identical shirt. James sees her for the first time. Narrator: “ทั้งคาบ เขาไม่เห็นเลยสักนิด… ว่าข้าง ๆ ใส่ตัวเดียวกัน”.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore as a friend teasing a mate she loves; James in a panicked whisper; one bored teacher line.
- **Why it holds** — HK09 starts from a feeling every viewer has had and promises a number that says they are wrong about it. The victim's hiding makes it worse each beat, and the ending proves the fact on him. Share trigger: “ไม่ต้องอาย คนเห็นน้อยกว่าที่คิดครึ่งนึง” sent to a friend who overthinks.
- **Research load for Boat** — General knowledge plus one named study: Gilovich, Medvec and Savitsky (2000), *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*. Leads: the predicted and actual noticing rates (about 50 % against about 23 %), the T-shirt and setting; later replications and how robust the spotlight effect is. The singer on James's shirt is invented; the real study's shirt is named only in the fact line.

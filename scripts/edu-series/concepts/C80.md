## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Subject:** animals / body (blood and gravity).
- **Genre:** animal-body puzzle tied to a feeling every viewer has had (standing up too fast and seeing stars), with a hand test the viewer can do on the spot.
- **Medium:** painted 3D camera (project default) for a Thai open zoo (a giraffe-feeding deck like the ones Thai families visit); the signature picture is a see-through giraffe neck: one long vein lined with small one-way doors that shut as the head goes down.
- **Our cast:** a Thai schoolgirl (four-head cast) on a zoo trip and one giraffe at the waterhole below the deck. She meets each problem on screen (ER02).
- **Logline:** A giraffe swings its head from treetop to puddle, nearly five metres, and never wobbles; she stands up from tying her shoe and sees stars. Its heart pushes twice as hard as hers, which solves going up but makes going down worse: all the blood in that neck should slide into its head. Small doors in the neck vein stop it, and she has the same doors in her own legs.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** the giraffe's real danger when it drinks is not too little blood in the head but too much: its neck is full of blood that wants to fall into its head, and one-way doors in the neck vein hold it back (`unchecked`: jugular valves; blood pressure about twice a human's).
- **Rule that flips (ER03):** "เลือดไหลลงที่ต่ำเสมอ". First it is the cost (head down, the blood in a two-metre neck falls into the head); the flip: the viewer's own lowest place is the feet, and the same kind of doors hold blood up in the viewer's legs.
- **Twist clue planted:** CL01 her hand hanging down at the rail swells its veins, raised it flattens (beat 4, a test the viewer can do); CL02 her legs ache after the long queue and she shakes out her calves (beat 1, background). Both return in the flip.

## Beats (five-step structure; times estimated)

1. **HOOK (0–7 s):** from the feeding deck (she rubs her calves after the queue, CL02), she watches the giraffe splay its front legs and drop its head from the treetops to the puddle. "ทำไมยีราฟก้มหัวกินน้ำแล้วไม่เป็นลม… ทั้งที่คุณแค่ลุกพรวดจากพื้น ยังหน้ามืดได้" Then, as the head rises again: "หัวมันขึ้นลงทีเกือบห้าเมตรเลยนะ"
2. **QUESTION + DELAY (7–17 s):** Question: "หรือมันค่อย ๆ เงยหัวขึ้น?" Delay: a kid's balloon pops on the deck; the giraffe whips its head straight up in one move, steady. She crouches to tie her shoe, stands fast and sways with stars in her eyes ("อุ๊ย"). "เงยพรวดเดียว ยีราฟยังนิ่ง… แต่คุณขยับหัวแค่เมตรเดียว ยังเซ" The guess fails on screen.
3. **REHOOK (17–24 s):** see-through giraffe: a big heart pumping hard up the long neck. "แต่ หัวใจยีราฟดันเลือดแรงกว่าคนเราราวสองเท่า เลือดเลยพุ่งขึ้นถึงหัว ต่อให้เงยเร็วแค่ไหน" Pays the "not fainting going up" half at ~40 % (~5 s answer), and in the same breath opens the next problem.
4. **QUESTION + DELAY #2 (24–35 s):** Question: "แต่ตอนก้มลงล่ะ… เลือดเต็มคอยาวสองเมตร ก็ต้องไหลลงไปกองที่หัวสิ" Delay: she lets one hand hang over the rail, and its veins swell; she lifts it up, and they flatten (CL01). "ลองปล่อยมือห้อยลงดู เส้นเลือดจะปูดขึ้นมา… เพราะเลือดไหลลงที่ต่ำเสมอ" (the viewer can try it now). The giraffe's head goes down again.
5. **ANSWER (35–50 s):** see-through neck: a row of small doors in the big neck vein swing shut as the head lowers. "เพราะในเส้นเลือดที่คอยีราฟ มีประตูเล็ก ๆ เรียงเป็นแถว พอก้มหัว ประตูก็ปิด เลือดในคอเลยไหลย้อนลงไปกองที่หัวไม่ได้" (~8 s). Flip: the see-through view slides to her own legs: the same small doors in her leg veins. "ประตูแบบนี้ คุณก็มีนะ… แต่อยู่ในขา เพราะที่ต่ำที่สุดของคุณ คือเท้า" ~2 s hold as she stops rubbing her calves and grins.

- **Ending:** closed on the flip, ~2 s hold. No tip (no "ลุกช้า ๆ นะ").
- **Voice / tone:** Kore, warm smiling storyteller, teasing ("นะ", "สิ"). The girl: "อุ๊ย" when she sways, a small "โห" at the giraffe, a laugh at the end.
- **Why it holds:** everyone has seen stars after standing up; the hand test is felt immediately; the flip moves the giraffe's secret into the viewer's own body.
- **Research load:** medium. Giraffe blood pressure and jugular valves are general knowledge, but how a giraffe protects its brain when the head goes down is partly debated (see Checks). ER07 risk sits on beats 4–5.

- Hand test: veins swell when the hand hangs, flatten when raised: true as shown.
- Standing up fast and seeing stars (blood briefly short in the head): true everyday wording for the hook.

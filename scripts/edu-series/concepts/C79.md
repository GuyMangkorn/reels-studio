## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Subject:** animals / physics of the body (turning in mid-air).
- **Genre:** everyday-animal mystery with a test the viewer can do on an office chair.
- **Medium:** painted 3D camera (project default) for a Thai house; the signature picture is a slowed-down fall where the cat's body visibly bends in the middle and turns one half at a time.
- **Our cast:** a Thai teenage boy (four-head cast) and his orange house cat; the neighbour's bobtailed cat (แมวหางกุด) for one beat. The boy meets each problem on screen (ER02). No person ever drops or throws a cat: every fall starts by itself (slip, roll off a ledge).
- **Logline:** His cat slips off the fridge upside down and still lands on four feet. Is it the tail? The neighbour's tailless cat does it too. Slow motion shows the cat twisting in two halves, and an office chair shows why that works with nothing to push against.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** a falling cat turns itself over with nothing to push against and no need for its tail: it bends at the middle and turns its front half first, then its back half (`unchecked`: tuck-and-turn; tailless cats right themselves).
- **Rule that flips (ER03):** "กลางอากาศ ไม่มีอะไรให้ยัน". First it is the puzzle and the cost (nothing to push on, so how can it turn?); the flip: with nothing else to push on, the cat pushes against itself, one half against the other.
- **Twist clue planted:** CL01 the slow-motion replay shows the bend in the middle (beat 3), CL02 on the swivel chair the boy's top half turns one way and his hips swing the other (beat 4). Both return in the answer.
- **Cut per ER07:** the high-floor follow-on (cats from very high floors faring better) is **not** in the beats. The known data come from cats brought to vets (cats that died on the spot are missing), and later studies disagree, so the honest answer is "results differ". Boat can confirm; if Boat finds a solid answer, it could replace delay #2 only with a recorded reason.

## Beats (five-step structure; times estimated)

1. **HOOK (0–6 s):** the cat stretches from the top of the fridge toward a gecko on the wall, slips and falls upside down; a blink later it lands on four feet and walks off. "ทำไมแมวตกจากที่สูงแล้วลงสี่ขาได้แทบทุกที… ทั้งที่กลางอากาศ ไม่มีอะไรให้มันยันเลยสักนิด"
2. **QUESTION + DELAY (6–17 s):** Question: "หรือหางมันช่วยเหวี่ยงตัว?" Delay: the neighbour's bobtailed cat, asleep on the back of the sofa, rolls off on its back; it still flips and lands on four feet ("เอ้า"). "แมวหางกุดข้างบ้าน ไม่มีหางให้เหวี่ยงเลย… ก็ยังลงสี่ขาสวย ๆ" The guess fails on screen; the answer is still held.
3. **REHOOK (17–24 s):** the boy replays his phone clip of the first fall in slow motion: the cat is not spinning like a log; its body bends at the middle, the head and front legs turn first, the back half follows (CL01). "แต่ พอเปิดสโลว์โมดู… แมวไม่ได้หมุนทั้งตัวนะ มันบิดทีละครึ่ง" The guess "it just spins" reverses; the picture pays part of the hook at ~40 %.
4. **QUESTION + DELAY #2 (24–35 s):** Question: "แต่ไม่มีอะไรให้ยัน แล้วมันบิดได้ยังไง?" Delay: the boy sits on an office swivel chair, lifts both feet and twists his shoulders to the right; his hips and the chair swing left on their own (CL02; "เฮ้ย"). "ลองนั่งเก้าอี้หมุน ยกเท้าขึ้น แล้วบิดตัวไปทางขวา… ก้นคุณหมุนไปทางซ้ายเอง" (the viewer can try it the second the line ends).
5. **ANSWER (35–50 s):** slowed fall again, the two halves outlined. "แมวก็โดนแบบนี้แหละ แต่มันโกง… หุบขาหน้า ท่อนหน้าเลยหมุนง่าย กางขาหลังไว้ ท่อนหลังเลยแทบไม่หมุนสวน แล้วค่อยสลับกัน" (~8 s). Flip: on the chair the boy tucks his arms, sticks his legs out, twists, swaps, and the chair comes all the way round; he grins at the cat. "กลางอากาศ ไม่มีอะไรให้ยัน… แมวเลยยันกับตัวมันเองซะเลย" ~2 s hold on the cat yawning.

- **Ending:** closed on the flip, ~2 s hold. No tip, no "อย่าปล่อยแมวตกจากที่สูง" line (safety is carried by the picture: no one drops a cat).
- **Voice / tone:** Kore, warm smiling storyteller, teasing ("นะ", "แหละ"). The boy: "เอ้า", "เฮ้ย" on the chair, a laugh at the end. The cat: a small surprised "แง้ว" on the slip.
- **Why it holds:** almost everyone has seen a cat land on its feet; the office-chair test is felt in the body; the "it pushes against itself" flip is a one-sentence retell.
- **Research load:** general knowledge (cat righting reflex, tuck-and-turn; tailless cats) plus one check that the high-floor beat is rightly cut. Light.

## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre:** deep-time "what if" (which period you land in).
- **Our cast:** a grandmother (60s) and her grandson (10) whose house, spirit house (ศาลพระภูมิ) and marigold garland included, lands in the Jurassic.
- **Logline:** "The age of dinosaurs" sounds like one place, but it lasted so long that where you land in it decides everything: which dinosaurs exist, and whether a single flower exists at all.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** the dinosaurs you know never met each other, and the flowers on your spirit house would be the strangest thing on a Jurassic Earth.
- **Structure: 2 (hook → question/delay #1 → rehook → question/delay #2 → answer).** Why: both answers are a size-of-time surprise that hits harder after the viewer has guessed wrong twice; a rules list would give the answer away.

### EP1 — คุณอาจไม่เจอทีเร็กซ์เลยสักตัว
- **Single question / answer:** Why could you land among dinosaurs and never see a T. rex? It had not appeared yet: T. rex lived closer in time to you than to Stegosaurus.
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าบ้านคุณหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์… คุณอาจไม่เจอทีเร็กซ์เลยสักตัว"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: the grandson runs out with his toy T. rex, scanning the trees.
  2. Question #1: why not? Delay: a Stegosaurus walks past the fence; he grins and keeps searching for the "big one".
  3. Rehook: "แต่ ทีเร็กซ์ไม่ได้ซ่อนอยู่หรอกนะ… มันยังไม่เกิด"
  4. Question #2: how far away is it? Answer (one cause, one body comparison): T. rex lived closer to us than to Stegosaurus; the soi as a timeline: Stegosaurus at one end, T. rex near the other, the grandson's house a step past it. `unchecked`
  5. Payoff flip: "ทีเร็กซ์… อยู่ใกล้คุณ มากกว่าอยู่ใกล้สเตโกซอรัส"
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold on the toy T. rex next to the real Stegosaurus; `_A_fade`.

### EP2 — ดอกไม้ดอกเดียวในโลก
- **Single question / answer:** Why does a dinosaur stop at the spirit house? In the Jurassic there are no flowering plants yet, so the marigold garland is the only flower on Earth.
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าบ้านคุณหลุดไปยุคจูราสสิก… พวงมาลัยบนศาลพระภูมิ คือของที่แปลกที่สุดในโลก"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: grandma hangs a fresh marigold garland on the spirit house as she does every morning.
  2. Question #1: why is a long-necked dinosaur leaning over the fence, staring at it? Delay: grandma waves it off; it keeps staring.
  3. Rehook: "แต่ ลองมองไปรอบ ๆ สิ… ไม่มีสีเหลือง ไม่มีสีชมพู ไม่มีทุ่งหญ้า" — the whole world is green and brown.
  4. Question #2: where are the flowers? Answer (one cause): flowering plants had not appeared yet; they come later, in the next age. `unchecked`
  5. Payoff flip: "ดาวเรืองพวงเดียวของยาย… คือดอกไม้ดอกเดียวบนโลกทั้งใบ"
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold on the yellow garland, the only warm colour in the frame; `_B_loop` is possible (the last frame returns to the morning garland).

- **Voice / tone:** a warm, wondering storyteller of deep time, slower and softer than the channel's brisk default (Gemini **Achernar**, female, atempo picked from the sample); grandson gasps and giggles, grandma one short "ไปไป๊" line. One read per episode.
- **Why it holds:** every viewer "knows" the dinosaurs as one cast in one movie; the timeline flip is a fact people quote at dinner; the spirit-house garland is a Thai image that turns a botany fact into a feeling; share trigger: "รู้มั้ย ทีเร็กซ์ใกล้เรามากกว่า…".
- **Research load:** general knowledge (dates of Stegosaurus and T. rex, first flowering plants, first grasses); film premise check.
- **Sound intent:** EP1 playful marimba and toy-squeak, heavy footfalls with a low thump for the Stegosaurus, a record-scratch-free pause (silence) before "มันยังไม่เกิด", a long sustained pad that stretches during the timeline walk, small chime on the payoff. EP2 morning birdless hush (no songbirds), soft wind in conifers, a curious dinosaur snuffle, music thins to a single held note as the colour drains, warm strings swell only on the garland reveal.

## ER08 pitch check

1. **Hook:** breaks a certainty ("the age of dinosaurs = T. rex and friends together"; "flowers are everywhere"), clear in ~3 s, no term. Pass.
2. **Who:** everyone who has seen a dinosaur toy or movie, and every Thai household with a spirit house. Pass.
3. **Beats:** question → wrong guess → "แต่…" → answer, one cause each, no mechanism block. Pass.
4. **Ending:** flip ("closer to you", "the only flower on Earth"). Pass.

**Score: 4/4.** Risk: the T. rex/Stegosaurus fact is popular trivia, so part of the audience may already know it (ER01 "answer they already know"); EP2 is the fresher episode and could lead the series.

## Pitch (Preaw)


- **Sub-genre:** body / sleep science.
- **Staging:** one character in a moonlit bedroom that melts into an abstract watercolour dreamscape (clouds, a cliff edge, falling through paint).
- **Logline:** A girl drifts off and suddenly "falls off a cliff", jerking awake. We follow her body falling asleep step by step to show what the jolt is, why it often comes with a falling dream, and why nobody knows the full answer yet.
- **Hook (Thai):** "เคยไหม… กำลังจะหลับ แล้วรู้สึกเหมือนตกจากที่สูงจนสะดุ้งตื่น"
- **Beats:**
  1. Bedroom, blue moonlight. The character's eyes close; the room's walls soften into watercolour clouds.
  2. Her body "switches off" in stages: muscles loosen, shown as painted lights going out along the limbs. `unchecked`
  3. Dream: she walks to a cliff edge, steps off, falls through paint. The body jerks; she bolts upright in bed. Name it: the hypnic jerk. `unchecked`
  4. Why: one leading idea is that the brain misreads the sudden muscle relaxation as falling and jerks to "catch" you; caffeine, stress and tiredness are linked to more of them. Labelled as a theory, not settled. `unchecked`
  5. Reassure: common and usually harmless. She lies back down, eyes close, clouds return.
- **Ending:** `C_seamless`. Hook/last-line pair: the last line "…แล้วคืนนี้ คุณจะลองสังเกตไหม ว่าตอนกำลังจะหลับ" runs straight into the hook "เคยไหม… กำลังจะหลับ…", and the last frame (eyes closing, walls softening) is the first frame.
- **Voice / tone:** soft, slower female narrator close to a whisper, warm and calm; one acted gasp on the jolt.
- **Why it holds:** almost everyone has felt it, so the hook is a recognition hit in under two seconds; the cliff fall is a showpiece for painted weather and light; the loop invites rewatching just before bed; honest "science doesn't fully know" is a shareable surprise.
- **Research load:** general knowledge, with a documented-uncertainty duty (causes are theories).
- **Sound intent:** low ambient pad and a ticking clock; wind rises during the fall; the jolt is a hard silence plus a single thump; the pad returns quietly for the loop seam.

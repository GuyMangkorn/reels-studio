## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Subject:** body / sound (hearing).
- **Genre:** everyday-body myth flip with a test the viewer can do on the spot.
- **Medium:** painted 3D camera (project default) for a Thai teen's bedroom; the signature picture is a painted see-through head: the voice travelling two roads, through the air into the ear and through the jaw and skull straight to the inner ear.
- **Our cast:** a Thai teenage girl (four-head cast) recording a birthday voice clip for her friend; the friend appears on a video call for one beat. She meets each problem on screen (ER02).
- **Logline:** She records a clip, plays it back and cringes: that's not her voice. A new mic doesn't fix it, and her friend says the clip sounds exactly like her. The voice in her head is the odd one, because she hears herself through her own skull.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** the voice in the recording is your real voice as everyone hears it; the one you hear while speaking sounds deeper because part of it reaches your inner ear through the bones of your head (`unchecked`: bone conduction; low frequencies favoured).
- **Rule that flips (ER03):** "คุณได้ยินเสียงตัวเอง ผ่านหัวตัวเอง". First it is the cost (her head lies to her, the clip sounds wrong); the flip: the voice she wants to delete is the one everyone already knows, and she is the only person who has never heard it.
- **Twist clue planted:** CL01 the friend's voice in the same clip sounds normal to her (beat 2), CL02 her own voice stays loud with both ears plugged (beat 4). Both return in the answer.

## Beats (five-step structure; times estimated)

1. **HOOK (0–6 s):** she plays the clip back, freezes, and slams the phone face down. "ทำไมเสียงตัวเองในคลิป ฟังแล้วแปลกจนอยากลบทิ้ง… ทั้งที่นั่นแหละ คือเสียงจริงของคุณ"
2. **QUESTION + DELAY (6–17 s):** Question: "หรือไมค์มือถือมันห่วย?" Delay: she borrows her brother's gaming mic, records again, plays it: still wrong ("เอ๊ะ?"). Then the friend's reply clip plays through the same phone and sounds perfectly normal (CL01). "ไมค์ใหม่ก็ยังแปลก… แต่เสียงเพื่อนในเครื่องเดียวกัน ปกติทุกอย่าง" The guess fails on screen; the answer is still held.
3. **REHOOK (17–24 s):** video call; she asks the friend which one is her real voice. The friend points at the clip. "แต่ พอถามเพื่อน… เพื่อนบอกว่าเสียงในคลิปนี่แหละ เหมือนแกเป๊ะ" The guess reverses: the clip is right, her head is the odd one. (Pays the hook's claim at ~45 %.)
4. **QUESTION + DELAY #2 (24–35 s):** Question: "แล้วเสียงในหัวเรา มาจากไหน?" Delay: she plugs both ears with her fingers and says "ฮัลโหล" — and still hears herself, loud and boomy (CL02). "อุดหูทั้งสองข้าง แล้วลองพูดดู… ทำไมยังได้ยินชัดขนาดนี้?" (the viewer can do it the second the line ends).
5. **ANSWER (35–50 s):** see-through head: one wave goes out the mouth and around to the ear; a second wave runs through the jaw and skull straight inside. "เพราะเสียงคุณวิ่งผ่านกระดูกหัว เข้าหูข้างในตรง ๆ ด้วย… และกระดูกส่งเสียงทุ้มได้ดีกว่าอากาศ เสียงในหัวคุณเลยทุ้มกว่าที่คนอื่นได้ยิน" (~8 s). Flip: she plays the clip again and doesn't cringe. "เสียงที่คุณอยากลบทิ้ง… คือเสียงที่ทุกคนรู้จักมาตลอด มีแค่คุณคนเดียว ที่เพิ่งได้ยินมันครั้งแรก" ~2 s hold on her small smile.

- **Ending:** closed on the flip, ~2 s hold. No tip (no "ฝึกฟังเสียงตัวเองบ่อย ๆ").
- **Voice / tone:** Kore, warm smiling storyteller, teasing ("นั่นแหละ", "หรอก"). The girl: a groan, "เอ๊ะ?", a muffled "ฮัลโหล" with plugged ears, a small laugh at the end.
- **Why it holds:** everyone has cringed at their own recording; the plug-your-ears test is felt in the body; the flip lands on the viewer, so it gets sent to the friend who hates their voice notes.
- **Research load:** general knowledge (bone conduction of one's own voice; low-frequency emphasis). Light.

## Checks for Boat

- Bone conduction carries part of one's own voice to the cochlea; it favours lower frequencies, so the self-heard voice sounds deeper: true one-line wording.
- "เสียงในคลิปคือเสียงที่คนอื่นได้ยิน": true as a simplification, given phone-mic colouring? Keep "เสียงจริงของคุณ" or soften to one narrow hedge.
- Plugged ears: one's own voice still heard (occlusion effect may even make it louder); true to say "ยังได้ยินชัด".

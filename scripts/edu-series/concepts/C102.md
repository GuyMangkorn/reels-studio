## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Absurd travel comedy. Nok, a sunrise-photo influencer, books the first holiday on Venus (in a magic suit that survives the heat and pressure: plainly a joke) to post one perfect sunrise.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 2D on the project defaults: an orange-yellow cloud world, Nok small in a bulky suit, a phone on a selfie stick.
- **Logline** — อินฟลูสายพระอาทิตย์ขึ้นไปดาวศุกร์เพื่อถ่ายรูปเดียว แต่ดาวดวงนี้ทำทุกอย่างกลับหัวและช้าจนเธอแทบลืมว่ามาทำอะไร

- **Counter-intuitive premise** — Venus spins backwards and so slowly that one turn takes longer than its whole trip around the Sun.

## Beats

1. **B01** — Nok lands at dusk, sets the tripod facing east, and sets an alarm for sunrise. The alarm says: 58 days (deadpan aside: "ซึ่งก็คือรอบบิลบัตรเครดิตสองรอบ").
2. **B02** — But the night keeps going. She runs out of snacks, then charger, then content ideas (rule of three).
3. **B03** — Bigger: a fact line lands while she waits: one spin of Venus takes longer than a Venus year (wow fact 1, with Boat's exact wording on sidereal vs solar day).
4. **B04** — Sunrise at last. She is staring east, camera ready. The light comes up behind her, in the west (wow fact 2, show first: her shadow points the wrong way).
5. **B05** — She spins round. The thick clouds hide the Sun anyway: just a glow. Punchline below.

- **Ending** — Callback and irony: she posts the only shot she got, the back of her own head in orange glow, captioned “พระอาทิตย์ขึ้นสวยมาก (เชื่อฉันเถอะ)”.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore teasing; Nok chirpy influencer voice that sags with each beat ("วันที่สามสิบสองค่ะทุกคน… ยังมืดอยู่").

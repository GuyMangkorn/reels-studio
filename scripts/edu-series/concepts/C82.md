## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Subject / genre:** Everyday physics / prediction comedy.
- **Medium:** Painted 3D camera + crisp SVG air/force insert. Proposed only; Film owns feasibility and production size.
- **Cast / situation:** A curious Thai adult passenger in a normal closed car; an off-screen driver is incidental. No audience driving challenge.
- **Logline:** A passenger expects everything to lean backward as the car accelerates. A helium balloon contradicts the prediction; a hanging object and a reversed acceleration reveal the invisible air as the missing actor.
- **Counter-intuitive claim:** In an enclosed accelerating vehicle, a tethered helium balloon leans toward the direction of acceleration rather than copying the passenger's initial backward lean.
- **One rule / reframe:** The balloon responds to the air around it; that same rule predicts the reversal when acceleration changes direction.
- **Clues:** CL01 a hanging object leans differently from the balloon; CL02 the balloon remains upright at a steady-speed interval before the final change.
- **Voice / tone:** channel Kore, warm smiling storyteller; curious and brisk. Same voice/pace in both openings; narrator setting still needs the normal sample selection before production.

## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Subject / genre:** Earth science / foundation mystery.
- **Medium:** Painted 3D camera + graphic grain-and-water cross-section. Proposed only; Film owns feasibility and production size.
- **Cast / situation:** One adult observer in a fictional schematic site, not a reconstruction of a sourced casualty event or a diagnosis of the viewer's home.
- **Logline:** A fictional building tilts while remaining visibly in one piece. The apparent building mystery becomes a ground-support mystery when the grain-and-water view shows why a firm-looking base can lose strength during shaking.
- **Counter-intuitive claim:** Some loose saturated ground can lose support in an earthquake, so an apparently unbroken building can tilt through failure of its foundation material.
- **One rule / reframe:** Support depends on the contacts between grains; an apparently solid base is a working arrangement, not a single solid block.
- **Clues:** CL01 the building remains one piece at the beginning; CL02 water is visible between loose grains in the first explanatory cutaway.
- **Voice / tone:** channel Kore, warm smiling storyteller; curious and brisk. Same voice/pace in both openings; narrator setting still needs the normal sample selection before production.

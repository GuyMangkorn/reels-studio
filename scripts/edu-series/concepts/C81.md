## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre:** myth flip that lands on the viewer: the red cape fools the crowd, not the bull.
- **Medium:** painted 3D camera (project default). Two sets: a Spanish bullring shown plainly in the first second (round sand arena, packed stands, a man in a tight glittering suit holding a red cloth, a black bull charging the cloth and passing beside him) so a Thai viewer who has never seen a bullfight reads it at once; and a Thai farm (grandpa's place in the countryside). Signature picture: the same arena seen "through the bull's eyes", the red cloth turned dull and muddy while it keeps swinging. No blood, no swords, no injured animal anywhere; the arena only shows the cape pass.
- **Our cast:** a Thai teenage boy (four-head cast) who wears a red shirt to his grandpa's farm and freezes near the grandpa's bull; grandpa for one beat. He meets each problem on screen (ER02).
- **Logline:** Everyone "knows" bulls hate red, so he freezes in his red shirt; the bull keeps eating grass, then trots toward a plastic bag flapping in the wind. In the bull's eyes red isn't even red. It charges whatever moves, so the red was never for the bull.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** a bull can't tell red from other dull colours; it charges the cape because the cape moves, and the red is there for the people watching (`unchecked`: cattle colour vision; moving-object tests; why the muleta is red).
- **Rule that flips (ER03):** "วัวไม่ได้ดูสี มันดูสิ่งที่ขยับ". First it shows his fear is aimed at the wrong thing (the red shirt); the flip: the red cape has been fooling the crowd all along, never the bull.
- **Twist clue planted:** CL01 the plastic bag flapping on the fence draws the bull while the still red shirt doesn't (beat 2), CL02 the man in the arena stands still beside the bull's path and is ignored (hook picture, background). Both return in the answer.

## Beats (five-step structure; times estimated)

1. **HOOK (0–6 s):** the bullring: the man in the glittering suit swings the red cloth, the black bull charges the cloth and thunders past him while he stands still (CL02). "ทำไมวัวกระทิงพุ่งใส่ผ้าแดง… ทั้งที่ในตามัน ผ้าผืนนั้นไม่ได้แดงเลย"
2. **QUESTION + DELAY (6–17 s):** Question: "หรือมันเกลียดสีแดงจริง ๆ?" Delay: grandpa's farm; the boy in a red shirt spots the bull and freezes stiff ("ซวยละ"). The bull looks at him and keeps chewing grass. A plastic bag snagged on the fence flaps in the wind; the bull's head snaps toward it and it trots over (CL01). "ใส่เสื้อแดงยืนนิ่ง ๆ วัวไม่สนเลย… แต่ถุงพลาสติกสะบัดนิดเดียว หันขวับ" The guess fails on screen.
3. **REHOOK (17–24 s):** the arena again, now in the bull's colours: the red cloth goes dull and muddy, close to the sand. "แต่ ที่จริงในตาวัว สีแดงไม่ได้เด่นเลยนะ มันแยกแดงออกจากสีหม่น ๆ แทบไม่ได้" Pays the hook's claim at ~40 % (~5 s) and the question turns: then what is it charging?
4. **QUESTION + DELAY #2 (24–35 s):** Question: "แล้วมันพุ่งใส่อะไรกันแน่?" Delay: in a ranch pen (no arena, no crowd), two cloths: a red one hanging still on a post, a blue one swung by a handler behind the fence. The bull lowers its head, paws the ground… "ผ้าแดงห้อยนิ่ง ๆ ผืนนึง… ผ้าฟ้าสะบัดอีกผืน… ทายสิ มันเลือกผืนไหน" (hold ~1 s before it charges the blue).
5. **ANSWER (35–50 s):** it charges the blue cloth. "มันพุ่งใส่ของที่ขยับต่างหาก ผ้าที่สะบัดไปมาตรงหน้า คือเป้าที่ชัดที่สุด สีอะไรก็ได้" (~6 s). Back on the farm the boy relaxes in his red shirt; grandpa ties the bag down. Flip over the arena crowd cheering at the red cloth: "สีแดงไม่ได้มีไว้หลอกวัว… มันมีไว้หลอกคนดูทั้งสนาม" ~2 s hold on the boy's face as he looks down at his red shirt.

- **Ending:** closed on the flip, ~2 s hold. No tip (no "เจอวัวอย่าวิ่งนะ").
- **Voice / tone:** Kore, warm smiling storyteller, teasing ("นะ", "ต่างหาก", "ทายสิ"). The boy: a whispered "ซวยละ", a relieved breath out, a small laugh at the end. Grandpa: one chuckle, no line.
- **Why it holds:** the myth comes from cartoons every Thai viewer saw; the plastic-bag moment is a farm truth; the flip turns the viewer into the one who was fooled, so it gets sent to a friend.

## Checks for Boat

- Cattle colour vision: two cone types, no separate red-sensitive cone, so red is not distinct from other dull colours: true one-line wording for "แยกแดงออกจากสีหม่น ๆ แทบไม่ได้" and the hook's "ในตามัน ผ้าผืนนั้นไม่ได้แดงเลย" (is the hook too strong? keep one narrow hedge if needed, ER10).
- Bulls charge the moving cloth regardless of colour, and a still red cloth draws little: documented tests (e.g. televised or published trials) to support beat 4 as shown.
- Why the muleta is red: tradition and the crowd's eye (often also said: it hides blood). Is "มีไว้หลอกคนดู" a fair simplification? Boat may prefer "มีไว้ให้คนดู".
- Wording "วัวกระทิง": in Thai กระทิง strictly means the wild gaur; "วัวกระทิง" is the common word for a fighting bull. Keep for the title, or use "วัวตัวผู้"/"วัวในสนามสู้วัว" in narration?
- Farm beat: cattle react to a flapping plastic bag (movement startles/draws them): true as shown; the boy is never shown in danger.

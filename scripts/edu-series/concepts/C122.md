- Status **pitched**, round **R21** (Preaw, 2026-11-21). Category **CAT01 Science (HIGH)**, animal learning; topic named by the user, so no category pick. Exotic pet: African grey parrots given up by owners.
- Channel `edu`. One Thai Short, about 50–60 s estimated. Profile: true story told with attitude through an invented keeper ([ER11](../../../channels/edu/script.md#er11) source 1); on-screen label "แต่งเติมจากเรื่องจริง". Hook type **HK01** broken certainty.

- **Care:** the keeper is an invented type with no name tied to the real park staff; real staff get no invented line. Every swear word is a bleep with a censor bar over the beak; no Thai or English profanity is spoken or written.

## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Workplace comedy. A UK wildlife park (lead: Lincolnshire Wildlife Park, 2020) took in five grey parrots from different owners in the same week, kept them together in quarantine, and they taught each other to swear (lead). The keeper wants a family-friendly park.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 3D camera (project default): one aviary, one keeper, a line of visiting families; bleeps and black censor bars as the running visual gag.
- **Logline** — พี่เลี้ยงสวนสัตว์อยากได้สวนสัตว์ครอบครัว แต่นกแก้วห้าตัวที่เพิ่งรับมา ด่าเก่งกว่าคนขับแท็กซี่ แล้วยิ่งคนหัวเราะ มันยิ่งด่า
- **Opening line** — "สวนสัตว์ที่นี่ต้องแยกนกแก้วออกจากกัน… เพราะมันสอนกันด่าคน"
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — Laughing at a swearing parrot is how you train it to swear more.

## Beats

1. **B01** — Five parrots arrive in one week, all in one quarantine room (lead). Keeper: "ห้าตัวอยู่ด้วยกัน จะได้มีเพื่อน". Deadpan: "ได้เพื่อนจริง… เพื่อนร่วมด่า".
2. **B02** — Opening day. A family walks up. Parrot: "สวัสดี… [บี๊บ]". Visual gag: censor bars getting longer than the parrot. Rule of three: a grandma, a kid, a school headteacher on a field trip; all get bleeped.
3. **B03** — But worse: the visitors laugh, and the parrots swear more (lead). The wow fact as a punchline: the laugh is the reward. Explanation in one breath: "นกแก้วพูดคำไหนแล้วได้เสียงตอบรับ มันจะพูดคำนั้นอีก คนหัวเราะ = ขนม".
4. **B04** — The keeper splits them into separate aviaries (lead). Character line: "แยกกันแล้วนะ ทำตัวดี ๆ". Today comparison: "เหมือนครูจับเด็กหลังห้องแยกโต๊ะ".
5. **B05** — Later the park reportedly gets more swearing parrots and tries the opposite: mix them into a big polite flock of about a hundred, hoping the good words win (lead: 2023 report). Keeper: "เสียงข้างมากต้องชนะสิ".

- **Ending** — Irony punchline: the narrator asks the one question: which way does it spread? Cut to the hundred-parrot flock, silent for a beat, then one polite parrot opens its beak: "[บี๊บ]". Hold 2 s on the keeper's face. (The ending is a joke; Boat confirms what the reports say actually happened, and the narration does not claim an outcome the sources lack.)
- **Voice and tone** — Kore as a friend telling office gossip about the worst new hires; the keeper hopeful then exhausted; parrots in a gravelly sing-song.
- **Why it holds** — A swearing animal in a family place is an instant laugh, and the bleep gag repeats for free. The reinforcement fact flips the viewer into the culprit. Share trigger: "ส่งให้เพื่อนที่สอนนกพูดคำหยาบ".
- **Research load for Boat** — Named real case plus general knowledge. Leads: Lincolnshire Wildlife Park, 2020 (five African greys, names, separation); the later report of mixing swearing parrots into a larger flock and its result; whether laughter reinforcing parrot speech is supported (vocal learning, social reward); grey parrots' lifespan or vocabulary as a possible second fact.
- **Sound intent (proposed)** — Bright park ukulele and glockenspiel, interrupted by each bleep (a clean 1 kHz tone, always the same so it becomes a callback); family laughter ambience that swells on B03; a "class dismissed" bell at B04; dead silence for 0.7 s before the final bleep, then the series closing sound.

# C115 — อยากให้คนเกลียดเลิกเกลียด ให้ไปขอยืมของเขา (CAT04)

- Status **pitched**, round **R20** (Preaw, 2026-11-21). Category **CAT04 Psychology (GOD)**, named in the user's prompt.
- Channel `edu`. One Thai Short, about 50–60 s estimated. Profile: invented office story carrying one real effect, with a real historical person as the callback ([ER11](../../../channels/edu/script.md#er11) source 2); on-screen label “เรื่องสมมุติ”. Hook type **HK01** broken certainty.

- **CAT04 care:** the effect is a brain glitch the story laughs at, not a manipulation tactic; nothing is framed as "use this on people". The evidence is modest (a small 1969 study and later replications, lead), so the fact line keeps one narrow hedge ("มัก").

## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Office sitcom. June (จูน), first month at work, sits next to Pi Lek (พี่เล็ก), the grumpiest senior in the building. She tries every way to make him like her, and each one backfires, until she gives up and asks to borrow his stapler.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 3D camera in an open-plan Thai office; one cutaway to an 18th-century Pennsylvania assembly room for the callback.
- **Logline** — พนักงานใหม่ทำทุกอย่างให้รุ่นพี่ขี้หงุดหงิดชอบ ทั้งกาแฟ ทั้งคำชม ทั้งของฝาก ยิ่งทำยิ่งพัง จนวันที่เธอหมดแรงแล้วขอยืมแม็กเย็บกระดาษ… เขากลับยิ้มให้
- **Opening line** — “อยากให้คนที่เกลียดคุณ เลิกเกลียด… อย่าไปช่วยเขา ให้เขาช่วยคุณ”
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — We like people more after we do them a favour, not after they do us one.

## Beats

1. **B01** — Coffee: she brings Pi Lek a latte. He drinks only black. Character line (Pi Lek): “ใส่นมมาทำไม ผมแพ้”.
2. **B02** — But worse: compliments. “เสื้อพี่สวยจัง” makes him suspicious; he checks his collar for a stain. Deadpan: “ระดับความเกลียดขึ้นจากสามเป็นห้า”.
3. **B03** — Then a souvenir from her trip: he thinks she wants something. Rule of three: coffee, compliment, gift… all three make it worse.
4. **B04** — The turn: her stapler breaks, and too tired to care she asks to borrow his. He lends it, grumbling. Next day he says good morning (the effect as the punchline, lead: people who do someone a favour tend to like them more; explanation in one breath: the brain tells itself “ช่วยเขาไปแล้ว แปลว่าเราต้องชอบเขาแหละ”).
5. **B05** — Callback to the source: Benjamin Franklin did the same with a hostile fellow legislator by asking to borrow a rare book (lead: his autobiography). Today comparison: “ยิ่งใหญ่ระดับพ่อผู้ก่อตั้งอเมริกา ยังใช้วิธีเดียวกับยืมแม็กเย็บกระดาษ”.

- **Ending** — The flip turns on her: Pi Lek asks to borrow her charger, she lends it… and now she cannot stop liking him. “ระวังนะ สมองคุณเองก็โดนแบบเดียวกัน” Last shot: the two swapping pens, best friends, a new hire staring at them.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore gossiping about the office, siding with June; Pi Lek in a dry, sulky voice that slowly warms.
- **Why it holds** — HK01 contradicts the obvious way to win someone over, and everyone has a Pi Lek. Share trigger: “ส่งให้เพื่อนที่มีหัวหน้าแบบนี้”.
- **Research load for Boat** — General knowledge plus a named historical case. Leads: Franklin's autobiography passage (the rival, the book, the outcome, the year); Jecker and Landy (1969) and later replications, effect size and limits; the plain cause (cognitive dissonance or self-perception) in one sentence. Story lines are invented and checked only for ER11 limits.
- **Sound intent (proposed)** — Light office-sitcom pizzicato; each failed attempt ends on a small deflating horn; one clean stapler click and a warm chord at the turn in B04; the theme loops back at the ending as June falls for the same trick.

# C120 — หมาทำหน้ารู้สึกผิด ทั้งที่แมวเป็นคนทำ (CAT04)

- Status **pitched**, round **R21** (Preaw, 2026-11-21). Category **CAT04 Psychology (GOD)**; topic named by the user, so no category pick. The psychology is the owner's misreading of a dog's face.
- Channel `edu`. One Thai Short, about 50–60 s estimated. Profile: absurd domestic story built on a real study ([ER11](../../../channels/edu/script.md#er11) source 2); on-screen label "เรื่องสมมุติ". Hook type **HK01** broken certainty.
- Not checked: every fact below is a pitch lead for Boat, not research.
- **Angle check:** not C70 (the eyebrow muscle behind puppy eyes) and not C5 or C72. This is about what the "guilty look" actually responds to.

## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Domestic whodunit comedy. Khaotu (ข้าวตู), a golden dog who only wants to be a good boy, keeps getting blamed for every crime in the house, because every time the owner comes home he makes the guilty face. The cat sits beside him, spotless.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 3D camera (project default) in one living room, with a crime-scene parody: chalk outline round a torn cushion, evidence tags, a courtroom-style close-up on each face.
- **Logline** — หมอนขาด หมาทำหน้าผิด ปลาหาย หมาทำหน้าผิด แจกันแตก หมาทำหน้าผิด ทั้งที่มันไม่ได้ทำสักอย่าง
- **Opening line** — "หน้ารู้สึกผิดของหมาเนี่ย… ไม่ได้แปลว่ามันทำผิดนะ"
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — The guiltiest-looking dog in the room can be the innocent one.

## Beats

1. **B01** — Crime one: torn cushion. Owner: "ใครทำ!" Khaotu: ears down, eyes sideways, tail tucked. Owner: "เห็นมั้ย หน้าฟ้องเลย". The cat licks its paw. Deadpan: "ซึ่งข้าวตูตอนนั้น… นอนอยู่อีกห้อง".
2. **B02** — The wow fact as a punchline: in a study, owners were told their dog ate a forbidden treat; the dogs looked guiltiest when scolded, whether they had eaten it or not, and innocent dogs that were scolded looked guiltiest of all (lead: Horowitz 2009). "หน้านั้นไม่ใช่ 'ผมผิด' แต่คือ 'อย่าดุผมเลย'".
3. **B03** — But worse: crime two, the fish from the counter. Rule of three on the faces: owner angry, dog guilty, cat smug. Character line, Khaotu's inner voice: "ผมยังไม่ได้ทำอะไรเลยนะ… แต่ขอโทษครับ".
4. **B04** — Worse still: crime three, the vase. Khaotu now makes the face the second the door opens, before anyone speaks. Today comparison: "เหมือนเราเดินผ่านตำรวจแล้วรู้สึกผิด ทั้งที่ไม่ได้ทำอะไร".
5. **B05** — The owner installs a camera. Second fact (lead): owners in another study could not tell from the greeting whether their dog had misbehaved (lead: Hecht et al. 2012).

- **Ending** — The camera reveal: the cat, all three times, looking straight into the lens. Owner turns to scold the cat. The cat blinks, unbothered, and walks away. Narrator: "ส่วนแมว… ไม่ทำหน้ารู้สึกผิด เพราะมันไม่รู้สึกจริง ๆ" (joke, not-factual). Hold 2 s on Khaotu's tail slowly starting to wag.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore as the friend who takes the dog's side loudly; the owner sharp, Khaotu's inner voice small and polite.
- **Why it holds** — Every dog owner has done B01, so the fact lands on the viewer, not on a stranger. Share trigger: "ส่งให้คนที่ชอบดุหมา" and cat-vs-dog comments.
- **Research load for Boat** — General knowledge from two named studies. Leads: Horowitz 2009 (Behavioural Processes, "guilty look" and owner scolding); Hecht, Miklósi and Gácsi 2012 (owners judging misbehaviour from greetings); whether later work changes the bold wording; what the guilty-look behaviours are (ears back, gaze aversion, tail low). Cat lines are jokes.
- **Sound intent (proposed)** — Detective-noir piano and brushed snare; a "dun-dun" sting on each guilty face, smaller each time until it is just a sad kazoo; silence for 0.5 s before the camera reveal, then a record scratch; a soft happy tail-thump under the hold.

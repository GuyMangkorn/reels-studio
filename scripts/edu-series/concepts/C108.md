# C108 — เทพเจ้าสายฟ้าในชุดเจ้าสาว (wild card) (CAT06)

- Status **pitched**, round **R19** (Preaw, 2026-11-19). Category **CAT06 Mythology (HIGH)**, picked by the user. **Wild card** of the round: the medium below needs a capability the kit and project defaults do not have.
- Channel `edu`. One Thai Short, about 50–60 s estimated. Profile: the myth is the story (CAT06 ER11 note), told with attitude; the fact is what the real world kept from it. On-screen label "ตำนานนอร์ส". Hook type **HK07** start at the climax.
- Not checked: every fact below is a pitch lead for Boat, not research.

## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Myth wedding farce. In the Norse poem *Þrymskviða*, the giant Thrym steals Thor's hammer and will return it only if the goddess Freyja marries him. Freyja says no. So Thor goes to the wedding as the bride.
- **Proposed medium (wild card)** — An animated embroidered tapestry: flat side-profile figures in stitched wool on linen, the camera scrolling along one long hanging like the Bayeux Tapestry, threads that wobble when figures move. Needs a stitched-textile look and a scrolling-tapestry layout the painted kit does not have.
- **Logline** — เทพเจ้าที่แกร่งที่สุดในตำนานอยากได้ค้อนคืน แต่ต้องปลอมเป็นเจ้าสาวไปแต่งงานกับยักษ์ และความหิวของตัวเองเกือบทำให้แผนพังทั้งงาน
- **Opening line** — “เทพเจ้าสายฟ้าที่แกร่งที่สุดในตำนานนอร์ส กำลังนั่งอยู่ในชุดเจ้าสาว… และเพิ่งกินวัวหมดไปทั้งตัว”
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — The toughest god of the Vikings' own stories wins his most famous fight in a wedding veil.

## Beats

1. **B01** — Climax first: a huge bride behind a veil, an empty ox carcass on the plate, the giant groom staring. “ย้อนไปสามวันก่อน”.
2. **B02** — Thor wakes up: hammer gone. Thrym's price: Freyja as his bride. Freyja is so furious her famous necklace bursts (visual gag).
3. **B03** — The gods' plan: put Thor in the dress, Loki goes as the bridesmaid. Thor: “ข้าไม่ใส่!” (character line). Deadpan: “ซึ่งก็ไม่มีใครถามความเห็นเขาอยู่ดี”.
4. **B04** — The feast, rule of three: a whole ox, eight salmon, then three barrels of mead. Thrym: “เจ้าสาวกินเก่งจัง” Loki: “นางอดอาหารมาแปดคืน เพราะคิดถึงท่าน” (character lines). Thrym lifts the veil, sees burning eyes; Loki again: “นางไม่ได้นอนมาแปดคืนเหมือนกัน” (callback).
5. **B05** — The hammer is laid in the bride's lap to bless the marriage. Deadpan, no gore: “งานแต่งงานจบเร็วกว่ากำหนด”.

- **Ending** — Flip into the real world: “และทุกวันนี้ วันหนึ่งในสัปดาห์ของภาษาอังกฤษ ยังตั้งชื่อตามเจ้าสาวคนนี้… Thursday วันของธอร์”. Possible tag for Thai viewers (lead): Thursday and วันพฤหัสบดี both trace to the same week of planets, Jupiter in Thai and Thor in English.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore as a friend retelling the best wedding disaster she ever heard; Thor gruff and sulking, Loki smooth and quick, Thrym dopey and in love.
- **Why it holds** — HK07 opens on the strangest frame of the story, so the viewer stays for "why is the thunder god in a dress". Every beat raises the risk of being caught. Share trigger: “Thursday มาจากเทพที่เคยใส่ชุดเจ้าสาว”.
- **Research load for Boat** — General knowledge plus one primary text. Leads: the *Þrymskviða* plot points (the necklace bursting, Heimdall's idea, Thor's objection, the ox, eight salmon, three casks of mead, Loki's two excuses, the hammer laid in the lap); that the poem survives in the Codex Regius manuscript (about 13th century); the Old English *Þunresdæg* → Thursday etymology; the claimed link between the Roman *dies Iovis*, Thor and the Thai วันพฤหัสบดี. Dialogue is played as jokes and paraphrases the poem.

## ER08 pitch check — 6/6

1. **Hook** — Breaks "the strongest god is a warrior, not a bride": he is sitting in a veil after eating an ox. Clear in about 3 s.
2. **Victim** — Thor; wants his hammer back, and every step to get it humiliates him more.
3. **Three bits** — (a) Character line: Thor's “ข้าไม่ใส่!” right before we see him in the dress; (b) rule of three: an ox, eight salmon, three barrels of mead; (c) callback: Loki's second "eight nights" excuse.
4. **Ending** — Thursday is still named after this bride.
5. **Wow fact** — **วัน Thursday ในภาษาอังกฤษ ตั้งชื่อตามธอร์ เทพเจ้าสายฟ้าองค์นี้**; second (lead): the poem is written down in a real medieval Icelandic manuscript that still exists.
6. **Category** — CAT06 Mythology.

## Film annotation (W0.2)

- **Visual approach (proposed, wild card)** — Animated embroidered hanging: wool stitches on unbleached linen, flat side-profile figures in four or five dyed-wool colours, a stitched border band top and bottom, the camera tracking sideways along one long strip as the Bayeux Tapestry is read. In 9:16 the strip runs as a horizontal band that fills the middle of the frame with linen above and below (subtitles and label sit on the plain linen), or the hanging is turned to scroll vertically; the band version keeps the reference recognisable. Figures move in small stitched steps (frame holds on twos or threes) with threads that wobble at the edges.
- **Buildability** — **`missing capability` in this checkout.** No stitched-textile renderer exists: the kit painter draws washes, flat fills and ink (`painter.ts`, `draw.ts`), and nothing produces satin/stem-stitch strokes, wool texture or a linen weave ground (checked `src/painted/kit/`, grep for stitch/tapestry/embroider: no hits). What it would take: (1) a stitch painter that fills a polygon with directional thread strokes along its contour plus a stem-stitch outline, seeded so it is stable per frame; (2) a linen ground and stitched border; (3) a long-strip layout with a sideways camera track (the kit `Camera` can pan a wide canvas; untested at strip length); (4) a profile figure style for Thor, Loki, Thrym, Freyja and the gods, ideally the kit rig's profile cards run through the stitch painter (unknown whether they read as embroidery; `svg-painter.tsx` could carry SVG pattern fills, untested). It is a look-defining asset: a still spike for user approval comes before any commitment. Story assets beyond the look: `new: Norse costumes (bride dress and veil, bridesmaid), new: giant Thrym, new: hammer, new: ox carcass, salmon, mead casks, new: Freyja's necklace bursting`. Fallback: the default painted 3D camera with a tapestry-framed title and end card only. **Size L** (new renderer plus a full new cast; the largest of the round).

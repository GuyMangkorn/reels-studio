## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre:** myth-buster ranking ladder with a twist ending (animals; fiction-free, real general knowledge).
- **Medium:** painted 3D camera (project default): an African river at dawn, a dirt path by a Thai rice field, a Thai bedroom at night.
- **Our cast:** one Thai woman in her twenties (four-head cast), a traveller who is proud she survived a hippo trip, then comes home to Thailand. She meets every problem on screen herself (ER02).
- **Logline:** She paddles past a yawning hippo, sure she just escaped the deadliest animal on Earth. Each rung of the real ranking is smaller, quieter and closer to home than the last, until the animal that kills the most people turns out to be the one buzzing by her ear in bed.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** the hippo, which the internet calls the world's deadliest animal, is nowhere near the top; the animals that kill the most people are the small ones we meet every day (F028: hippo sits hundreds to thousands of times below mosquito-borne disease, snakes and dogs; F020–F022).
- **Rule that flips (ER03):** "ที่ฆ่าคนเยอะ ไม่ใช่ตัวที่ดุที่สุด… แต่เป็นตัวที่เจอคนบ่อยที่สุด". Early it explains the hippo (it shares the river people use every day). The flip: the animal we meet most often is the one we don't even fear, so the size and fierceness we watch for is exactly the wrong signal.
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าคุณคิดว่าฮิปโป คือสัตว์ที่ฆ่าคนเยอะที่สุดในโลก… มันไม่ติดแม้แต่สามอันดับแรกนะ" (F028: excluding humans, mosquito, snake and dog are the top three; alternative Boat allows: "ตัวจริงฆ่าเยอะกว่ามัน หลายร้อยเท่า").
- **Objection by ~7 s:** viewer: "แต่ฮิปโปดุมากนะ" → narrator: "ดุจริง… แต่ดุ ไม่ได้แปลว่าฆ่าเยอะ"
- **Twist clue planted (fair twist):** CL01 a single mosquito circles her head in the canoe in beat 1 and she slaps at it without looking (background, a burden); CL02 the rule itself, spoken at beat 2. Both return in the flip.
- **Beats (each opens the next with "แต่…"; times estimated):**
  1. **Hook (0–7 s):** dawn, dugout canoe; a hippo surfaces and yawns huge beside her; she freezes, then paddles off grinning, filming a selfie "รอดแล้ว!". CL01 mosquito buzzes, she swats.
  2. **Problem 1 (~7–17 s):** "แต่ ฮิปโปกินหญ้า… ทำไมถึงฆ่าคนได้?" The hippo charges and stops short of the canoe; she backpaddles. Answer (one cause): it doesn't hunt people; most attacks happen on or at the edge of its water, where people paddle and set nets every day (F046 herbivore, F040 water and canoes; "guards its water" only as "ในน้ำ", F042 reported). Rule stated (CL02): "ฆ่าคนเยอะ ไม่ใช่เพราะดุที่สุด… แต่เพราะเจอคนบ่อยที่สุด". No hippo death number is spoken (F002, F014, F027: not measured).
  3. **Problem 2 (~17–27 s):** "แต่ ในแม่น้ำเดียวกันนี้ มีตัวที่ฆ่าคนเยอะกว่ามันอีก" Two eyes and a log by the bank where people fetch water; she lifts her paddle out of the water slowly. Answer: it waits right where people come to the water's edge every day. Hook payoff at ~30 %: "ตัวเท่ารถ… ยังแพ้ตัวที่นอนนิ่งเหมือนขอนไม้" (F015, F030: crocodiles and elephants kill more than hippos in the Zimbabwe, Mozambique and Zambia records; no crocodile number is spoken, F024 is unreconciled. Boat checks that "ในแม่น้ำเดียวกัน มีตัวที่ฆ่าคนเยอะกว่า" is a fair claim from three countries, not a source phrase; if not, swap in the dog rung below.)
  4. **Problem 3 (~27–38 s):** "แต่ กลับมาบ้าน ก็ยังไม่จบ" She walks a dirt path by her grandmother's rice field; a snake lies across it a step ahead; she stops mid-step. Answer: farmers walk past it every day, often far from a hospital. Refrain: "เจอบ่อยที่สุด". (F021: WHO 81,410–137,880 a year, spoken as "ราว ๆ แสนคนต่อปี" or as a comparison; "farmers walk past every day, far from a hospital" is `unchecked`, Boat.)
  5. **Problem 4 (~38–48 s):** "แต่ ตัวที่ฆ่าคนเยอะที่สุด… ไม่ต้องสู้กับใครเลย" Night, her bedroom, fan on, she is finally relaxed under the blanket. CL01 returns: the same thin buzz. Answer (one cause): it carries a germ from one person's blood to the next, every night, everywhere ("ยุงนำโรค", not "ยุงกัดตาย", per Boat's gap 8; F020: hundreds of thousands a year, WHO ~610,000 malaria deaths in 2024).
  6. **Payoff flip (~48–55 s):** she freezes, eyes open, as the buzz passes her ear; the camera holds. "สัตว์ที่ฆ่าคนเยอะที่สุดในโลก… ไม่ได้อยู่ในแม่น้ำ มันอยู่ข้างหูคุณ" ~2 s hold.
- **Ending:** closed on the flip, ~2 s hold on her open eyes in the dark; `_A_fade`. Not a tip (no "ใช้มุ้งนะ"), no call to action (ER03).
- **Voice / tone:** channel default narrator **Kore**, warm smiling storyteller, teasing her confidence ("รอดแล้วจริงเหรอ…", "นะ", "หรอก"). She reacts with small sounds: a gasp at the yawn, "เฮ้ย!" at the charge, a held breath at the snake, a tiny whimper at the buzz.
- **Why it holds:** the hook attacks a belief the viewer probably shared last week (the "hippo is deadliest" meme); each rung is smaller, quieter and closer to the viewer's own home (Africa → Thai rice field → your bedroom), so the stakes rise as the animals shrink; the planted mosquito makes the last beat feel inevitable on a rewatch; the ending moves the danger into the viewer's room tonight.
- **Twist depends on research (mapped to R1):**
  - The shape (big scary animal → small everyday animal) is supported: F020–F022 and F028 put mosquito-borne disease, snakes and dogs hundreds to thousands of times above the hippo.
  - Boat's angle 1 ("the famous 500 has no data behind it", F001–F005, F016) is the most honest finding but is **not** the twist here: narrating "nobody really counted" is an "evidence unclear" beat (ER07) and a source/evidence-quality phrase (ER10). Accuracy is kept by never speaking a hippo number. If the user wants that angle as the twist, it needs a rule decision first (open question below).
  - Angle 4 ("when it attacks it often kills", F013, F031) is hypothesis-level and site-specific; not used (ER07).
  - Optional rung: dogs through rabies (F022, WHO ~44,000 a year, solid). Default is out: dogs are the subject of C70–C72 in the same round and the river continuity reads better with the crocodile. It becomes the default if Boat finds the crocodile claim too narrow (three countries, F015); as rung 3 it also fills the "third place" the hook names.
  - Humans are left out of the ladder (F023; the brief is animals).
  - Cut by Boat's ER07 list and kept out: calves, bite force, Fenner origin, Lewison or Templer quotes, hippo-vs-lion, "deadliest large land mammal", crocodile 1,000.
- **Research load:** medium, mostly done in Boat's R1 packet; open items are listed under "Checks for Boat".
- **Length risk (ER09/ER02):** four problems in ~55 s. Numbers are spoken only as orders of magnitude or comparisons ("หลายร้อยเท่า", "ราว ๆ แสนคน"), never a hippo figure.

## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Revenge comedy from inside a fish bowl. Thongyip (ทองหยิบ), a goldfish, lives with a teenager who treats her badly "because she forgets in three seconds anyway". She does not forget. She remembers everything.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 3D camera (project default), mostly from inside the round bowl: the room bent by the glass (a fish-eye warp), the teenager's giant face looming in.
- **Logline** — เจ้าของคิดว่าปลาทองจำได้สามวิ เลยสัญญาอะไรไว้ก็ไม่ทำ แต่ทองหยิบจำได้ทุกอย่าง ทุกวัน ทุกคำ
- **Opening line** — "ปลาทองความจำสามวินาที… ปลาทองตัวนี้ได้ยินแล้ว และจำได้"
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — The one in the room with the three-second memory is not the fish.

## Beats

1. **B01** — The teenager taps the glass: "เดี๋ยวให้ข้าวนะ… เอ๊ะ ช่างเหอะ เดี๋ยวมันก็ลืม". Thongyip, flat stare: "สามวิผ่านไปแล้ว ยังจำได้นะ". Deadpan: "และจะจำต่อไปอีกนาน".
2. **B02** — The wow fact as a punchline: goldfish remember for months, not seconds; they can be trained to come to a sound or a light at feeding time (lead). Visual gag: Thongyip's "memory wall" of grudges inside a castle ornament, like a detective board.
3. **B03** — But worse: the teenager forgets to feed her for a weekend, then moves the bowl next to the TV. Rule of three: no food, loud TV, a cat moving in. Character line: "จดไว้แล้ว ทั้งสามข้อ".
4. **B04** — Second fact (lead): a round bowl is a poor home; goldfish grow far bigger than people think, and some cities banned round bowls (lead: Monza or Rome, 2000s). Today comparison: "เหมือนให้คนอยู่ห้องน้ำทั้งชีวิต แล้วบอกว่าเขาไม่สังเกตหรอก".
5. **B05** — The biggest wow fact: scientists built a small robotic tank and taught goldfish to drive it across a room to a target for food, even from new starting points (lead: Ben-Gurion University, 2022). Thongyip watches this on the TV next to her bowl. "อ้อ… ทำได้ด้วยเหรอ".

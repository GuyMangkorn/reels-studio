## Pitch (Preaw)


- **Sub-genre:** drop physics and air drag (terminal speed; the real shape of a raindrop).
- **Staging:** `girl-school + school` under a Bangkok bus-stop awning in a downpour; the camera follows one drop down from a cloud with a speed tag beside it; a split moment shows the same drop in a world without air.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** a raindrop falls from about two kilometres up, yet lands about as fast as a bicycle, and it is not shaped like a tear.
- **Logline:** Without air, a drop falling from a rain cloud would hit at hundreds of kilometres an hour and every storm would shred roofs and skin. Air pushes back harder the faster the drop falls, so it stops speeding up at about the pace of a bicycle. And the air sets a size limit too: a big drop flattens like a burger bun, puffs up like a bag and bursts into small ones, so a ball-sized raindrop can never happen.
- **Hook (Thai):** "เม็ดฝนตกมาจากสูงสองกิโล… ถ้าไม่มีอากาศ มันจะโดนหัวคุณเร็วกว่ารถไฟความเร็วสูงสองเท่า"
- **Hook claim status:** `unchecked`, `model` (free fall from 2,000 m without drag is about 200 m/s, about 700 km/h; high-speed train cruise about 300–350 km/h; Boat recomputes, checks typical rain-cloud base height and picks the comparison).
- **Rule that flips (ER03):** "ยิ่งตกเร็ว… อากาศยิ่งดันกลับแรง" (the faster it falls, the harder the air pushes back). Beats 1–3 it is the brake; beat 4 the same push tears big drops apart; beat 5 it is why rain is gentle on your hand.
- **Key reveal image:** two drops side by side on a speed gauge: the no-air drop's needle past 700, the real drop's needle stuck near 30, the girl's open hand under the real one.
- **Beats:**
  1. No air: the drop leaves the cloud and keeps speeding up; it punches through a leaf and dents a car roof like a pellet. `unchecked`, `model`
  2. แต่… there is air: as the drop speeds up, the push grows until it matches the drop's weight, and the needle stops climbing, near 30 km/h. `unchecked`, `settled` (Boat sources the terminal speed of the largest drops, about 9 m/s)
  3. แต่… then why not a giant drop in a big storm, the size of a ball? `unchecked` (the question beat)
  4. แต่… the air flattens a big drop into a bun, not a tear; it puffs up like a bag and bursts into small drops, so there is a size limit, about half a centimetre. `unchecked`, `settled` (Boat sources drop shape and break-up size)
  5. Flip and payoff: the girl puts her hand out; drops pat it. Last line: "ทุกเม็ดที่ตกใส่มือคุณ… ถูกเบรกมาตลอดทาง โดยสิ่งที่คุณมองไม่เห็นเลย" `unchecked`, `settled`
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold on her hand in the rain.
- **Voice / tone:** Kore, brisk deadpan; the girl one "โอ๊ย!" in the no-air beat and a small laugh at the end.
- **ER08 pitch check:** (1) Hook: breaks "rain is gentle"; one picture (a drop as a bullet), no term. Pass. (2) Who: everyone in a Thai rainy season. Pass. (3) Beats: no air → แต่ air → แต่ why no giant drop → แต่ the bun bursts; beat 2's mechanism is the speed needle stalling, not a term block ("terminal velocity" never said). Pass. (4) Ending: a payoff that flips "air is nothing". Pass. **4/4.**
- **Why it holds:** EP05's scale-what-if shape with stakes in the first second, on the most common weather a Thai viewer lives with; the tear-shape myth is a free second surprise.
- **Evidence notes (thin spots):** textbook physics; numbers are calculations Boat redoes. Drop break-up size and the bun shape need one sourced paper or textbook figure. Kept out: hail (a different story) and "rain falling from aircraft height" exaggerations.
- **Research load:** general physics plus two sourced figures (terminal speed of large drops; maximum drop size and shape).
- **Kit check:** `girl-school + school, P?, neutral` then `shock`, then a smile; ENV-FALL `rain` exists for the downpour. New: bus-stop awning set, single-drop macro with bun and burst stages, cloud-to-ground drop follow, speed-gauge screen layer, leaf and car-roof dent insert.
- **Sound intent:** steady rain on a tin awning and distant traffic; the no-air beat drops the rain bed to silence and gives each drop a sharp pellet hit; a low riser as the needle climbs, a soft brake whoosh when it stalls; small pops for the bursting drop; soft rain and a light pizzicato tag under the last line.

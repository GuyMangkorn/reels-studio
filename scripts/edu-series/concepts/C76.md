## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Subject:** physics / electricity (with birds as the characters, not an animal-biology topic).
- **Genre:** everyday-physics puzzle with a chilling flip onto the viewer.
- **Medium:** painted 3D camera (project default) for a Thai soi with a tangle of power lines; the signature picture is a painted "current view": the wire glows with flowing light that ignores the bird's body, then floods through a big bird the instant its wing touches a second wire.
- **Our cast:** a Thai teenage boy (four-head cast) waiting at a soi bus stop, watching a row of sparrows on the line; a big crow arrives for the rehook. He meets each problem on screen (ER02).
- **Logline:** Sparrows sit on a wire that could kill a person in an instant. Their feet aren't insulated; even wet they're fine. But a big bird spreads its wings onto a second wire and the whole soi goes dark. Electricity doesn't hurt what touches it, only what it can flow through.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** a bird on a live wire is safe not because its feet insulate it, but because both feet are on the same wire, so the current has no reason to go through its body; touch a second wire or a pole and it becomes the path (`unchecked`: potential difference; birds and squirrels as a common cause of outages).
- **Rule that flips (ER03):** "ไฟไม่ได้ทำร้ายสิ่งที่แตะมัน… มันทำร้ายสิ่งที่เป็นทางให้มันผ่าน". First it protects the sparrow; the flip turns it on the viewer: the bird is safe because it never touches the ground, and a person always does.
- **Twist clue planted:** CL01 the boy's feet on the wet pavement (background in beat 1), CL02 every sparrow sits on one wire, none spans two (beat 4). CL01 returns in the flip.

## Checks for Boat

- Same-wire safety: both feet at (nearly) the same voltage, so negligible current through the body: true one-line wording without the term "ศักย์".
- Large birds touching two conductors or a conductor and a grounded part, causing faults and outages: true and common in Thailand?
- "ทำให้คนตายได้ในพริบตา": strong but true for distribution lines? Keep or hedge with one narrow word.
- Squirrels on lines: fine on one wire; check the line is plausible for a Thai soi.
- Flip line: "a person standing on the ground becomes the path" is true as a simplification; confirm it does not imply touching a single insulated low-voltage cable is deadly in every case (keep the picture at a distance).

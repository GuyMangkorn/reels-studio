## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre:** local deep time ("your province, 130 million years ago").
- **Our cast:** a family running a noodle shop in a Khon Kaen / Khorat housing estate; the shop, its plastic stools and the family's dog land in Early Cretaceous Isan.
- **Logline:** Drop a Thai street into the dinosaur age and it does not meet American movie dinosaurs; it meets its own, with Thai names. Each episode introduces one neighbour from Isan's real fossils.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** the dinosaurs next to a Thai house are not T. rex and friends but Thai species, named after a Thai place, a Thai princess and a creature from Thai legend.
- **Structure: 2 (hook → question/delay #1 → rehook → question/delay #2 → answer).** Why: "who is this neighbour?" is a guessing game, and the name reveal works as the final answer after two wrong guesses; a rule opening has no rule to give here.

### EP1 — เพื่อนบ้านคอยาว ที่มีชื่อไทย
- **Single question / answer:** Who is the giant outside the shop? A long-necked plant-eater found in Phu Wiang and named after Phu Wiang and Princess Sirindhorn (Phuwiangosaurus sirindhornae).
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าบ้านคุณอยู่ขอนแก่น แล้วหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์… เพื่อนบ้านคนแรกของคุณ ยาวกว่ารถเมล์"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: a neck rises over the shop's tin roof; the dog barks once and hides.
  2. Question #1: a brontosaurus like in the books? Delay: the son flips through a dinosaur picture book; none match.
  3. Rehook: "แต่ ตัวนี้ไม่มีในหนังสือฝรั่งเล่มไหน… เพราะมันเจอที่นี่"
  4. Question #2: where, and what is it called? Answer (one cause): its bones were dug up in Phu Wiang, so it carries the place's name and the Princess's. `unchecked`
  5. Payoff flip: "ไดโนเสาร์ตัวแรกที่คุณเจอ… เกิดก่อนประเทศไทยร้อยกว่าล้านปี แต่มีชื่อไทย"
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold; `_A_fade`.

### EP2 — ไดโนเสาร์ชื่อกินรี
- **Single question / answer:** What is the fast, bird-like runner stealing from the shop? A small ostrich-like dinosaur named after the กินรี of Thai legend (Kinnareemimus).
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าคุณหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์ที่อีสาน… คุณอาจเจอไดโนเสาร์ที่ชื่อ กินรี"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: something sprints past the stools and snatches a meatball off a bowl.
  2. Question #1: a bird? a lizard? Delay: the mother chases it; it outruns her easily.
  3. Rehook: "แต่ คนที่ตั้งชื่อมัน ไม่ได้คิดถึงนก… คิดถึงวรรณคดี"
  4. Answer (one cause): it looked half-bird, so it was named after กินรี, half-bird, half-woman. `unchecked` (Boat: naming reason, how well known the animal is; cut the episode if its status is too thin, ER07)
  5. Payoff flip: "ชื่อในตำนาน… ใกล้ความจริงกว่าที่ตั้งใจ" (dinosaurs and birds are close kin) `unchecked`
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold on the meatball thief by a mural-style กินรี silhouette; `_A_fade`.

### EP3 — นักล่าของอีสาน ไม่ใช่ทีเร็กซ์
- **Single question / answer:** Who is the big hunter of Isan? Not T. rex, which never lived here, but a Thai meat-eater named only a few years ago (Siamraptor suwati, candidate).
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าคุณหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์ที่อีสาน… ทีเร็กซ์ไม่มาหาคุณหรอก แต่มีอีกตัวมาแทน"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: heavy steps at night; the family switches off the shop lights.
  2. Question #1: T. rex? Delay: the son whispers "ทีเร็กซ์!"; the father shakes his head.
  3. Rehook: "แต่ ทีเร็กซ์อยู่อีกฟากโลก และอีกยุค"
  4. Question #2: then who? Answer (one cause): Isan had its own big hunter, found and named in Thailand only recently. `unchecked` (Boat: size, date of naming, group; keep only what is solid)
  5. Payoff flip: "นักล่าตัวใหญ่ที่สุดของอีสาน… เพิ่งได้ชื่อไม่กี่ปีมานี้เอง ทั้งที่มันรอคุณอยู่ใต้ดินร้อยกว่าล้านปี"
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold on its eye in the dark shop window; `_A_fade`.

- **Voice / tone:** friendly, proud local guide, warm and lively, like a neighbour showing you around (Gemini **Achird** or **Sadachbia**); Standard Thai narration (no imitation of a regional accent); family small sounds and one shout "กินรีขโมยลูกชิ้น!". One read per episode.
- **Why it holds:** home pride and surprise ("Thailand has its own dinosaurs, with Thai names") is a strong share trigger for a Thai audience; the noodle shop gives the 4-head cast everyday props to react with; each name reveal is a fact people repeat.
- **Research load:** **named real cases** (Phuwiangosaurus sirindhornae, Kinnareemimus khonkaenensis, Siamraptor suwati: discovery place, naming, size, status) plus general knowledge; scientific names are terms (ER05), so narration uses Thai descriptions and at most one name per episode, after the picture. - **Sound intent:** EP1 shop ambience (pot simmer, radio luk thung far off, no copyrighted song) cut to a giant low breath and leaf crunch over the roof; page flips; a proud brass motif on the name. EP2 fast scurry and a ceramic clink as the meatball goes, khaen-like reed phrase (original, generated) on the กินรี reveal. EP3 night insects stop all at once (silence is the clue that something big is near), deep footsteps, a held breath, low drum tag.

## ER08 pitch check

1. **Hook:** breaks a certainty ("dinosaurs are foreign, from movies") in ~3 s; no term (species names come after the picture). Pass.
2. **Who:** every Thai viewer can picture their own street; strongest in Isan. Pass.
3. **Beats:** guess → "แต่…" → one cause; no list of facts. Risk: three name reveals are similar in shape; the series must vary them (place / legend / recent discovery). Pass with risk.
4. **Ending:** payoffs (a Thai name older than Thailand; legend closer to truth; a hunter that waited underground). Pass.

**Score: 4/4** (risks: named-case accuracy and EP2 evidence depth, ER07).

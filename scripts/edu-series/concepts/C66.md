## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre:** "what if" told as a group chat (screen-life medium).
- **Medium (why it is the wild card):** the story lives on one phone screen: the housing estate's LINE-style group chat (a generic chat UI, no real app branding), with neighbours' posts, voice-note bubbles and blurry phone photos, cut with short painted inserts of what the photo shows. The kit and project defaults cannot make it: the `PhoneChat` component is planned, not built (docs/planned-visual-components.md), and "phone photo" painted inserts are a new look. Film scopes it in W0.2.
- **Our cast (chat names, fictional):** "ป้าแดง ร้านชำ", "ลุงช่างไฟ", "น้องภูมิ ม.2", "นิติฯ หมู่บ้าน".
- **Logline:** A housing estate wakes up in the age of dinosaurs and does what every Thai estate does: argues in the group chat. Each day one post asks the wrong question, and the answer is stranger than the dinosaurs outside.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** the first thing that breaks is the clock, amber cannot carry dinosaur DNA even 66 million years, and the estate already had dinosaurs before it left: the chickens.
- **Structure: 2 (hook → question/delay #1 → rehook → question/delay #2 → answer).** Why: a group chat is a natural delay machine: wrong guesses from neighbours are the delay, a new post is the rehook, and the narrator gives the answer.

### EP1 (Day 1) — นาฬิกาทั้งหมู่บ้านเพี้ยน
- **Single question / answer:** Why is every clock in the estate wrong against the sun? Earth spun a little faster then, so a day was about half an hour shorter (Late Cretaceous estimate).
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าหมู่บ้านคุณหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์… สิ่งแรกที่จะพัง ไม่ใช่บ้าน แต่คือนาฬิกา"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: chat ping, ลุงช่างไฟ: "ทำไมพระอาทิตย์ตกเร็ว นาฬิกาใครเพี้ยนบ้าง". Photo: sunset at 17:40 on his phone.
  2. Question #1: whose clock is wrong? Delay: everyone posts a different guess (time zone, phone update, ป้าแดง "ผีหลอก").
  3. Rehook: น้องภูมิ, Day 2: "แต่ วันนี้มันเพี้ยนเพิ่มอีก"
  4. Question #2: why does it keep growing? Answer (one cause): back then the Earth turned faster; each day was about half an hour shorter, so the clocks fall further behind every day. `unchecked`
  5. Payoff flip: "นาฬิกาไม่ได้ช้า… โลกต่างหากที่หมุนเร็วกว่า"
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold on a chat bubble "งั้นเราตื่นกี่โมงดี"; `_A_fade`.

### EP2 (Day 3) — ยุงในยางไม้
- **Single question / answer:** Can น้องภูมิ trap a mosquito in tree resin so people in the future clone dinosaurs? No: amber can keep the body for 66 million years, but DNA falls apart long before that.
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าหมู่บ้านคุณหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์ แล้วเด็กข้างบ้านจับยุงใส่ยางไม้… เพื่อส่งไปให้คนในอนาคต"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: น้องภูมิ posts a photo: a mosquito sinking into a drop of resin, "ภารกิจ จูราสสิก!!".
  2. Question #1: will it work? Delay: adults cheer, argue, ลุงช่างไฟ: "ยุงตัวนี้สดสุด ๆ ไม่ต้องรอ 66 ล้านปี".
  3. Rehook: "แต่ ปัญหาไม่ใช่ยุง… ปัญหาคือ 66 ล้านปีนั้นเอง"
  4. Answer (one cause): DNA breaks down with time even when sealed; after that long nothing readable is left. `unchecked` (Boat: amber DNA studies, the number to avoid in narration per ER10)
  5. Payoff flip: "ยางไม้เก็บยุงได้ 66 ล้านปี… แต่เก็บไดโนเสาร์ไว้ในตัวมันไม่ได้"
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold on the amber drop catching the light; `_A_fade`.

### EP3 (Day 7) — ไดโนเสาร์ที่ติดมาจากหมู่บ้าน
- **Single question / answer:** Did a dinosaur come along from the estate? Yes: birds are dinosaurs, so ป้าแดง's chickens are the estate's own dinosaurs.
- **Hook (Thai):** "หมู่บ้านคุณหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์… แต่มีไดโนเสาร์ตัวหนึ่ง ติดมาจากบ้านคุณด้วย"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: นิติฯ posts a "census" of everything that came with the estate.
  2. Question #1: which one is a dinosaur? Delay: guesses: the gecko on the wall, the monitor lizard by the canal, the cat.
  3. Rehook: "แต่ จิ้งจกไม่ใช่ไดโนเสาร์นะ" (lizards are a different line). `unchecked`; keep only if it fits in one line (ER09).
  4. Answer (one cause): birds come from dinosaurs, so a bird is a dinosaur that is still alive. `unchecked`
  5. Payoff flip: ป้าแดง's photo of her chickens, staring back at a dinosaur through the fence. "หมู่บ้านเราไม่ได้หลุดไปหาไดโนเสาร์… เราเลี้ยงมันไว้หลังบ้านมาตลอด"
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold; `_B_loop` possible (the last bubble returns to the Day 1 group header). Series ending: the estate always had dinosaurs.

- **Voice / tone:** a casual, amused friend scrolling the chat with you, dry and quick (Gemini **Zubenelgenubi** or **Puck**); neighbours speak only as text and short voice-note bubbles (one or two Gemini character voices, ≤ 2 s each) so the narrator stays the single explaining voice. One read per episode.
- **Why it holds:** every Thai viewer is in a หมู่บ้าน or family group chat, so the format is instantly familiar and funny; each episode ends on a strong flip (the planet sped up, amber lies, chickens are dinosaurs); EP3's last line is the most shareable in the round.
- **Research load:** general knowledge (Late Cretaceous day length estimate, ancient DNA decay in amber, birds as dinosaurs); film premise check.
- **Sound intent:** chat pings and typing ticks as rhythm (each ping is a new open question), a light lo-fi bed under the chat, painted inserts get their own world sound (jungle, distant roar) that cuts back to the phone's dry room tone; EP1 a clock tick that speeds up under the answer; EP2 a slow resin "gloop" and silence on "66 ล้านปี"; EP3 a chicken cluck answered by a low dinosaur call, then a warm pluck tag. Sound never replaces the clue: the clock photo, the amber drop and the chicken photo are on screen.

## ER08 pitch check

1. **Hook:** breaks certainties ("the dinosaurs are the big change", "amber keeps DNA", "dinosaurs are gone"); clear in ~3 s; no term. Pass.
2. **Who:** everyone with a group chat; Thai housing-estate chat culture is near-universal. Pass.
3. **Beats:** each post opens a question, neighbour guesses are the delay, "แต่…" rehooks, one cause each. Risk: chat text plus captions may crowd the frame and slow reading; Film decides how much text is on screen. Pass with risk.
4. **Ending:** flips ("the planet is faster", "amber can't keep the dinosaur", "we keep them in the backyard"). Pass.

**Score: 4/4** (risk: on-screen text load in the chat medium).

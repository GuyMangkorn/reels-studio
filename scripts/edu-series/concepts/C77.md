## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Subject:** plants.
- **Genre:** everyday-nature reveal with a "moving house" flip.
- **Medium:** painted 3D camera (project default) for a Thai school yard in the dry season; the signature picture is a painted inside-view of one leaf as a little house: green machinery being taken apart and carried down the stem into the branch, leaving yellow walls behind. A painted time-lapse of the tree yellowing frames the answer.
- **Our cast:** a Thai student on sweeping duty (เวรกวาดใบไม้, four-head cast) under a big school-yard tree (Film picks a Thai species that yellows before it drops). She meets each problem on screen (ER02).
- **Logline:** She sweeps yellow leaves every morning and thinks leaves turn yellow as they die. But the yellow was in the leaf all along, hidden under the green, and the tree takes the green apart on purpose before it lets go. Her pile is empty boxes after the move.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** leaves don't turn yellow; the yellow is already in a green leaf, covered by the green. Before dropping the leaf, the tree dismantles the green and pulls the valuable parts back into the branch, and the yellow is what's left (`unchecked`: carotenoids present all season; chlorophyll breakdown and nutrient resorption during senescence).
- **Rule that flips (ER03):** "ก่อนทิ้ง ต้นไม้เก็บของ". First the yellow looks like decay (she thinks the leaf is dying, rubbish); the flip: the yellow leaf is the tree packing up and moving out, and what she sweeps is an empty box.
- **Twist clue planted:** CL01 a green leaf with one yellow edge where she tore it (beat 2), CL02 the branch already holding tiny buds above the falling leaves (beat 4). Both return in the answer.

## Beats (five-step structure; times estimated)

1. **HOOK (0–6 s):** morning, she sweeps a pile; one more yellow leaf lands on her head. "ทำไมใบไม้แก่ ถึงกลายเป็นสีเหลือง… ทั้งที่สีเหลืองนั้น อยู่ในใบมาตั้งแต่วันแรก"
2. **QUESTION + DELAY (6–16 s):** Question: "ถ้าเหลืองอยู่แล้ว ทำไมเราไม่เคยเห็น?" Delay: she picks a fresh green leaf, holds it to the sun, scratches it, tears it: green all through; only a thin yellowish edge shows where the green is crushed away (CL01), and she doesn't notice. "ส่องแดดก็เขียว ขูดก็เขียว ฉีกก็ยังเขียว… เหลืองอยู่ตรงไหน?"
3. **REHOOK (16–24 s):** inside-view of the leaf: green paint over a yellow wall; then the green starts disappearing piece by piece. "เพราะสีเขียวทาทับไว้หมด… แต่ สีเขียวไม่ได้ซีดหายไปเองนะ ต้นไม้เป็นคนรื้อมันออกเอง" (pays the hook at ~40 % and opens Q2 in the same breath, ER02).
4. **QUESTION + DELAY #2 (24–34 s):** Question: "ใบจะร่วงอยู่แล้ว… จะรื้อทำไม?" Delay: time-lapse: the tree yellows over a week while she keeps sweeping, more and more each day, groaning; she looks up and sees tiny buds on the same branch (CL02). "ทั้งที่ใบนั้นกำลังจะร่วง ต้นไม้ยังลงแรงรื้อมันทีละใบ ทั่วทั้งต้น"
5. **ANSWER (34–50 s):** the leaf-house view: little packages carried down the stem into the branch, toward the buds. "เพราะใต้สีเขียว มีของดีที่ต้นไม้หาได้ยาก มันเลยรื้อสีเขียวออก แล้วขนของดีกลับเข้ากิ่ง เก็บไว้แตกใบใหม่ สีเหลืองที่เหลือ คือของที่ไม่คุ้มขน" (~9 s). Flip: she looks at her broom and the pile, then up at the buds. "ใบเหลืองที่คุณกวาดทิ้งทุกเช้า… คือกล่องเปล่า ที่ต้นไม้ขนของออกไปหมดแล้ว" ~2 s hold on the pile with one bud above.

- **Ending:** closed on the flip, ~2 s hold. No tip.
- **Voice / tone:** Kore, warm smiling storyteller ("นะ", "เอง"). The student: a groan as the leaf hits her, "หืม?", a surprised "อ้าว" at the buds, a soft laugh.
- **Why it holds:** every Thai student has swept leaves; the "it was yellow all along" line is a small shock; the moving-house image makes a dull chore feel clever. Built-in share: "ส่งให้เพื่อนเวรกวาดใบไม้".
- **Research load:** general botany (pigments, senescence, nutrient resorption). Light to medium for the wording.

## Checks for Boat

- Yellow pigments present in green leaves all along, masked by green: true for the yellow (not for red, which is made new; the concept leaves red out on purpose, ER07).
- "ต้นไม้เป็นคนรื้อมันออกเอง": senescence is an active, controlled process. True wording?
- **Key wording risk:** the green pigment itself is mostly broken down and left in the leaf; what the tree salvages is mainly the nitrogen in the proteins it was bound to. The answer line says "รื้อสีเขียวออก แล้วขนของดีกลับเข้ากิ่ง" (not "ดูดสีเขียวกลับ") to stay true; confirm or reword.
- "เก็บไว้แตกใบใหม่": resorbed nutrients are stored and reused for new growth: true one-line wording?
- Pick a common Thai school-yard tree that yellows (not red) before dropping, so the picture matches the claim.

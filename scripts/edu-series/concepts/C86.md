## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Subject / genre:** Food / comparative sensory biology.
- **Medium:** Practical/documentary split-screen food-and-animal footage + crisp explanatory graphics. Proposed only; Film owns feasibility and production size.
- **Cast / situation:** A fictional human kitchen observer and ordinary non-feeding chicken footage; the split screen is a comparison, not a new animal experiment.
- **Logline:** A familiar human reaction to chilli is paired with the chicken receptor contrast. What looked like a hot fruit becomes a story about different readers of the same chemical signal, without implying immunity to heat or pain.
- **Counter-intuitive claim:** The chemical that gives people a chilli burn is not sensed the same way by the studied chicken receptor counterpart; heat sensation still exists.
- **One rule / reframe:** The same object can produce different experiences when the sensory receiver is different.
- **Clues:** CL01 a room-temperature chilli stays on the same plate; CL02 the split-screen chicken is never treated as proof it cannot feel a genuinely hot surface.
- **Voice / tone:** channel Kore, warm smiling storyteller; curious and brisk. Same voice/pace in both openings; narrator setting still needs the normal sample selection before production.

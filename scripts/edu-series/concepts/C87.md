## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Subject / genre:** Space experiment / visual wildcard.
- **Medium:** Evidence-led experiment footage/still comparison + graphic flow visualization (visual wildcard). Proposed only; Film owns feasibility and production size.
- **Cast / situation:** A curious viewer represented by an off-screen narrator and one restrained observer insert; no astronaut casually lighting an open cabin candle.
- **Logline:** A familiar upward-pointing candle is compared with a rounded controlled microgravity flame. Following the airflow turns the question around: the Earth flame was also being shaped by its environment all along.
- **Counter-intuitive claim:** A flame need not be a familiar upward teardrop; low-flow microgravity experiments can produce rounded flames in an oxygen-containing chamber.
- **One rule / reframe:** Combustion gives the fire; the surrounding flow helps draw its shape.
- **Clues:** CL01 the experimental enclosure and air label appear from the first frame; CL02 the Earth flame's moving hot-gas trace returns in the final comparison.
- **Voice / tone:** channel Kore, warm smiling storyteller; curious and brisk. Same voice/pace in both openings; narrator setting still needs the normal sample selection before production.

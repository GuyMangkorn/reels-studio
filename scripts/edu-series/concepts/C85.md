## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Subject / genre:** Body in space / temporary-growth reframe.
- **Medium:** Painted 3D camera + simplified body silhouette insert. Proposed only; Film owns feasibility and production size.
- **Cast / situation:** One fictional adult astronaut with a recurring height mark and a friend at the later Earth measurement; no named astronaut anecdote.
- **Logline:** An adult astronaut celebrates a higher height mark. The spine reveal explains why the apparent growth is reversible; the return measurement turns the boast into a distinction between height and permanent growth.
- **Counter-intuitive claim:** Adults can measure taller during spaceflight without undergoing ordinary childhood growth, and the additional height is temporary.
- **One rule / reframe:** A body's measured height can change when spinal loading and shape change; height gained is not automatically permanent growth.
- **Clues:** CL01 the old ruler mark stays visible throughout; CL02 the character's adult photo/ID remains unchanged rather than becoming a child-to-adult montage.
- **Voice / tone:** channel Kore, warm smiling storyteller; curious and brisk. Same voice/pace in both openings; narrator setting still needs the normal sample selection before production.

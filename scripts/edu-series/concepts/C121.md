## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Sports-underdog parody turned upside down. Alabama, 2016 (lead): a bloodhound named Ludivine is let out in the morning to pee, wanders to the start line of a local half marathon, runs the whole 21 km with the field, and finishes seventh. Our invented runner, Somchai, trained six months for this race.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 3D camera (project default) with a sports-broadcast layer: lower-third name bars, a live race-position tracker, a split-time clock that the dog keeps breaking.
- **Logline** — สมชายซ้อมวิ่งมาหกเดือน วันแข่งจริงมีคู่แข่งหน้าใหม่โผล่มา หูยาว ไม่มีเบอร์ ไม่ได้สมัคร และออกจากบ้านมาเพื่อฉี่
- **Opening line** — "หมาตัวนี้ออกจากบ้านไปฉี่… แล้วกลับมาพร้อมเหรียญฮาล์ฟมาราธอน"
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — You can train half a year and lose to someone who did not know there was a race.

## Beats

1. **B01** — Start line. Somchai stretches, gel packs, smart watch. Next to him, a bloodhound with no number. Somchai: "น้อง… มาส่งใครรึเปล่า". Deadpan: "ไม่ได้มาส่งใคร มาแข่ง".
2. **B02** — The gun. The dog runs. Visual gag: the race tracker shows "?? — ไม่มีเบอร์" climbing past runners one by one.
3. **B03** — But: she stops to sniff, wanders into a field, rolls in something (lead: reported detours) and still catches up. Rule of three: Somchai drinks water, eats a gel, looks back; the dog is in front again. Character line: "มันแวะดมดอกไม้ทำไมยังนำอีก!"
4. **B04** — The finish. The wow fact as a punchline: she finished seventh, in about one and a half hours (lead: roughly 1:32), out of more than a hundred runners (lead), and the organisers gave her a medal. Today comparison: "เหมือนไปสอบโดยไม่รู้ว่ามีสอบ แล้วได้ที่เจ็ดของห้อง".
5. **B05** — The owner only found out when people sent her the news (lead). Deadpan: "เจ้าของปล่อยไปฉี่สิบนาที ได้นักกีฬากลับมาหนึ่งตัว".

- **Ending** — Callback: next year Somchai comes back for revenge, crosses the line, gets his medal, and on it is a picture of a bloodhound (lead: the race was reportedly renamed and themed after her; Boat to confirm). Somchai: "ปีที่แล้วแพ้มัน ปีนี้ต้องห้อยหน้ามัน". Hold 2 s on the medal.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore as a sports commentator friend getting more hysterical; Somchai earnest, then betrayed.
- **Why it holds** — The hook is a joke you can retell in one breath, and it is real. Share trigger: "ส่งให้เพื่อนสายวิ่ง".
- **Research load for Boat** — Named real case (an animal and a local race). Leads: Ludivine, Elkmont Trackside Half Marathon, Alabama, January 2016; finishing place, time and field size; the detours; the medal; how the owner learned; whether the race was renamed (e.g. "Hound Dog Half Marathon") and whether medals showed a hound. One body fact if it earns a punchline (dogs cool by panting; scent hounds' stamina), otherwise left out.
- **Sound intent (proposed)** — Epic training-montage brass for Somchai, deflated to a tuba each time the dog passes; crowd ambience; a starting-gun crack; a sniff-sniff SFX on each detour; a finish-line horn and a bark on the medal; the series closing sound after the hold.

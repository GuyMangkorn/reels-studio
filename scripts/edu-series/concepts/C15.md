# C15 — ถ้าดวงอาทิตย์หายไปตอนนี้

- Status: **made** as `EDU-EP07-SHORT` (published).
- Pitched in round R3; split out of the old `concepts.md` on 2026-11-13 (full round text: commit 8cf02ff).

## Pitch (Preaw)


- **Sub-genre:** what-if physics (speed of light and of gravity).
- **Staging:** split frame: top, black space where the Sun just vanished; bottom, a sunny Bangkok street where a vendor (`woman-ponytail + vendor`) and a customer carry on as normal, with a painted countdown clock.
- **Logline:** The Sun disappears. For eight minutes nobody on Earth can tell: the light already on its way keeps arriving, and even Earth's orbit keeps curving, because gravity's "news" also travels at the speed of light. Then the sky goes dark, the Moon goes dark a second later, and Jupiter keeps shining for a while.
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าดวงอาทิตย์หายไปตอนนี้… คุณจะยังไม่รู้ตัวไปอีก 8 นาที"
- **Hook claim status:** `unchecked`, `settled` (average light travel time about 8 min 20 s).
- **Key reveal image:** the split frame: empty space with Earth on its orbit line above, the warm street below still in full afternoon sun, clock at 04:59.
- **Beats:**
  1. Afternoon soi, somtam stall; cut up to space: the Sun blinks out with a soft pop.
  2. Down here nothing changes; the clock counts. Sunlight takes about 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach us. `unchecked`, `settled`
  3. Earth keeps orbiting too: changes in gravity travel at the speed of light, confirmed in 2017 when gravitational waves and light from the same neutron-star crash arrived together. `unchecked`, `settled`
  4. 8:20: the sky goes black in one move; about a second later the Moon goes dark; Jupiter still shines for roughly 35 to 50 minutes. `unchecked`
  5. Twist: it works the other way too: the sunlight on your skin right now left the Sun eight minutes ago, and every star is a picture of its past. Last line: "แสงแดดที่โดนตัวคุณอยู่ตอนนี้… ออกจากดวงอาทิตย์มาตั้งแต่ 8 นาทีที่แล้ว"
- **Ending:** `B_loop`.
- **Voice / tone:** calm, slightly eerie narrator; the vendor and customer have one exchange of small talk, unaware ("ร้อนจังวันนี้"), which turns funny under the countdown.
- **Why it holds:** the strongest pure hook of the six (a catastrophe nobody notices); dramatic irony carries the middle; the Jupiter detail is a fresh surprise inside a known fact; the twist turns the viewer's own sunlit moment into the past.
- **Research load:** general knowledge (light travel time, GW170817, Moon and Jupiter light-delay figures). Known trivia, so the Jupiter and gravity beats carry the novelty.
- **Kit check:** people from `PEOPLE` + `vendor` outfit; ENV-SKY (`paintSkyDisc` for the Sun, bands for day and dark); `glow.tsx` for the afternoon key. New: space set (star field, Earth on an orbit line, Jupiter, Moon), street-food stall, split-frame layout, painted clock.
- **Sound intent:** cheerful street ambience and a light ukulele loop that keeps playing obliviously; under it a slow clock tick that grows; total silence at the blackout as the stinger; one distant dog bark in the dark, then the soft music returns for the twist.

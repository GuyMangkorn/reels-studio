- **Genre / story shape:** period comedy with a merchant and a king (both invented, no real names); a con that turns out to be half true.

- **One rule (refrain):** "ของจริง… แค่ไม่ใช่อย่างที่คุณคิด"
- **Clues:** CL01 the horn's tight spiral is shown close in B01 (planted as beauty); CL02 a fishy smell gag on the merchant's crate from Greenland/the far north in B02 (background).

## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Market negotiation in a fictional Northern Song-era Sichuan market.
- **Proposed medium** — Painted 2D market cut-out with the tabletop, an iron-coin string and the paper note in the same field of view.
- **Logline** — สมมุติว่าคุณขายข้าวอยู่ในเสฉวน ลูกค้าคนสุดท้ายอยากซื้อข้าวทั้งกระสอบ แต่สินค้าที่คุณต้องซื้อกลับบ้านกำลังจะหมด แล้วเขาก็ยื่นกระดาษให้แทนเหรียญ
- **Opening line** — “ถ้าคุณขายข้าวทั้งกระสอบ แล้วคนซื้อจ่ายด้วยกระดาษแค่ใบเดียว คุณจะรับไหม?”
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — In this setting a paper note can complete a trade that a large pile of iron coins would slow down.

## Beats

1. **B01** — You grip the sack. The customer puts one printed note on your counter while you watch the stall that has your last jar of oil.
2. **B02** — You ask for coins. The customer shows you a short string of iron coins and can go for the rest, but you watch the oil seller start packing.
3. **B03** — You ask the oil seller whether they accept the same kind of note. The seller says yes. The period fact lands while your own choice is still live, with paper money circulating alongside a world of iron coins.
4. **B04** — You hand over the rice, take the paper and immediately spend it on the oil. The stall closes as you lift the jar home.

- **Ending** — Two linked trades close the story. Hold on the empty rice stand and the oil jar in your hand.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore with an easy bargaining rhythm and a pause just before the choice.
- **Retention and share hypothesis** — The decision is legible to anyone who has been offered an unfamiliar payment. The same piece of paper goes through two sets of hands and turns into the object you needed.

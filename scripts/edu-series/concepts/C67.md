## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre:** "what if" thought experiment, game-theory chain told as a survival story.
- **Our cast:** one viewer-stand-in, a young Thai woman at night on a rooftop with a home-made radio dish (the four-head cast). Her small planet-sized fear is the story; the aliens are never shown, only lights far away.
- **Logline:** A signal arrives and replying feels like the friendly, obvious move. Three "แต่…" later, the viewer sees why, under three simple rules, every smart civilization would keep its mouth shut, and why a crowded sky would sound exactly like an empty one.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** a silent sky is not proof that nobody is there; under one simple set of rules, silence is exactly what a crowded universe would sound like.
- **Rule that flips (ER03):** "ไม่รู้ใจกัน… ก็ต้องเงียบไว้ก่อน" (you cannot know their intent, so you stay quiet). Refrain on each beat. The flip: the same rule that makes her afraid explains the silence she started with.
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าวันหนึ่งคุณได้ยินเสียงจากต่างดาว… สิ่งที่ฉลาดที่สุด อาจเป็นการไม่ตอบ"
- **Objection by ~7 s (ER01):** "ทำไมล่ะ ถ้าเขาเป็นมิตรล่ะ?" → "นั่นแหละปัญหา คุณไม่มีทางรู้เลย"
- **Beats (each opens the next with "แต่…"):**
  1. Hook: her dish pings; a blinking point of light in the sky. She grins and reaches for the reply button.
  2. Problem 1, distance: one message there and back takes longer than a lifetime, so you cannot "chat" to check if they are nice. "แต่ จดหมายฉบับเดียว ไปกลับก็หมดชีวิตคุณแล้ว" (example star distance for the picture `unchecked`; a nearby star some tens of light-years away is enough).
  3. Problem 2, suspicion chain (one cause): even if you are kind, they cannot know that, and they know you cannot know they are kind. "แต่ ต่อให้คุณใจดี… เขาก็ไม่รู้ว่าคุณใจดี" Visual: two lights each imagining the other as a monster, mirror images.
  4. Problem 3, the head start: while your letter travels, the other side may leap ahead in technology. "แต่ ระหว่างที่จดหมายเดินทาง… คนที่อ่อนกว่า อาจกลายเป็นคนที่แข็งกว่า" (logic step of the thought experiment, no factual claim).
  5. Payoff flip: she takes her finger off the button; the camera pulls back to a black sky full of lights quietly going dark. "ป่าที่ไม่มีใครเลย กับป่าที่ทุกคนกลั้นหายใจ… จากตรงนี้ ฟังดูเหมือนกันทุกอย่าง"
- **Ending:** closed on the flip, ~2 s hold on the black sky with her one small light; `_A_fade`.
- **Voice / tone:** channel default narrator **Kore**, warm smiling storyteller, but one notch quieter from beat 4 on; she reacts with small sounds (a happy gasp at the ping, a swallow at beat 3, a held breath at the end).
- **Why it holds:** the hook flips the friendly instinct everyone has ("of course I'd reply"); every beat is a decision the viewer makes in their head; the last line is quotable and reframes the real silence of the sky, which is shareable to the friend who says "we must be alone".
- **Research load:** general knowledge (Fermi paradox origin, the novel's premise, star distances for the picture).
- **Sound intent:** night rooftop ambience (distant city hum, insects) under a low warm pad; the signal ping is a bright stinger on the hook; music thins on each "แต่…" and a soft riser carries beat 4; full silence for ~1 s before the last line, then a single low sustained tone as the lights go out. Sound never replaces a clue: the reply button, the mirrored monsters and the dark sky are all on screen.

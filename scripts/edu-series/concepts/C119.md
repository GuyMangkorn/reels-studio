## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Spy-thriller parody that collapses. In the 1960s the CIA wired a cat with a microphone and a radio to eavesdrop on Soviet conversations (lead: "Acoustic Kitty"). The plan was flawless, except for the cat.
- **Proposed medium** — Declassified spy-file collage: typewritten memo pages, redaction bars, stamped "TOP SECRET", 1960s Cold War poster colours (two-tone red and cream), with painted cat cut-outs moving across the pages. Fallback: the painted 3D camera in a 1960s lab and park.
- **Logline** — CIA อยากได้สายลับที่ไม่มีใครสงสัย เลยเลือกแมว ติดไมค์ ติดเสาอากาศ ซ้อมมาอย่างดี แต่แมวก็ยังเป็นแมว
- **Opening line** — "CIA เคยใช้เงินหลายล้านดอลลาร์… เพื่อฝึกแมวหนึ่งตัวให้เป็นสายลับ"
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — The world's best-funded spy agency lost to the one thing every cat owner already knows.

## Beats

1. **B01** — The plan: a cat can sit next to anyone and nobody suspects it. Agent (generic): "ไม่มีใครระแวงแมวหรอก". Deadpan: "ก็จริง… เพราะแมวไม่ทำงานให้ใคร".
2. **B02** — The build (lead): a microphone in the ear, a battery and transmitter in the body, an antenna woven into the tail fur. Visual gag: the cat on a spy-gadget blueprint like a James Bond car.
3. **B03** — But: in tests the cat walks off whenever it is hungry, bored or sees something (lead). Rule of three: target on the left, a bird on the right, a sunny spot in the middle; the cat picks the sunny spot. Character line, agent into the radio: "เป้าหมายอยู่ซ้าย… ซ้าย… มันนอนแล้วครับ".
4. **B04** — Worse: the fix was reportedly another operation to stop it feeling hungry (lead; off screen). Today comparison: "เหมือนจ้างเด็กฝึกงานแล้วผ่าตัดไม่ให้หิวข้าวเที่ยง".
5. **B05** — The wow fact as a punchline: the project reportedly cost about USD 10–20 million in its day (lead: figure from a former CIA official's account, disputed), and the declassified memo on it concluded that cats "can be trained", but the method was not practical (lead: "Views on Trained Cats", declassified 2001).

- **Ending** — Irony: "หมดไปหลายล้าน กับเอกสารลับระดับชาติ… เพื่อสรุปสิ่งที่คนเลี้ยงแมวรู้ฟรี ๆ: แมวไม่ทำตามสั่ง" Hold 2 s on the memo page, the cat asleep on the "TOP SECRET" stamp.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore as a friend gossiping about a boss with a terrible idea; agents in clipped spy-movie voices that slowly crack.
- **Why it holds** — "The CIA did what?" is instant; every cat owner feels smug by B03. Share trigger: "ส่งให้ทาสแมว".
- **Research load for Boat** — Named real case (a programme, no living private person). Leads: the CIA Acoustic Kitty project (1960s); the 2001 declassified memo "Views on Trained Cats" and its exact wording; the cost figure and who claims it (Victor Marchetti); the second-surgery claim; the competing accounts of the first test (taxi vs the cat surviving with equipment removed, Robert Wallace); the dates.
- **Sound intent (proposed)** — Cold War spy theme on muted trumpet and bongos, cut dead each time the cat quits; radio static and a squelch on every agent line; typewriter clacks and a rubber stamp thud on page turns; a single sleepy purr under the ending hold, then the series closing sound.

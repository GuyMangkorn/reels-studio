## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Subject:** Earth science / water cycle.
- **Genre:** nature mystery with a reversal (the suspect is the innocent-looking one).
- **Medium:** **second medium of the round:** flat SVG cut-paper motion graphic (layered paper river, sea, sun and cloud, a paper character), with one time-lapse beat; Film confirms buildability. The signature picture is one paper drop of water lifting off as vapour while its grain of salt is left behind, repeated thousands of times as a sped-up loop.
- **Our cast:** a Thai girl (cut-paper version of the four-head cast) on a family trip from the river to the coast, ending at a salt farm (นาเกลือ) in Samut Sakhon. She meets each problem on screen (ER02).
- **Logline:** Every river on Earth pours fresh water into the sea, so why is the sea salty? Not from hidden salt mines: most of the salt came from those rivers. The sun can lift the water out but never the salt, so it piles up.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** the sea is salty because of fresh water: rain and rivers wash tiny amounts of salt from rock into the sea, evaporation takes out only the water, and the salt stays and builds up over a very long time (`unchecked`: USGS / NOAA explainers; rivers are the main source, sea-floor vents add some).
- **Rule that flips (ER03):** "น้ำไปได้ เกลือไปไม่ได้". First it is the puzzle (fresh water in, salty sea); the flip: the fresh river is the delivery truck, and the salt farm in the last beat uses the same rule on purpose.
- **Twist clue planted:** CL01 the girl's fresh river water in a bottle (beat 2), CL02 a white rim left in a cup she forgot in the sun (beat 4). Both return in the answer.

## Beats (five-step structure; times estimated)

1. **HOOK (0–6 s):** at the river mouth she sips from her bottle of river water (filtered, labelled "น้ำแม่น้ำ"), then a splash of sea hits her lips: "แหวะ!". "ทำไมน้ำทะเลถึงเค็ม… ทั้งที่น้ำที่ไหลลงทะเลทุกวัน เป็นน้ำจืดทั้งนั้น"
2. **QUESTION + DELAY (6–16 s):** Question: "หรือใต้ทะเล มีบ่อเกลือซ่อนอยู่?" Delay: she pours her fresh bottle (CL01) into a bucket of sea water and tastes again: still salty. A paper map shows every river on Earth pouring in. "แม่น้ำทั้งโลก เทน้ำจืดลงไปทุกวัน… ทะเลน่าจะจืดลงเรื่อย ๆ สิ แต่มันไม่เคยจืดเลย" The obvious answer is held.
3. **REHOOK (16–24 s):** zoom into her river bottle: a few tiny salt grains drift in it. "แต่ ความจริงกลับหัว… เกลือในทะเล ส่วนใหญ่มาจากแม่น้ำนี่แหละ" Rain wears tiny bits off rocks and the river carries them down. (Pays the hook at ~40 %.)
4. **QUESTION + DELAY #2 (24–34 s):** Question: "แล้วทำไมแม่น้ำไม่เค็ม?" Delay: she tastes the river water again: fresh. "เกลือมีจริง แต่น้อยจนลิ้นจับไม่ได้… แล้วน้อยแค่นี้ ทำไมทะเลเค็มได้ขนาดนี้?" She notices the cup she left on the car roof: the water is gone and a white rim is left (CL02), but she doesn't get it yet.
5. **ANSWER (34–50 s):** paper sun over the sea: drops lift off as vapour, each grain stays behind; the loop speeds up and the sea's salt layer thickens. "เพราะแดดยกขึ้นไปได้แต่น้ำ เกลือตามไปไม่ได้ น้ำกลายเป็นเมฆ เป็นฝน ไหลกลับมาพร้อมเกลือใหม่ ส่วนเกลือเก่า ค้างอยู่ในทะเล มาเป็นล้าน ๆ ปี" (~9 s). Flip: the family car stops at a salt farm, white heaps in the sun. "ทะเลเค็ม… เพราะน้ำจืดนี่แหละ ที่ขนเกลือมาให้ทุกวัน" ~2 s hold on the salt heaps.

- **Ending:** closed on the flip, ~2 s hold. No tip.
- **Voice / tone:** Kore, warm smiling storyteller, playful about the "แหวะ". The girl: "แหวะ!", a thinking "อืม…", a delighted "อ๋อ!" at the salt farm.
- **Why it holds:** a question every child asks and most adults can't answer; the reversal (the fresh river is the culprit) is quotable; the salt farm is a Thai picture the viewer already knows.
- **Research load:** general knowledge (river input, evaporation, residence time). Light.

## Checks for Boat

- Rivers as the main source of sea salt; sea-floor vents as a second source. Is "ส่วนใหญ่มาจากแม่น้ำ" true for the salt as a whole, or only for some ions (sodium vs chloride)? If chloride's main source is different, reword the rehook to what is solid.
- "แดดยกขึ้นไปได้แต่น้ำ": true simplification for evaporation (sea spray carries some salt; leave it out unless it breaks the claim).
- "มาเป็นล้าน ๆ ปี": sea saltiness has been roughly steady for a long time (salt leaves into sediment). Does "ค้างอยู่… มาเป็นล้าน ๆ ปี" imply the sea keeps getting saltier? Reword if needed.
- Salt farms in Samut Sakhon make salt by evaporating sea water in the sun: confirm.

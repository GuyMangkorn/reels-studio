## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — Body comedy. Boy, an office worker, wins a lucky draw: one lap in a Formula 1 car. He only wants one cool selfie for Instagram. The car has other plans for his neck.
- **Proposed medium** — Cockpit POV in Three.js toon (gloved hands, wheel, visor edge) over a painted track, cut with painted 2D outside shots of the car. A second medium next to the default painted 2D.
- **Logline** — หนุ่มออฟฟิศได้ขับรถ F1 หนึ่งรอบ อยากได้แค่รูปเท่ ๆ รูปเดียว แต่ทุกโค้งทุกเบรก ร่างกายเขาพังไปทีละส่วน

- **Counter-intuitive premise** — The hard part of driving F1 is not speed but force: braking and cornering make the head and helmet press like several times their weight, and drivers sweat out kilos in a race.

## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre:** physics "what if" (sky and planet surprises).
- **Our cast:** a father (40s) and daughter (12) on the roof of their townhouse on the first night after the drop, trying to work out where (and when) home is.
- **Logline:** You can survive the dinosaurs and still be lost, because the sky and the planet's own compass changed. The one thing everyone expects to change, the Moon, barely did.
- **Counter-intuitive claim (ER01):** in the dinosaur age the stars are strangers and your compass may point south, but the Moon looks almost the same.
- **Structure: 2 (hook → question/delay #1 → rehook → question/delay #2 → answer).** Why: each episode turns on a wrong guess the viewer makes along with the characters (the stars are hidden by clouds? the compass is broken?), so the delay is where the fun is.

### EP1 — ดาวไม่ใช่ดาวเดิม ดวงจันทร์ยังเป็นดวงเดิม
- **Single question / answer (split into two problems for ER09):** (a) Why can't you find ดาวไถ? Stars move on their own, and over tens of millions of years every star pattern falls apart. (b) Is the Moon bigger? Barely; it was only slightly closer.
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าคุณหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์ แล้วมองฟ้าตอนกลางคืน… คุณจะหาดาวไถไม่เจอ"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: the daughter points where ดาวไถ should be; the father squints. Rich, unpolluted starfield, no line of three.
  2. Question #1: is it cloudy? the wrong season? Delay: they turn around the whole sky.
  3. Answer (a), one cause: every star drifts on its own; give it tens of millions of years and the patterns scatter. `unchecked`
  4. Rehook: "แต่ ดวงจันทร์ล่ะ… ยุคไดโนเสาร์ ต้องใหญ่กว่านี้แน่ ๆ"
  5. Answer (b) and payoff flip: it is almost the same size. "ฟ้าทั้งฟ้าเปลี่ยนหมด… ยกเว้นสิ่งเดียวที่ทุกคนคิดว่าเปลี่ยน" `unchecked` (Boat: how much closer, and whether "almost the same" holds to the eye)
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold on the familiar Moon over a strange sky; `_A_fade`.

### EP2 — เข็มทิศชี้ใต้
- **Single question / answer:** Why might a compass point south in the dinosaur age? Earth's magnetic north and south have swapped many times; the rocks record it.
- **Hook (Thai):** "ถ้าคุณหลุดไปยุคไดโนเสาร์ เข็มทิศของคุณ… อาจชี้ไปทางใต้"
- **Beats:**
  1. Hook: next morning the father opens a compass app / an old scout compass to walk to the river; the sun rises on the "wrong" side of the needle.
  2. Question #1: broken compass? Delay: they try the phone, the scout compass, a magnet on a cork in a bowl; all agree, all "wrong".
  3. Rehook: "แต่ ถ้าคุณหลุดไปผิดปีสักหน่อย… มันกลับชี้ถูก"
  4. Question #2: why does the year matter? Answer (one cause): the Earth's magnet flips back and forth over long stretches of time; a compass just follows it. `unchecked` (Boat: pick a drop date and state the polarity then; the hedge "อาจ" stays only if the date is left open, ER10)
  5. Payoff flip: "เข็มทิศไม่ได้เสีย… โลกต่างหากที่กลับขั้ว"
- **Ending:** closed, ~2 s hold on the needle pointing at the sunrise's wrong side; `_B_loop` possible (the needle swings back to the opening frame).

- **Voice / tone:** hushed, breathy night-sky narrator, close to the mic, like whispering on a roof so as not to wake the jungle (Gemini **Enceladus**); the daughter's "เอ๊ะ?" and the father's sigh. EP2 can lift slightly brighter for the morning. One read per episode.
- **Why it holds:** two everyday tools for "where am I" (the sky, the compass) fail in a way the viewer never imagined; EP1's twist is the opposite of the expected change, which is quotable; the painted dark-sky starfield with no light pollution is a beautiful image on its own.
- **Research load:** general knowledge (star proper motion over tens of millions of years, lunar recession, geomagnetic reversals and their dating); film premise check.
- **Sound intent:** EP1 night insects and distant low dinosaur calls, a thin high shimmer under the starfield, a soft "ting" for each star the daughter tries, silence before the Moon line, a warm low pad on the flip. EP2 dawn bed with a rising synth, the compass needle's tiny metallic tick and wobble (the visual clue is still the needle on screen), three ticks for three tools agreeing, a reversed-cymbal swell on "กลับขั้ว".

## ER08 pitch check

1. **Hook:** EP2 breaks a certainty ("a compass always points north") in ~3 s. EP1 assumes the viewer knows one star pattern; ดาวไถ is the most familiar to Thai viewers, but city viewers may not use it. Partial.
2. **Who:** everyone for EP2; EP1 lands best with viewers who have ever spotted ดาวไถ. Partial.
3. **Beats:** guess → "แต่…" → one cause; EP1 is split into two problems for ER09. Pass.
4. **Ending:** flips ("the one thing that didn't change", "the planet flipped, not the compass"). Pass.

**Score: 3/4: who/hook = EP1 needs a viewer who knows ดาวไถ.** A fix: lead EP1 with the Moon question and use the stars as the rehook.

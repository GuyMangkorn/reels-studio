## Pitch (Preaw)

- **Genre and setup** — One-room deadline comedy in a fictional hand-operated print shop.
- **Proposed medium** — Wild card. Practical first-person macro film with hands, a functioning replica or accessible press, real paper and impression. This version calls for filming an actual paper impression in the press.
- **Logline** — สมมุติว่าคุณเป็นคนเรียงตัวอักษรในโรงพิมพ์ก่อนยุคดิจิทัล ป้ายประกาศใบแรกต้องเสร็จก่อนคนมารับ แต่ตัวหนังสือตรงหน้าคุณกลับด้านทุกตัว
- **Opening line** — “ถ้าคุณต้องพิมพ์ป้ายให้คนทั้งเมืองอ่าน แต่บนแท่นพิมพ์ตัวหนังสือกลับด้านหมดล่ะ?”
- **Counter-intuitive premise** — A backwards-looking raised letter prints a forward-reading letter on paper.

## Beats

1. **B01** — You pick up a reversed metal letter from the tray. The messenger says they are taking the signs as soon as you finish.
2. **B02** — You are about to replace the letter because it looks wrong. Your coworker puts one scrap beside the valuable stack. You can print the batch by eye or risk a little time on a trial impression.
3. **B03** — You print the scrap. The main letters read correctly on paper, but the important hour in the notice is wrong. In-story fact line ties the relief impression to the reversed letter already in your hand.
4. **B04** — You replace the wrong numeral and make the corrected first sheet. You hold the readable notice up from the same position where the shot began with mirrored metal.

- **Ending** — Closed hands-on reveal. The paper flips into the viewer’s line of sight and remains long enough to read one corrected detail.
- **Voice and tone** — Kore with workshop intimacy, short amused hesitation and the physical press sound left room to land.
- **Retention and share hypothesis** — The camera lets the viewer try to read the metal before the sheet arrives. A real impression in one continuous move gives the ending its own reason to rewatch.
- **Research load for Boat** — General craft history. Verify period press, typesetting and proof workflow with object collection or practitioner. The shop, notice, numeral error and deadline are fictional. [Source lead](https://www.nationalprintmuseum.ie/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Primary-School-Teachers-Fact-and-Activity-Pack-New-2013.pdf).

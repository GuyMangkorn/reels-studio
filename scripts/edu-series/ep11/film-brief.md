## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Everyone has stood alone in a lift and wondered "what if". The first frame must say *lift, alone, evening* without a word (UT01): steel walls, a lit button panel, a floor display reading 20, a young woman on her phone. Then the fear in one picture: the lights stutter and her hair starts to rise. From there the picture alternates between **inside** (her body tells us she is weightless and helpless) and **the section** (the shaft cut open beside the car tells us the physics: shared speed, distance, rails). Feelings by beat: dread → a laugh at "ชั้นสิบเจ็ด" → comic frustration (ceiling bonk) → a hard stop into black → curiosity at "แต่เดี๋ยวนะ" → warm wonder in a sepia 1854 hall → relief when the jaws bite → calm at the alarm button.
- **Story first.** WOMAN is about 28, back from the office around 19:30; she rides down from her condo's 20th floor to meet a food delivery in the lobby (not told, just the phone in her hand). She is composed and a little tired; when the car drops she is scared but practical (grab the rail, plan the jump), then annoyed with herself after the bonk, then shaky and relieved. The lift is an ordinary Bangkok condo lift: small (1.40 × 1.30 m), brushed steel, one cool LED ceiling panel, a waist-height handrail on the left wall, a button panel on the left wall with a red-orange floor display and an amber bell button that glows softly (CL03). The 1854 insert is a crowded exhibition hall of timber and iron under a glass roof, warm daylight and dust; a showman in a top hat stands calmly on a raised open platform between two tall notched timber uprights. 1857 is a plain cast-iron store front on a street, a small cage lift visible in a cut-open bay.
- **Medium.** Painted 2D p5.brush through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`, `cam-moves.ts`) in world metres for every set. **One world for the lift:** the car (SET-CAR) hangs inside the shaft (SET-SHAFT), so the section view is literally the car seen from further back with the shaft's front cut away; the same WOMAN rig figure is inside in both. Characters are 4-head kit rig cards squared to the lens. Screen layers (HUD, labels, captions, stamp, inset) are Remotion DOM above the painted canvas. No footage, no photos, no Three.js (Blender deactivated).

- **Truth and safety limits on picture** (script N5, fact-check, Film R6; binding on every shot):
  - the whole fall is a hypothetical: tag "ถ้า…ร่วงจริง" on the HUD every frame the HUD shows (B01–B03, B04-SH01 frozen, B05-SH01–SH04);
  - **no impact image or sound, ever**: the car never reaches the pit on screen (the pit floor stays out of frame in B03-SH04; car bottom ≥ 3.5 m above the pit at the cut); cut to black at f795 with the riser cut dead; no crash, no dust cloud, no debris, no casualty, no hospital, no siren;
  - the only contact gag is a **soft comic bonk** of her head on the ceiling panel (B03-SH03): no lump, no stars, no blood, no tears; a hand on the head and a grimace;
  - FIX-2 ([VD18](#scene-decisions)): the brake is never shown stopping the 120 km/h fall; B05 is a fresh run from floor 20 with the needle near zero; the jaws catch with the needle only a few notches up; she settles at modest force (no slam, no fling);
  - the 1854 catch and the modern jaws are drawn **differently** (timber notches + bow spring + iron pawls in sepia vs a steel T-rail + wedge jaws in cool steel; [VD16](#scene-decisions), [VD20](#scene-decisions)); both labelled "ภาพประกอบ"; nothing says or implies "the same brake";
  - the 1854 scene is a reconstruction (caption "นิวยอร์ก 1854" only, no hall name, no portrait likeness of Otis, no period-photo look); the cutter is an assistant on Otis's order (staging; narration names only the order, F012);
  - no cable count, no trip speeds, no metres on screen; numbers on screen only as listed in [VD24](#scene-decisions);
  - no survival tip in picture (no lying flat, no bracing); L16's alarm press is the only "what to do" and it is for a stuck car;
  - the fall section stays short for viewers with a lift phobia: the longest continuous fall shot is B02-SH01 (4.6 s, diagram-like section, no face in fear).

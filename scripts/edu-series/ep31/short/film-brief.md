## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** The viewer knows from the hook that the plan is absurd and watches a heist movie that only exists in the robber's head, cut hard against how flat and plain the real cameras saw him. The narrator is a friend who cannot believe her own story. The plan is the joke; Wheeler is never mocked as a person (L14 is played sincere), the teller is never a joke or shown threatened, and nobody is hurt or threatened on screen.
- **Two worlds (VD31-01).** *His head*: painted 3D camera, warm amber key with teal shadows, low heroic angles, slow-motion walks, slow push-ins, lemon yellow as the one saturated accent. *Reality*: the same bank seen by its own corner CCTV camera (grey-green, low resolution, timestamp, scan noise, frame stutter), the CRT TV, a flat fluorescent police room. Today (B01-SH04, B04-SH01) is full bright daylight colour at a Thai som tam stall.
- **Colour and value key.** Bank heist: warm sunlight shaft from the entrance (lightest), oak and teal-green walls (mid), Wheeler's windbreaker the strongest colour mass. Apartment (January night): tungsten pendant pool over the table, cool blue window with snow on the sill. TV corner: dark room lit by the blue-white screen. Police room: cool green-grey fluorescent, low contrast (the setback; retention camera "light marks the chapters"). Office (B07): warm green-shaded desk lamp. Today: saturated midday colour.
- **Lemon motif.** A yellow lemon is in every 1995 shot of Wheeler until the arrest (hand, cheek, bowl, lemonade). The CCTV grade turns it grey: the camera does not see the lemon either. It reappears once, on the psychologist's desk in B07, as the story that sent him off, never linked to Wheeler's face or name.
- **Camera language.** Every shot moves inside its set (yaw arc 6–10°, push 5–15 %). Large moves only for the hook push (B01-SH01), the whip pan between the two banks (B01-SH02), the CCTV zoom-and-freeze (B04-SH02, the reveal) and the push into the TV (B05-SH01→SH02). One fixed vantage repeats on purpose: the CCTV camera, and its freeze frame **CCTV-STILL-01** returns on the TV (B05) and as the print slid across the police table (B06).
- **Conceal/reveal.** The blank print stays hidden until L06; the camera direction in the selfie is never shown as fact (B03-SH02 keeps the camera out of frame; the ceiling gag in B03-SH04 is labelled the whole time); the broadcast is hidden until L10; the print across the table until L13; the research topic until L15.
- **Picture honesty (script brief notes, FC01, FC04, FC06, FC07, F016).** Label from frame 0 to the end. No gun in any shot (VD31-10). No clock anywhere in B05 (VD31-11). No name card or caption with Wheeler's name; no picture links his face or name to the research. CCTV timestamp shows only the documented date. The news is an original reconstruction: no real station, logo or Crime Stoppers branding. No sentence, prison or "today" Wheeler.

## Sound direction

- **Music.** One original **heist groove** (slick 1970s-style crime-funk: upright or electric bass riff, brushed kit, finger snaps, muted trumpet stabs, vibraphone; 96–100 bpm, warm hi-fi) that lives in his head, against the flat hiss of reality. Energy arc: B01 groove from f0 with a confident snap, medium-high, **stops dead for one beat before "เพราะเขาทาน้ำมะนาว"**, stays out under L02 (street ambience takes over) → B02 back, sly and lighter (bass and snaps only) → B03 drops to a ticking "experiment" bed (soft clock-like woodblock tick and pizzicato) under L05, silence for the develop, a short triumphant brass hit on "ผ่าน!" rolling straight into the groove at full swagger → B04 full groove, highest energy; it is **band-limited to a tinny CCTV-speaker version** under the CCTV shot (the reality cut is heard as well as seen), full again on the sofa → B05 groove cuts to tape hiss; an original cheesy 1990s local-news sting (synth brass, timpani roll, about 2 s) on the TV click-on → B06 music out → B07 the groove returns small, sad and slowed (muted horn or kazoo lead), pulls back on the "…" of L15.
- **Ending (UT15, Film's choice).** Right after "ไม่ได้รู้จริงเลย", a small deflating kazoo dip (0.5 s); under the 2.0 s hold the groove's bass plays its two-note tag slowly and ends on **one dry finger snap** (the callback of the f0 snap), ringing out in a small room. `NEEDS-GEN` (the EP02 pizzicato tag is not in the heist family).
- **Ambience per location.** Bank K/K2: soft 1990s lobby room tone, low murmur, a far dot-matrix printer chattering, entrance door whoosh. Apartment H: January night, fridge hum, radiator tick, far traffic hiss on wet snow. TV corner V: CRT whine and hum, quiet dark room. News studio N: none (it is inside the TV; the TV speaker carries the anchor). Police P: steady fluorescent buzz, HVAC, far phones. Office U: quiet room, page turns, a far campus bird. Today T: Thai street, scooters passing, krok pestle "pok-pok", vendor chatter (unintelligible).
- **SFX language.** In his head: heist accents (snaps, whooshes, brass stabs, a "ting" glint on the cheek, the reveal shimmer). In reality: real foley played straight (instant-camera shutter and eject whirr, paper, phones, door, CCTV servo and record beep, CRT click-on). The narrator's punctures: soft comedy deflation (dry bonk, deflating squeak, kazoo). **No siren, no gunshot, no door knock, no handcuff click** (research; FC07; RC03 taken: the arrest is the stopping tick and one flat thud). The TV sting, CCTV and police room are generated reconstructions, never real recordings.
- **Silence policy.** Planned silences: one dead beat before "เพราะเขาทาน้ำมะนาว" (B01-SH02 end); half a beat after "จริงนะ…" (B02-SH03); the develop under the "…" of L06 (B03-SH03, only the faint print-shake flaps); a tiny pause before "น้ำมะนาวได้ผลเหรอเนี่ย" (B04-SH03); after the ANCHOR's "ครับ" and the "…" of L12 held dry (B05-SH02/SH03); about 0.3 s total silence after "นี่คุณใช่ไหม" before L14 (B06), with nothing under L14; the music pull-back on the "…" of L15.
- **Voices and mix.** NARR (Kore) clean and on top; music ducked about 14 dB under VO. ANCHOR heard through the TV speaker (band-limited, slight CRT room); POLICE and WHEELER dry in a small hard room. No SFX onset on a key word unless the row says so. Each cue is designed per shot in [storyboard.md](storyboard.md); a library ID appears only as a **candidate** where the file seems to fit and Ton confirms the fit ([fit beats reuse](../../../../docs/sound-generation-workflow.md#fit-beats-reuse)); everything else is `NEEDS-GEN`.

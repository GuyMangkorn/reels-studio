## H1 — HK01 broken certainty (recommended)

- **Spoken Thai (0–5 s):** "ปี 1995 ผู้ชายคนนี้กับเพื่อน ปล้นธนาคารสองแห่งในวันเดียว ไม่ใส่หน้ากากเลย… เพราะเขาทาน้ำมะนาวที่หน้าไว้แล้ว" (beat) "น้ำมะนาว ใช่ ที่บีบใส่ส้มตำนั่นแหละ"
- **Beat shape:** claim over a bare-faced man walking into a bank lobby, lemon in hand → the friend's tip (lemon juice is invisible ink, so cameras can't see you) → the Polaroid "test" comes out blank, he is sure → two banks, full face to the CCTV → he stays free three and a half months, then his photo airs on the 11 pm news and he is caught in under an hour → police show the photo: "แต่ผมทาน้ำมะนาวแล้วนะ" → punchline ending: one psychologist reads the story and a whole research topic starts from it.
- **Why it works:** it is the share line in true words, a viewer would bet it is false, and the picture proves the stakes in the first second (no mask, a lemon). The deadpan topper lands the first bit inside 5 s. It saves the two best wow lines (under an hour after the news; the documented quote) for mid-story punchlines, where they land harder.
- **Leans on:** F001 (6 Jan 1995, two men, two banks, no disguise), F004 (lemon-juice belief; the idea from Johnson comes in B01, not the hook).
- **Rules it bends:** none. It is ER01's default shape, so no override is recorded. The "ส้มตำ" topper is a deadpan aside (not-factual, ER13).

## H2 — HK07 start at the climax (the quote first)

- **Spoken Thai (0–5 s):** "ตำรวจยื่นรูปจากกล้องธนาคารให้ดู หน้าเขาชัดแจ๋ว… เขาพูดว่า" MAN (flat): "แต่ผมทาน้ำมะนาวแล้วนะ" NARRATOR: "ย้อนไปสามเดือนกว่าก่อนหน้านั้น"
- **Beat shape:** the interrogation table first (a CCTV print slid across, his confused face, the line) → rewind to 6 Jan 1995: the friend's tip, the Polaroid test, two banks bare-faced → three and a half months free → 11 pm news, caught in under an hour → back at the table, now the line means something → punchline ending on the psychologist.
- **Why it works:** the line is the funniest true thing in the story and it is a puzzle on its own (what does lemon juice have to do with it?), so the rewind is pulled by curiosity, and the second hearing of the line pays as a callback.
- **Leans on:** F010 (documented line, reported wording; he was shown photos, not a tape), F001 and F009 for "สามเดือนกว่า" (6 Jan to 20 Apr).
- **Rules it bends:** ER01: opens on an event, not a contradicting claim; "no mask on purpose" is stated after the rewind (about 7–9 s). It spends the story's best character line at second 3, so the middle needs the "under an hour" line as its wow punchline. The line is played twice; the second time it must be the same documented wording (no extra lines invented for him). Factual exposure: the quote's English wording varies across copies ("But I wore the juice"); Boat confirms the Thai rendering at W1.3.

## H3 — HK08 true extreme number (the time gap)

- **Spoken Thai (0–5 s):** "โจรคนนี้ลอยนวลได้สามเดือนครึ่ง… พอหน้าขึ้นข่าวสิบเอ็ดทุ่ม อยู่ได้ไม่ถึงชั่วโมง ตำรวจมาเคาะประตูแล้ว"
- **Beat shape:** a calendar flipping January to April, then a TV switching on and a clock racing to 00:10 → "หน้าเขาชัดขนาดนั้นได้ไง ก็เขาไม่ใส่หน้ากากน่ะสิ… เพราะน้ำมะนาว" → rewind through the friend's tip, the Polaroid test, the two banks → the arrest again in context → police show the photo: "แต่ผมทาน้ำมะนาวแล้วนะ" → punchline ending.
- **Why it works:** the contrast (months free, then under an hour) is a true number set against an everyday scale, and it raises the question the lemon answers.
- **Leans on:** F009 (11 pm news on 19 Apr 1995, arrest 12:10 a.m. on 20 Apr, under an hour after the broadcast; robbery 6 Jan). "ตำรวจมาเคาะประตู" is a picture of the arrest; where and how he was arrested is not in the packet, so Boat checks it at W1.3 or the line becomes "ตำรวจจับได้แล้ว".
- **Rules it bends:** ER01: the broken certainty (robbing bare-faced on purpose because of lemon juice) arrives at about 6–8 s; the first claim is a set-up. It spends the "under an hour" wow line in the hook. "โจรคนนี้" is fine for Wheeler alone here because the arrest was his (Johnson was caught on 12 Jan, F008).

## H4 — new: the test that proved the plan (irony + HK10 visual-first layer)

- **Spoken Thai (0–5 s):** (first second: a Polaroid sliding out of the camera, blank) "ก่อนไปปล้นธนาคาร เขาทดสอบแผนก่อนนะ… ถ่ายรูปตัวเอง แล้วหน้าก็หายไปจริง ๆ"
- **Beat shape:** blank Polaroid in his hand, his delighted face → "ทาน้ำมะนาวไว้ เพื่อนบอกว่ากล้องจะมองไม่เห็น" → police later guess bad film or a camera held the wrong way (an obvious visual gag shows the lens pointed off his face, labelled as the police's guess) → two banks bare-faced → three and a half months free, 11 pm news, under an hour → "แต่ผมทาน้ำมะนาวแล้วนะ" → punchline ending.
- **Why it works:** the viewer sees "proof" that the viewer knows cannot be true, so the hook is irony with a picture, and the shouting-at-the-screen moment C113 promised comes in the first 5 s.
- **Leans on:** F006 (Polaroid test, missing from the picture, cause unproven), F004 (belief from his partner).
- **Rules it bends:** ER01: an irony hook, not a broken certainty; the stake (he robs a bank on this proof) is stated at about 5–7 s. Factual exposure: "หน้าก็หายไปจริง ๆ" must stay a statement of what the print showed, never a cause; the police-guess gag must read as a guess on screen, or Boat flags it (F006).

## Recommendation

**H1 (HK01).** It is the story's share line in true words, the lemon and the bare face prove it in the first second, a deadpan bit lands inside 5 s, and it keeps both wow punchlines (under an hour after the news; "แต่ผมทาน้ำมะนาวแล้วนะ") for the middle, where they land as escalation; it needs no channel override and carries the least factual exposure.

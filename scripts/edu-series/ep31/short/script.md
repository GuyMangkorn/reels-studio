## Story core

- **Logline.** Pittsburgh, 1995: a friend tells McArthur Wheeler that lemon juice is invisible ink, so a face rubbed with it cannot be seen by cameras. He doubts it, so he tests it: the Polaroid comes out with no face. He and his friend rob two banks bare-faced. He stays free three and a half months; then his bank-camera photo airs on the 11 pm news and police have him in under an hour. Shown the photo, he says he wore the lemon juice. A psychologist reads the story and goes on to research why people feel so sure when they don't really know the thing.
- **Central question.** Why would a man rob a bank with no mask, on purpose, and what happens to him?
- **Victim, intention and obstacle.** Wheeler wants to be invisible. Every step that should hide him shows him more: his own test fools him, the bank camera gets his whole face, the TV shows it to the city, and his story ends up starting research on why people feel sure when they don't really know (ER02). The comedy aims at the plan, never at the teller or any victim.
- **Feeling.** A heist-movie parody told by a friend who cannot believe her own story; the "จริงดิ" that the test photo really was blank; the snap of "under an hour"; the closing irony that a man this sure of himself sends a psychologist off to study exactly that.
- **Takeaway.** In 1995 a real robber went bare-faced because he believed lemon juice hid him from cameras; he was caught less than an hour after his photo aired; a psychologist who read his story went on to research why people feel sure about a thing they don't really know (the 1999 paper "Unskilled and Unaware of It", named in the description only).

## Truth and ending

- **What actually happened (research R1, secondary sources; fact-check pending).** On 6 Jan 1995 Wheeler and Clifton Earl Johnson robbed two banks in the Greater Pittsburgh area without masks or disguise (F001, F002). Wheeler later said Johnson told him lemon juice makes a person invisible to cameras, the way it works as invisible ink, and that he was at first skeptical (F004). Lemon-juice invisible ink is real on paper (F005). Wheeler put lemon juice on his face, took a Polaroid of himself and was missing from the picture (F006; cause unproven). His surveillance photo aired with the 11 pm news on 19 Apr 1995 and he was arrested at 12:10 a.m. on 20 Apr, less than an hour later (F009). Shown the photos, he said "But I wore the lemon juice. I wore the lemon juice." (F010). David Dunning read the story and, with his student Justin Kruger, studied whether people's felt competence matches their real competence; the 1999 paper is "Unskilled and Unaware of It" (F013, F014; inspiration only, F015).
- **Kept off narration on purpose.** The gun (F003; no scene needs it); any "nobody was hurt" line (F003, no source); the cause of the blank Polaroid (F006, three police guesses, unproven; the guess is a labelled picture gag only); smiling at the camera (F011); the sentence (F012); Johnson's name, arrest and testimony (F008); Wheeler today (F016); the term "Dunning–Kruger effect", the percentile figures and the statistical dispute (F014, F015); the lemon-in-the-eyes story (F007, unsupported).
- **Joke lines that must stay jokes.** L02 "ที่บีบใส่ส้มตำนั่นแหละ" (deadpan topper); L04 "หน้าคนไม่ใช่กระดาษจ้ะ" (deadpan; the ink fact is true); L07 "ผลทดสอบ… ผ่าน! ได้เวลาไปปล้นธนาคาร" (narrator's heist-movie voice, not Wheeler's words or thoughts); L08's "เราเปิดกล้องหน้ายังหามุมก่อน" (today comparison); L09 "น้ำมะนาวได้ผลเหรอเนี่ย" (irony; the viewer knows). The ANCHOR (L11) and POLICE (L13) lines are dramatized wording of documented acts, not quotes.
- **Ending.** L15 punchline (R2): a psychologist read the story and went as far as researching why people can be this sure when they don't really know the thing; the last words "ไม่ได้รู้จริงเลย" land after a pause, then a 2.0 s hold (ER03). It pays CL01 (the man who "tested" his own belief and was fooled) and points back at L14 with "ขนาดนี้" (how sure he still is). The line states inspiration only ("อ่านเรื่องนี้ ถึงกับไปวิจัย"), never that Wheeler was studied, tested or diagnosed (F015, FC06). "เรื่องนั้น" keeps the finding to a specific skill, so it does not say the unskilled know nothing about anything. No paper title is spoken.

## Story core

- **Logline:** A girl drifts off, feels herself step off a cliff that is not there and jerks awake. We follow the moment of falling asleep to name the jolt, lay out the competing ideas about why it happens, and admit that nobody has proven any of them.
- **Central question:** What is the "falling" jolt you get just as you fall asleep, and why does it happen?
- **Character intention / obstacle:** she wants to sleep; her own body yanks her back at the edge of sleep.
- **Intended feeling:** recognition ("that happens to me"), a small shock at the jolt, then calm curiosity; ends gentle enough to rewatch in bed.
- **Learning takeaway:** it has a name (hypnic jerk / อาการกระตุกตอนเคลิ้มหลับ), it is common and usually harmless, it is linked to caffeine, stress, too little sleep and hard late exercise, and its cause is still unproven; see a doctor if it keeps you from sleeping or other jerking comes with it.

## Truth and ending

- **What happens in the story:** she closes her eyes, her muscles slowly loosen, an image of a cliff edge drifts in, she "steps off", falls through paint, her body jerks, she gasps and opens her eyes in bed. Later she lies back, eyes close, the room softens again (loop seam).
- **Verified (per research, pending W1.3 on this wording):** name and definition (F001), timing at the wake-to-sleep transition (F002), commonness as "most people", no number (F003), the falling feeling and onset imagery as accompaniments, not REM dreams (F005, F006), cause not established (F007), hypotheses A and B (F008, F009), trigger associations (F011), benign (F012), when to see a doctor (F013), gradual muscle relaxation (F014, lightly sourced).
- **Remaining uncertainty kept on screen/in VO:** cause unproven (L08, L09, L12); hypotheses only (L10, L11); triggers are associations ("เชื่อมโยงกับ", L13).
- **Deliberately not used:** any percentage (F003 number, F004); "dream"/REM framing; "sleep stages" or limb-by-limb shutdown; tree-ancestor idea (F010) is held as an optional line ALT-A below, not in the main cut; "bolts upright".
- **Twist / reveal logic:** the reveal is that science does not know. It is fair because L04 plants the muscle relaxation that L11's hypothesis reuses ("กล้ามเนื้อที่คลายลง"), and L05 plants that the cliff "ไม่มีอยู่จริง", so the fall is already marked as something the mind supplies.
- **Ending:** `C_seamless`. Last line L16 "คืนนี้… ลองสังเกตดูนะ ว่าตอนที่คุณ…" is written to run into L01 "กำลังจะหลับ… แล้วจู่ ๆ ก็รู้สึกเหมือนตกจากที่สูง และตัวกระตุก". First viewing: L01 stands alone. On loop: "ลองสังเกตดูนะ ว่าตอนที่คุณกำลังจะหลับ แล้วจู่ ๆ ก็รู้สึกเหมือนตกจากที่สูง…" reads as one sentence. The payoff (name, hypotheses, doctor line) lands before the seam. Last frame = first frame: eyes closing, bedroom walls starting to soften into watercolour. Keep the final ~0.5 s free of speech.

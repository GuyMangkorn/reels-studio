## Story core

- **Logline:** a person shrinks to ant size, body and weight together. A breeze blows them off a tenth-floor balcony and they land unhurt; then a dew drop grabs their hand, the shade chills them and food becomes a constant need. Just as being small looks like nothing but trouble, an ant strolls past with a huge load, and the tiny human finds they can lift the crumb they could not finish.
- **Central question:** what changes when you get small, if the world (and gravity) stays the same?
- **Intention / obstacle:** TINY wants to get through the macro world; the forces that hardly matter at human size (air, water's surface, heat loss) now run their life.
- **Feeling:** wonder and a laugh at the fall, "ugh" at the sticky drop, sympathy in the cold, a small triumph at the crumb lift.
- **Takeaway:** gravity does not change; what changes is the balance. "ตัวยิ่งเล็ก…" is the refrain: the smaller the body, the more air resists it compared with its weight (falls get gentle), the more skin it has for its size (heat escapes much faster) and the faster weight shrinks compared with strength (it lifts more relative to itself). Water's surface tension, which we never feel, can hold a tiny body.

## Truth and ending

- **Premise (spoken, L03):** the body shrinks in mass too (A1, C01 premise; research myth 6). Without it the hook is false.
- **Supported directions (spoken as fact):** C03 (gravity same; air beats weight at small size), C08 (area to volume, heat escapes fast), C14 (strength vs weight is geometry, not better muscle).
- **Hedged (spoken with ราว ๆ / อาจ / แทบ / บางชนิด / หลายเท่า):** C01 hook "แทบไม่เป็นอะไรเลย" (Boat's safety margin) and the chair-jump equivalent (`calc`, "ราว ๆ", ~0.45 m drop); C06 water "อาจแรงพอ" and "ได้หลายเท่า" (no number: the ~20x `calc` needs the whole body wetted, the pictured wrist gives ~2x); C09 "ต้องกินแทบตลอดเวลา"; C11 cold placed "ในร่ม", never "in sunlight"; C12 "มดบางชนิด… ได้หลายเท่า" (SRC008: ~8x max for leaf-cutters, not tens); C15 as direction only, named as strength relative to body weight, no multiple and no pointer to the ant's feat (L16).
- **Setting:** early morning (SRC009: dew forms overnight and is gone within about 4 h of sunrise), so L07 keeps "น้ำค้าง" and the backlit-dew look; the cool shade in L11 fits it.
- **Illustrated only (dramatization, no narrator fact):** C07 dew drop size (drawn about a body length; any larger is exaggeration), the crumb bigger than TINY's head (C09 gag), the crumb lift (C15 picture of the direction), TINY shivering (a warm-blooded human; real ants are cold-blooded, C08 note).
- **Not used:** C02 Haldane quote (length; available for a Long), C04 ant 6 km/h, C05, C10 shrew/hummingbird, C13 5,000x neck figure, X01 blurred vision (would fight the painted macro look; kept off), X02, X03, X04, X05.
- **Twist logic:** B02–B04 build "small = everything is harder" (water traps, cold, hunger), and the crumb (CL01) is planted as a burden: too big to eat. The ant (CL02, glimpsed in the background in B04) returns carrying a load many times its weight, and L15 gives the cause in the same "ตัวยิ่งเล็ก…" pattern the viewer already learned twice (L05, L12). The reversal: the same scaling that froze TINY also makes them strong for their size (L16 names it as strength relative to body weight, not "as strong as an ant"), so TINY lifts the crumb overhead. The upside was hiding in the rule all along; rewatching, the refrain and the crumb are both there.
- **Ending `A_fade` (closed payoff), chosen over loop and seamless:** the hook is a startling promise ("you would survive ten storeys"), not a question; the story's best moment is the crumb-lift reversal, and a loop hand-back line would spend the last seconds on "start again" instead of letting that triumph land. `C_seamless` would also need the final ~0.5 s free and a line paired to "ถ้าคุณตัวเล็กเท่ามด…"; any such pairing ("แล้วถ้า…") reads as a restart and blunts the payoff. A question ending was considered ("ถ้าเลือกได้ คุณจะยอมตัวเล็กไหม?") and dropped: it is a weaker comment bait than the closing image. The user does not require `B_loop` (task.md W0). L17 closes the idea; after it TINY's triumphant squeak (V08), a ~2 s hold on TINY holding the crumb high beside the ant, then fade.

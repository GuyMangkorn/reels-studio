## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** The viewer is "คุณ": a promise that sounds impossible (survive ten storeys), then the price of being small (water grabs, cold, hunger), then the turn: the same rule that hurt also makes you strong for your size. Feelings in order: wonder and a laugh at the drift (B01–B02), "ugh" at the sticky drop (B03), sympathy and a smile at the cold gag (B04), a small triumph at the lift (B05). Every event is **dramatization**; the picture never shows a measurement, a speed, a temperature or a multiple.
- **Story first.** A young woman (21, student age, chin-length black bob) on her tenth-floor condo balcony at about 06:30, in pastel pyjamas, sipping coffee from a mug, leaning back on the rail beside a long planter of ornamental grass beaded with dew; the city is waking up below in blue haze, the sun just over the far towers. She is curious and game rather than heroic: she touches the dewy grass, shrinks with a pop, and from then on the world is a forest. Below, the ground-floor condo garden is a lawn still wet with dew, half in long cool shadow, with one sunlit clearing where she lands. A dropped breadcrumb sits at the shade edge. A reddish-brown ant (a generic garden ant, no species named on screen) is going about its business, carrying a fresh green leaf fragment held up like a sail, a piece far bigger than itself and taller than TINY, so the viewer reads "that ant carries something huge" at a glance.
- **Medium.** Painted 2D, p5.brush, [painted 3D camera](../../../docs/film-visual-brief-workflow.md#painted-3d-camera) (project default, user 5 Nov 2026): every set in world units (metres at human scale; **set metres `sm` at the macro scale**, see the projection contract), seen through the kit yaw camera (`kit/ycam.ts`); every shot moves the camera inside the set; TINY is the 4-head kit rig drawn in one of four views squared to the lens (`squareToCam`). The macro world borrows the language of **watercolour macro photography** (W0.2 annotation): backlit translucent blades, dew beads catching the sun, near and far layers dissolved into soft washes (painted "depth of field", not a runtime blur). Backgrounds are washes without outlines; TINY, the ant, the drop, the crumb and the hero blades get one thin ink line and one cel shade. Overlays (the L15 bars, the warmth puff) are painted, not UI. No SVG characters.
- **Scale as the joke.** The camera stays at TINY's eye level in the macro sets (eye 0.6–1.6 sm), so blades read as trees and the drop as a glass boulder. The only human-scale views are the balcony (B01) and one wide of the building face with TINY as a speck (B02-SH03): the speck is the point of that shot.

## Sound direction

(2) No hard impact anywhere in the fall: the landing is a soft tap and a small boing. (3) After V08: no speech in the ~2 s hold; the end chord, low birds and breeze, fading with the picture to true silence on the last frame.

## Answers to Preaw's N4

- **(a) L16:** as written, **no, not safe**. "ก็ได้เปรียบแบบนั้นด้วย" with "แบบนั้น" pointing at the ant's feat can be heard as "you would lift tens of times your weight like the ant" (C15 `unsupported` as a multiple), and "แล้วคุณล่ะ" can also be heard as the human-size viewer. Fix: name the advantage as relative strength vs weight and drop the pointer to the ant's feat. Proposed: **"แล้วคุณล่ะ… ตัวเล็กขนาดนี้ก็ได้เปรียบเรื่องแรงเมื่อเทียบกับน้ำหนักตัวด้วย"**. Direction only, no multiple. (Research C14 note: the gain does not run backwards, a human cannot lift 50x; the picture of TINY lifting a crumb overhead is a labelled dramatization of the direction, N3.)
- **(b) L10:** needs a change; the condition alone is not enough. Re-derivation: the research 20x uses a wetted perimeter of about 5 mm (the whole body length around the drop): 0.072 N/m x 5 mm = 0.36 mN against weight 17 microN. The shot shows one hand/wrist in the drop. A scaled wrist (circumference about 17 cm x 5/1700 = about 0.5 mm) gives about 36 microN, about **2x** weight, not 20x. So "ราว ๆ ยี่สิบเท่า" overstates the pictured case by about 10x, and the premise (wettable human skin, drop covering the contact) is also unspoken. Range honestly supported: **a few times up to about 20x**, `calc`, A1. Proposed (option A, recommended): **"แรงกว่าน้ำหนักตัวคุณ… ได้หลายเท่า"**. Option B, only if Film shows the whole body wetted: "ถ้าตัวเปียกน้ำง่าย… อาจแรงกว่าน้ำหนักตัวคุณราว ๆ ยี่สิบเท่า". Option A is shorter (about -4 syllables, helps N1). The W1.5 keep/cut choice for the vivid number is now moot unless the user wants option B. Research C06 line is corrected accordingly: the 20x is a whole-body-perimeter upper estimate.
- **(c) L17:** pass. "โลกใบเดียวกันแท้ ๆ" says the world (laws) is the same and "แค่ขนาดตัวเปลี่ยน" names the only change; L05 already states gravity is unchanged. "เกมก็เปลี่ยน" is the idiom for who has the advantage, and the sentence structure blocks the "laws change" reading. Optional tightening if Preaw wants it risk-free: **"โลกใบเดียวกันแท้ ๆ… แค่ขนาดตัวเปลี่ยน ใครได้เปรียบก็เปลี่ยนตาม"** (similar length).
- **(d) L06:** "ขั้นเดียว" is **not fair**. The research equivalent is a free fall of about 0.46 m (3 m/s). A normal stair riser is about 17–18 cm, so 45 cm is about 2.5 steps, and the honest speed range 2–5 m/s is about 0.2–1.3 m. Also the research wording was "ก้าวลงจากขั้นบันได" as a loose analogy, not "one step". A 45 cm drop matches a chair seat height. Proposed: **"ตกสิบชั้น… ถึงพื้นก็แค่ราว ๆ กระโดดลงจากเก้าอี้"**. Keeps "ราว ๆ" before the `calc`. Drops "แรง" (force), which was ambiguous. The picture (landing in grass, small bounce, no hard thud) stays.
- **(e) tenth-floor balcony premise:** fine. Terminal speed is reached within about 1 m (research calc), so the landing speed is the same from ten storeys or a hundred; 10th-floor balcony is about 27–30 m up. "สิบชั้น" in L02/L04/L06 is loose by one floor and does not matter. Conditions to keep in the picture: a soft or ordinary landing (grass is used), no gust throwing TINY into a wall (V02 cue "slow against the building face" must not show a collision; drift alongside, not a hit). Real wind could carry TINY far sideways; dramatization.

## Rulings on the items you flagged

**R1. L05 mechanism (Q1, F04): optional hedge, not a cut, not must-fix.** What stands: no shouting is observed (SRC001, high). Why is Pia's account. I found no independent physiology source; DAN-1 is the same author chain as SRC003 (Vittone from Pia, one original). The claim is a plain, physically plausible one (speech is made on the outgoing breath; the mouth is under water part of the time), the direction is true, and the article says "แทบ" in L04 and L07. Under the finding-severity rule it is not a wrong takeaway: the line does not change what the viewer should do. Recommendation **O1: add "แทบ" to L05** ("ลมที่ได้มา ต้องเอาไว้หายใจ… แทบไม่เหลือไว้ตะโกน", +1 syllable, ER10: one hedge in the line); that matches the research's own usable wording and costs about 0.2 s. Cutting L05 (D4 alternative) is not needed. L14 restates the same model inside the scene; I leave it (R2).

**R2. L14 hedge (Q2): keep as written; the flip is scene-bound, optional hedge.** In context ("คนที่เงียบต่างหาก" follows the man holding the ring) it does not say every quiet swimmer is drowning, and L03 already says "มัก". The generalising risk is a viewer who afterwards treats any quiet swimmer as in danger, which is a harmless false alarm; the reverse risk (a noisy swimmer taken as safe) is handled by L13's "ก็ต้องช่วย". If Preaw wants the extra margin: "คนที่เงียบต่างหาก… ที่อาจไม่เหลือลมจะร้องแล้ว" (+1 syllable); I would not, since it softens the payoff. Not must-fix.

**R3. L09 "อาจไม่ถึงสองนาที" (SRC001 reopened): stays, with a correction to my own packet.** Reopened wording: abstract "All persons drowned within 2 min"; Discussion "at most, 2 min to identify and recognise a person who drowns", "attempts not to drown had a mean duration of 92 s, during which they resurfaced 13 times". **Correction to research F03/SRC001:** Table 2 gives 92 as a **median** (IQR 62–100, range 53–102) for a row labelled "length of resurfacing"; the text calls it a mean; the paper does not define it and does not say whether it is measured before final submersion. The closest defined row is "duration of visible drowning behaviour above the water until it stopped": median 94.5 s (n = 4). The paper also reports, for the four not-rescued people, a start-to-rescue interval of median 229 s (range 52–264 s) that it does not reconcile with "within 2 min" (the start of the incident is not defined). So the figures are n = 4–5, children-heavy (58 % children/teens, 25 % adults), internally inconsistent in the paper, and cover the visible struggle, not necessarily "from the start". What L09 says ("from sinking to disappearing under the water ... may be under two minutes") is still the right order of magnitude (about 1.5 min median visible struggle, none over about 2 min), "อาจ" is the correct size of hedge, and nothing in the video says "under a minute" or gives a second count. Not must-fix. Do not turn OT02 into "92 วินาที" or "2 นาที" without "อาจ".

**R4. Rope-pull posture (Q4): no source for kneeling or staying low; fine as picture, never as a taught technique.** SRC004, SRC009 and the Red Cross search report give throw/reach and not entering, and nothing on posture. "Stay low on the deck behind the edge" is a staging choice (and a plausible safety habit), not sourced. The narration claims none, so no change. **Film note:** show her on the deck pulling the rope, feet on the tiles, rope to the ring; no text or line that says "kneel/lie down to avoid being pulled in". Pulling a thrown ring in on its rope is the standard use of a ring buoy (Red Cross SAC equipment list: ring buoy and rescue line), so that act is sound.

**R5. L02 "คุณจะคิดว่า" (Q3): keep.** It is a hook sentence inside a labelled dramatization about a viewer standing at a pool, not a measured claim, and "คุณอาจคิดว่า" would soften the contradiction ER01 wants. Basis (F07) is popular, unmeasured: drowning people being mistaken for players is reported by lifeguards and in Vittone/DAN-1, no study; the wording makes no number claim. Optional only (O6).

**R6. Open leads closed or still open.** SRC001: reopened, R3. SRC006: still 404, snippet only (label: lead-only; it supports "มัก" and L13, nothing in the script depends on it alone). SRC008: Red Cross page still 403, the rule confirmed through a search report and the two inspected sources (SRC009, SRC004) that carry the script's wording, so F11/F12 stand without it. Independent physiology source: not found (R1). Claim table F01–F13: F01 documented (sample); F02 split (arms documented, mouth/upright Pia); F03 R3; F04 hypothesis; F05 observation documented, cause Pia; F07 popular claim, framing only; F08 documented ("มัก"); F11 and F12 documented guidance; F13 model, consistent with SRC006/DAN-1. ## Title options (D1)

All four are factually usable for narration-level claims; none is must-fix. Ranked weakest first:

1. **T4 "เงียบในสระ… อาจอันตรายถึงชีวิต" (weakest).** Reads as "quiet in a pool = dangerous", which is the over-broad takeaway (RLSS: the test is ineffective action, not silence; most quiet swimmers are fine), and it ties the lesson to pools (Thai child deaths: pools 1.8 % in 2018–2022, 5.8 % in Mar–May 2024 per SRC010 and SRC009). The vague stakes also give no hook.
2. **T3 "คนที่จมน้ำเสียชีวิต มักไม่ได้ร้องช่วยด้วย".** "มัก" is right, but the study's people include 19 rescued and 4 not rescued; it does not give outcomes per behaviour, so "those who died" is narrower than the evidence. Weak, not false.
3. **T2 "เขากำลังจมน้ำตาย… แต่คุณคิดว่าเขาเล่นน้ำ".** States what the viewer thinks (F07, unmeasured) and, in the title, "เขา" has no scene yet to anchor it, so "ภาพจำลอง" is not in the title. Acceptable if the cover carries "ภาพจำลอง".
4. **T1 "คนที่กำลังจมน้ำ ดูเหมือนกำลังเล่นน้ำ" (strongest, default).** A flat generic: it can be read as "every drowning person looks like they are playing". The narration itself says "มัก" and the sample has noisy patterns (backward milling, 19 of 42, with splashes) that arguably do look like play, so the flat form is slightly over the evidence but not false, so **optional (O5)**: if the user wants margin, "คนที่กำลังจมน้ำ มักดูเหมือนกำลังเล่นน้ำ" (one hedge). Unlike EP18's title it states no number. The T1 hook rests on the F07 popular claim (reported, unmeasured), which the video frames as a dramatization.

Cover (N5): the waterline picture with the still head, no limp or submerged body, is consistent with the sources and the YouTube check; if the shrunk/boy scene is not shown, "ภาพจำลอง" is still advisable on the cover.

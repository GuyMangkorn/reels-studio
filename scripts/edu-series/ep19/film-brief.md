## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and feeling.** Frame 0 hands the viewer the wrong emergency: a boy yelling "ช่วยด้วย" and splashing in a bright, loud pool. The narrator says someone is drowning three metres away; the camera leaves the boy and finds the quiet man, who looks like he is resting. From then on the viewer knows more than the pool does: every picture of the man is a calm face at the waterline while the crowd faces the other way. The danger is carried by the waterline, not by the face: how much of his mouth is above the water. The rescue is quick and practical; the flip lands on the boy (loud = air left) and closes on the man coughing, alive.
- **One visual rule: loud is in the sun, quiet is in the shade.** A slow cloud shadow drifts over the deep end from L03 to the shout (V04): the man and the woman sit in cool shade while the boy and the crowd stay in hard sun. When she shouts and every head turns, the sun comes back over the corner (warm payoff, the [retention camera](../../../docs/cinematography-principles.md#retention-camera)'s cool setback / warm payoff). It is motivated light, never a grade.
- **Colour and value key.** Hard Sunday afternoon (14:30, sun from the south-west, about 45° up). Shallow water bright turquoise `#46bfc8` with a caustic net on the floor; deep end darker teal `#1f7f9a` (the floor drops away); white coping `#f2efe6`; sandstone deck `#d8c7a8`; white loungers with navy cushions; pergola slats throw shade stripes on the right deck. Cast colours echo each person's kit outfit so they read as the same people out of their clothes: WOMAN plum one-piece + mustard open cover-up shirt + tortoiseshell sunglasses; MAN navy swim shorts, pale bare torso; BOY light-blue rash guard + orange-red swim shorts. Ring buoy orange-red and white with grey rope (the one saturated warm object near the corner: K03 should be findable).
- **Camera language.** The painted 3D camera in metres with a small eased move on every shot; the two big moves are the hook glide (SH01, past the boy to the corner) and nothing else; the split-level shot (SH06) is the one "new view" reveal (what the arms do under the surface). Profile is used where the waterline at the mouth is the information (SH01 end, SH04, SH07); front where both arms are the information (SH03, SH05, SH06).
- **Conceal / reveal.** K02 (the man) is in the first wide, small, back to us, unremarked; L02 is the first time the camera points at him. K03 (ring on its post by the corner) is in the first wide and in SH01's end frame. K04 (the first real "ช่วยด้วย" is hers) is the only line shot as a close shout. K05 (depth mark) optional, see Q-F1.
- <a id="truth-limits"></a>**Truth limits (Requirement for this deliverable).** (1) He is upright in the water; head dips go at most to the eye line, the top of the head never goes under (O7). (2) His face stays calm (eyes open, looking up, brows level or slightly worried); never `scared`, `terrified` or choking. (3) No splashing from him beyond small ring ripples from the press strokes; no waving; no ladder-climbing arms (F06 cut). (4) The ring lands on or against his arms; the rope pull brings him in with the ring against his chest; he never reaches up and grabs it (O4). (5) She never leaves the deck: no frame of her in the air or in the water; the near-jump is knees bent and stopped, both feet on the tiles every frame. (6) No posture is taught: she pulls the rope standing with her feet on the tiles a step back from the coping; no text and no camera emphasis on stance (R4). (7) No imagined struggle at L11 (no one pulled under, no vision shot). (8) No on-screen text except "ภาพจำลอง" (OT01) and the optional diegetic depth mark (Q-F1); OT02/OT03 stay off (D3). (9) No breath-hold or water game a viewer could copy: the boy only splashes and shouts, standing in shallow water at chest depth. (10) Everyone alive at the end; no blood, no limp body.

## Sound direction

Designed from the story first; library IDs only where the file description fits, every one "not listened" (Ton checks it against the description before use, [fit beats reuse](../../../docs/sound-generation-workflow.md#fit-beats-reuse)). Editor sets levels.

- **Music family and arc.** B01: a small diegetic poolside speaker playing light tropical pop (ukulele, steel-pan, soft kick, about 112 bpm, tinny, distant, outdoor) is the only music: the pool's own noise. On L02's glide it muffles as if heard from under the surface, and by L03 it is gone. B02–B04: no score; the bed is a muffled underwater tone with a slow soft pulse. B05: a warm, low-energy felt-piano and soft pad bed enters on the ring's plop (L12), thins under L13, and leaves room for the cough. **Ending (UT15, Film's choice):** after the cough, one warm felt-mallet two-note resolve (vibraphone-like, rising a fourth, about 2.5 s, soft attack, no reverb wash) on the held frame: "breath came back". It must not cover the cough.
- **Ambience per location (one location, SET-POOL19).** Above water: a busy condo pool (kids, splashes, chatter, a far traffic hum, open outdoor air, hard sun) at full in B01, back at full on V03, hushed on V04, thinning to the boy's distant splashing and near-silence on L14. Near the man (close shots): soft lapping at a tiled edge and at a chin, intimate. Underwater duck (L02 → V03): low-passed crowd, pressure hum.
- **SFX language.** Water is the vocabulary: big bright play splashes for the boy; small soft hollow presses for the man (palms pushing water, not splashes); one tiny wet gasp when his mouth surfaces (never a cry); wet bare-foot slaps on tile for every run; rope and ring sounds for the rescue. Transitions are carried by the duck (in on L02, slammed out on V03) and by silence (L10), not by whooshes. No siren, no crowd scream, no real recording.
- **Silence policy.** L10's freeze at the edge is the one near-silence: everything drops except a faint lapping; L11 adds a single low tone. L14 is near-silence with lapping. Narration is never under music louder than the bed.

## Technical handoff

- **Composition.** All picture through the painted p5.brush plate per shot ([DF-20261029-painted-timeline-dissolve](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261029-painted-timeline-dissolve): one canvas per composition). Three.js rows `N/A (painted 2D)`.
- **Scale / projection.** One projection owner: kit `ycam` with the set in `P` metres. Apparent size from distance only; never resize a figure for readability (fix by camera).
- **Coordinates / camera.** As the projection contract; screen space pixels, origin top-left, y down. Every figure squared to the mid-shot camera with `squareToCam`; arcs stay under ±40°.
- **Depth / alpha and paint order (waterline).** Per figure in the water: (1) the pool floor and walls; (2) the submerged body with an **opaque base** then the teal tint and wobble ([DF-20261031-painted-opaque-cast](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261031-painted-opaque-cast)); (3) the water surface band and ripple rings at y −0.15; (4) the above-water body. An opaque water layer hides earlier marks, so the surface band goes on after the submerged body, never before ([DF-20261030-painted-layer-order](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-painted-layer-order)). The split-level camera (SH06) draws the surface as a horizontal band across the frame with the underwater half lower in value.
- **Contact.** The boy's feet on the shallow floor (y −1.05); the woman's feet on y 0 every frame; the ring: on the post hook → in both her hands → in the air on a parabola (rope trailing from her left hand) → floating at y −0.15 → across his right forearm → against his chest while towed; the rope a straight-to-slightly-sagging line from her hands to the ring.
- **Motion / clock.** Ambient clocks: water ripples, caustics, cloud drift, crowd idle, the man's press strokes and bob between named events ([DF-20261030-painted-story-hold-ambient-clock](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-painted-story-hold-ambient-clock)).

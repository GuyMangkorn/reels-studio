## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** The viewer plays along with a stubborn ten-year-old who keeps losing hide-and-seek to her mum's tracking dog. Each round is the same game in the same field from the same vantages, so the only thing that changes is her plan and the dog's answer. The picture must make two ideas visible without a word: (1) the dog works the prints **one after another** before it chooses (CL03), and (2) the **order** of the prints is the arrow (CL04 glow, dim to bright; jumbled on the shuffled mats). Mood: sunny, sly, comic, with one held breath (W-SNIFF) and one stillness (the dog's head tilt).
- **Colour and value key.** Late afternoon in a grassy training field. Lit grass warm green `#86a24c`, shade `#4f6b46`, sky peach to pale blue, long blue-green shadows. Accents belong to story elements only ([UT12a](../../../../docs/user-taste.md#ut-set-under-subject)): the girl's **coral tee** `#e0654f`, the dog's **orange harness** `#e8892f` on a black coat, the warm-white glow. The mats are muted EVA tones (teal `#4f8f8a`, terracotta `#b8654a`, ochre `#c9a24a`, slate `#6d7a86`), never brighter than the girl's face. The light marks the chapters: gold in B01, flatter and a touch cooler after the wait (B02, the setback), deep gold for the win (B03 end–B04).
- **Camera language.** Painted 3D camera in metres, every shot a small eased move (yaw arc 6–10°, push 5–15 %), large moves only for the hook wide, the L08 slide and the L10 crane. Mostly low: the dog's eye height (0.3–0.6 m) when the dog works, the girl's eye height (1.0–1.2 m) when she plots. One action axis: the **trail line** from the swing (screen left) to the tree (screen right). Every camera stays on the open-lawn side (+z) so the tree is always screen right; the two exceptions are end-on inserts named in the sheets (B02-SH03, B03-SH02).
- **Conceal and reveal.** The mat stack is scenery by the fence from frame 0 (CL02) and gets no push until L07. The dog's failure appears only in W-MATS / L08. The girl's hiding spot is always the tree; she is never shown to the dog's side of the trunk.
- **Things not to show.** No breed cue that reads "bloodhound, the best tracker" (F011): no long ears, no wrinkles. No glow on any print during the wait (W-WAIT, [N4](script.md#n4)) and no glow anywhere the dog is not sniffing. No realistic scent trail, haze or particle stream: the glow is a small comic halo plus scent curls, plainly a drawing ([fact-check Q6](fact-check.md#answers-to-preaws-questions)). No on-screen text, numbers or labels besides subtitles and the channel mark; the stopwatch digits are a prop in picture.

## Sound direction

- **Music.** One family: a sneaky hide-and-seek chamber bed, pizzicato strings + soft marimba + light woodblock, about 100–104 bpm, energy 2 of 5 under narration. Candidate `bed_comic-pizz-marimba-pulse_01` (library, 104 bpm, 56 s, not listened: Ton confirms by ear that it reads "tiptoe, sly" and that it can stop and restart cleanly; otherwise **M1 NEEDS-GEN** to that description). Arc: B01 tiptoe bed that pauses on "รู้ได้ไง" and steps up on the dog's turn; B02 thins to a sly two-note figure (L04), drops out for the wait (ticks take over), goes to near-silence for W-SNIFF, returns on the turn; B03 bright pluck on the idea, quick montage rhythm for W-MATS, **stops dead** when the dog sits; B04 a warm resolve (**M2 NEEDS-GEN**: nylon guitar + pizzicato, 3–4 bars resolving on a major chord at "ต่างหากที่ซ่อนเธอ").
- **Ending sound ([UT15](../../../../channels/edu/channel.md#ut-edu-end-tag), Film's choice).** **E1 NEEDS-GEN "the glow motif, unscrambled":** the five glow tones play once jumbled (high, low, high, low, mid) on celesta, then rise in order and land on a warm marimba-and-guitar major chord, 2.4 s, over the hold, with field ambience fading under `A_fade`. It closes the story's idea in sound (the order is the arrow) and is distinct from the EP02 tag.
- **Ambience per location.** A1 training field late afternoon (NEEDS-GEN): light wind in short grass, two or three distant bird calls, a far-off kid on a playground, very sparse; continuous across all field shots with the event clock, lower under narration. A2 soi at dusk inside the L03b inset (NEEDS-GEN): wok sizzle and a street-stall hum, distant motorbike, warm and close; it plays only while the vignette is open.
- **SFX language.** Foley first, cartoon-light: sniff bursts (quick in B01, slow and deliberate in W-SNIFF), paws on grass, harness clip jingle, long-line swish, girl's sneaker patter, mat slaps on grass, a rubbery scrape when mats are swapped, a stopwatch click and fast ticking for the time-lapse. **Glow tones** (G1–G5, NEEDS-GEN as a pitched set): soft celesta/glass plinks, one per print as it lights, rising a pentatonic step per print in true order (the sound doubles the picture, it never carries the clue); on the mats they jump high-low with no line. Comic accents are few: a smug little ding on V03, a soft deflation "bonk" on V04, a bright idea pluck on L07.
- **Silence policy.** Two silences are story: W-SNIFF (music out, only slow sniffs and grass, the few seconds must be felt) and the dog's head tilt in B03-SH07 (music out, one whine, then stillness; no comic stinger). Narration is never under a loud cue; girl lines play in the clear.
- **Ducking.** Music −8 dB under NAR and girl lines; A1 −6 dB under NAR; glow tones and sniffs ride above the music bed but under the voice.

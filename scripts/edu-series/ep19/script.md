## Story core

- **Logline:** at a busy condo pool a boy splashes and yells "ช่วยด้วย!" as a game and everyone looks at him. Three metres from the woman, a man stands upright in the deep end, head back, silent. Real drowning is usually quiet: his mouth is barely above the water, so the air he gets goes to breathing, none left to shout; his arms press on the water to keep his head up, so they barely come up to wave. And it may last under two minutes. She runs to the edge; jumping in could pull her under too, so she shouts for help, throws the ring buoy and pulls him in from the deck. The one who can still shout still has air. The quiet one is the one with none left.
- **Central question:** why does someone drowning three metres away look like someone playing?
- **Intention / obstacle:** `woman-curly` is lying on a lounger at a crowded Sunday pool (she wants a quiet afternoon). Obstacles: the noise points her the wrong way (the boy), the man makes no sound, the clock, and her own urge to jump in.
- **Feeling:** a jolt at the hook; unease as the camera finds the still head; "oh, that's why" at the mouth and the arms; dread at the clock; a gasp when she almost jumps; relief at the throw; a quiet chill at the flip.
- **Takeaway:** a person in real trouble in the water is often quiet and upright, not waving or shouting; watch for the quiet one, and help from the edge (shout, throw, reach), not by jumping in.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "คนจมน้ำจริง ๆ… เงียบ" (real drowning is quiet). L03 states it as the reason we miss him (a cost: silence hides him). L04–L07 explain the silence. L13–L14 flip it: the shout we all turned to means air is left; the silence is the alarm.

## Truth and ending

- **Spoken as settled, plain:** people who are drowning usually do not wave or shout (F01, "มัก", L03); his arms press the water to keep the head up, so they barely come up to wave (F05, "แทบ", L07); a bystander who jumps in may be grabbed and pulled under (F12, "อาจ", L11); shout for help, throw something that floats, or reach (F11, L12).
- **Hypothesis kept with one narrow hedge (ER10):** the reason he cannot shout is Pia's account (F04): L04 "แทบไม่พ้นน้ำ" carries the hedge, L05 states the consequence. Boat decides whether L05 needs its own hedge or is cut to the observation only ([Q1](#open-questions)).
- **Measured order of magnitude, hedged:** L09 "อาจไม่ถึงสองนาที" (F03, SRC001 pilot: unrescued mean 92 s, all within 2 min). Never "under a minute".
- **Flip as a model line:** L13–L14 (F13). L13 carries the coordinator's clause "ก็ต้องช่วย" so a shouting swimmer is not read as safe; L14 says only that the quiet one has no air left to shout.
- **Dramatization only:** the pool, the three characters, the boy's play shout, the man's posture and the near-jump, the throw and the pull (OT01 "ภาพจำลอง"). L02 "คุณจะคิดว่า เขากำลังเล่นน้ำอยู่" is the scene's framing, not a statistic (F07).
- **Not used (cut, not hedged; ER07):** "no splashing" (7a), "20–60 s" and "under a minute" (7b, F03), "everyone drowns this way" (7c, F08), "climbing a ladder" (7d, F06), "silent drowning" as a term (SRC011), "children go quiet" (7f), Pia's pool-edge push-up analogy (research Q2; no line needed it), any Thai statistic and "ช่วงปิดเทอม" (F09, F10; no line implies pools are where Thai children drown), any rescue that enters the water.
- **Knowledge twist (rule flip, ER03):** the viewer is planted with the loud one (V01, K01) as the emergency. The silence first reads as calm (L02) and then as the reason (L03–L07). The flip (L13–L14) turns the loud shout into a sign of air left, and the silence into the alarm. Fair clues: the boy's shout (K01) and the still head in the far corner of the very first wide (K02), visible before L02 points to it; the ring buoy on the fence (K03); the first real "ช่วยด้วย" in the video comes from the woman on the deck, never from the man (K04).
- **Ending (closed):** the man at the edge, holding the ring, coughing, alive; the woman kneeling beside him on the deck; the boy behind has stopped splashing and stares. L14 lands; hold ~2.5 s. No call to action, no tip in the last line.

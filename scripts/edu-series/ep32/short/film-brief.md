## Visual intent

- **The joke the picture tells (R2).** The CIA's plan is a neat paper model on a desk; the real world does not follow the model. B01–B02 are the **plan**: a filed report with paper cut-outs under a desk lamp (the cat already the one piece that will not stay in its slot). On "แต่" (L06) the camera dives into the pop-up park and the paper falls away: the **test** happens in a real field at full scale, where the same coats now sit on blank mannequins and a real cat chooses a patch of sun. The story ends back at the same desk, now seen at full scale: the memo lies there, and the real cat is asleep on it in the lamp's warm pool, where the paper cat stood at the start. The comedy aims at the plan and the agency, never at the cat.
- **Audience knowledge.** f0 already shows the strange thing: a cat cut-out on a spy blueprint (hook proof). The bridge tells the viewer, without words, "this was the plan; now reality". By L08 the viewer has watched the real cat choose the sun over the target, so the CIA's own admission lands as confirmation. L11–L13 are the real words (memo, CIA page) on real paper on the real desk.
- **Mood.** Cold War spy-movie swagger on the paper desk (low glide, warm lamp pool, red and cream poster colours), deflating into an ordinary sunny afternoon in a field, a dull institutional corridor, and a night office where a cat sleeps on top-secret paperwork.
- **Colour and value key.** Paper section B01–B02 unchanged: signal red `#c8342b`, cream `#efe4cc`, ink navy `#1f2a3a`, kraft `#b8946a`, walnut `#5a3a26`, blueprint cyan `#2f6f9e`, pop-up green `#7f9a5a`, lamp `#ffd890`, poster `Grade` + `Grain`. Real section: the project default painted grade, naturalistic. Field (B03, B04-SH01): overcast soft light `#c9cfc4` sky, mown green `#6f9150`, then a warm sun `#ffe2a0` patch and cool blue-green canopy shade. Corridor (B04-SH02/SH03): pale institutional green `#a9bfa6` walls, cream lino `#ddd3bd`, cool fluorescent `#e8f0ec`, the only warm thing the steel bowl's highlight. Office (B05–B06): the B01 desk palette at full scale (walnut, lamp warm, navy room, red folder bands and stamp), never empty black (UT03). The modern printout (B06-SH01) is the one bright neutral white page. Red stays the accent that links the plan, the target circle and the stamp.
- **Camera language.** Painted 3D camera throughout (one `YCam` per plate). Paper section: low desk-skimming eye, small eased moves, page slides. Bridge: the episode's one large move (dive into the pop-up, VD32-18). Real section: the cat's eye height in the field (0.35–0.65 m) so the world reads full-size, the agent at his own eye height, documents as close inserts; every shot keeps a small eased move (yaw arc 6–10°, push 5–15 %). Characters square to the lens (`squareToCam`), no orbit past ±40°.
- **Conceal and reveal.** The sun patch is hidden until L07 (overcast until the cloud passes in B03-SH03); the memo until L11; its verdict stamp until "ใช้งานจริงไม่ได้" in L12; the CIA page until L13; the real cat on the real desk until B06-SH02.
- **Never on screen (binding, unchanged).** A car, cab, bus, road, traffic, siren light (F006); an incision, wound, blood, operating table, instrument, monitor or anything surgical (F003); a transmitter at any body part, a collar device, a tail antenna (F002); a number of cats (F011); a named or recognisable real person, a badge or name on the agent; a printed date such as "March 1967" (N3); any text on the memo or the web page that is not the real text (SF01); "TOP SECRET" or "แมวไม่ฟัง"-type words on any memo or page in B05–B06; the CIA logo or the real site's design. R2 adds: no road or parked vehicle at the field edge, no light seam or shape behind the corridor door, no hand placing the cat.

## Sound direction

- **Music family.** One original 1960s spy-jazz cue, about 116 bpm, minor key: muted trumpet lead, bongos, walking upright bass, a twangy surf-guitar lick, vibraphone colour. Confident and a little cheesy, so the cat can deflate it. `NEEDS-GEN` (no spy-jazz bed in the library; `sound-library.py search spy|jazz|bongo`, 2026-11-21). Ton generates it as stems or sections so Editor can cut it dead.
- **Energy arc.** B01 full swagger under L01, **cut dead** after L01, L02 dry · B02 sly, walking bass and brushes only · B03 the bridge opens the sound up (below), then a ticking "mission" bed (bongos + muted guitar ticks) rising under L07, **dropping out** on "มันไปนอนตากแดด", silence before "ยกเว้น" · B04 one low suspicious bowed-bass note (library candidate `tone_bowed-bass-drone-d_01` if Ton's fit and key check pass) · B05 pompous slow brass building to L11's quote (library candidate `bed_brass-chorale-admiring_01`) · B06 the theme slowed to a lone muted trumpet, amused.
- **Ending plan (UT15, Film's choice).** A lazy three-note muted-trumpet spy tag whose last note bends down like a yawn, about 2.0 s, over the purr in the closing hold (`NEEDS-GEN`). **R3:** it starts on the first hold frame of B06-SH02 and plays across the cut, its yawning last note resolving on the B06-SH03 stamp insert; a soft tail-tip brush on paper (`NEEDS-GEN`) is the insert's only new effect.
- **The bridge in sound (R2).** Paper world = small, dry and narrow (page slides, card snaps, a park bed played thin as if from the page). On the B03-SH01 crossfade the sound **opens**: a soft rush of air like a page turned past the ear blooms into a full-width outdoor bed (breeze through leaves, two near birds, distant open space), so the ear arrives in the real field with the eye (`NEEDS-GEN`, design in the B03-SH01 row). After the bridge, foley is real-world (grass, bench wood, steel bowl, paper on a real desk); paper foley returns only for real paper (memo, printout, clipboard).
- **Ambience per location.** Paper desk (B01–B02): quiet evening office room tone with a faint lamp-bulb hum (`NEEDS-GEN`), park birds high-passed and narrow under B02 (`outdoor_park-birds-breeze_01` candidate). Field (B03, B04-SH01): open afternoon field, breeze in a few trees, one or two birds, no traffic wash (`nature_training-field-afternoon_01` candidate; Ton checks for car wash). Corridor (B04-SH02/SH03): empty 1960s institutional corridor, steady fluorescent hum, faint building air, nothing from behind the door (`NEEDS-GEN`). Office at night (B05–B06): the same room tone as B01, so the ending sounds like the opening.
- **SFX language.** B01–B02 paper foley (page slides on felt, pop-up snaps, card slides). B03–B04 real foley: cat paws on grass and on the bench slats, a small sparrow flutter and chirp, the canopy hiss of the sun coming out (a warm shimmer, not a switch), radio squelch and static on both ends of L07 (`electric_radio-static-burst_01` candidate; squelch `NEEDS-GEN`), a wooden door with a latch, a steel bowl on lino, kibble. B05–B06: typewriter strikes (`foley_typewriter-keys-run_01` candidate), rubber stamp (`foley_rubber-stamp-thunk_01` candidate), paper on a desk. Cat: one bored "mrrp" (`creature_cat-mrrp_01` candidate) at L02 and again at L14 (callback), a purr under the hold (`creature_cat-purr_01` candidate). Comic punctuation: a muted-brass deflate after L08 (`comedy_brass-note-wah-droop_01` candidate).
- **Forbidden sounds (binding).** No taxi, siren, brakes, horn, engine or traffic; no surgical sound (saw, drill, beep monitor); nothing heard behind the closed door.
- **Silence policy.** One beat of dead air after L01, half a beat after "ก็จริง…", the drop before "ยกเว้น", the "…" in L09 and L10, a full beat on the "…" of L12 before the stamp, the pull-back on the "…" of L13. Narration is never under a loud cue; music ducks under every line.

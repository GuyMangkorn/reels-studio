## Answers to Preaw's seven questions

1. **L11 bolder form ("ฝึกแมวให้เดินระยะสั้น ๆ ได้จริง… แล้วเรียกว่า 'ความสำเร็จ…'")? No, keep the safe line.** Re-read on the scan: "This is in itself a remarkable scientific achievement" sits after a redacted gap, and "Knowing that cats can indeed be trained to move short distances" is a **separate, later sentence** that begins "Knowing that", treating short-distance walking as already known. The two are probably related, but the memo does not say the achievement is the short walk, and the antecedent of "This" is hidden. The bolder form would put words in a redacted gap and would also change the joke from "praised" to "a few steps", which the memo does not say. If Preaw wants the laugh anyway: a separate, unlinked line such as "เอกสารเขียนว่า ฝึกแมวให้เดินระยะสั้น ๆ ได้" is true (readable text) but only as its own statement, never as "that is the achievement". Not needed; R1 L11 stands (supported-with-note, medium-high confidence on the referent of "การฝึกแมว", high on the quote and the verdict in L12).
2. **L13 referent: confirmed.** Opened the CIA page: the clause "something all cat owners are quite familiar with" attaches to "the problem", defined in the same sentence as the cat having a mind of its own and being impossible to control. The R1 line is accurate; the fallback "ควบคุมไม่ได้" is not needed. (Optional, N5: the real page says "impossible to control" too, so "แมว 'มีความคิดของมันเอง'" is a safe partial quote.)
3. **L03 rendering: fair, with one word to change (SF02).** "Foreign agents and their Soviet handlers" = spies (agents of other countries) and the Soviet officers running them. "สายลับ…คนคุมฝั่งโซเวียต" gives the right picture; "ในสวน" for "such as on park benches in public places" is a narrowing but not a false one. "คนคุม" can be heard as a prison guard or minder; "หัวหน้า" or "เจ้านายโซเวียต" is clearer and exact enough for a handler (the person who directs the agent).
4. **L09: one "เล่าว่า" is enough.** ER10 allows one narrow hedge inside a claim; "อดีตเจ้าหน้าที่คนหนึ่งเล่าว่า" opens a single sentence whose every clause is reported speech, the closed-door picture shows nothing, and L10 is plainly a joke. The risks are elsewhere: "แล้วก็…" must not run into L08 without a breath (the hedge resets it), and "เดินสาย" (SF04). Marchetti stays unnamed; the line must not drop the hedge in a trim.
5. **Taxi left out: acceptable, no replacement needed.** Neither version has a primary document; the CIA page calls the cab story a 1979 joke, the other side is one recollection chain (F006). Leaving it out asserts nothing false and avoids a 6 s attributed dispute. The no-taxi sound and picture limits in script.md stand. Keep the dispute in the description line only.
6. **Label wording: covers the gag and the generic agent lines, not enough on its own (SF01).** "เรื่องจริง เล่าแบบขำ · ภาพและบทพูดจำลอง" says the story is real, told as a comedy, with pictures and spoken lines that are staged; it does not make the quoted fact lines look invented because it labels pictures and character speech, not the narrator. Weak points: it is a first-frame label, and the L07 gag is 20 s later and runs straight into "CIA ยอมรับเองว่า" (Q7).
7. **L07 beside L08: plain enough as a joke, but tag it.** A cat that lies in the sun is absurd and the radio line is stylised, so the gag reads as comic. The risk is that it looks like a re-enactment of the field test the CIA then "admits" in the next line. Recommendation SF01: a small tag on screen during L07 ("ฉากจำลอง" or "ฉากแต่งขำ") plus the persistent episode label; no sound that implies a real recording (the radio squelch is already marked stylised).

## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** The viewer starts where the owner is: a loving, wide-eyed gaze must be love. The cat's rejection (CL01) plants doubt; the cat-side reframe flips it (the stare may read as pressure); the counted slow blink is the fix; the cat's half-close answer is the small win; the closing blink-back is the warm glow. Feelings in order: a laugh of recognition (B01), "oh no" (B02), calm (B03), a held breath then delight (B04), quiet warmth (B05). Every cat action, the glare repaint, the glow, the chime and the purr are **dramatization**; the two study lines (L09–L10) are spoken over story picture, never over a study graphic.
- **Story first.** A woman in her mid-forties, home after work, about 21:00 in a small Bangkok condo living room. She has changed into soft lounge clothes: a loose oatmeal knit cardigan over a dusty-rose T-shirt, grey lounge trousers, cream socks. She is a devoted, slightly over-eager cat person: she sits on a two-seat teal sofa under a warm floor lamp, her orange tabby on the other cushion, and she cannot stop gazing at it. The cat is an ordinary, healthy house cat with its own mind: at first relaxed with soft half-closed eyes (CL03), then pressed by her stare, it flattens its ears and moves to the little rattan armchair beside the sofa, sitting half-turned away. Later, when she sits back and slowly blinks, it looks back, softens its eyes, walks over along the sofa arm, rubs its head into her hand and curls up beside her. The room is lived-in and calm: a throw on the sofa arm, a mug on a low round table, a feather toy on the rug, a wall clock, a window with the city's night glow.
- **Medium.** Painted 2D, p5.brush, [painted 3D camera](../../../docs/film-visual-brief-workflow.md#painted-3d-camera) (project default, user 5 Nov 2026): the living room built in world metres, seen through the kit yaw camera (`kit/ycam.ts`), the camera moving inside the set every shot; the owner is the 4-head kit rig as a flat card in one of four views squared to the lens (`squareToCam`); the cat is the kit animal rig (`left`/`right`/`front`). The **eye close-ups are their own small painted sets** in centimetre units (the EP02 eye-macro convention): her eye (reusing the EP02 cook's eye plate, which is the same person) and a new cat-face close plate. Backgrounds are washes without outlines; characters, sofa edge and hero props get one thin ink line and one cel shade. No SVG characters; no UI.
- **The eyes are the argument.** The film alternates two pairs of eyes. Her eye: wide (B01) → hard glare (B02, imagined) → slow blink (B03) → soft smile (B05). The cat's face: tension (B01) → turned away → answer half-close (B04) → blink-back (B05). Same framing each time, a different signal each time; the alternation carries the knowledge.

- **Light arc.** B01 warm lamp, cosy; B02 the glare state cools only her (and her frame), hard shadow from the brow, then drains back; B03 warm again, a touch dimmer and calmer; B04 the silent beat keeps the lamp but the halo breathes up on the cat's half-close; the cutaway is cool daylight (another day, another person); B05 the warmest frames of the film, the window glow dimmer so the lamp pool holds both of them. Light carries feeling only; nothing in the light is a claim.
- **Camera language.** Vertical 1080×1920, eye level of the subject: seated owner eye ~1.12 m, cat on a cushion eye ~0.70 m, two-shots at ~0.95 m between them. Close and intimate ([UT13](../../../docs/user-taste.md#ut-close-camera)): two-shots r 1.2–1.5 m, face close-ups r 0.6–0.9 m; the wider frames are B03-SH01 (r ~2.0 m), which shows the "about a metre" gap between them, and B05-SH01 (the same framing for L11, user Q-R1 2026-11-07). Moves are small: slow arcs (≤ 15° per shot), slow pushes, one truck following the cat's walk. No shake, no whip, no zoom across scales (room ↔ eye plates is always a cut). The cat-side frames (B02-SH01, B02-SH03, B04-SH02) sit low at the cat's eye height near the armchair: a **vantage**, never a cat-vision filter (fact-check N4c).
- **Frame zones.** Captions: white with the edu stroke, no overlay band (series default), `captionStyle.bottomEdgeY` 1344, ≤ 2 lines, band y 1176–1344; eyes, faces, the hand contact and the cat's head stay above y 1160 in every shot. `@TamMaiLa` channel mark top-left (`video.chrome` default); nothing important above y 140 (cat ear tips included).
- **Conceal / reveal.** CL03 (the cat's soft half-closed eyes before her lean-in) is visible in B01-SH01 but never centred, lit or held on. CL02 (her unblinking eyes) is shown in the first second: her rig face holds `eyes: 'wide'` with **no blink** through B01 (the scene does not call `blinking()`). The glare repaint starts on or after "ลองนึก…" (fact-check condition). The cat's answer (B04-SH01) is the first time the cat looks straight at her after V01.
- **Truth limits on picture** (fact-check, binding): no study graphic, chart, caption or on-screen text in L09–L10, and nothing that implies a stare-vs-blink test (the study's control was "looking away"); the cutaway shows an **unfamiliar person's hand**, the owner is never the subject of the approach finding; the cat's answer and the closing blink are drawn as a **half-close** (lid ≤ 0.6), not a full close; no hearts, sparkles or text that assert love; no caption like "one blink = a reply" or "it loves her"; the glare is painterly (light and shadow on her own eyes), not a colour/blur "how cats see" filter; the purr is mood, never explained.

Library: `reveal_glass-shimmer-soft_01` is the **glass chime** candidate on the cat's half-close (B04-SH01; "soft glassy shimmer accent"), and a quieter copy is the optional closing chime (Editor's call at mix, script Q6); `ui_notification-ding_01` rejected (reads as a phone). **NEEDS-GEN (SFX):** (S1) cat "mrrp", short annoyed chirp-grumble, close, dry, 0.6 s (Stable Audio as `sfx`; a licensed real recording is acceptable as fiction, fact-check V01); (S2) soft cat purr, close, steady, loopable 8 s (V02); (S3) soft paw landing thump on a fabric cushion, 0.3 s; (S4) light cat paw steps on a sofa arm and cushion, 2.5 s; (S5) small cat sniffs, 2–3 quick soft sniffs, 0.8 s; (S6) fur rubbing against a palm, two soft rubs, 1.2 s; (S7) sofa creak + cushion compress as she leans in, 0.8 s; (S8) cushion settle as the cat curls up, 0.8 s. ## Technical handoff

- **Scale baseline** as in the tables above (room in metres; EYE and CATFACE at 1 set-m ≈ 1 cm). Pixel size from `camUnit(cam, point)`, never a fixed scale. Seated values for `woman-curly` are estimates from the 4-head rig (1.62 m, head 0.405 m): Boss measures the seated eye line on the first plate and reports it; the scene sheets then use the measured value.
- **Projection contract** as above: one yaw camera per shot, flat cards squared to the mid-shot camera, the visitor's forearm as a world-space card, the glow motes as a screen-space light layer, close plates as separate sets reached by a cut.
- **Depth / layers.** Painter's order by `camDist`: window/sky → back wall, print, clock → lamp halo (behind actors, `paintHalo` painted into the plate) → sofa back → owner and cat in camDist order (the cat on the right cushion is behind her lean-in arm when they overlap; the cat on the arm is in front of the sofa back) → sofa arm front faces and cushions' front edges → armchair → coffee table, mug → rug foreground → glow motes → captions (stroke, no shade band) → chrome (DF-20261030-painted-layer-order). Large wall, floor and sofa fills stay washes (DF-20261030-p5brush-large-fill-mottle); the teal sofa gets an opaque base before its glaze (DF-20261031-painted-opaque-cast).
- **Contact.** Her hips on the seat plane, feet on the floor (soft contact shadow, DF-20261030-zcpaint-6head-seated-lead for seated lead and floor contact); the cat's paws on the cushion / armchair seat / sofa arm top (contact shadow on fabric, small); the head-rub contact: the cat's `top` anchor meets her palm point within ±6 px; the tail tip lies on her knee anchor in B05-SH02; the visitor's palm stays still (no drift > 2 px/frame). Her arms in depth: elbows back, never splayed (DF-20261030-painted-arm-depth) in the lean-in and the offered palm.
- **Motion / clock.** Shot-local event clocks for the lid curves (EYE and CATFACE), the glare grade, pupils, whiskers, ears, head yaw, the step-up, the walk, the sniff, the rub, the curl and the glow motes (seeded, deterministic on seek).

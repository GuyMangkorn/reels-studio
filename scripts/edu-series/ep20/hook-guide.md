## The opening contract

**Approach: question/prediction.** Ask the viewer to choose a direction, let the passenger commit to the obvious guess, then visibly disprove it. The promise is a directional surprise, not an abstract physics lesson.

Proposed spoken opening, L01:

> รถเร่ง...ลูกโป่งจะเอนไปทางไหน?

L02 immediately identifies the buoyant helium balloon, low tether and closed cabin. In the first approximately 7 s, the viewer must understand the car's front/rear, that it is accelerating, and that the balloon floats but cannot fly loose. Direction cues should be readable from frame 0; the question begins inside the first 2 s. No greeting, title card or mechanism terminology before the prediction.

Do not ask the viewer to perform a driving experiment. The demonstration is staged; the restrained passenger observes and the driver never handles the balloon.

## Promise, payment and next question

| Phase | Script anchors / estimated placement | Editorial purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prediction | L01–L03, 0–12 s | Invite a guess; identify the special balloon and compare with the passenger's relative backward tendency. Keep the same continuous situation visible. |
| First payment | L04, 12–14 s | The balloon leans toward the front. Pay the actual hook by roughly the first 30% of the estimated 47 s runtime. Do not hide the answer until the final second. |
| Renew curiosity | L05, 14–16.5 s | “What pushes it?” grows directly from the observed result. |
| One-cause answer | L06–L07, 16.5–23 s | Surrounding-air pressure, in one short explanation. No effective-gravity, inertia or pressure-gradient terminology lecture. |
| Counterexample | L08–L10, 23–34 s | Constant straight speed after settling tests whether speed was the cause. |
| Final application | L11–L13, 34–44 s | Ask again during forward-travel braking, then reverse the passenger/balloon contrast; L13 names the side that now pushes harder (front over rear). Hold about 3 s. |

These times are estimated. The script's open-question map, not this Guide, owns line timing. Another agent must recount/reproject at the eventual selected voice setting before lock.

## Protect these boundaries

- Keep **helium, floating, tethered below, enclosed cabin, straight travel and acceleration**. Ordinary air-filled balloons are not a swap. Front/rear means relative to the cabin, not a balloon outrunning the car.
- Preserve L09's settled interval. Constant straight speed removes this horizontal bias only after residual swinging; no instant upright snap or measured angle.
- The explanation includes the surrounding air. No claim that helium lacks inertia, no giant wall of air or cabin gale. Explanatory graphics are schematics, not motion measurements.
- Braking is backward acceleration while still moving forward. Keep the opposite tendency, not an exact transient trace. No unsafe demonstration invitation.
- The final reversal belongs late. Revealing both acceleration and braking in the opening would spend the strongest payoff before the viewer has used the rule.

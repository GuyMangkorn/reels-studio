# EDU-EP15-SHORT — research packet (W1.1, Boat)

- Deliverable: `EDU-EP15-SHORT` (Short, Thai, educational, concept C43 "จิ้งจกบนเพดาน ห้อยคุณไว้ได้ทั้งตัว", `scripts/edu-series/concepts.md` `### C43`). Research date: 2026-11-10. Mode: non-fiction explainer with one imagined shot ("ภาพจำลอง"). No script is written here.
- Short answer:
  1. **The hook's species must change.** Every whole-animal number belongs to the **tokay gecko (ตุ๊กแก, Gekko gecko)**. I found no measured or estimated whole-animal figure for the Thai house gecko (จิ้งจก, Hemidactylus frenatus), which is much smaller (7.5–15 cm, SRC013). "จิ้งจกบนเพดาน… ห้อยตัวคุณไว้ได้" as written attaches a tokay number to the wrong animal. `unchecked` for จิ้งจก, `model` for ตุ๊กแก.
  2. **"Could hold a person" is a tokay upper-bound calculation with a count dispute.** Single seta: 194 µN ± 25 (N = 28), about 10 times what whole-animal data predicted (SRC001, SRC002). Autumn and Peattie 2002: 6.5 million setae of one tokay "attached maximally could generate 130 kg force" (SRC003). The 2000 press release says about 2 million setae (SRC002); 2 M x 194 µN is about 390 N, about 40 kg (own arithmetic). So the supported range is **about 40 to 130 kg, all setae at once, never observed**. A live tokay's measured clinging is far lower (about 20 N for two front feet, secondary, SRC009). "About one adult" sits inside the range; "a person" at 60–70 kg is safe only with "ประมาณ" and "ถ้าทุกเส้นจับพร้อมกัน".
  3. **No glue, no suction: `settled`.** Dry adhesion, van der Waals, shown by adhesion on both water-repelling and water-loving surfaces (SRC004). **Caveat:** later single-spatula work finds humidity matters, through adsorbed water layers, so "van der Waals only" is too strong; "capillary action is not needed" is the defensible form (SRC005, SRC004 follow-up). Electrostatics was ruled out in 2000 with an ionized-air test, with a later challenge (SRC001, SRC006).
  4. **Toe peeling: `settled`.** The toes curl up and back, the setae lever up to about 30°, and the spatulae let go; release in about 15 ms (SRC003 abstract), step interval about 20 ms (SRC007).
  5. **Self-cleaning: `settled` for tokay.** Dirty setae recover grip after a few steps (SRC008). So "walks across dust" is supported, "never gets dirty" is not.
  6. **Dust-on-screen analogy: weak.** Dust clinging to a screen is mainly static electricity, and the gecko's grip is not (SRC001). Keep it as an everyday "tiny things cling" image only, or cut it (see Wording fixes).
- Tooling limits: web search excerpts plus fetched pages. Autumn et al. 2000 (Nature) full text sat behind a login redirect; I read its abstract in a catalogue and a press release. Two PDFs fetched as binary and unreadable (no PDF tools here). No Thai source consulted. One search result page carried an embedded instruction; I ignored it.

## Wording fixes for Preaw (C43 and the hook)

1. **Hook species (must-fix).** "จิ้งจกบนเพดานบ้านคุณ… ห้อยตัวคุณไว้ได้ทั้งตัว" claims the figure for the wrong animal. Safe options:
   - A: "ตุ๊กแกบนเพดานบ้านคุณ… เท้ามันไม่มีกาว ไม่มีแรงดูด แต่ถ้าทุกเส้นขนจับพร้อมกัน แรงพอจะห้อยคนไว้ได้ทั้งตัว" (names the tokay; "ถ้า… พอจะ" carries the model status).
   - B: keep the จิ้งจก in the opening scene (it is what lives on a Thai ceiling) and name the animal behind the number at the reveal: "จิ้งจกใช้หลักเดียวกับตุ๊กแก… ตุ๊กแกตัวเดียว ถ้าทุกเส้นจับพร้อมกัน…". Needs "หลักเดียวกัน" to be hedged: house geckos share the dry-hair adhesion type but I found no spatula-level source for them, only the foot structure leads (SRC012, SRC014). Prefer "จิ้งจกและตุ๊กแกเกาะด้วยขนละเอียดเหมือนกัน" only as `lead-only`; A is safest.
2. **"ทั้งตัว" and "ห้อยคุณไว้" (optional).** Keep "ห้อย" imagined; the picture is labelled "ภาพจำลอง" as the pitch says. If a number is spoken: "หลายสิบกิโล" is inside every estimate; "130 กิโล" is the top, from the larger setae count; avoid it.
3. **Beat 3 (optional, analogy).** Dust-on-screen is a misleading-analogy because screen dust sticks mostly by static, and the gecko's grip is not static. Options: (a) cut the screen and use the pull of two flat wet-free surfaces, (b) say "เหมือนฝุ่นที่ติดจอ" only as a feeling and not as the cause. Do not say the two share a cause. Clarity pass: "ใกล้จนมีแรงดึงเล็ก ๆ ทุกจุด และรวมกันเป็นแรงมาก" is plain and true.
4. **Beat 4 (optional).** "many times a second" -> "ในเสี้ยววินาที". "Lets go at once" is fine; peeling is not instant for the whole toe.
5. **Last line (optional).** "เก่งที่สุดในบ้านคุณ" is an unranked superlative; swap to "เก่งที่สุดคนหนึ่ง" or "เก่งกว่าที่คิด". Not a wrong takeaway, so optional.
6. **Beat 2 "leave nothing behind"** (optional). Not tested here; use "ไม่มีกาว".

## Premise, reveal and learning impact

- **Premise ("no glue, no suction") holds.** Beats 1, 2 and 4 hold.
- **Hook and reveal are impacted (must-fix item 1).** Whole-animal number is tokay only; the reveal image (man hanging from one gecko) stays valid if the animal is called a ตุ๊กแก or the text says "ตุ๊กแก" at the reveal. The imagined shot's gecko art (new: house gecko) should then be a tokay look or be tagged as a stand-in; that is Film's choice, not a fact issue.
- **Learning:** one rule: the closer the contact, the stronger the hold, and release is by peeling. Both are `settled`. The "person" number is `model`.
- Not changed: plot, order of beats, the flip in beat 4, ending.

## Uncertainties and access gaps

- Nature 2000 full text not read; per-seta figures and the 30° angle taken from abstract-level text and SRC002/SRC003.
- The 2 M vs 6.5 M seta count (and SRC007's 200,000 per toe, SRC008's 14,400/mm²) is an unresolved dispute in the secondary material; the resulting range is 40 to 130 kg. Qualitative confidence: high that the order is tens of kg, low on the exact figure.
- No source on house gecko whole-animal adhesion; SRC012 and SRC014 are search excerpts with unconfirmed citations.
- Screen-dust cause rests on my knowledge, not on an inspected source.
- Thai species names not checked against ราชบัณฑิตยสภา.
- Not checked: why geckos fall on people, tail-dropping, tape products (out of scope per C43).

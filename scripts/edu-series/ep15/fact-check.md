## Answers to Preaw's worry list

**A1. L09 contrast: does it imply dust and gecko stick the same way? Pass; optional soften (O3).** The narrator says one thing about dust ("พอเข้าใจ" = I get that) and asks a separate question about the hairs. "แต่" reads as contrast, not as "same cause". Two residual risks, both small. (a) "พอเข้าใจ" suggests the viewer understands why dust clings; screen dust is mostly static, which the gecko's grip is not (SRC001; my knowledge for the screen, not an inspected source). (b) L10–L11 then say "ดึงกันนิดนึง", and a viewer may link it back to the TV fluff. That second link is not false: fine dust also clings through short-range surface forces, so no wrong takeaway forms. Optional wording that keeps the feeling without "I understand why": "ฝุ่นเกาะจอทีวีน่ะเห็นบ่อย… แต่ขนแห้ง ๆ เกาะเพดานกลับหัวได้ยังไง" (same length). Keep the picture as fluff or lint, not as a charged screen. T1 in the script's N1 (cut the first half) is also safe.

**A2. One hedge in L02 plus the condition in L01: enough for "about one person"? Yes.** The claim is a model, never observed, and the script marks that three ways: the condition "ถ้า… ทุกเส้น… พร้อมกัน", the soft "แรงพอจะ… ประมาณ", and "ภาพจำลอง" on the picture. ER10 wants one narrow hedge; this has it. The figure is safe because "a person" sits inside 40–130 kg and no number is spoken. The one thing that would break it is dropping "ถ้า" (or "ทุกเส้น") in a trim or in a subtitle; keep L01 and L02 as one continuous sentence in the subtitles and do not cut "ทุกเส้น". Do not move "ภาพจำลอง" off the L02 tease. Wording to avoid anywhere else (on the cover, description, comments): "ตุ๊กแกห้อยคนได้" as a plain fact (see T1).

**A3. L07 "สี่เท้ารวมกันหลายล้านเส้น": pass.** Sources give about 2 M, about 4 M and 6.5 M setae for the whole animal; "หลายล้าน" covers 2–6.5 M. It is the whole animal, so "สี่เท้ารวมกัน" is the right scope. Not `must-fix`: the count dispute is a factor of 3 and no number is spoken. Optional (O2): if a listener hears "หลายล้าน" as at least 3 M, the 2 M figure is slightly under it; "เป็นล้านเส้น" is the loosest fully safe form, but the current wording is acceptable.

**A4. L11 "ล้าน ๆ จุด": direction true, optional fix (O4).** Spatulae (the tips that touch) number about 1 billion by SRC002, and 2–6.5 M setae times 100–1,000 tips gives roughly 200 million to several billion. "ล้าน ๆ" in casual Thai means "many millions, loads", so it is not false, but it sits 2–3 orders below the real count if heard as a few million, and a few listeners may hear it as "ล้านล้าน" (trillions). The real number is a cleaner figure than the sentence. Not `must-fix` (a loose plural, same direction, takeaway "tiny pulls add up" unchanged). Suggested wording, +1 syllable: "ชิดขนาดนั้น จะดึงกันนิดนึง… นับพันล้านจุดรวมกันก็แรงมาก" ("นับพันล้าน" is supported by SRC002 "roughly a billion"). A number-free alternative: "หลายร้อยล้านจุด" (floor of the range). Keep the on-screen glints as unlabelled "many", no number on screen.

**A5. L05 against the partial dust recovery (36 % after 8 steps): optional but recommended (O1).** The spoken line says the dust drops off within a few steps and the foot is fine; the paper says recovery is partial and slow in live digits. "ไม่กี่ก้าว" (8 steps is not "a few" next to a 36 % figure with huge scatter) and "หลุดเอง" (all of it) overstate. The picture (some specks fall, foot not spotless) is already right. The test used 2.5 µm ceramic beads on a fully contaminated digit, not household dust; extending it to a dusty ceiling is a fair simplification but a simplification. Not `must-fix`: the takeaway "dust does not stop it and it cleans itself as it walks" is the paper's own message, and it is not "never dirty". Suggested wording (about +2 syllables, no hedge word needed): "แต่ตุ๊กแกเดินต่อได้ ฝุ่นร่วงออกทีละก้าว". If Preaw keeps the original, the only lost accuracy is "ไม่กี่", so a one-word swap is the minimum: "ฝุ่นหลุดเองทีละก้าว".

**A6. The peel line "หลุดในเสี้ยววินาที": pass.** SRC003: detach "in just 15 ms"; SRC007: step interval about 20 ms; SRC011 cites about 50 ms. Every one of these is a fraction of a second, so "เสี้ยววินาที" is true across the range. The refrain flip is also true: peeling lifts the setae so the tips lose close contact and the foot releases. The picture "row by row" is the peel model (SRC007). No per-second rate is spoken, as intended.

**A7. The closing line: pass.** "เก่งขนาดนี้" is not a ranking (CL12 resolved) and "ไม่ได้ใช้กาวสักหยดเลย" is CL01 (no glue; the pad is dry hairs, SRC004, SRC008). It does not say "leave nothing behind". No hedge needed. Wording that stays out: "เก่งที่สุด", "ไม่ทิ้งอะไรไว้".

**A8. Working title "ตุ๊กแกบนเพดาน ห้อยคนได้ทั้งตัว": fails as a plain claim, `must-fix` for title and cover only (T1).** Flat "ห้อยคนได้" says the real animal can do it. The only support is a never-observed all-at-once calculation; live clinging was measured far lower (about 20 N for two front feet, secondary SRC009, `lead-only`), and SRC002 says geckos use a fraction. The script is hedged; a title carries no "ถ้า". It names the right species (tokay) and so avoids the CL06 problem. Safe forms, in order:
1. **"ถ้าตุ๊กแกจับเต็มแรง ห้อยคนได้ทั้งตัว"** (condition in the headline; no other hedge needed).
2. **"ตุ๊กแกบนเพดาน ห้อยคนได้ทั้งตัว?"** with "ภาพจำลอง" on the cover picture of the hanging man. A question mark is the minimum; acceptable only with the label.
3. **"แรงเกาะของตุ๊กแก รวมกันห้อยคนได้ทั้งตัว"** (describes the combined potential force, not a feat; plain, no "if").
Not safe: the flat title; "ตุ๊กแกห้อยคนได้จริง"; any "จิ้งจก" version (CL06).

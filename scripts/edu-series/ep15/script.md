## Story core

- **Logline:** the tokay on your ceiling has no glue and no suction on its feet, yet if every hair under its toes gripped at once it could hang about one person. A suction cup would drop off a dusty ceiling; the tokay keeps walking, and the dust drops off its feet a step at a time. Under each toe are rows of tiny hairs, millions over four feet, each split into hundreds of tips. The tips are so fine they press right along the ceiling, and that close any two things pull on each other a little; a billion or so little pulls add up. The closer it touches, the harder it holds. That same rule is how it lets go: it curls its toes up from the tip like peeling tape, the hairs stop touching closely, and the foot comes free in a split second.
- **Central question:** how can dry feet with no glue and no suction hold that much, and still walk?
- **Intention / obstacle:** the woman wants to understand why the tokay does not fall; her first guess (suction cup) fails on the dusty ceiling. Then the puzzle turns: a grip that strong should trap the tokay, and the answer is the same rule turned around.
- **Feeling:** a small "huh" at the ceiling; disbelief at "one person"; mild gross-out turning into wonder at the zoom; satisfaction at "ยิ่งแนบชิด… ยิ่งเกาะแน่น"; a laugh at the hanging man begging "อย่าปล่อยนะ"; calm at the last line.
- **Takeaway:** a tokay's feet stick dry, with no glue and no suction: millions of very fine hairs press so close to the surface that tiny pulls add up. The closer the contact, the stronger the hold, and it lets go by peeling its toes up from the tip.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "ยิ่งแนบชิด… ยิ่งเกาะแน่น". L10–L11 build it, L12 says it (why it sticks); L15 flips it ("พอขนไม่แนบชิด… ก็หลุด", how it lets go); W-HANG plays the flip as a joke (a toe lifts, the man panics).

- **Knowledge twist (rule flip, ER03):** close contact = grip (B03), so the obvious next problem is that it should be stuck (B04 "แต่…"); the same rule run backwards (lift the edge, lose the closeness) is the release. Fair clues: dust on the ceiling (K01) and the tokay's toes curled up at rest (K02), both in shot 1.
- **Ending (closed):** W-HANG pays the hook in picture; the real tokay peels and lunges for the moth; L16 lands; V03 call; hold ~2 s. No call to action, no tip.

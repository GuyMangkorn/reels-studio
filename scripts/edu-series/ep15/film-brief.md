## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Every Thai viewer has seen a ตุ๊กแก or จิ้งจก on the ceiling at night and never asked why it does not fall. The first frame must say *a living room at night, someone on the sofa, a big lizard on the ceiling by the lamp* with no words (UT01). Feelings by beat: a small "huh" at the ceiling → disbelief at "one person" (the absurd hang tease) → recognition (no glue, she guesses suction) → a little comic deflation (the plunger drops) → mild gross-out turning into wonder as we dive under the toe → "huh?" at the dry hairs → satisfaction at "ยิ่งแนบชิด… ยิ่งเกาะแน่น" → a second puzzle ("then how does it walk?") → the click of the peel → a laugh at the hanging man's panic → calm warmth as the tokay settles and calls.
- **Story first (designed before the kit was opened).** **WOMAN** is in her mid-forties, at home late on a weeknight (about 21:10 by the wall clock), in loose home clothes, half lying on the sofa scrolling her phone after dinner: the person who would notice the tokay only when it moves. She is curious, not scared: she looks up, guesses, leans in, smiles at the end. The **room** is the living room of an older Bangkok townhouse: a teal fabric sofa with a mustard throw, a low wooden coffee table with a plate of sliced guava, a glass of water and the TV remote, cream ceramic floor tiles, cream-yellow walls with a wall clock, a framed family photo and a textless calendar, an aluminium sliding window with a steel grille and a half-drawn curtain onto the night soi, a standing fan switched off in the corner, a potted pothos, slippers by the sofa, and behind the camera a TV on a low cabinet (off; its black glass carries dust). The **ceiling** is the star set: rough, stippled plaster painted off-white years ago, a grey dust bloom around the lamp where the warm air rises, a cobweb in the back corner, a faint water stain, a slow ceiling fan; the round frosted ceiling lamp throws a warm pool on the plaster and a moth circles it. The **tokay** is a real Gekko gecko, big for a house lizard: blue-grey with rust-orange and pale blue-white spots, a broad head, big yellow eyes with vertical slit pupils, granular skin, a banded tail, wide toe pads. On the ceiling it hangs belly-up (its spotted back faces the room), still, toe tips lifted at rest (K02), facing the lamp and the moth (K03). The **man** in the imagined shots is a young everyman (rust jacket, jeans, white sneakers), held up by the tokay itself (R1.1, user: the tokay grips him, the picture is about the force): the tokay hangs head-up from its two front feet pressed flat on the plaster and clasps his wrists with its hind feet; his feet kick a hand's width above the floor, absurdly heavy next to the small calm animal. Under the toe the world changes scale: a toe pad with pale ridges, each ridge a dense brush of hairs, each hair splitting into hundreds of tiny flat tips that settle into the plaster's bumps and glint where they touch.
- **Medium.** Painted 2D p5.brush through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`, `cam-moves.ts`) in world units for every set: the room and ceiling in metres, the toe in millimetres, the hair and tips in micrometres. People are 4-head kit rig cards squared to the lens; the tokay is a new painted animal card with views and states (method of the EP13 flea card, `kit/flea.ts`). Screen layers ("ภาพจำลอง" label, thought-bubble outline, captions, channel mark) are Remotion DOM above the painted canvas, as in the series. No footage, no photos, no Three.js (Blender deactivated).

- **Light.** One warm ceiling lamp (3000 K) is the key for the whole room: a soft pool on the ceiling plaster and a downward spill; the tokay sits at the edge of the pool, so its spotted back (facing the room) is lit by the lamp's grazing spill on the lamp side and by warm bounce from the floor and walls, never left as a dark silhouette (UT03, UT04). The phone adds a cool soft glow on her face; the window is cool night blue with one warm neighbour's window; the TV is off. In grazing ceiling-level shots the lamp rakes across the plaster, so every bump throws a tiny shadow (the roughness reads). Macro: the lamp is a soft warm bokeh disc, hairs rim-lit from its side. Micro: dark field with a warm rim, glints where tips touch. Imagined hang: the same room, desaturated and cool. End: unchanged light; calm comes from the slower camera and the stillness.
- **Camera language.** B01 starts observational and low (a sofa-side view looking up, both her and the ceiling in the 9:16 frame), then cranes up to the feet; the tease snaps into the imagined hang on the same patch of plaster (close on the feet), pulls back along the line of force to the whole man, and snaps back. B02 stays in the room and on the ceiling (her close, the bubble, a straight-up look at the walking tokay). B03 is the scale dive: continuous in-canvas "powers of ten" moves through the toe (mm) and the hair (µm), broken by two real-room cuts (TV glass, over her shoulder) for the question. B04 returns to the room, then to the ceiling-level side view for the peel, so the refrain (B03-SH07) and its flip (B04-SH02/SH03) share one camera. B05 replays the tease framing for the payoff, then the real room, and ends on a slow crane from her smile to the tokay by the lamp (a bookend of the opening crane, slower and wider).
- **Conceal and reveal.** K01 (dust and rough plaster round the lamp) reads as an old house's ceiling in the first frame; it is the reason the plunger fails (B02) and the patch the tokay walks over. K02 (toe tips lifted at rest) is seen small in shot 1 and clearly on the hind foot in the ceiling-level close of shot 3; paid when the toe curls in L14. K03 (moth round the lamp) is in every lamp-wide shot until W-MOTH. The mechanism is held back: no hair is seen before L07; no contact glint before L11.
- **Truth limits on picture (binding on every shot).** "ภาพจำลอง" in the label slot on every hanging-man picture (L02 tease, W-HANG, V02) and on every drawn-scale macro (L07–L08, L10–L11, and the L15 macro part, D03). No number anywhere on screen (no scale bar digits, no kg, no counts). The hanging man is never shown in the real (full-colour) room. In the hang the tokay holds him with its own feet (front feet flat on the plaster, hind feet on his wrists; no sling, rope or prop); the force reads only from the pressed, splayed front feet, the straight forelimbs, the long straight body and his slack weight, never from a glow, line or glint (those stay held back until L11); the payoff curls one whole foot while the other stays flat (never a one-toe release). The moth catch (W-MOTH) shows the moth whole and still in the jaws for about a third of a second, then one swallow: no chewing, no legs, nothing broken or wet. Dust on the TV is shown, never linked by picture to the tokay's hold (no shared glint, no matching graphic, no split screen with the hairs). The tokay never uses its tail and the tail never drops (kept-out list). No glue blob, no wet shine, no suction dome on any pad: pads press flat and dry. The dust recovery stays partial: specks fall over several steps and a few stay on the toes; the foot is never shown spotless. The peel is toes rolling up and back from the tip (SRC007), hairs letting go in rows, not the whole pad lifting at once. Hairs are drawn as many and uniform; tips are drawn as hundreds per hair (about 200 visible), never as a count. Static electricity, water layers or any term never appear as text or picture; the plunger bubble is her guess, framed as a thought, not a model of the tokay.

## Sound direction

Following the script's [sound intent](script.md#sound-intent-english-notes-narration-unchanged) (its B01–B05 rows are the base); each cue was designed from the shot first, and a library ID is named only where its description fits ([fit beats reuse](../../../docs/sound-generation-workflow.md#fit-beats-reuse)); Ton makes every NEEDS-GEN item, Editor sets levels and places exactly the cue sheet at W5.3. Sound never carries a clue: K01–K03 are visual. Prompt fields: source / action / material / space / length / feeling.

(Library `bed_mystery-investigation_01` "pizzicato, felt piano, ticking, 100 bpm; not scary" is close in colour; its steady ticking would fight the tokay's step ticks: fallback only.)

## H1 — HK05 one rule (recommended)

- **Spoken Thai (0–4 s):**
  - NARR: "โจมีกฎข้อเดียวตอนเซฟจุดโทษ… กฎข้อที่หนึ่ง… อย่าพุ่ง"
  - JO (muttering, on the line): "อย่าพุ่ง… อย่าพุ่ง… อย่าพุ่ง…"
- **Beat shape:** rule at 0–4 s → test 1 obeying it costs him (booed) → test 2 breaking it also fails but he is cheered → fact punchline → final kick, the muttered rule returns as callback → save without a step → headline flip.
- **Why it works:** the rule sounds wrong to every fan who has screamed "พุ่งสิ!", so it breaks a certainty in about 3 s (ER01 met). "กฎข้อที่หนึ่ง" is its own open loop, Jo's mutter is a character line from second 2 (ER04, ER12 kind 1), and the same mutter is the planted callback for the ending (ER03). It is the hook the user's pick already names (HK05 in delivery.md).
- **Fact it leans on:** F002 (keepers dive almost every time; standing still is rare) and, paid later, F007 safe wording. The hook itself states no fact: the rule is Jo's, not the narrator's advice, so it never claims standing still is right (F013).
- **Channel rules it bends:** HK05's catalogue shape has the rule saving the character in each test; here tests 1–2 cost him, which serves ER02 escalation better but delays the rule's payoff to the last kick. "ถ้าคุณ…" from the pitch line is dropped for Jo, so ER11 has a named victim and the rule cannot be heard as viewer advice.

## H2 — HK01 broken certainty (fact first)

- **Spoken Thai (0–5 s):**
  - NARR: "ในงานวิจัยหนึ่ง โกลมืออาชีพพุ่งเกือบทุกลูก… ทั้งที่ข้อมูลบอกว่า ยืนกลางประตูเซฟได้มากกว่า"
  - NARR: "แล้วโจก็ดันอ่านเจองานวิจัยนี้ ก่อนนัดชิงหนึ่งคืน"
- **Beat shape:** wow fact at 0–5 s → Jo decides to obey the numbers → kick one booed, kick two cheered (irony) → second fact as the mid punchline (F005: keepers said conceding after standing still feels worse) → chip, save, headline.
- **Why it works:** the boldest true line arrives before anyone can swipe; "ทั้งที่" sets up the contradiction cleanly, and the second line makes Jo the victim of that knowledge.
- **Fact it leans on:** F007 (safe wording verbatim), F002.
- **Channel rules it bends:** ER07 and ER02 show-then-tell: the wow fact is spent before Jo meets it, so B03 needs F005 as its punchline instead. Jo enters at about 5 s, and the hook carries two hedges ("ในงานวิจัยหนึ่ง", "ข้อมูลบอกว่า"), one more than ER10's narrow hedge. Slower to the first laugh (first bit at about 8 s).

## H3 — HK07 start at the climax

- **Spoken Thai (0–6 s):**
  - (Final kick, Jo planted on the line, striker running in, crowd roaring.)
  - FAN: "พุ่งสิโว้ยยย!"
  - JO: "อย่าพุ่ง… อย่าพุ่ง…"
  - NARR: "นี่คือโจ กำลังจะทำสิ่งที่โกลแทบไม่มีใครทำ… ยืนเฉย ๆ / ย้อนไปสามลูกก่อน"
- **Beat shape:** freeze on the kick, result unseen → rewind → kicks one and two → fact punchline → back to the same frame at about 40 s, now it means something → chip, save, headline.
- **Why it works:** the most tense picture in the story comes first, with two character lines fighting (fan vs Jo), and the viewer stays to see whether the ball goes in.
- **Fact it leans on:** F002 ("แทบไม่มีใครทำ" = about 6 stays in 100 in the study).
- **Channel rules it bends:** ER01's default shape (no claim, a picture instead). The repeated shot costs about 3–4 s of a 60 s budget, and the cut must stop before the striker's choice so the chip stays a surprise (ER03 plant). Film's fixed angles make the repeat cheap.

## H4 — HK10 visual-first layered on HK08 true number

- **Spoken Thai (0–5 s):**
  - (First second silent: a wall of 100 painted keepers on one goal line; at the whistle 94 fling themselves sideways, 6 stay standing.)
  - NARR: "ในงานวิจัยหนึ่ง จุดโทษร้อยลูก โกลพุ่งไปราวเก้าสิบสี่… ยืนเฉยแค่ราวหก"
  - NARR: "และคืนนี้ โจตั้งใจจะเป็นหนึ่งในหกคนนั้น"
- **Beat shape:** silent visual gag → number → Jo picks the rare side → kicks one and two → save-rate fact as punchline (F003 safe wording: "ราวหนึ่งในสาม" against "ราวหนึ่งในเจ็ด") → chip, save, headline.
- **Why it works:** the picture is a joke before a word is spoken (ER12 kind 5), and the 94-to-6 split reads with no units.
- **Fact it leans on:** F002 (93.7% dives, about 6.3% stays by subtraction; medium-high, second-hand).
- **Channel rules it bends:** HK08 is the weakest hook shape in channel data (gecko 58.6 % stayed, below "you" hooks). A new crowd-of-keepers asset adds build cost to Film's size M. The number line must keep "ราว" and "ในงานวิจัยหนึ่ง" (two hedges, as H2).

## Recommendation

**H1 (HK05).** The story is about one stubborn rule against a whole stadium, so the rule should be the first thing heard, in Jo's own mouth: it hooks in 3 s, gives a character line at once, plants the mutter that pays the last kick, and keeps the hedged study line for the punchline spot where ER07 wants it. H3 is the strongest alternative if the user wants the tension of the final kick up front.

## Story core

- **Logline.** Jo, an amateur keeper in a township cup final, has one rule for penalties: don't dive. Standing still on kick one gets him booed; diving the wrong way on kick two gets him cheered for the same goal. A study says pros dive almost every time even though standing in the middle saved more in its data. On the deciding kick the whole stadium screams "พุ่ง", Jo mutters his rule, the striker chips it down the middle and Jo catches it without a step. The paper calls it a lucky freeze.
- **Central question.** Will Jo's stubborn rule ever pay off before the whole stadium gives up on him?
- **Intention and obstacle.** Jo wants to win the cup and keep his rule. The crowd, and his own nerve, punish him for standing still (ER02: booed, then the rule broken, then the whole stadium against him).
- **Feeling.** Laughing with Jo against the crowd, the "จริงดิ" that real pros do the same thing for the same reason, then the irony that the one time it works nobody credits him.
- **Takeaway.** In one well-known study, professional keepers dived on almost every penalty, though in that data standing in the middle saved more; keepers said conceding after standing still would feel worse. A penalty reaches the line in about 0.4 s, so the keeper has to guess.

## Truth and ending

- **What happens in the story.** One invented evening: the shoot-out of a township cup final, then the next morning's paper. Kick one goes into a corner while Jo stands still (consistent with F014: a stay mostly saves centre kicks). Kick two goes the other way while Jo dives. The deciding kick is a soft chip down the middle; Jo catches it on his chest without moving. Jo's team wins.
- **Verified facts used (pending Boat fact-check).** F007 (built on F002 and F003), F005, F010 with F011's safe guess wording. See the fact-line table below.
- **Remaining uncertainty, kept off narration.** The 2007 study's numbers are second-hand (paper not read in full); the centre-stay group is tiny (about 18 kicks); the reading is disputed (F013: diving may be a near-optimal mix). The survey keepers (Israeli leagues) are a different group from the 286 kicks. The narrator never says standing still is right or always better; the save in B05 needs a centre kick, and the headline's "ดวงดี" leaves luck in the story (F013 note).
- **Ending.** L20 headline punchline "โกลกลัวจนตัวแข็ง… แต่ดวงดี", then a 2.0 s hold (ER03). Irony: the viewer knows it was the rule (CL01), the paper sees only a frozen keeper. The ending was chosen first; every clue below feeds it.

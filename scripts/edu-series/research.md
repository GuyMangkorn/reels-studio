## Supported story angles

1. "Almost everyone has had it, and science has not settled why": common (F003), benign (F012), unexplained (F007).
2. Honest hypothesis ladder: normal shutdown misfire (A) vs "misread falling" (B) vs tree-sleeping relic (C, speculative, optional). Each marked by evidence strength.
3. Practical: avoid late caffeine, stress and hard evening workouts (associations only), doctor if it keeps you from sleeping.

## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Everyone has switched queues and lost. Feelings in order: recognition and laughs at the switching (B01–B02), a small "aha" at the plan model (B03), a guilty smile at being caught watching the other lanes (B04), a wry nod at memory (B05), calm (B06), and a settled close as MAN finally just waits his turn and is served (L20, closed ending) [R1.3]. The picture never shows a measurement: the plan is a **model drawing**, the tallies and bubbles are **his perception**, and the shared queue shows **no wait-time result**.
- **Story first.** A mid-size neighbourhood supermarket at the after-work rush, early evening. MAN (42, navy blazer, tie loosened) wants to get home; a red hand basket with instant noodles, eggs and a bunch of bananas. He is clever-looking and twitchy: he reads the lanes like a stock ticker and is wrong every time. The three cashiers are relaxed regulars in green store aprons; the shoppers are ordinary neighbours (an elder with a coin purse, a mother with her schoolgirl, a broad man with a trolley). The store is bright, a little too cheerful, under flat fluorescent light; through the glass front on the right the street is blue dusk with warm shopfronts across the road (background only, never featured; the R1.2 rival-shop feature left with L19) [R1.3].
- **Medium.** Painted 2D, p5.brush, [painted 3D camera](../../../docs/film-visual-brief-workflow.md#painted-3d-camera) (project default, user 5 Nov 2026): `SET-CHECKOUT` in world metres seen through the kit yaw camera (`kit/ycam.ts`); every store shot moves the camera inside the set; the cast are 4-head kit people drawn in one of the four views squared to the lens (`squareToCam`). Backgrounds are washes without outlines; cast, counters, baskets and hero props get one thin ink line and one cel shade. The plan model (B03) is a separate painted diagram on paper (VD06). Doodles (tallies, bubbles, stamp crack) are chalk/brush line drawn on, never UI. No SVG characters (UT11).
- **Lane colour code (continuity device).** Each lane carries one colour everywhere: lane 1 teal `#3fa59a`, lane 2 amber `#e8a33a`, lane 3 coral `#e8795a`: the till pole light, a floor stripe along the lane and the lane in the plan model. The viewer tracks "which lane moves" by colour across the side view, the plan and back, even at 270 px.

- **Camera language.** Vertical 1080×1920. The hero vantage looks from behind the till line down the lanes at about 25–35° to the lanes, so the three lanes recede diagonally and stack in depth instead of standing side by side (three parallel lanes do not fit a 1080 px width at a readable size). Customers walk toward the tills (+z), so the queue faces the hero camera (`front` view). MAN is close (1.2–2.6 m, [UT13](../../../docs/user-taste.md#ut-close-camera)) on every acting shot; wide shots only for the store/three-lane reads (L01 settle, L17 refit), which UT13 allows for a crowd. Moves are short arcs, dollies, trucks and one crane (L06 rise). The **"queue close" vantage** bookends the film [R1.3]: L01 (MAN in lane 2, eyes darting, tense) ↔ L20 end (MAN at the till, eyes still, relaxed) at the same head size, so the closing frame answers the opening one without handing back to it.
- **Frame zones.** Captions bottom-anchored as in EP02 VD16 (`captionStyle.bottomEdgeY` 1344, at most two lines, band y 1176–1344); faces, the hero props, the plan fractions and every doodle stay above y 1160. `@TamMaiLa` channel mark top-left (`video.chrome` default); nothing else above y 140. Title (L02) in y 220–520. The `แบบจำลอง` tag (`scene.eyebrow`) top-right on every plan shot.

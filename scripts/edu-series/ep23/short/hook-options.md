## A · คุยอยู่ก็พลาดได้ · HK01 · Recommended

> ถ้าคนตรงหน้าถูกสลับ คุณอาจไม่ทันสังเกต

- **Beat.** 0–4 s, challenge the viewer's certainty as they point down the soi. By ~7 s, the door crosses between them and the stranger and a different man emerges. Pay by ~15 s when the character finishes giving directions to the second man.
- **ER fit.** ER01 and ER02 defaults. The hypothetical carries F003 with one narrow hedge (ER10).
- **Why this one.** It puts the viewer in the same bind as the character immediately. The first swap gives the claim a visible payoff and leaves room for the later reveal to surprise.

## B · คนเมื่อกี้ไปไหน · HK07

> สมมุติว่าคุณชี้ทางอยู่… เอ๊ะ คนเมื่อกี้ไปไหน?

- **Beat.** 0–4 s, open on the character still pointing to the replacement in the soi. By ~7 s, go back to the stranger asking for directions. By ~15 s, replay the first door pass and let it answer where the first man went.
- **ER bend.** Replaces ER01's early claim with an unexplained outcome and a question. ER02 still pays early. The opening rewind adds an edit before the later viewer-test window, which needs to play continuously.

## C · ประตูบังแวบเดียว · HK10

> ถ้าประตูบังแวบเดียว คุณยังคุยกับคนเดิมอยู่ไหม?

- **Beat.** 0–1 s, a door unexpectedly starts crossing the foreground while the viewer-character points down the soi. Give the first stranger a clean glimpse before the door covers him. Bring the voices in as it starts to clear. By ~5 s, the question lands on the person now answering. By ~15 s, the character finishes the directions and the clip confirms the swap.
- **ER bend.** Layers HK10 onto the ER01 premise. The picture hooks in the first second and the spoken question replaces the early claim. ER02 still pays early.

## Bounds shared by all three

- The soi encounter is hypothetical. The first door pass shows the person swap. Plant the cap and sign in view and reserve their specific changes for the end frame comparison. Keep both states legible and the viewer-test change window continuous. Use ordinary wood and footstep sounds on the first pass and keep the later change free of an attention cue.
- The factual payoff can name the 1998 door study (F001), then say “พอถามว่าเห็นอะไรแปลกไหม 8 จาก 15 คนไม่ได้บอกว่าคนถามทางเปลี่ยนไป” (F002). The open question is part of what the count measures. Keep the study's count with the study and let the frame comparison show what changed in the clip.
- The takeaway is that a person can miss a change while still talking to someone (F003). Build the payoff around that observation. F004 stays in the research notes.

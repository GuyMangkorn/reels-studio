## Must-fix list

**M1 (L17-L18; premise/takeaway; user's twist preserved).** As written, "ไม่มีใครเลย…" and "ที่ไม่เคยมีใครนับ" assert that nobody ever counted hippo deaths. That is false: Zambia, Zimbabwe and Mozambique have official or study counts that include hippo deaths (F010-F012, F015). The true, still-surprising claim: no count and no clear source stand behind the 500 Africa-wide.

Proposed replacement (one short line each, same beat, same hold):

- L17: **"ไม่มีใครนับได้จริงเลย…"** (FB1 unchanged)
- L18: **"สัตว์ที่ขึ้นชื่อว่าฆ่าคนเยอะที่สุด… ขึ้นชื่อด้วยตัวเลข ที่ไม่มีใครชี้ที่มาได้ชัด"**

Why this pair, and why not the other fallbacks:

- FB1 L17: true for the Africa-wide 500 (no total count exists, gap 1; F016, F005, SRC007). L16 "ห้าร้อยของฮิปโป… ใครเป็นคนนับ?" anchors what "นับ" refers to, so it reads as "nobody counted that 500", not "nobody counts hippo deaths".
- New L18 uses F002's safe wording ("ไม่มีใครชี้ที่มาที่แน่ชัดได้"): supported by SRC007 ("fail to present clear, concrete sources") and SRC004 ("very few data support this notion"). It is a plain claim, not a source or method phrase (ER10), and the same length class as the original (override kept, no hedge stack).
- FB2 ("ที่ไม่ได้มาจากการนับ") is rejected: it asserts the number's origin was not counting, but the origin is untraced (gap 2; Fenner lead unverified). That would be an unverified positive claim.
- FB3 ("ไม่มีใครรู้เลย") is rejected: overbroad, because national records and OWID's estimate give some knowledge (tens per year in the places counted).
- The original "ไม่เคยมีใครนับ" is rejected for the reason above.

The ending still lands the flip ("the famous number has nothing solid under it") and the timing is unchanged (L18 is about the same syllable count).

## Answers to Preaw's open questions

1. **Q1: No, not as written (M1).** "ไม่มีใครเลย / ไม่เคยมีใครนับ" reads as "hippo deaths are never counted", which national records contradict; use L17 "ไม่มีใครนับได้จริงเลย…" (FB1) and L18 "…ขึ้นชื่อด้วยตัวเลข ที่ไม่มีใครชี้ที่มาได้ชัด"; reject FB2 (unverified origin claim) and FB3 (overbroad).
2. **Q2: Confirmed.** One source family (OWID, built on WHO figures): mosquitoes 760,000, humans 600,000, snakes 100,000, dogs 40,000, hippos ~50 (rank 17); WHO alone gives malaria 610,000, snakes 81,410-137,880, rabies 44,203; true with or without humans, and true even at 500.
3. **Q3: No extra line needed.** "Maybe more than 500?" does not hurt the hook because L02 stays true even if the hippo number is several times 500 (the third rung alone is about 40,000); do not add a line (it would need an unsupported hippo figure; F014 is hypothesis).
4. **Q4: Acceptable.** "ตัวเลขที่ใคร ๆ ก็แชร์กัน" states only that it is widely repeated (F001, SRC007), is plain story framing with no source or method phrase, and sets up L16-L18 fairly.
5. **Q5: Words are fine, pictures carry the risk (optional).** "ใกล้ตัว" means everyday animals, but a Thai bedroom under L14 could suggest malaria deaths happen in Thailand; keep the line, make the B05 picture placeless or show no illness, and do not swap the disease (no research needed).
6. **Q6: Mostly fine, both optional.** L10 is WHO's 5.4 M people bitten (not envenomings, which are 1.8-2.7 M), so "ห้าล้านครั้ง" is acceptable, "ห้าล้านคน" is slightly tighter; L11 "ราว ๆ แสน" covers 81,410-137,880 correctly; L08 stadium is right at order of magnitude for a large national stadium (lead-only, capacities ~50,000 or more), optional "สนามฟุตบอลใหญ่ ๆ", and T1 may cut it freely.
7. **Q7: Fine.** "หมาที่มีเชื้อพิษสุนัขบ้า ทำให้คนตาย" matches WHO (up to 99% of human rabies cases transmitted by dogs; 44,203 deaths) and the "carries a germ" framing of L13; ensure the dog on screen is calm and not shown foaming.

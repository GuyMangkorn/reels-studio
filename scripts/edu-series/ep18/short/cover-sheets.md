## Shared rules for both paintings

- **Where:** paint in `src/painted/edu18/cover-shots.tsx` (new, never imported by `src/painted/edu18/shots/*` or `src/videos/edu-ep18/`); list in a new cover inventory `src/painted/shots/edu18-covers.tsx` (`edu18CoverShots`), appended to `src/painted/registry.ts` after `edu18Shots`; registry IDs `EDU18-CV1`, `EDU18-CV2`, static (any `local` ≥ 15 gives the same picture). Precedent: EDU19-CV1/CV2 (`src/painted/edu19/cover-shots.tsx`). **Pin consequence:** the registry line changes every painted deliverable's shared render-input hash, so an EP18 (or any pinned episode) re-render afterwards needs `--keep-pinned` or a repaint; the EP18 delivery is already rendered and published, so nothing has to re-render now.
- **Reuse, do not fork:** the episode's own river set (`src/painted/edu18/river.ts`, SET-RIVER18 space `R`, metres), the hippo `HippoInSet` (hippo3d v8, `src/painted/edu18/hippo3d.tsx`, poses as in the film), the girl (`src/painted/edu18/girl.ts`) and the episode look (LF01 family, ink EDU18-INK-SINGLE). A fix to shared art goes to its owner module, not into the cover file.
- **Truth limits that bind the cover (brief 1, 2, 6):** no number or text painted in (text is pick 2's overlay); the hippo never touches the canoe or her, no bite, no blood, no foam, no capsizing; a visible strip of water always separates the hippo from the canoe; no real place, flag or sign. Nothing happens that the film does not show: the yawn beside the canoe (B01-SH01) and the charge that stops short (B01-SH03); only lens, framing, scale emphasis and light are pushed.
- **Light (both):** the film's dawn: sun just above the far ridge, direction to the sun ≈ (−0.62, 0.10, −0.78) in `R`, pushed to poster contrast: hard warm rim on the hippo's wet back and head and on her hair, the near faces in cool shade with a warm bounce, mist glowing behind; the frame edges dark (the cover grade adds a vignette; paint the plate itself already darker at the edges than the film's).
- **Calm band:** top, y 240–500, dark sky or mist with no detail brighter than the subject; channel mark area x < 330, y < 140 clear.
- **Never shown:** the post, its 500, any other animal of the ladder (dog, snake, mosquito), any harm.
- **Acceptance (both):** at 120 px wide the hippo's head and her face are the two largest-contrast shapes; the subject sits inside x 40–960 and y 240–1536; the water gap between hippo and canoe reads at 270 px.

- **Camera (est):** low and long, from ahead of the bow on the water's level, looking back along the charge so the hippo comes head-on toward the lens over her shoulder: `cam((7.0, 0.55, −0.3), 84, 11.5, 0.45, 3000, 860, +2)` (camera ≈ (18.4, 0.45, 0.9), f 3000 compresses the 9 m between them).

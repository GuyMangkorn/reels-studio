## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Every viewer "knows" the hippo is the world's deadliest animal (the meme). Frame 0 shows the strange thing with no words: dawn mist on an African river, a girl in a dugout canoe, and the water beside her already bulging as a hippo rises (UT01, retention camera). Feelings by beat (script story core): smug thrill (escape) → "huh?" (not even top 3) → unease as danger moves closer to home (gate, path, bed) → the flip: a small, sheepish "wait…" at her own post → a quiet wow. The picture never mocks the hippo or the viewer; it mocks only her certainty, gently.
- **Story first (designed before the kit was opened).** **GIRL**: a Thai woman in her early twenties on her first big trip abroad; a bit of a show-off, phone always within reach, quick to grin and quick to flinch. Africa: khaki short-sleeve shirt over a white tee, olive cargo shorts, trail sandals, a small cross-body phone pouch, long black hair loose; in the canoe an orange life vest over it (a real-world safety cue that also keeps her readable against the grey-green water). Home: the same travel clothes plus a grey backpack when she arrives at the gate (story time: the same afternoon she gets back); at night a faded oversized tee and soft shorts under a thin blanket. **The hippo**: big and heavy, wet grey-purple skin with a pink flush round the eyes and ears, eyes, ears and nostrils on top of the head so it can lie almost hidden; the yawn is huge and a little comic, the charge is real (a bow wave, head high), and it never touches the canoe. **The village dog**: a lean tan Thai street dog, calm and curious, ears up, tail low then wagging; nothing about it is sick or menacing (rabies is invisible; the beat is about a number). **The snake**: slim, olive-brown with darker irregular saddles, plain round head, no hood, no bright colours, no cartoon eyes; not a recognisable species, not cute. **The mosquito**: at room scale a tiny dark dot with a faint blur; at macro scale a lean dark mosquito with striped legs and blurred wings, rim-lit, against nothing. **Places**: a wide brown African river at dawn (papyrus and reeds, mist on the water, acacia silhouettes on the far bank, low sun); the same river's sandbank in clear morning; her grandmother's house in a Thai village (white concrete gate posts, a green steel swing gate, bougainvillea, a wooden house on stilts, a mango tree, a laterite lane); a raised dirt bund through green rice paddies at golden hour (sugar palms, a far tree line); her bedroom at night in the same wooden house (plank walls, low wooden bed, floral sheet, a floor fan, a bedside lamp, wooden shutters with moonlight, a knotted-up mosquito net hanging unused above the bed). Then one placeless dark ground for the mosquito close, and one illustrative stadium.
- **Medium (VD01).** Painted 2D p5.brush through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`, `cam-moves.ts`) in world metres for every set (mm for the macro); people are 4-head kit rig cards, the dog a kit `ANIMALS` card, the hippo a creature-kit build (`survival/beast*.ts` lofted volumes on a bone rig, the AS-RAPTOR method), the snake and the mosquito new kit cards. Crisp Remotion SVG (project default PHONE-SVG) only for the phone screen (her post). Captions and the channel mark are DOM/screen layers above the painted canvas, as in the series. No footage, no photos, no Three.js, no Blender (deactivated).

- **Light (colour marks the chapters, retention camera).** River dawn: low sun behind the far bank at screen left-back, warm rim on the hippo's wet back and her hair, cool fill from the sky on the water; mist catches the light. Bank morning: clean high sun, neutral, bright (her pride). Village afternoon: warm sun from screen right-front (about 15:30, elevation about 35°), soft shade under the mango. Rice path: golden hour, sun low behind her (backlight and long shadows toward the lens), cooling to dusk blue as the camera cranes up in B04-SH04. Bedroom: cool moonlight only (B05), then the warm lamp flood on the click (B06) with the phone's cool glow on her face for the end. Stadium: flat overcast, quiet. Macro: one cool rim light on black.
- **Camera language.** Every shot moves inside its set (retention camera: an eased yaw arc of 6–10° and a slow push of 5–15 % of the camera distance). Large moves only for the hook wide (B01-SH05 crane), the stadium reveal (B03-SH04), the rice-field crane (B04-SH04) and the macro pull-back to a dot (B05-SH03). Each home set keeps one action axis: the gate shots stay on the yard side looking out to the lane (the house behind the camera), the path shots stay low on the path facing her, the bedroom stays on the open side of the bed. The phone insert framing (FPOV over the phone, hand at frame bottom) is used twice on purpose, B02-SH02 and B06-SH02, so the post in B06 reads instantly as "that post".
- **Conceal and reveal.** CL01 (the post's "500 คน/ปี") is designed as a proud, confident caption in B02; nothing on screen doubts it before L15 (no "?", no red, no stamp). CL03 (the mosquito) is a dot plus the buzz in B02, never pointed at by the camera; it is paid in B05 with the same sound and the same dot. CL04 (share counter) only rises (B02 ticks to 1.2K; B06 shows 58K and is still ticking). In B06 the reveal is only the push-in onto the 500 with nothing behind it: no stamp, no "0", no crossed-out hippo, no question mark replacing the number (script Film brief; Boat R2 Film-brief row).
- **Truth limits on picture (binding on every shot).**
  1. **The only number drawn in the picture is the post's 500** (CL01). No hippo figure other than it; no "deadliest" banner or ranking graphic; no "610,000"; numbers for L07, L10, L11, L14 appear only in the subtitles at the spoken magnitude (script subtitle note; VD12).
  2. **No harm shown:** the hippo never touches the canoe and stops about a canoe-length (5.5 m) short; no bite, wound, blood, foam, sick or feverish person anywhere; the dog is calm (no bared teeth, no foam, no limp); the snake does not strike; the mosquito is never shown biting.
  3. **B05 L13–L14 are placeless** (Boat Q5, R2 B05 row): the macro has no room, Thai marker, bed or person; it starts on the first frame of L13 and holds to the end of L14.
  4. **Stadium (L08) is a size picture only:** a large stadium full to the top, people as still soft dots, no cheering, no grief imagery, no captions; it shows about 40,000 at order of magnitude (Boat Q6, lead-only stadium sizes ~50,000).
  5. **The twist picture (L17–L18)** says "nobody can clearly trace this number", never "the number is fake" or "hippo deaths are not recorded" (F010–F012, F015 not contradicted): the 500 simply stays on screen.
  6. **No real place, person or incident:** no named river, no flag, no park sign, no Templer, no real social platform (generic post UI, fictional handle).
  7. **Sound never carries a clue or a claim** (script sound intent).

- **Ending (UT15, Film's choice): END1, the kalimba shrug.** NEEDS-GEN: the first two notes of the M1 kalimba riff played slowly, the second a step lower and left ringing with a soft thumb-piano buzz, then a tiny muted marimba tap on the root under it / one phrase / kalimba, marimba / small dry room, gentle tail / 2.0 s / "huh… so that number came from nowhere", wry and sheepish, not triumphant. Reason: the episode's confidence was the M1 riff; the ending is that same riff losing its swagger, which is exactly her feeling.

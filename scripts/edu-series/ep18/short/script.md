## Story core

- **Logline:** a young Thai traveller paddles past a yawning hippo, escapes its charge and posts a selfie: "survived the animal that kills the most people on Earth, 500 a year". Back home, each animal she meets kills far more than that number: a village dog, a snake on the rice-field path, a mosquito in her bedroom. Then the rule she has been following ("don't look at how fierce it is, look at the number") turns on her own post: nobody actually counted that 500, and nobody can point to where it came from.
- **Central question:** is the hippo really the animal that kills the most people, and where does its famous number come from?
- **Intention / obstacle:** she wants her brag to be true (her post, CL01). Each rung shrinks the brag: three ordinary animals outrank the hippo. The last obstacle is the number in her own post.
- **Feeling:** smug thrill (escape) → "huh?" (not even top 3) → unease as the danger moves closer to home (gate, path, bed) → the flip: a small, sheepish "wait…" at her own post, then a quiet wow.
- **Learning takeaway:** the animals that cause the most human deaths are everyday ones (mosquito-borne disease, snakes, dogs through rabies), far above the hippo; and the hippo's famous "500 a year" is a number passed around with no count and no clear source behind it.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "อย่าดูที่ความดุ… ดูที่ตัวเลข" (L05). It ranks every rung (L07, L10–L11, L14 are numbers, not fierceness). L15 sends it back to the start ("ดูที่ตัวเลขมาตลอด…"), and the flip is that the one number we never looked at is the hippo's own: the rule we used against fierceness exposes that the hippo's number has nothing solid under it. The rule turns from a tool for ranking others into the thing that undoes the premise.
- **Change from C69 (recorded reason):** C69's rule ("ที่ฆ่าคนเยอะ ไม่ใช่ตัวที่ดุที่สุด แต่เป็นตัวที่เจอคนบ่อยที่สุด") explained the ladder but cannot carry the "500 has no count behind it" flip, so it is replaced by the number rule above. The C69 crocodile rung is replaced by the dog rung: a crocodile-beats-hippo claim rests on national hippo-death records (F010, F015), which would pull the viewer toward small local counts and blur the flip about the Africa-wide 500, and the crocodile has no reconciled number (F024). The dog rung (F022, solid) also completes the "top three" the hook names. The "why the hippo attacks" problem is cut (it does not serve the number twist); its objection survives as L03.

## Truth and ending

- **What happens in the story:** she survives a hippo charge (it stops short), posts a selfie with a meme caption, goes home, meets a dog, a snake and a mosquito unharmed, and in bed rereads her post. Nobody is hurt.
- **Spoken as settled, plain:** hippo is not in the top three (L02; F028, true with humans counted or not, F023); mosquito carries a germ from one person's blood to another (L13; F020, SRC002); a single disease carried by mosquitoes kills hundreds of thousands a year (L14; F020); the dog is not number one (L09); nobody actually counted the 500 and nobody can point clearly to its source (L17–L18; the user's twist in M1 form, see below).
- **Spoken with one narrow hedge:** rabies from dogs ≈ 40,000 deaths a year (L07 "ราว ๆ"; F022); about 5 million people bitten by snakes a year (L10 "ราว ๆ"; SRC001 5.4 M people); snakebite deaths ≈ 100,000 a year (L11 "ราว ๆ"; F021, 81,410–137,880).
- **Spoken only as hearsay, never as fact:** "500 a year" (L04, framed as "ตัวเลขที่ใคร ๆ ก็แชร์กัน"; F001 documents only that it is widely repeated). No hippo figure is asserted anywhere (not 500, not 50; F014, F027).
- **Not spoken (Boat's not-supported list and ER07):** any real hippo total, "deadliest large land mammal" (F030), calves (F043), bite force (F045), case-fatality rates (F013, F031), hippo vs lion, crocodile numbers (F024), Lewison/Templer quotes or names, Fenner origin (F003), "mosquito kills" as a bite (gap 8), any claim about where the 500 did come from (gap 2; FB2 rejected by Boat), any claim that hippo deaths are never recorded (false: F010–F012, F015).
- **Remaining uncertainty (not narrated):** the 500's origin is untraced (gap 2); Africa-wide hippo deaths are unknown (gap 1). The twist claims only that no count and no clear source stand behind the 500 (Boat: negative result, medium-high confidence). A viewer may wonder "so maybe it's more?"; Boat Q3: no extra line needed, L02 stays true even at several times 500 (the third rung alone is about 40,000).
- **Ending:** closed. L18 lands the flip; ~2 s hold on her face lit by the phone, the "500" in her post; `A_fade`. No tip, no call to action (ER03).

# EDU-EP18-SHORT — research packet (W1.1, Boat)

- Deliverable: `EDU-EP18-SHORT` (Short, Thai, educational, concept C50 "ถ้าคุณตัวเท่ายุง เม็ดฝนหนักเท่ารถตู้", `scripts/edu-series/concepts.md` `### C50`, R9). Research date: 2026-11-11. Mode: non-fiction scale what-if; the shrunk woman and the van-sized drop are imagined ("ภาพจำลอง"). No script is written here.
- Short answer:
  1. **The study is real and supports beats 2–3.** Dickerson, Shankles, Madhavan, Hu, PNAS 109(25):9822–9827 (2012), doi 10.1073/pnas.1205446109: mosquitoes survive raindrop hits "by virtue of their low mass". The drop loses little momentum, the mosquito does not resist ("simply go with the flow"), is carried on the front of the drop, then separates sideways (legs and wings drag rotate it off). Hit force 100–300 g (SRC001–SRC006).
  2. **Mass ratio is a range, not "50×".** Paper: drops 4–100 mg against a mosquito of about 2 mg, so **2× to 50×**. "50 times" is the largest drop. Press repeats "50 times" as if typical (SRC002–SRC005).
  3. **For a 60 kg person the vehicle depends on drop size.** 50× = **3,000 kg**; 2× = **120 kg**. A Hiace-class van (about 1.9–2.6 t kerb, Australian specs) matches a ratio of about 32–44×, a drop about **5–5.5 mm** across: the top of the real size range, not an ordinary raindrop. A common heavy-rain drop of about 3 mm (14 mg, about 7×) is **about 420 kg** for the person. "รถตู้" is true only if worded as the biggest drops (CL04–CL06).
  4. **Ride distance: supported.** The mosquito stays on the drop's front for **up to 20 body lengths** (press: "5 to 20"), at about 9 m/s (SRC002, SRC003, SRC005). At human scale 20 body lengths is about 34 m of falling, not a number that needs a source on screen.
  5. **Near-ground result: only partly supported; beat 4 needs hedging.** The authors say the danger is separating too late: Hu, "otherwise the mosquito will be thrown into the ground at the speed of a falling raindrop". A press line says a hit "too close to the ground" may not be survivable. **No inspected source reports a measured near-ground test, a death in rain, or a mosquito "pinned/pressed into a puddle"** (one Discover summary mentions a mosquito driven into a puddle that "perished"; single source, not corroborated, not used). So "the ground kills, not the rain" is an author-reasoned risk, a `hypothesis`, not a measured finding (CL10–CL12).
  6. **Fallback for beat 4:** if the user wants no hedge, beat 4 becomes the ride itself (5–20 body lengths on the drop, then slide off), and the last line shifts from "พื้นต่างหาก" to the ride (open question 3).
- Tooling limits: the PNAS paper and PMC copy were **not opened** (403 / reCAPTCHA). All study facts come from fetched press pages (Georgia Tech release via ScienceDaily, phys.org, EurekAlert, Science News, National Geographic, Discover) and a search-engine abstract summary of the paper (drop mass range 4–100 mg, ratio 2–50, 6–9 m/s, 2.1 m/s in 1.5 ms, hit every 25 s). Fetched pages were summarised by a small model; quotes are summary-level, not verbatim-checked. Fact-check must open the paper. Thai vehicle weights not sourced from a Thai spec.

## Numbers (own arithmetic, reproducible)

Drop mass m = 0.5236 × d³ mg (d in mm); mosquito 2 mg (inferred); person 60 kg.

| Drop diameter | Drop mass | Ratio to mosquito | Same ratio for a 60 kg person | Speed kept after sticking (m/(m+2 mg)) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 mm | 4.2 mg | 2× | 126 kg | 68 % |
| 3 mm | 14 mg | 7× | 424 kg | 88 % |
| 4 mm | 33.5 mg | 17× | 1,005 kg | 94 % |
| 5 mm | 65 mg | 33× | 1,960 kg | 97 % |
| 5.5 mm | 87 mg | 44× | 2,610 kg | 98 % |
| 5.8 mm | 102 mg | 51× | about 3,060 kg | 98 % |

- Paper's range 4–100 mg → 2–50× → 120–3,000 kg. 5.8 mm is about the paper's top.
- Hiace-class van 1,865–2,615 kg (SRC010) ↔ ratio 31–44× ↔ drop 62–87 mg ↔ d about 4.9–5.5 mm. NASA: drops up to about 6 mm, break-up near the top (SRC008): such drops exist but are rare.
- "50 times" at 60 kg = 3,000 kg: a loaded van, not an empty one; "หนักเท่ารถตู้ที่มีคนนั่งเต็ม" is also approximately true (payload 1,095 kg, SRC010), but needs a stated assumption. Plain: "หนักราวรถตู้" for the largest drops.
- Collision as perfectly sticky: speed after = M/(M+m) × v. At 50×, 98 %, at 7× 88 %.
- Human scale of the ride: 20 body lengths × 1.7 m person = 34 m. Discover equates 20 body lengths with a 12-storey fall (about 36 m): same order (SRC007).

## Wording fixes for Preaw (C50)

1. **Hook (optional, accuracy; must-fix only if "ทุกเม็ด" or ordinary drops are implied).** "เม็ดฝนใหญ่ ๆ หนักเท่ารถตู้" or "ฝนเม็ดใหญ่ที่สุด หนักเท่ารถตู้" is true; "ฝนหนึ่งเม็ด หนักเท่ารถตู้" as a flat statement overstates ordinary rain by 5–25× (CL04, CL05). An easy true alternative that keeps the surprise: "ฝนเม็ดใหญ่ ๆ หนักกว่าตัวคุณหลายสิบเท่า" with "ภาพจำลอง" on screen.
2. **Hook, "บินกลางฝนได้สบาย" (optional).** Lab evidence is survival of hits (CL02); "ได้สบาย" is the animator's wording and not a claim; "ยุงรอด" is safer.
3. **Beat 2 (optional).** The drop "ไม่ช้าลงมาก" is accurate for the big drop (CL03). The feather picture: the paper's cause is mass, so "เบาจนเม็ดฝนแทบไม่ช้าลง" is correct. Do not add "like a feather hit by a thrown ball" with a number.
4. **Beat 3 (supported).** "ไปกับมัน" then "ไหลออกด้านข้าง" is the paper's own account (CL07–CL09). "ขาและปีกช่วยหมุนตัวออก" is true if a clause is wanted.
5. **Beat 4 (must-fix: the pinned picture is `hypothesis`).** If kept: "ถ้าเม็ดฝนมาถึงตอนยุงบินต่ำติดพื้น ก็อาจไม่มีที่ให้ไหลออก" (hedge "อาจ"). The hook for the beat must not show a death or a puddle pin as a documented result. If the user will not accept the hedge, use the fallback: beat 4 is the ride (up to 20 body lengths, about 34 m at her size) and the last line is about the ride (CL08).
6. **Last line "ฝนไม่ได้ฆ่ายุงหรอก… พื้นต่างหาก" (must-fix as a flat statement).** Not measured (CL12). Safe versions: "ที่น่ากลัวสำหรับยุง ไม่ใช่เม็ดฝน แต่คือพื้น" is still an inference; with a hedge: "อันตรายของยุงคือพื้นข้างล่าง" is closer to the authors' reasoning. Preaw may keep "พื้นต่างหาก" if the beat-4 hedge is present and the picture does not show a death.
7. **Premise note.** The study is about what a mosquito does, not about "rain clears mosquitoes". Do not claim anything about mosquitoes in rainy season (not needed, ER07).
8. **Left out (qualifications that would empty a beat; recommend cutting):** the jet vs real drop difference, six mosquitoes, 100–300 g, 75 % legs/wings, wind as another danger, Hedrick's caveat. None should become narration.

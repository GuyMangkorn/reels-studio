## Story core

- **Logline:** in the dinosaur world the medicine most likely to save a life in your village is not in the pharmacy, it is in the kitchen. The phone cannot call a doctor (it is a torch now), the shop's few boxes of pills run out and nobody can make more, and plain diarrhoea drains a child's body of water and salt. Boiled water, sugar and salt, in the right amounts, put it back.
- **Central question:** with no doctor and few pills, what saves a sick child?
- **Intention / obstacle:** ยาย wants to get เก่ง well; the world has taken her phone signal and her pharmacy.
- **Feeling:** worry at the hook; a smile at ยาย up the ladder waving the phone; "of course, call a doctor" deflated twice; a wince at "ก็มันใสออก…"; fear at "ทรุดเร็ว"; relief and a small smile at the end.
- **Takeaway:** unboiled water can give you diarrhoea; diarrhoea is dangerous because the body loses water and salt (spoken from R2.2), faster in children; a drink of 1 litre of boiled water, 6 level teaspoons of sugar and half a level teaspoon of salt, sipped slowly, replaces them ("replaces" is shown, not spoken, from R2.1); in real life, a child who cannot drink or is very ill goes to a doctor fast.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "ใช้แล้วหมด" (pharmacy medicine runs out, L04); L09 flips it: the medicine that saved him was in your kitchen all along.

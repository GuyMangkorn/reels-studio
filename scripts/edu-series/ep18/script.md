## Story core

- **Logline:** shrink to a mosquito and the biggest raindrops weigh about as much as a van. A mosquito hit by one still lives. It is not crushed, because it is so light the drop barely slows: like a feather hit by a ball, it does not resist, it goes along. But the drop does not stop; it carries you down toward the ground, up to twenty body lengths. Near the ground there may not be time to get off. The way out: go with it, tilt and slide off the side before the ground. The rain is not what hurts a mosquito; the ground below may be.
- **Central question:** how does a mosquito live through a drop that, at its size, weighs a van?
- **Intention / obstacle:** `woman-curly` runs for cover in a soi downpour while a mosquito keeps flying at her ankle (she wants to stay dry and unbitten). Shrunk, she faces the drop beside the mosquito; the drop, then the fall toward the ground, are the obstacles each beat tests.
- **Feeling:** disbelief at the hook; dread at "ไม่นะ!"; relief and a small laugh at the feather; a jolt at the rehook ("it is taking you down"); tension as the puddle rushes up; release at the slide-off; a dry laugh at the slap that misses.
- **Takeaway:** a mosquito survives a raindrop because it is so light the drop hardly slows: it does not resist, it rides the drop and slides off the side. What can hurt it is being too close to the ground to get off in time.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "ไม่ต้าน… (แค่/แต่) ไปกับมัน" (do not resist, go with it). L07 says it for the feather (why the hit does not crush), L11 for the mosquito (the way out). L10 and L13 turn it: going with the drop only works while there is room below; near the ground, the same ride heads straight into it.

## Truth and ending

- **Spoken as settled, plain:** the largest raindrops, scaled to a mosquito-sized person, weigh about a van (CL04 `model`, "ราว", L02); a mosquito hit by one lives (CL02, lab, L03); shrunk whole, you weigh as little as a mosquito (CL06 premise, L04); the hit does not crush the mosquito because it is so light the drop barely slows (CL01, CL03, L05–L06); it does not resist, it goes with the drop (CL07, L07, L11); the drop carries it down, up to twenty body lengths (CL08, L08–L09); it tilts and slides off the side (CL09, L12).
- **Hypothesis, hedged once (ER10):** the near-ground danger is the authors' reasoning, not a measured result (CL10, CL11): L10 "…ก็อาจหลุดออกไม่ทัน"; the picture cuts away before contact and shows no death and no mosquito pinned in the puddle. The last line keeps the ground as the danger only with "อาจ" (CL12, L13).
- **One narrow hedge per line (ER10):** L02 "ราว", L06 "แทบ", L10 "อาจ", L13 "อาจ"; L09 "ถึง" marks the maximum, not a hedge. No source, method or evidence phrase anywhere.
- **Dramatization only:** the shrink, the woman at mosquito size beside the mosquito, her riding the drop's front, the van-sized drop (all "ภาพจำลอง", OT01); the feather-and-ball insert (an analogy, no numbers); the soi, awning and puddle set; the slap at the end. **The woman's fate on the drop is never stated:** survival words name the mosquito (L03, L05, L11); at L12 the picture follows the mosquito off the drop and the what-if ends on the cut back to full size (CL06, Dickerson: at human scale "we would not survive").
- **Not used:** "ฝนหนึ่งเม็ด/ทุกเม็ด หนักเท่ารถตู้" (CL04, CL05); the school-bus analogy (SRC005); "บินกลางฝนได้สบาย" (CL02); "ฝนไม่ได้ฆ่ายุง… พื้นต่างหาก" flat (CL12); any picture of a mosquito pinned or dead (CL11, SRC007 single source); 100–300 g, six mosquitoes, the water jet, 75 % legs/wings, wind (wording fix 8, ER07); mosquito mass in milligrams (SRC012 unsourced); anything about mosquitoes in rainy season (wording fix 7).
- **Knowledge twist (rule flip, ER03):** "go with it" first reads as the whole answer (L07: that is why the hit does not crush). The rehook L08 shows going with it has a cost: the drop takes you toward the ground. L11–L12 make the rule the way out (go with it, then slide off). L13 flips the danger: not the drop that should have crushed it, but the ground the ride heads into. Fair clues: the puddle at the awning's edge (K01) and the mosquito flying low at her ankle (K02), both in the opening shot.
- **Ending (closed):** back to full size under the awning, the mosquito still at her ankle just above the puddle; L13 lands; she slaps and misses; hold ~2 s. No call to action, no tip.

## Rulings on the items you listed (Q1)

**R1. Does lab survival cover the largest drops (L03, L05)? Partly; the authors' conclusion covers it, the experiments do not directly.** What was tested: (a) a 9 m/s water jet on six mosquitoes, all survived, but the jet was "pulsatile … a combination of drops and jetting streams", so it is not a single 100 mg drop; (b) 17 live-mosquito hits by single drops of radius 0.15–0.25 cm, i.e. 3–5 mm across, about 14–65 mg, ratio about 7–33x (own arithmetic from m = 0.5236 d³ mg), at 0.1–2.6 m/s, **slower than rain**; all 17 separated and flew. No test used a drop of the 100 mg (50x) maximum falling at 6–9 m/s. The paper's argument is physical (a 2 mg mosquito barely slows any drop), and the abstract-level conclusion is that the mosquito "survives" raindrops, so "ก็ยังรอด" for "เม็ดแบบนี้" is the authors' reading, supported with a small extrapolation at the very top. Not a wrong takeaway, not must-fix. **Optional O3:** no wording change needed (narration may not carry a method phrase, ER10); keep L03 as is. If Preaw wants a narrower edge without a source phrase, "แต่ยุงโดนเม็ดใหญ่ ๆ ตรง ๆ… ก็ยังรอด" is the same truth ("เม็ดใหญ่ ๆ" instead of "เม็ดแบบนี้", which points at the single largest drop) at the same length. L05 passes: 17 of 17.

**R2. "ราวรถตู้" at 50x: pass as a model with "ราว".** Arithmetic redone: 50 x 60 kg = 3,000 kg (the paper's own ratio top, 100 mg / 2 mg). The HiAce Commuter page opened (SRC010, Australia) gives kerb 2,615 kg, GVM 3,710 kg: 3,000 kg lies between empty and full, 15 % above kerb, so a van with a few people and the word "ราว" is honest; an empty van is 87 % of the figure. The research's lower range (1.9–2.6 t) came from earlier lead-only specs; only the 2,615 kg figure was re-opened. **Thai Commuter weight: not found** on ZigWheels TH, Headlightmag TH or Bangkok Post (dimensions and engine only): access gap. Impact nil: no kg, brand or model is spoken or shown (script line 91), the Australian vehicle is the same 15-seat HiAce-family van class, and a 20 % weight error would not change "รถตู้". The comparison also depends on the unspoken 60 kg person and on the model "her mass is 1 mosquito" (see O4). It is consistent with the authors' own comparison of the ratio to a bus wheel (a bus is a bigger vehicle than the script's van, so the van is the cautious choice). The qualifier "ฝนเม็ดใหญ่ที่สุด" is correct: the 4–100 mg range puts 50x at the top (a 100 mg drop is about 5.8 mm; NASA lead says up to about 6 mm, rare).

**R3. The ground (L10, L13): what the authors say, and the ER07 answer.** The paper states it twice, in its discussion: "Mosquitoes may experience life-threatening impacts if flying very low to the ground" and "it is imperative a mosquito does not fly too low during rain or it will suffer an secondary impact with the ground." It is **not tested** as a variable, so there is no measured near-ground survival rate. The paper's one death is different: a mosquito that landed in a puddle died by drowning (adhesion to the water surface), seen in the jet and drop-chamber runs; that is not an impact result and not the picture the script shows (the script never shows a mosquito in the puddle). So the research's "puddle death single-source" is now partly corroborated by the paper (drowning), but "pinned/pressed into a puddle" is still not in it. Honest answer to the ER07 test ("does this beat have a supported answer?"): **yes for the weaker form, no for the stronger form.** Supported: "near the ground the drop may not leave time to get off" (L10), because the authors say a too-low mosquito risks a secondary impact. Not supported: "the ground is what actually threatens mosquitoes in rain" as a finding (a mortality claim; nothing measured). L10 as written, with "อาจ" and a cut before contact, states only the authors' risk and passes. L13's flip rests on that same untested inference; it passes with its "อาจ" and no death shown. **My recommendation: keep L10 and L13 as written (D2 default stands), and do not extend either: no pinning, no puddle, no death.** The honest alternative if the user wants zero inference is Preaw's D2 cut (end on the ride); I do not require it, because the authors' own sentence is the source. Dependent fact that makes the beat stronger and keeps it honest: the danger zone is only about 20 body lengths (about 6 cm for the mosquito, about 34 m at her size), which is why "ใกล้พื้น" is the right word. Qualitative confidence low-medium.

**R4. Feather and ball (L07) and "ไม่ต้าน แค่ไปกับมัน" (L07, L11): pass, direction true.** The paper's physics: the heavy drop is barely slowed, the light mosquito is pushed along at the drop's speed. A ball hitting a feather behaves the same way (ball barely slows, feather ends up moving with it), so the analogy points the right way and carries no number. The wording "ไม่ต้าน" matches Hu's press quote "Mosquitoes don't resist at all, but simply go with the flow" (SRC002; the paper does not use the phrase), one voice, but a plain-language description of "pushed at the same speed", so it is fine as narration without a source phrase. One limit (O5, optional): a feather hit by a ball is usually flung off, while the mosquito rides the front for several body lengths and then slips off; the analogy covers "the heavy thing does not slow", not "rides along". The following line L08 shows the riding, so no cut is needed.

**R5. "เม็ดฝนไม่ทำร้ายยุงหรอก" (L13): true as an observed outcome (survival), over-flat as "ไม่ทำร้าย" (O2).** Lab evidence: all 17 drop hits and all 6 jet hits survived and flew away; accelerations of 80 ± 35 g (jets) were survived; the force of the hit is called "easily survivable". No follow-up of injury or lifespan is reported, and "ไม่ทำร้าย" can be read as zero harm. It is also lab-only, and the L03/L05 beats already set that up, so the line is not a false premise. Replacement (optional, recommended, same length, same flip, one hedge): **"เม็ดฝนฆ่ายุงไม่ได้หรอก… ที่อาจเล่นงานมัน คือพื้นข้างล่าง"** (16 → 17 syllables, +0.2 s). If Preaw keeps the original it is acceptable. Do not turn it into "ฝนไม่ได้ฆ่ายุง… พื้นต่างหาก" (flat, research fix 6).

**R6. Title (N5, D4): must-fix M1.** "เม็ดฝนหนักเท่ารถตู้" reads as every raindrop, and L02's "ฝนเม็ดใหญ่ที่สุด" exists precisely because that is false: a common 3 mm drop is about 7x, about 420 kg for a 60 kg person (own arithmetic), a quarter of the van. A title and cover are audience-facing statements and the first false hook the viewer meets, so this is a false hook, not an optional polish. **Replacement: "ถ้าคุณตัวเท่ายุง ฝนเม็ดใหญ่หนักเท่ารถตู้"** (D4; "ฝนเม็ดใหญ่" = the biggest ones, same wording family as L02). Still a conditional ("ถ้า") and the cover must carry "ภาพจำลอง" if it shows the shrunk woman. Alternative if the user wants the stronger hook: "ถ้าคุณตัวเท่ายุง ฝนเม็ดใหญ่ ๆ หนักราวรถตู้" (adds the hedge on the title). Do not use the unhedged concept title on the cover, in the description or in the file name shown to viewers.

## Story core

- **Logline:** on your village's first night in the dinosaur age the sky is full of stars, yet not one pattern you know is there. Every star drifts on its own, and over a hundred million years every pattern comes apart; the two bright stars beside ดาวไถ are not even born yet. Everyone expects a giant Moon; it is barely bigger, because it moves away only about four centimetres a year, as slowly as your nails grow. The whole sky changed, and the Moon is the one face you still know.
- **Central question:** where did the stars you know go?
- **Intention / obstacle:** เก่ง climbs to the roof with ยาย to find ดาวไถ (the one pattern he knows) and get his bearings; the sky has none of it. His guess (clouds) is wrong; ยาย's guess (a giant Moon) is wrong.
- **Feeling:** awe at a dense, strange sky; unease at "none you know"; the "what?" of a star younger than dinosaurs; a smile at the fingernail; a quiet, warm landing on the Moon.
- **Takeaway:** stars are not fixed: each drifts slowly, so over tens of millions of years every star pattern changes, and some bright stars are younger than the dinosaurs. The Moon was only slightly closer, so it looked almost the same.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "เปลี่ยนหมด". L04 says it (every pattern changed, a loss); L09 flips it: the whole sky changed, and one face did not.

## Truth and ending

- **Spoken as settled, plain:** stars drift on their own, so over a hundred million years the patterns are gone (F32, SRC05: constellations "didn't exist, at least not in their current forms"); Rigel and Betelgeuse, the bright stars either side of Orion's belt (ดาวไถ), are younger than the dinosaurs' era (SRC05; spoken as "ดาวสว่างสองดวงข้างดาวไถ", no names); the Moon was only slightly bigger (F31); it moves away about 3.8 cm a year (SRC04, "ราวสี่เซนติเมตร").
- **`unchecked` (not in the ledger):** "ดาวก็มีเกิดมีดับ" (L06, textbook astronomy); the fingernail comparison "ช้าพอ ๆ กับเล็บคุณงอก" (L08, a popular comparison: nails grow roughly 3–4 cm a year); "ดาวไถ" = Orion's belt with Betelgeuse and Rigel beside it (L05, star-lore identification).
- **Hedged (one word):** L07 "ราว".
- **Fiction:** the drop, the roof, the cloudless night, the dinosaur call far off.
- **Knowledge twist (rule flip):** "เปลี่ยนหมด" first takes away ดาวไถ; at the end it frames the one thing that stayed. Fair clue: the Moon is already low behind the conifers in B01, ignored (K01).
- **Ending (closed):** L09 over ยาย and เก่ง on the roof, the Moon above the conifers; hold ~2.5 s. No call to action, no tip.

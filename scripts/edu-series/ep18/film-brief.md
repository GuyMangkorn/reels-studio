## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Every Thai viewer has run for a shop awning in a sudden soi downpour, and every viewer has been bitten at the ankle by a mosquito that the rain did not seem to bother. The first frame must say *a mosquito hanging steady in pouring rain at ankle height while someone runs past* with no words (UT01). Feelings by beat: disbelief (a van-heavy drop, and it lives?) → dread at "ไม่นะ!" → relief and a small laugh (not flattened; the feather) → a jolt at "แต่…" (it is taking you down) → tension as the puddle rushes up → release at the slide-off → a dry laugh at the slap that misses.
- **Story first (designed before the kit was opened).** **WOMAN** is a Bangkok auntie in her forties walking home from the market at dusk in the rainy season, caught by a sudden downpour two doors from home: curly dark hair, a mustard short-sleeve blouse, a plum knee skirt, flip-flops, a canvas tote bag that she holds over her head as an umbrella. She is practical and a little cross: running is an inconvenience, the mosquito at her ankle is an insult. Shrunk, she is out of her depth but game; back at full size she is out of breath and still cross. **The mosquito** is the striped Thai ยุงลาย every viewer knows from the soi (Q-F1, user 2026-11-11): a slim black body with white bands across the abdomen, a white lyre mark on the thorax, long black legs with crisp white rings at each joint, two clear wings, a long dark proboscis with a white band near its tip; it is calm, persistent and indifferent to the rain. **The soi** is a narrow concrete lane at about 18:20 in heavy rain: a small family shop (โชห่วย) with its steel shutter half up and warm tungsten light inside, a corrugated tin awning (สังกะสี) streaming a curtain of water off its lip, a puddle filling just outside the drip line, a parked motorbike under a rain cover, crates of drinking water, across the lane a damp wall with a gate, a mango tree and a pole with one cool LED street lamp. **The macro world** is the same soi seen from 22 cm above the puddle at mosquito size: raindrops hang and wobble like slow, glassy, van-sized buns, the shop light glows through them upside down, the puddle below is a lake of warm reflections.
- **Medium.** Painted 2D p5.brush in world units for every place: the soi in metres (`H`) and the macro world in set metres (`M`, EP05 method) through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`); drop-attached shots in drop-local metres (`Dl`); the feather insert in its own small metre set (`I`); the balance as a fixed-plane diagram (`D`). People are 4-head kit rig cards squared to the lens. The mosquito is a new painted insect card on the kit `flea.ts` method (body-length units, one drawing at both scales). Screen layers (OT01–OT03, captions, channel mark) are Remotion DOM above the painted canvas. No footage, no photos, no Three.js (Blender deactivated).

- **Light.** One warm light in a cool world (painted-2d default): the shop's tungsten glow from inside the half-open shutter is the warm key, from behind the awning toward the lane; the overcast dusk sky is a cool soft fill from above; the LED lamp across the lane adds a cool rim on wet edges. No hard shadows (overcast), soft contact shading under feet. In the macro world the shop glow sits behind the drops, so every drop carries a warm upside-down shop inside it and a bright rim (backlight); faces are lit by the cool fill, never by the warm glow (keep the glow off faces). **Chapter light** ([retention camera](../../../docs/cinematography-principles.md#retention-camera)): B01–B02 neutral; the setback B03–B04 cools (grade −8 % warmth, blue `#7f93a8` multiply 12 %), L10's hypothetical coldest (also −20 % saturation); the answer B05 warms (the shop glow brightened +15 % behind the mosquito as it slides off); full size at the end, the warm shop light on her.
- **Camera language.** Frame 0 already shows the strange thing: a mosquito at ankle height, close, steady in the downpour, her running feet splashing behind it. Every shot carries a small eased move (yaw arc 6–10° or a 5–15 % push); the large moves are kept for the scale change (the tilt up to the drop, SH03), the main reveal (the tilt down to the ground, B03-SH01) and the fall shots, where the camera falls with the drop so the world streams upward. At mosquito size the camera stays at her eye height or just below the drop, so the drop reads as huge; one human-scale view returns at W-BACK. The macro world runs in slow motion (about 1/450 real time), so rain hangs and wobbles and the mosquito's wings beat slowly enough to see.
- **Conceal and reveal.** K01 (the puddle filling under the drip line) is planted in B01-SH02 in the middle ground (above the caption band) and again far below in B03-SH01, where it is the ground she is carried toward; paid in B04-SH02 (it rushes up, hypothetical) and in B05-SH04/SH05 (the mosquito hovers just above it). K02 (the mosquito at her ankle, unbothered by the rain) is frame 0; paid in B05-SH04 and the slap.

  - L03 is **not a bounce** (O3): the drop carries the mosquito down out of frame; it flies back up a beat later.
  - L12: the **spread legs** catch the air and tip it off (O1); the wings stay folded back along the body while it rides and open only after it is clear.

## Sound direction

Designed from the story first (the script's [sound intent](script.md#sound-intent-english-notes-narration-unchanged) B01–B05 is the base); a library ID is named only where its description fits the cue as well as a new sound would ([fit beats reuse](../../../docs/sound-generation-workflow.md#fit-beats-reuse)); each named ID is a candidate Ton tests against the description. Ton makes every NEEDS-GEN item after the timed sheets (W5.1); Editor places exactly the cue sheet. Sound never carries a clue: K01–K02 are visual. Shot-local frames per cue are in each shot's Sound line in [storyboard.md](storyboard.md) (estimated).

**The one sound idea: scale changes pitch and time.** At full size the mosquito is a thin high whine and rain is a dense hiss; at mosquito size everything slows and drops: the whine becomes a slow, deep wingbeat thrum, single raindrops become distant soft booms, and the air is a low hush. The shrink and the return are the two pitch moves that carry the viewer across.

1. **Music (one family).** **NEEDS-GEN M1** "small curious pluck motif": kalimba lead with pizzicato strings, a soft marimba and a muted upright bass, a light shaker, 96 bpm (one bar 2.5 s, phrases end cleanly every 2 bars so Editor can stop and restart), curious and friendly, never cute-silly; three energy states as separate stems or takes: **M1-thin** (kalimba and bass only), **M1-relief** (+ pizzicato), **M1-bright** (+ marimba, a step up). **NEEDS-GEN M1b** the tension variant in the same key and tempo: a low muted pizzicato ostinato in eighths with a soft clock-like tick, rising slightly in level, no melody, 8 s, for the ride. **NEEDS-GEN M0** one low bowed double-bass note with a soft swell and a faint high air harmonic, 2.2 s, ominous but not horror (L02, before any music). Arc: no music in the open (rain and whine are the sound) → M0 under L02 → M1-thin enters at L04 (the answer to "why a van?") → silence for W-HIT → M1-relief at L05 → M1-bright at L07 → **drops out on "แต่…"** (L08) → M1b pulse under L09–L10, swelling → **cut to silence** on the cut-away before contact → M1-thin returns after the rewind (L11), M1-relief by L12 → **hard stop** on the splash cut (W-BACK) → no music under L13 → ending E1. (Library `bed_kalimba-rain-explainer_01` "kalimba melody, warm marimba chords, light pizzicato bass, shaker, 96 bpm" is close in colour but is EP14's identity and one flat take with no states: fallback only.)
   - **Ending (UT15, Film's choice): the mosquito wins.** After the slap misses, **E1** two quick descending kalimba plucks, a playful "nope", dry, 0.7 s, in M1's colour (candidate `comedy_kalimba-nope_01`: "two quick descending kalimba plucks, playful not-that, dry, 0.7 s" fits); then the mosquito's whine carries on alone over the rain and fades out over the last 20 f. E1 must not cover the whine's return. **Strings tag (user, 2026-11-11, replaces "EP02 tag not used"):** the episode closes with the EP14 strings ending, the library tag `sfx/stinger/stinger_pizz-tag-ending_01` (pizzicato and marimba, 2.5 s, as EP14 placed it in `scripts/edu-series/ep14/sound-mix.py`: it lands after the last line and E1, rings through the hold, then a short fade to silence). Ton checks it fits this ending's key and mood against M1/E1; Film/Editor re-peg its frame at W5.1 so it does not cover the whine's return.
2. **Ambience per location.**
   - Soi, full size: **A1** rain on the tin awning: heavy rain drumming on corrugated tin, dense metallic patter, water pouring off the lip, heard from under or just outside it (candidate `weather_tin-roof-rain-shelter_01`: "heavy rain drumming on a corrugated tin bus-shelter roof heard from underneath, steady, 8 s loop" fits the material and space; Ton checks the loop seam). **NEEDS-GEN A2** the soi floor in heavy rain: rain hissing on wet concrete, the drip curtain splattering into a filling puddle (deep plops among the hiss), a gutter gurgling, no traffic, no voices, no thunder / steady / water on concrete and water / a narrow lane between low walls, slightly enclosed / 14 s loop / wet, close, cosy-annoying. (Library `city_wet-road-rain-bus_01` has traffic and `weather_rain-thunder-distant_01` has thunder: wrong place.)
   - Macro world: **NEEDS-GEN A3** "slowed giant rain": a low soft hush of air, and every 1–2 s one deep, soft, distant boom of a huge water drop landing somewhere far off (a slowed splash, low and round, long reverb tail), no patter, no hiss / sparse, irregular / water mass and air / vast open space / 16 s loop / the world slowed down and enormous. (No library file is a slowed rain.)
   - Hypothetical (B04-SH02): A3 low-passed about 500 Hz by Editor and −4 dB, with M1b: colder and closer.
3. **Transitions.**
   - **S1 shrink** (B01-SH02 +8): **NEEDS-GEN** a fast downward-then-up pitch zip: the full-size rain and whine stretch and smear upward in pitch as she shrinks, ending in a soft round pop and a breath of air as the slowed world opens (the pop on the puff peak) / one gesture, 0.9 s / air, glassy smear / from close soi to vast / "the world just got huge". (Library `comedy_shrink-pop-gliss_01`, EP05's "shrink pop with rising sparkle glissando", is a cartoon sparkle: it would read cute, not uncanny; considered and not used.)
   - **S2 return** (W-BACK +0): **NEEDS-GEN** the reverse: a low slowed rumble zipping up to normal speed and pitch in 0.5 s, landing exactly on the full-size rain (A1+A2 back at full level on the same frame) / "snap back".
   - **S3 rewind** (B05-SH01 +12): candidate `reveal_rewind-whirr_01` ("fast reverse sweep rising, stops abruptly, 0.44 s") fits "back to that hit"; the stop lands on the frame the replay starts (+25).
   - **S4 riser into the look-down** (B03-SH01): **NEEDS-GEN** a light airy riser (breath and a thin rising tone, not dark), 1.0 s, stopping dead on "แต่…" (+0 is the stop; the riser runs in the last 30 f of B02-SH05). (Library `reveal_riser-tension_01` is "dark": too horror for this channel.)
4. **Foley and creatures.**

Panned to the mosquito's screen side.

   - **D1 the drop overhead** (B01-SH03, B01-SH05): **NEEDS-GEN** a vast mass of water descending overhead: a deep slow wobbling "wub… wub" of the water body oscillating, with a soft low roar of air pushed ahead of it, growing / 3 s / water, air / close above, big / dread.

## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** The viewer arrives with the movie belief: no helmet in space means you explode or freeze. The hook shows the helmet leaving and the sound dying, then takes both myths away one after the other with cartoon gags. The real problems arrive as "แต่…": the glitter at the lips (saliva starting to boil), a real case told from behind in sepia, and the instinct planted in the first two seconds (the gasp and sealed lips, CL01) turning out to be the danger. The release is the long glitter breath across the Earth; the relief is the airlock. Feelings in order: jolt and sudden silence (B01), a grin as the myths fail (B02), "eh?" then a chill (B03), held tension then release (B04), quiet relief (B05). Every action, the timer, the glitter, the gags and the rescue are **dramatization** of hedged facts; the narration carries every claim.
- **Story first.** A spacewalk on a low-Earth-orbit station, the day side of the Earth filling the lower frame. "คุณ" is played by the series' everyday young woman (`woman-bob`, the "คุณ" of EP01 and EP05; see VD02): a little out of her depth in a bulky white suit, tethered to a gold handrail on a grey lattice truss, a few metres from an open airlock hatch where her crewmate floats. She is not a hero; she is the viewer dropped into a "what if". Her crewmate is anonymous behind a gold visor, red stripes on the suit legs, calm, quick, kind. The station is clean industrial: white module skins with quilted insulation, a radiator panel, the truss with its gold handrails, a round hatch. The second place is the airlock: a tight padded cylinder with handrails, cool panel light, one round gauge, and an amber lamp that comes on when the air starts to return. The third is a 1960s test chamber in sepia, a suited figure seen only from behind. Explanations are short painted cards (a film strip, a flask, a water drop, a chest outline), never labels on the body.
- **Medium.** Painted 2D p5.brush through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`, `cam-moves.ts`) in world metres for the three sets; the Earth is a **far backdrop at infinity** (moves with camera yaw and pitch only, never with camera translation). Characters are 4-head kit rig cards in four views squared to the lens (`squareToCam`), floating (no ground) in orbit and the airlock. Inserts: the helmet neck-ring close plate (set-cm) and screen-space cards (film strip, flask, water drop) plus one overlay on the body (chest outline). Backgrounds are washes without outlines; characters, truss edges, hatch rims and hero props get one thin ink line and one cel shade ([DF-20261030-p5brush-large-fill-mottle](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-p5brush-large-fill-mottle)). No SVG characters; no UI except the timer ring and the stamp.

- **Earth limb (est).** At about 400 km altitude the limb sits **19.8° below the local horizontal** (dip = acos(6371 / 6771)) and is a circle around the nadir, so in a level camera it projects as a convex arc that drops toward the frame edges: `limbY(centre) ≈ hy + f·tan(19.8° + pitch)` with ycam pitch positive up. Every orbit shot looks a little down (pitch −5° to −30°) so the Earth fills at least 40 % of the frame ([UT03](../../../docs/user-taste.md#ut-frame): no large empty black). Altitude is a staging value only; no altitude is said or shown (F010c not used).

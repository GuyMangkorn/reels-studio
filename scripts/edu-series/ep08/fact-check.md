## Answers to Preaw's N4

- **(a) L06 "แค่ด้วยความอุ่นในตัว": pass.** Water boils at body temperature (about 37 C) once ambient pressure is at or below roughly 47 mmHg (SRC001, SRC002, SRC013); in orbit pressure is far below that. Saliva is water-based and sits on an exposed moist surface, so the mechanism transfers (SRC006, SRC008 agree). The spoken wording stays fair because it says "เริ่ม" (timing) and the mechanism is stated in terms of pressure ("ไม่มีอากาศกด"), not "your body is hot". Limit: that a person's tongue does this is one retold report (F005), not a measurement. "จริง ๆ นะ" is emphasis on the mechanism, acceptable; if W1.4 needs trimming, dropping "จริง ๆ นะ" (N1) is also safe.
- **(b) L07 "ชุด… เสียแรงดัน" and "ตามรายงาน ปี 1966": pass with one recommended tweak.** Hose or connection came off the suit and the suit lost pressure in a vacuum test chamber: SRC009, SRC011 (same lineage; "3.8 to 0.1 psi" is SRC011 only and is not used). "เสียแรงดัน" is a fair plain description. Year: 1966 in SRC009/010/011, 1965 in NASA's FAQ lineage (SRC006, SRC008); "ตามรายงาน ปี 1966" carries the hedge and the 1965/1966 conflict stays unresolved for the audience. Acceptable. The subtitle "ปี 1966" in digits is fine; Film must not add a date card or source caption (script already says so). Role label: SRC009 says "NASA engineer", others "technician" or "test subject". Recommended fix 2 below. "ห้องสุญญากาศ" is accurate for a vacuum test chamber (near vacuum, not perfect); fine.
- **(c) L08/L09: pass.** L08 paraphrases the retold last-memory (SRC012 lead-only, SRC006 independently says last memory was saliva starting to boil): "สิ่งสุดท้ายที่จำได้ คือน้ำลายบนลิ้นเริ่มเป็นฟอง ก่อนจะหมดสติ" matches the content, "เริ่มเป็นฟอง" matches "starting to bubble", no quotation marks, "เขาเล่าว่า" marks it as his account. L09 "แล้วเขาก็ฟื้น… ตอนห้องมีอากาศกลับมา" matches F005 (regained consciousness during repressurisation; sources disagree on the time, so no time is stated, which is right). Not stated and must stay unstated: a repressurisation time, "คนเดียวที่รอด", lasting harm (sources mention sore ears only). Picture: the needle swing in L09 may be quick but must not carry a number or label.
- **(d) L10 "อากาศในปอดจะดันออก… ปอดอาจบาดเจ็บได้": pass.** Physiology: air held in the lungs expands against near-zero outside pressure (SRC006; SRC003 title is lung damage in explosive suit decompression). "อาจบาดเจ็บ" is the right strength; it does not say "most dangerous" (F007 overstatement avoided). "ข้างนอกไม่มีอากาศดันกลับ" is correct in plain terms.
- **(e) L12 "ยังไม่เคยทดลองกับคนเต็มรูปแบบ" next to the 1966 accident: pass as accurate, recommended clarity fix.** SRC002: human data are "one prolonged unprotected exposure and several anecdotal short-term exposures", most understanding from 1940s–60s animal work; SRC001: literature "very limited". So "no full human experiment" is true, and an accident is not an experiment. The risk is only perception: after L07–L09 a viewer may hear "never tested on people" as contradicting the story just told. Recommended fix 3 names the evidence base (animals plus a few accidents), which also carries the "ในหลายการทดลองกับสัตว์และอุบัติเหตุจริง" part of hedge 1 and 4. If the original is kept, W4 delivery must pause slightly before "ยังไม่เคย…" and stress "เต็มรูปแบบ".
- **(f) L13 "สิ่งที่ควรทำ คือหายใจออก": pass, no extra not-advice line needed.** Exhale-on-decompression is settled aerospace-medicine advice, and the line sits inside a spoken "สมมติ" (L03) about a situation no viewer can be in, directly after the L12 "ไม่เคยทดลอง" hedge. Hedge 5 applies only if it reads as instructions; it does not. Conditions: L03 "นี่แค่สมมติ" stays in the cut (N1 already forbids trimming it), and L12 stays directly before L13 (do not reorder).
- **(g) L04 "ผิวหนังยังรั้งไว้ได้": pass.** Plain wording of F001: skin and tissue hold, ebullism (vapour in tissue) rather than bursting is the injury (SRC001, SRC002). "รั้งไว้" means holds in, which is the right reading. "ตัวอาจบวมขึ้น" is hedged ("อาจ"), no number, no picture; the dog figure "about twice" is not used. User kept this hedge, so it stays. Picture rule holds in the cue ("no puffed cheeks, no swelling shape").

## L05 vacuum claim and ER02 comparison (N9)

- **L05 "ไม่แข็งทันที… เพราะไม่มีอากาศมาพาความร้อนไป": supported.** Heat leaves a body by conduction and convection (need a medium) and radiation (no medium) and evaporation; in vacuum only radiation and evaporation remain, so cooling is slow, not instant (SRC006 "will not instantly freeze"; SRC014 for the same mechanism in a flask). Spoken wording gives the cause that matters. It omits that moist surfaces (mouth, eyes) can chill by evaporation (F002); not needed and eye detail is banned on screen.
- **Comparison cleared as an optional addition:** "เหมือนกระติกน้ำร้อน…" is a fair qualitative analogy, supported by SRC014 (flask gap largely blocks conduction and convection). Limits to keep: it is an analogy for "no air to carry heat", not a claim the body keeps heat like a flask (radiation and evaporation still act, flask heat mostly escapes through the neck), and a real flask is a partial vacuum. Suggested wording, inline fix 4: L05 "และไม่แข็งทันทีด้วย… เหมือนกระติกน้ำร้อน ไม่มีอากาศมาพาความร้อนไป" (replaces "เพราะ", about +5 syllables, about +1.2 s; W1.4 decides if it fits, since the video is already near 58 s). Picture: a plain flask insert is fine; no body shown.

## Story core

- **Logline:** a flea jumps tens of times its own height, so a person-sized flea should leap over a city tower like a movie monster. It would not: grow a body and its legs get stronger, but its weight grows with them, like legs twice as strong carrying a backpack twice as heavy. The tiny flea even needs a trick a big body does not need as much: its legs are so short that muscle cannot push in time, so it squeezes an elastic pad and lets go like a slingshot. The giant flea, in the model, jumps tens of centimetres, the same order as you. And measured in centimetres, you usually out-jump the real flea.
- **Central question:** would a person-sized flea jump over a building, and if not, why not?
- **Intention / obstacle:** the giant flea "wants" its super-jump (the trailer); the obstacle is its own weight growing with it.
- **Feeling:** spectacle and awe at the skyline leap; a comic deflation (the trailer breaks); "oh" at the backpack; surprise at the slingshot; a smug little win for the viewer at the end.
- **Takeaway:** if an animal grows in every direction, its strength and its weight grow together, so, in the simple model, jump height hardly changes with size; big and small jumpers land in tens of centimetres. The flea looks like a champion because it is tiny, and it uses an elastic pad because its legs are too short to push in time.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "ตัวใหญ่ขึ้น… กระโดดไม่ได้สูงขึ้นตาม". Cost to the giant flea (L02, L06, L12); flipped into the viewer's win in L13–L14.

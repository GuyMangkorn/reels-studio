Search summaries of JEB papers (leafhopper 2008; JEB 214:2997, 2011) and citing papers (Science 2018, Nature 2022): small animals jump by catapult because muscle work is limited by the force-velocity curve; the model predicts take-off **time** depends on leg length (shorter leg, shorter take-off time) and velocity does not; short-legged leafhoppers showed the shorter time. ## Dog ruling (N4 a): not acceptable as drawn; use a neutral macro and no scratching

- Narration-silent is not enough. A scratching dog followed by a macro flea on its fur shows "this dog has fleas" in picture, and the brief (research short answer 6; coordinator limits) bans the claim, pet-health content and anything gross. The ending scratch repeats it.
- Also not fabric on the sofa: a flea on the sofa where the dog and the man sit implies an infested home.
- Allowed: the macro flea on a neutral surface with no home or pet link (a plain studio-style macro backdrop, a leaf on the window sill), a scale bar, labelled as an illustration. The dog stays in the living room as a relaxed pet, not scratching, not near the macro cut. The flea growing "beside MAN at the chart" is fiction and fine; it must not appear on or jump from the dog.
- No itching, no bites, no bare skin, no close fur shots; "ภาพจำลอง" label on the macro flea. This changes no spoken word and no beat; the story and ending (man and dog on the sofa, sigh) survive.

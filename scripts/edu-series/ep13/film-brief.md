## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Everyone has seen a flea or a pet; almost nobody has thought about what size does to a jump. The first frame must say *home, afternoon, relaxed* with no words (UT01): a sunlit Thai living room, a man slouched on a sage sofa, a golden retriever asleep beside him, a height chart on the wall. Feelings by beat: curiosity and awe (the tiny flea's leap, the giant's tower arc) → comic deflation (the arc droops, "เฮ้ย") → "oh" (the backpack) → surprise (the slingshot snap) → a held-breath freeze and a shrug (they jump about the same) → a small smug win for the viewer (your mark is higher) → calm (the sofa, the dog's sigh).
- **Story first (designed before the kit was opened).** MAN is about 30, home on a weekend afternoon, barefoot-casual, slow and good-natured; the dog is his, healthy, sleepy and indifferent to the whole thought experiment, which is the joke at the end. The flea is a **cartoon specimen**, not a pest: glossy chestnut, clean, a friendly simple eye, never near the dog. The room is lived-in (UT12): a teak floor, a sage three-seat sofa with a mustard throw, a woven rug, a low cabinet with a plant, a framed print, a wall clock, a standing fan, a window with afternoon sun and a soi beyond. The **height chart** is a family thing on the wall (painted wooden board, coloured 50 cm steps, a cartoon giraffe sticker at the top), and it is the episode's ruler. The **studio** (a seamless pale paper sweep) is the explanation space: every macro and diagram picture happens there, so the viewer learns "paper = illustration, room = story" without a word. The **street** is the one imagined spectacle, now staged as a **monster-movie trailer** (TR01, script R3): the same Bangkok soi and 30-storey condo tower at dusk, a long shadow over a tiny MAN, the giant's crouch and launch, its silhouette arcing over the tower against a city skyline under the card "ถ้าคิดแบบง่าย ๆ"; "ไม่หรอก" stops the film like a tape machine and drops us back into the plain afternoon soi. The trailer is the naive guess made glamorous, so its deflation is the joke.
- **Medium.** Painted 2D p5.brush through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`, `cam-moves.ts`) in world units for every set: room and street in metres, the macro studio in millimetres, the backpack insert studio in metres (same drawing, other scale). Characters are 4-head kit rig cards squared to the lens; the flea is a new flat card (profile main view). Screen layers (labels, counter, arrows, icons, band, tags, captions, channel mark) are Remotion DOM above the painted canvas, the series practice. No footage, no photos, no Three.js (Blender deactivated).

- **Trailer look (TR01, VD26).** Dusk: a low amber sun disc behind the tower, sky bands from violet-teal `#3b4a6b` at the top through dusty rose `#c98a7a` to amber `#f2a65a` at the horizon; teal haze `#6f8f9a` on distance; shadows cool teal, rims warm `#ffc27a`; film grain and a soft vignette **on the three trailer plates only** (DF-20261030-painted-screen-layers-overlay). No letterbox bars: in 9:16 they would eat the frame and cover the channel mark and the caption band. The card "ถ้าคิดแบบง่าย ๆ" in trailer type: the series caption font, bold, wide tracking, white with a soft warm glow, centred in slot TC (rows 1010–1100, est). The tape-stop drains the grade to flat grey before the cut back to the plain afternoon.
- **Light.** Room: low warm sun from the window at screen right (about 16:00), hard-edged patches on the floor and the lower wall, soft warm fill from the room, cool bounce `#c7d3dc` at 15 % in the shadows; the light never changes during the episode (the giant is imagined, not a weather event). Studio: soft top-front light, one small soft contact shadow, no hard shadow. Street: bright overcast haze, the tower top a little brighter (it is the goal). The see-through thorax glows from inside (amber) and dims after the release.
- **Camera language.** Story shots in the room move gently inside the set (small yaw drifts and push-ins). **The truth shot is locked** (VD21): square to the wall, long lens, no move, because a moving camera would change apparent heights while the viewer measures. The street shots are the only steep, low perspectives and the trailer the only long-lens spectacle; the truth shot is flat and measured; that contrast is the argument in pictures. Macro shots move with the flea or push in slowly.
- **Conceal and reveal.** The viewer sees the grown flea before MAN does (SH03, dramatic irony), paid off by V01. The giant's height in the jump is **not** revealed as equal: the band is broad and the giant peaks a little below MAN (VD21, Boat N4 e). The real flea mark appears only in B05.
- **Truth and safety (binding, Boat).** No scratching, itching, bite, bare-skin or close-fur shot anywhere. The dog only rests and sleeps (FIX-5). The real-size flea appears only on the studio sweep, labelled "ภาพจำลอง", never on fur, sofa, floor or near the dog. The giant flea never appears in a frame with the dog, never on or jumping from the dog. No digits beyond "2 มม.", "×10 … ×50" and "×2" (script list); the chart has ticks only. No flea mouthparts, blood, eggs or larvae; no collapse, no breathlessness, no "impossible" picture.

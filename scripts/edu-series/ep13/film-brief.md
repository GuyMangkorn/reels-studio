## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Everyone has seen a flea or a pet; almost nobody has thought about what size does to a jump. The first frame must say *home, afternoon, relaxed* with no words (UT01): a sunlit Thai living room, a man slouched on a sage sofa, a golden retriever asleep beside him, a height chart on the wall. Feelings by beat: curiosity and awe (the tiny flea's leap, the giant's tower arc) → comic deflation (the arc droops, "เฮ้ย") → "oh" (the backpack) → surprise (the slingshot snap) → a held-breath freeze and a shrug (they jump about the same) → a small smug win for the viewer (your mark is higher) → calm (the sofa, the dog's sigh).
- **Story first (designed before the kit was opened).** MAN is about 30, home on a weekend afternoon, barefoot-casual, slow and good-natured; the dog is his, healthy, sleepy and indifferent to the whole thought experiment, which is the joke at the end. The flea is a **cartoon specimen**, not a pest: glossy chestnut, clean, a friendly simple eye, never near the dog. The room is lived-in (UT12): a teak floor, a sage three-seat sofa with a mustard throw, a woven rug, a low cabinet with a plant, a framed print, a wall clock, a standing fan, a window with afternoon sun and a soi beyond. The **height chart** is a family thing on the wall (painted wooden board, coloured 50 cm steps, a cartoon giraffe sticker at the top), and it is the episode's ruler. The **studio** (a seamless pale paper sweep) is the explanation space: every macro and diagram picture happens there, so the viewer learns "paper = illustration, room = story" without a word. The **street** is the one imagined spectacle: a bright Bangkok soi with a 30-storey condo tower.
- **Medium.** Painted 2D p5.brush through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`, `cam-moves.ts`) in world units for every set: room and street in metres, the macro studio in millimetres, the backpack insert studio in metres (same drawing, other scale). Characters are 4-head kit rig cards squared to the lens; the flea is a new flat card (profile main view). Screen layers (labels, counter, arrows, icons, band, tags, captions, channel mark) are Remotion DOM above the painted canvas, the series practice. No footage, no photos, no Three.js (Blender deactivated).

- **Light.** Room: low warm sun from the window at screen right (about 16:00), hard-edged patches on the floor and the lower wall, soft warm fill from the room, cool bounce `#c7d3dc` at 15 % in the shadows; the light never changes during the episode (the giant is imagined, not a weather event). Studio: soft top-front light, one small soft contact shadow, no hard shadow. Street: bright overcast haze, the tower top a little brighter (it is the goal). The see-through thorax glows from inside (amber) and dims after the release.
- **Camera language.** Story shots in the room move gently inside the set (small yaw drifts and push-ins). **The truth shot is locked** (VD21): square to the wall, long lens, no move, because a moving camera would change apparent heights while the viewer measures. The myth shot is the only steep, low perspective; the truth shot is flat and measured; that contrast is the argument in pictures. Macro shots move with the flea or push in slowly.
- **Conceal and reveal.** The viewer sees the grown flea before MAN does (SH03, dramatic irony), paid off by V01. The giant's height in the jump is **not** revealed as equal: the band is broad and the giant peaks a little below MAN (VD21, Boat N4 e). The real flea mark appears only in B05.
- **Truth and safety (binding, Boat).** No scratching, itching, bite, bare-skin or close-fur shot anywhere. The dog only rests and sleeps (FIX-5). The real-size flea appears only on the studio sweep, labelled "ภาพจำลอง", never on fur, sofa, floor or near the dog. The giant flea never appears in a frame with the dog, never on or jumping from the dog. No digits beyond "2 มม.", "×10 … ×50" and "×2" (script list); the chart has ticks only. No flea mouthparts, blood, eggs or larvae; no collapse, no breathlessness, no "impossible" picture.

## Sound direction

Following the script's [sound intent](script.md#sound-intent-english-notes-narration-unchanged) (its B01–B05 rows are the base); each cue below was designed from the shot first, and a library ID is named only where the description fits ([fit beats reuse](../../../docs/sound-generation-workflow.md#fit-beats-reuse)); Ton makes every NEEDS-GEN item, Editor sets levels and places exactly the cue sheet at W5.3. Sound never carries a clue: CL01 (chart) and CL02 (folded legs) are visual.

- **Music family and arc.** A light comic pizzicato-and-marimba pulse, friendly and dry, the series' explainer colour but brighter. Energy (of 5): B01 2 → 3 (macro leap, grow) → 3.5 (hopeful swell on the tower arc) → 1 (deflating droop) → 0.5 (half-beat dropout for "เฮ้ย") → B02 2 (lighter pulse) → B03 1.5 (thins to a held pad in the slow motion) → snap → B04 2.5 (pulse back, building through the crouch) → **0 at the apex freeze** → 2 on the landing → B05 2 (warm, a bit smug) → the closing tag → room tone only.
  - **NEEDS-GEN M1** comic explainer pulse: playful pizzicato strings with soft marimba and a light woodblock tick, dry and bouncy, curious and cheeky, no drums or bass drop, 104 bpm, 60 s with clean phrase ends every 4 bars so Editor can drop it out and back. (Library `bed_mystery-investigation_01` and `bed_curious-explainer-light_01` were checked: the first is "investigation", a mystery colour; the second has guitar and no pizzicato; neither fits the comic deflations; fallback only.)
  - **Ending tag (UT15, Film's choice):** `stinger_pizz-tag-ending_01` (warm playful pizzicato and marimba tag resolving on a ringing chord, 2.5 s; matches M1's colour and the warm smug close; not listened, Ton confirms on a listen, else NEEDS-GEN in the same words).
- **Ambience per location.**
  - Room (SET-LR13): `city_soi-afternoon-day_01` (sunny Bangkok soi afternoon, far traffic, birds; fits the window) low, under **NEEDS-GEN A1** standing fan: a domestic standing fan on medium, soft steady motor hum with a gentle blade whirr and a faint slow oscillation, small tiled-and-wood living room, 3 m away, 12 s loop, calm.
  - Studio (SET-STUDIO, macro and insert): **NEEDS-GEN A2** studio hush: a very quiet small photo-studio room tone, soft air, no hum, no events, close and dry, 10 s loop; it should feel like holding your breath over a specimen. Room tone ducks out on every cut to the studio and back in on the cut out.
  - Street (SET-SOI): `city_soi-afternoon-day_01` at full level plus **NEEDS-GEN A3** high open air: a soft wide outdoor breeze high between buildings, airy, 6 s, the feeling of looking up.

- Mode: educational explainer on a real phenomenon. Central question: why does cutting an onion make us cry, and which tricks work? Learning takeaway: the tears are a chemical reaction that starts only on cutting; barriers work best, chilling and "cure" folk tricks are weak or unproven.

## Supported story angles

1. The reveal "nothing happens until the cut" (F001–F005): enzyme and amino-acid compound stay apart; cutting lets them meet; a second enzyme (LFS) makes the gas.
2. "Tears are our eye's reflex to wash it off" (F006), not the onion's message; the onion's defence function is a hypothesis (F019). This keeps the "alarm" framing fair.
3. Trick tournament with honest grading: barrier (goggles) works; sharp knife and slow cutting reduce droplets (but tears not measured); fan partial; bread, gum, spoon rejected (mouth is irrelevant); candle and cold are unproven or mixed.
4. Japan tearless-onion coda: the 2002 discovery of LFS led to an onion made without the enzyme (Smile Ball); different from Sunions (F021, F022).

## Coordinator-style rules for the script (wording)

1. Name TRPA1 nowhere; say "ปลายประสาทที่กระจกตา" or "เส้นประสาทที่ผิวตา".
2. Never state that chilling works; either drop chilling or give it as "หลักฐานยังไม่ชัด". Do not make "cold + sharp knife = dry eyes" the payoff.
3. Say sharp knife and slow cutting "ลดละอองที่กระเด็น" (droplet evidence), not "ไม่ร้องไห้"; goggles/sealed eyewear are the best-supported barrier (informal tests, no controlled trial).
4. "Alarm/security" is a metaphor and a hypothesis (defence against pests); not intent, not a warning aimed at humans. Mark visually as illustration if a literal alarm icon is used.
5. Gas colour (lilac mist) and any sound are illustrations; say so on screen or in the sound plan.
6. Do not use "sulfuric acid", "peeling order", "root end", "everyone", or any percentage from the tips pages.
7. If the tearless onion appears: House Foods (Japan) found the enzyme; the onion with less of it exists (Smile Ball); do not name Sunions as House Foods' product; no claim on today's availability.
8. Fiction: the cook's trials are dramatised. Do not present the comic trick outcomes as test results of the cook.

## Correction note (Boat, W1.3, 2026-11-05)

- SRC003 / F021: the Sci Rep 2016 abstract (Europe PMC record, PMID 27048847, Kato et al.; seen through a summarizing fetch) says tearless, non-pungent onions were made non-transgenically by neon-ion irradiation of seeds (20 Gy), and the low pungency came from **reduced alliinase activity (very low alliinase mRNA)**, not LFS deficiency as written above. Not used in the EP02 script; fix before any later coda uses it.
- F004: Plant Physiol 2008 (SRC002) does **not** state compartmentation (only that the enzyme must act on the substrate "in close proximity" in crushed tissue). Classic model (Lancaster and Collin 1981: substrate cytoplasm, alliinase vacuole) is seen only as a statement in later reviews via search summary. A 2024 Frontiers in Plant Science study (10.3389/fpls.2024.1488553, abstract via summarizer) hypothesizes that many onion alliinases lack a vacuolar signal and act in intact cells (isoallicin, not shown to be the tear gas) as a "dual-defense mechanism"; defence role inferred from expression data, not tested on pests.

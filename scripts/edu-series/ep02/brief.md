## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Everyone has cried over an onion; few know the tears start only at the cut. Feelings in order: comic sympathy (B01 wail), a deadpan "nothing happens" (B02), a small "oh" inside the cell (B03), recognition at the eye (B04), laughs at the honest trick grades (B05), a satisfied deadpan payoff with goggles (B06), a warm figure-of-speech close (B07). The picture never shows a trick result as data: outcomes are comedy, the VO carries the evidence status.
- **Story first.** One home cook, alone in her own warm kitchen on a late afternoon, low gold window light, a pot ticking on the stove. She is cheerful and stubborn: every failed trick is met with a shrug and another try. The onion is a plain yellow onion with no face (no intent, F020). The two "inside" worlds are the same warm paint, magnified: the onion cells are translucent cream-gold watercolour chambers lit from behind like the window light; the eye is a big wet painted dome. The only cool colours are the lilac gas (illustration) and the fridge's cold light.
- **Medium.** Painted 2D, p5.brush, [painted 3D camera](../../../docs/film-visual-brief-workflow.md#painted-3d-camera) (project default, user 5 Nov 2026): the kitchen is built in world metres and seen through the kit yaw camera (`kit/ycam.ts`); every shot moves the camera inside the set; the cook is drawn in one of four body views squared to the lens (`squareToCam`). Backgrounds are washes without outlines; the cook, counter, board, knife and hero props get one thin ink line and one cel shade. Doodles (the "?", the ✗, the alarm bell) are chalk-white brush line, drawn on, never type. No SVG characters (UT11).
- **Colour and value key** (est; Boss/Film tune at W3.1).

- **Camera language.** Vertical 1080×1920, eye level and observational, close on the lone cook (0.9–1.9 m, [UT13](../../../docs/user-taste.md#ut-close-camera)); the set stands at about 35–50° to the lens so the island and back counter run diagonally. ## Sound direction

Following the script's "Sound intent" (R1.1); Editor sets levels and final picks at W4+. Library checked 2026-11-05 at `~/ReelsStore/library/sound/INDEX.md` (171 lines). Sound never carries a fact; the gas is silent in reality, every gas/cell/eye/sting sound is comedy or illustration.

- **Music family.** A light comic pizzicato kitchen bed (pizz strings + marimba/plucks, ~100 bpm, energy 2/5 → 3/5). Arc: enters on L01; ducks under the L03 wail; small and dry in B02; thins to a curious plucked line for the cell view (B03); a touch busier in B04 with a near-silence beat before L09; bouncy, montage-cut in B05; **drops out** on the goggles snap (B06-SH02) for ~0.5 s, returns brighter and confident; softens to a short warm tag in B07 and rings out across the end hold and fade. No loop tail. Candidates: `bed_curious-explainer-light_01` (marimba + plucked guitar, 95 bpm, loopable; partial fit, not pizzicato); `pluck_soft-note-01_01`…`06_01` for the B03 curious line; `stinger_outro-resolve_01` only if the warm tag needs a chord (5.4 s, probably too long). Gaps: NEEDS-GEN comic pizzicato kitchen bed 60 s ~100 bpm with a clean drop point; NEEDS-GEN short warm pizz tag/ring-out 2 s.
- **Ambience per location.** Kitchen (LF01): `room_soi-morning-quiet_01` (quiet room tone, faint birds; reads as a window-lit home) under `room_pot-steam-hiss_01` (low pot steam, matches the stove pot) very low. Fridge POV (B05-SH04): `room_shop-fridge-hum_01` for the opened fridge, short. Cell macro (LF02) and eye macro (LF03): no room tone; a soft wet bed (NEEDS-GEN wet cell texture loop 6 s, fiction).
- **SFX language.** Kitchen foley is crisp and close; macro sounds are soft, wet and round; comedy stings are small. Library: `foley_fridge-seal-pop_01` + `foley_fridge-seal-thump_01` (fridge, both "generated, not listened"), `reveal_reverse-swell-airy_01` / `whoosh_air-swish-rising_01` (gas rising), `ui_pop_01` (doodle "?" appears), `ui_click-tick_01` (nerve-ending sparks, fiction), `water_mug-pour-soft_01` (partial fit for the tear flush). NEEDS-GEN: knife chop on a wooden board (hard and fast, B01), sharp slow knife slice (B06-SH04), whole onion "tok" on wood, wet cell-burst pops, comic water-gush tear swell, bread crunch/muffled, match strike + flame, comic "wah-wah" sting (bread only), small shrug sting (two-note "hm?", candle and fridge), rubber goggles-strap snap, chalk doodle scribble (optional), toy alarm "beep-beep" doodle sound (B07, optional, never over the VO). Vocal: the cook's sobs/sniffs/muffled sob are non-verbal character takes at W4 (gemini-tts vocal sounds or a performer), not from the sound library.
- **Silence policy.** (1) Music ducks under the L03 wail. (2) A tiny deadpan pause after L04 "…แทบไม่มีอะไรเกิดขึ้น" (ambience only). (3) Near-silence (ambience only, ~0.4 s) before L09. (4) ~0.5 s true music silence on the goggles reveal before L15, the strap snap the only sound. (5) After L18: no VO; the bed's warm tag rings out over the ~1.0 s hold and fades with the picture.
- **Not claimed.** No sound is presented as a recording of the chemistry; the `ภาพจำลอง` tag (VD08) covers picture and sound of B03–B04.

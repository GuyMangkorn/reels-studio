## ALT-A decision: OUT

Reason: (1) F019 is a hypothesis; the only new support found (2024 Frontiers abstract, "dual-defense mechanism") is inferred from expression data, not tested on pests, and SRC002 makes no defence claim. (2) The wording “เคมีชุดนี้ อาจช่วยกันแมลงที่มากัดมัน” names a specific purpose (insects) for "this chemistry" (which includes the tear gas) that no inspected source supports, and the 2024 idea is that the defence works in intact tissue, which sits oddly beside L18's "เฉพาะตอนโดนบุก". (3) It costs about 3.5 s (to 57-59 s) and L17-L18 already close the metaphor honestly without it. If it is ever used: “บางคนเดาว่า สารกำมะถันพวกนี้ อาจช่วยให้หัวหอมป้องกันตัว แต่ยังไม่มีการพิสูจน์” (general sulfur chemistry, not the tear gas, no insects), placed before L17.

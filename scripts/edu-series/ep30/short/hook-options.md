## H1 — HK01 broken certainty (recommended)

- **Spoken Thai (0–5 s):** "ออสเตรเลียเคยส่งทหารพร้อมปืนกล ไปรบกับนกอีมู… แล้วต้องถอนทัพ ไม่ใช่รอบเดียวนะ สองรอบ"
- **Beat shape:** claim over the picture (heroic low angle on the gun crew, reverse wide on emus strolling past) → B01 farmers ask, three men and two guns arrive → plan A scatter, plan B dam jam, plan C truck can't keep up → first withdrawal, second try, recall → punchline ending on the coat-of-arms callback ("ยังอยู่").
- **Why it works:** it is the share line itself ("ออสเตรเลียแพ้สงครามให้นก" in true words), a viewer would bet it is false, and the picture proves it in the first second. "สองรอบ" lands the first bit (deadpan topper) inside 5 s and plants the escalation: the viewer stays to see how it could go wrong twice.
- **Leans on:** F001 (withdrawn twice, Nov–Dec 1932), F002 (Meredith plus two gunners, two Lewis guns).
- **Rules it bends:** none. It is ER01's default shape, so no override is recorded.

## H2 — HK08 true extreme number

- **Spoken Thai (0–5 s):** "ปืนกลสองกระบอก กระสุนหนึ่งหมื่นนัด… ส่วนฝั่งศัตรู มีราว ๆ สองหมื่นตัว แล้วศัตรูก็คือ… นก"
- **Beat shape:** an army-briefing tally board fills up (guns, rounds), then the enemy count, then the reveal of an emu → same plan A/B/C chain → the score at the end (Meredith's own claimed ratio, attributed to his report, F008) or the coat-of-arms callback.
- **Why it works:** rule of three in the hook itself (guns, rounds, enemy), with the turn on the last word; the numbers make it feel like a real war briefing before the reveal undercuts it.
- **Leans on:** F002 (two Lewis guns, 10,000 rounds), F003 (about 20,000 emus, an estimate; the hedge "ราว ๆ" carries it).
- **Rules it bends:** ER01 lightly: the broken certainty (army loses to birds) arrives at about 6–8 s instead of inside 5 s, so the first claim is a set-up, not the contradiction. ER10: the 20,000 is an estimate and keeps its one hedge.

## H3 — HK07 start at the climax

- **Spoken Thai (0–5 s):** "นกอีมูเป็นพันตัว เดินเข้ามาในระยะยิงเอง ทหารกดไก… ยิงไปได้สิบกว่าตัว ปืนกลก็ติด" (beat) "ย้อนไปสองวันก่อน"
- **Beat shape:** the dam ambush peak first (flock walking in, gun barking, then a dry click and one shell) → rewind to 2 Nov, farmers and the three-man unit → plan A scatter → back at the dam, now the jam means something → plan C truck → withdrawal → punchline ending.
- **Why it works:** the jam is the funniest picture in the story and a visual gag on its own (the gun dies at the one moment the birds cooperate); opening on it buys a laugh before any context.
- **Leans on:** F005 (4 Nov 1932 dam, more than 1,000 emus, gun jammed after about 12 kills); F001 for "สองวันก่อน" (troops arrived 2 Nov).
- **Rules it bends:** ER01: opens on an event, not a contradicting claim; the "army loses to birds" stake is stated after the rewind (about 8–10 s). Factual caution: accounts differ on which day the jam happened (research dispute 5); the line keeps to the safe wording and the date only appears as "สองวันก่อน", which goes back to Boat at fact-check.

## H4 — new: the enemy's praise (quote hook)

- **Spoken Thai (0–5 s):** "แมเจอร์ที่ถูกส่งไปยิงนก… พูดเองว่านกพวกนี้ ทนปืนกลได้อย่างกับรถถัง"
- **Beat shape:** a press-room flashbulb on the major (generic face) as the narrator relays the remark → "เรื่องมันเริ่มจาก…" farmers and the unit → plan A/B/C → withdrawals → punchline ending on the coat-of-arms callback; the quote is not repeated as a lament after losing.
- **Why it works:** the irony is immediate: the man sent to kill the birds is the one praising them; "รถถัง" next to a fluffy bird is a picture gag.
- **Leans on:** F010 (spoken to the press, reported wording, tense varies; medium confidence that he said it). The narrator relays it, it is not voiced by the major as a quote card.
- **Rules it bends:** ER01: an irony hook rather than a broken certainty; the stake (army withdraws) comes at about 10 s. It spends the story's best wow line in the first 5 s, so the middle needs another "จริงดิ" (F009 "ไม่มีใครบาดเจ็บ นอกจากศักดิ์ศรี", reported wording). Highest factual exposure of the four: the venue and tense are unverified (research open question 3).

## Recommendation

**H1 (HK01).** It is the story's share line in true words, the picture proves it at once, "สองรอบ" puts a bit inside 5 s and seeds the escalation, and it saves the "tanks" remark for a mid-story wow punchline where it lands harder; it needs no channel override.

## Sound intent for the hook (English notes; Ton designs later)

- H1: one heroic military snare roll and brass sting under the claim, cut dead to silence on "ถอนทัพ", one soft emu boom on "สองรอบ".
- H2: typewriter or tally-board clacks per number, a march building, record-scratch stop on "นก".
- H3: Lewis gun burst, then a dry metal click and a single shell tinkle in silence; a tape-rewind riser on "ย้อนไป".
- H4: flashbulb pop and newsreel projector whirr; a tank-tread clank stinger under "รถถัง".

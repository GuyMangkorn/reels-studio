## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** The viewer is told the ending in the first five seconds (two withdrawals) and watches with the narrator, who is plainly on the birds' side. The fun is the gap between war-film grammar played straight (low heroic angles, inspection line-ups, ambush, chase, retreat bugle) and birds who do not care. The men are never the joke: the gear, the ground and the birds are ([CAT02 check](../research.md#cat02-handling-check-real-named-people)).
- **Medium.** Painted 3D camera (project default): p5.brush sets in world metres through the kit yaw camera, four-head cast squared to the lens, mitten hands. Over the 1932 picture sits a **newsreel screen layer** (VD30-01). Today (B07) is full colour without it.
- **Colour and value key.** 1932 = amber-tinted near-monochrome (tinted-print look, not a desaturated colour picture): white-hot pale sky (lightest value), pale gold wheat (light), red-brown earth and khaki (mid), emus dark grey-brown (darkest mass), so a flock reads as dark shapes on light wheat at 270 px. Setbacks (the two withdrawals) shift the tint cooler and greyer with low west light ([retention camera](../../../../docs/cinematography-principles.md#retention-camera): light marks the chapters). Today = warm full colour, blue sky, sunlit sandstone: the first saturated frame of the video is the emblem.
- **Camera language.** Every shot moves inside the set (arc 6–10°, push 5–15 %); large moves only for the hook, the B02 crane, the B03 scatter top shot, the B05 chase and the B07 crane. Two fixed vantages repeat on purpose: the gun position (B01, B02, B03) and the **retreat vantage** at the fence (B05-SH03, B06-SH03, B06-SH04), so the second withdrawal lands as a rerun.
- **Conceal/reveal.** The jam is hidden until L10 "ปืนกลติด" (B04-SH01–SH03 play as if the plan works). The coat of arms is hidden until L17 (no emblem anywhere before B07, no plant). No kill counts, tallies, score, "lost" or "war declared" text anywhere ([script brief notes](script.md#film-brief-notes)).

## Sound direction

- **Music.** Original mock-heroic 1930s newsreel march (2/4, 112–116 bpm): snare, cornet and trombone lead, tuba bass, glockenspiel, played by a small brass band and heard through an old-record tone (band-limited about 300 Hz–5 kHz, light wow, faint crackle) for B01–B06. Energy arc: B01 snare roll and brass sting, cut dead on "ถอนทัพ" → B02 jaunty medium → B03 confident → B04 drops to a sneaky low pizzicato ambush vamp → B05 fast bouncy galop (about 140 bpm), deflating tuba slide into "ถอนทัพ" → B06 low admiring brass chorale under L14, trailer hit on "ภาคสอง", march returns slower and slightly detuned → B07 the old-record filter opens to full range and an original warm anthem-style brass pad rises (never the real anthem).
- **Ending (UT15, Film's choice).** The B07 pad pulls back after "…" so "ไม่เคยถอยสักรอบ" lands dry; on the line's end the emu boom (callback of B01, now clean hi-fi), then the pad resolves on a warm major chord with one clean snare flam button "ta-dum" (the newsreel march's drum, out of the filter), ringing through the 2.0 s hold. `NEEDS-GEN` (no library ending fits a brass-and-snare button; `stinger_pizz-tag-ending_01` is pizzicato and marimba).
- **Ambience per location.** Paddock (space P): dry hot wind hissing through ripe wheat, occasional far raven "aah" and a passing fly, sparse emu drumming far off. Dam (space D): lighter wind, soft water lap at the earth bank, a slow windmill creak. Arms (space A, today): clean city exterior, soft far traffic, light breeze, one magpie warble, no filter.
- **Newsreel bed.** A faint projector whirr and crackle under every 1932 shot (the layer's sound); it is the "silence" of the newsreel. It winds down with the grade at the B07 cut.
- **SFX language.** Period mechanical sounds played straight (Lewis gun rattle with a dry slap off the open plain, pan-magazine click, 1930s four-cylinder truck, leaf springs, brass casings), emu sounds from life (deep neck-sac drumming boom, low grunts, heavy padded three-toed footfalls on dry stalks, feather shakes), and sparse comic accents (service-bell ding, sputtering fanfare, record scratch, slide whistle, tuba slide, off-key bugle) inside the same old-record tone. All gunfire, engine and emu sounds are generated reconstruction, never presented as 1932 recordings.
- **Silence policy.** Five planned silences (whirr only): the dead cut on "แล้วต้องถอนทัพ" (B01-SH02), half a beat after the burst (B03-SH02), from "…" in L10 to the click and one beat after (B04-SH03/SH04), one beat before the emu's bow (B06-SH02), and the music pull-back before "ไม่เคยถอยสักรอบ" (B07-SH02). **Ringing silence (R2, [VD30-11](#vd30-11)):** the two silences after gunfire are not empty: a faint high ring (ears after gunfire) and the plain's last echo decay under the whirr; B04's also carries two or three cooling ticks of the hot barrel shroud. The ring is gone before the B04-SH04 click, which lands on dead silence.
- **Gunfire (R2, [VD30-11](#vd30-11)).** The two bursts are the loudest moments of the 1932 part: one long continuous take each (about 9 rounds a second, the Lewis's open-bolt clatter between cracks), through the old-record tone but with its low-mid punch kept. Music drops out on the first round (the gun takes the soundtrack). Under the words the burst ducks about 6 dB below NARR; it swells to full in the "…" gap. B03 ends on the bolt catching open plus the ring tail; B04 stops dead on "…" (no tail, a hard cut to silence), then the ring and the ticks.
- **Voices and mix.** NARR (Kore) clean and on top; music ducked about 14 dB under VO; no SFX onset on a key word unless the row says so. FARMER and EMU are in-world: Film recommends the same light band-limit as the newsreel so they sit in the picture (Editor's call at the mix). Each cue is designed per shot in [storyboard.md](storyboard.md); a library ID appears only where the file fits ([fit beats reuse](../../../../docs/sound-generation-workflow.md#fit-beats-reuse)); everything else is `NEEDS-GEN` for Ton.

## Gunfire beats (VD30-11)

<a id="vd30-11"></a>Decision **VD30-11**, Film, BRIEF-R2, 2026-11-19, from the user's request (via the coordinator): make the gunfire a more prominent scene by expanding B03-SH01 and B04-SH03; no new shot, no added run time (est. 59.4–63 s already; the cut order stays [open question 4](#open-questions)). Shot rows: [storyboard.md](storyboard.md) B03-SH01, B04-SH03 (`EDU-EP30-BOARD-R2`).

- **Story.** L06 and L10 are the only moments the army actually fires. A war newsreel would make them the big action beat, so they are played straight and loud: the gun is impressive and the crew competent. The joke is the birds' reaction (B03: they scatter out of range; B04: they flinch and keep queueing) and the dead cut to silence at "…" in L10. CAT02 and the script: no bird is shown hit, wounded or falling; strikes land short of or around the birds; faces stay calm.
- **Timing (no run-time change).** The burst starts on the onset of "ยิง" (VO-relative, `estimated`). B03-SH01: from "ยิง" through the "…" gap to 2 frames before the cut (about 1.2–1.6 s, 11–15 rounds; BOARD-R1 had about 0.9 s). B04-SH03: from "ยิง" to the "…" onset, the whole phrase "ยิงไปได้แค่สิบกว่าตัว" (about 1.3–1.5 s, 12–14 rounds; BOARD-R1 had 0.8 s). Integer frames come from the voice-lock measure file at the timed pass.
- **Per-round grammar** (story clock, seeded; one round every 3 or 4 frames at 30 fps, about 550 rounds/min, estimate for a Lewis gun):
  1. *Flash.* At the `muzzle` anchor of `buildLewisGun`: on the round frame n a pure-white painted core 0.25–0.35 m across (three seeded shapes: four-point star, forward cone, round bloom), on n+1 a half-size warm decay, then off. Over it a screen halation (`Spot`, ≤ 0.3 peak per the kit rule) centred on the projected muzzle, radius about 180 px (B03) / 260 px (B04), and a newsreel exposure pop: frame luma +8 % on n, +3 % on n+1 (the layer's flicker driven by the rounds).
  2. *Smoke.* One grey-brown puff per round at the muzzle, darker than the wheat, so the white core reads against the pale field under the full tint; the puffs build into a low haze drifting screen left (estimate) and thin over about 1.5 s after the last round.
  3. *Recoil shudder.* The gun moves 5 mm back along the barrel with 0.3° of muzzle rise on n and returns by n+2; tripod feet stay planted. The pan magazine turns one step per round (47-round pan, 7.66°, `magTurn`). GUNNER-1's shoulders and hat jolt 1.5 cm back on n; mittens stay on grip and stock.
  4. *Casings.* One brass .303 case (56 mm) per round, out of the receiver's right side (estimate; Boat may verify), arc 0.3–0.5 m up, landing 0.8–1.5 m right of the gun, two or three spins per arc, one glint frame at the top. B03: one bounce on the dirt. B04: plops into the shallows or pings on the slope. They stay where they land.
  5. *Camera shake.* An impulse per round on the picture canvas only (captions, mark, label and title overlays never move; [DF-20261030-painted-screen-layers-overlay](../../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-painted-screen-layers-overlay)), seeded direction, decaying over 3 frames: B03 ±5 px x, ±7 px y, ±0.3° roll; B04 ±7 px, ±9 px, ±0.4°; plus a ±2 px tremor while the burst runs. Plate overscan ≥ 24 px per side (adds to the newsreel weave). Shake reads the shot clock ([DF-20261030-qa-plate-overlay-clock](../../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-qa-plate-overlay-clock)).
  6. *Muzzle blast.* From round 1 the stalks or grass 1–3 m ahead of the muzzle flatten and whip, and dust and chaff lift into a ground cloud that grows over the burst.
  7. *Strikes.* Spurts walk along the ground short of the birds and never reach one, one per round at seeded positions, each about 8 frames: B03 dust and chaff in the wheat at z −14..−26, 0.6–1.2 m tall; B04 mud spurts on the shore and thin water spouts in the shallows along the queue's line, 0.4–0.9 m.
- **Birds.** B03: 2 frames after round 1 the near birds' heads snap up; from about round 3 the band tears into three or four clumps bolting left and right with dust trails; the scatter continues as the six groups of B03-SH02 (row unchanged). B04: the nearest five or six birds flinch (heads duck, stubby wings flap), two hop sideways into the shallows with a splash, the line bunches, and before the burst ends it has re-formed and keeps walking. "Breaks/continues": Film picks *continues*, because B04-SH05 needs the whole queue intact and staring (open question 6).
- **Stop.** B03 ends on the last round 2 frames before the cut (the bolt catches open, smoke hangs, the shake decays). B04 stops dead on the "…" onset frame: no wind-down and no shake decay, the next frame is still; smoke drifts and the queue walks on in silence until the cut. The jam stays hidden until "ปืนกลติด" (B04-SH04): B04-SH03 shows no click, no puff from the action and no reaction from the gunner.
- **Value.** Under the full tint the sky is the lightest area, so each flash core sits below the horizon against mid-value wheat or bank, is painted pure white (the lightest value in the frame on its frame) and is wrapped by darker smoke; in B04 the flash also mirrors in the water ([lf02-r4](#lf02-r4) reflection rules).
- **Continuity.** B03 casings stay on the dirt right of G1 (in B03-SH04 only if that side of the gun is in frame; the row is unchanged). B04 casings lie on the slope and at the waterline, behind the hero casing that still tinkles on its stone in B04-SH04. G1 traverses from yaw 0.55 rad (LF02, waiting) to aim down the south shore at the queue (about 90° yaw, estimate) between B04-SH01 and B04-SH03, while the queue walks in (B04-SH02).
- **Fallback order** if effects are late or slow: keep flash and halation, shake, casings and recoil (the four that read at 270 px); drop the B03 strike spurts first, then the muzzle-blast dust. Never replace any of it with hits.
- **Cost.** Up to 15 flash, smoke and casing marks per frame on top of up to 60 birds: draw casings, flash cores and spurts as plain fills and strokes, not brush fills ([DF-20261031-paint-seed-defer](../../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261031-paint-seed-defer)).

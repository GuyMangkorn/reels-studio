## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** The viewer is asked to try something with their own hand in the first second, and fails. The picture must be the viewer's own hand first (frame 0 is a hand flat on a table seen from the eyes of its owner), then a friendly home scene that laughs at the failure with them, then a clean inside view that answers "why" with one image: puppet strings, one of them shared and tied. Feelings in order: participation and a laugh at the stuck finger (B01); "eh?" when the other fingers lift easily and grandpa fails too (B02); satisfaction as the hand turns see-through and the reason lights up cord by cord (B03); a small widening of the world, pianists (B04); a smile and the invitation to try again (B05, loop). Characters, tries, taps, glows and inserts are **dramatization**; the see-through hand is a **diagram**, labelled "ภาพประกอบ"; the narration carries every claim.
- **Story first.** An ordinary Thai home after dinner, about 19:30. A grandfather and his grandson (about 9, still in his school uniform, a homework notebook pushed aside) sit side by side at a wooden dining table under one warm pendant lamp. Grandpa is gentle, amused, quick to stop a child doing something silly; the boy is competitive and outraged when his own finger will not obey. The table carries the evening's leftovers of life: two glasses of water, a jug, a plate of cut mango, the notebook and pencil, a tissue box; behind them a cream wall with a wall clock, a calendar, a shelf with a rice cooker and a thermos, a stand fan, and a window going deep blue. The hero object is a hand: a child's left hand, chubby and simple, palm down on warm wood. When it turns see-through it becomes a calm teaching diagram (cream cords, one coral muscle, amber and teal highlights, coral ties), never anatomy-book gore. The pianist is a pair of anonymous adult hands on keys in a cooler, quieter light: someone else, somewhere else.
- **Medium.** Painted 2D p5.brush through the kit yaw camera (`ycam.ts`, `cam-moves.ts`) in world metres for SET-DINING, and set-cm close plates (1 set-m ≈ 1 cm, the EP02/EP06/EP08 close-plate convention) for HAND-CLOSE (the hand macro and its see-through layer), HAND-ROW (the three-hands diagram row) and PIANO-CLOSE (keys; piano only, Q-F2). Characters are 4-head kit rig cards in four views squared to the lens (`squareToCam`), seated. Backgrounds are washes without outlines; characters, the table edge, hero props and every macro hand get one thin ink line and one cel shade ([DF-20261030-p5brush-large-fill-mottle](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-p5brush-large-fill-mottle)). Macro hands use crisp opaque foreground fills (painted-2d skill: `hardFill` for the foreground subject of a close-up). Thai on screen: captions and the "ภาพประกอบ" label only, both Remotion DOM (no painted text).

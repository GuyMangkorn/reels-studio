## Quick numbers

- Adult hand length is 189 mm (men) and 172 mm (women) in US Air Force and Army surveys (E, recall, not inspected). The elder (1.25x) and pianist (1.2x) hands are therefore about 1.3x a 9-year-old, not 1.2-1.25x (E).
- Pianist: long fingers are an artistic choice, not an anthropometric one (E). Keep the ratios and raise only the finger-to-palm length by about 5%.

## Drawing mistakes (only what a source states)

- No inspected drawing source gave measurements. Clip Studio tips pages (S7, F) say these things:
  - Middle finger is about the palm length.
  - Little finger is about half to two thirds of the middle.
  - The thumb tip is placed at the index first joint, at the middle of the last segment, or at the second joint, so the tip landing varies between guides (F).
  - Palm width is about the width of four fingers (F).
  - The wrist is narrower than the palm and oval (F).
- A "thumb too long, fingers short" mistake is NOT stated by any inspected source (no claim made).

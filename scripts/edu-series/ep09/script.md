## Story core

- **Logline:** lay your hand flat, fold the middle finger under, try to lift the ring finger: for most people it barely moves or stays down. Thumb, index and little finger lift fine, so it is not weakness. Inside, the lifting muscle sits in the forearm and pulls the fingers on cords like a puppet; the index and little finger have an extra muscle of their own, the ring finger does not and shares its set with its neighbours, with small bands tying the cords. The nervous system plays a part too, and people differ.
- **Central question:** why does my ring finger refuse to lift when every other finger can?
- **Intention / obstacle:** BOY wants to lift the ring finger; the obstacle is his own hand's wiring.
- **Feeling:** instant participation (the viewer tries in the first 4 s); a laugh at the failure; "eh?" when the other fingers lift easily; the satisfying see-through reveal; relief that it is normal and not a weakness; a smile at the last line, then the loop invites a second try.
- **Takeaway:** the ring finger has no lifting muscle of its own; it shares one set with the middle and little finger, and small bands link the cords, so it cannot move fully alone. The brain and nerves add to it, it varies between people, and it is not weakness. Musicians who play for years get better independence than most people.
- **The one rule (ER03 refrain):** "สายร่วม / ชุดเดียวกัน" (shared strings). It explains every beat: the other fingers lift because index and little have strings of their own (L04/L07), the ring finger sticks because its string is shared and tied (L08–L09), musicians improve but play on the same set (L12–L13), and the last line turns "lazy" into "shares its string" (L15).

## Truth and ending

- **Spoken as settled, plain:** the lifting muscle is in the forearm and pulls the fingers through tendons (F003, L06); index and little finger have an extra muscle of their own, the ring finger has none (F005, L07); it uses the same set as the middle and little finger, and small bands link the tendons (F006 safe wording, F004, L08); it is not weakness (F012, L05); musicians have more independent fingers than most people (F013, L12).
- **Spoken with hedges:** the test result "ยกได้นิดเดียว หรือไม่ขึ้นเลยใช่ไหม" (F001: not "everyone", no percentage; it names both outcomes); the pinning explanation "มักอธิบายกันว่า" (F007 `model`, L09); "ไม่ใช่แค่เส้นเอ็นนะ… ระบบประสาทก็มีส่วน และแต่ละคนไม่เท่ากัน" (F008/F012 split not stated, F002 variation, L10).
- **Safety (F017):** L03 "เบา ๆ นะ อย่าเอามืออีกข้างดึง" right after the try. No stretching, exercise, training, surgery or "free your ring finger" advice; L12–L13 describe musicians, not a method.
- **Dramatization only:** ELDER and BOY, their tries and reactions, the taps, the see-through hand (diagram, labelled "ภาพประกอบ"), the cords going taut, the piano insert (fictional, generic hands, no named player).
- **Not used:** Schumann (user Q2, cut), the fold-index / fold-little / fold-ring variants (F009 `uncertain`; the user's try is one person, not a finding), "never fully free" for musicians (F014), "everyone fails" or any percentage, "it is genetic", "your ring finger is weak", brain-area names (F018), "tied only to the middle finger" (F004: the bands also link ring and little finger).
- **Knowledge twist (myth reversal, ER03):** the viewer reads the stuck finger as weakness or laziness. B02 removes weakness with a fair clue (thumb, index, little lift easily: CL02) and ELDER fails too (CL01), so it is not age or strength. B03 shows the cause is structure plus wiring. The last line names the myth and flips it: not lazy, it shares its string.
- **Ending `B_loop` (loop pair):** L15 "นิ้วนางคุณไม่ได้ขี้เกียจหรอก… มันแค่ใช้สายร่วมกับนิ้วข้าง ๆ" ends on the neighbouring fingers; the loop restarts on L01 "วางมือแบนบนโต๊ะ พับนิ้วกลางลงไป… แล้วลองยกนิ้วนางดูสิ", which reads as "now try again knowing why". Closing picture holds ~2.5 s on the plain hand back on the table (see-through faded), BOY's ring finger trembling a hair (CL03); no speech in the hold. Boat's suggested rewrite of the C28 last line is followed in substance ("แบ่งสายไฟ" → "ใช้สายร่วม", one metaphor across the episode; see [N3](#notes)).

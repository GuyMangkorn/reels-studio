- **Q1a, L13 (NEW-01) "แต่สายในมือ ก็ยังเป็นชุดเดียวกันอยู่ดี": keep, with the cut C1 as the first trim.** It claims only that a pianist's hand has the same cords and bands as anyone's. It is an inference from absence of evidence, not a sourced finding, so keep the wording exactly as drafted: it says "same set", not "never fully free" (F014 stays unused) and not "training does nothing". - **Q1b, extensor digiti minimi (F005): verified at textbook level, accessed via Gray-derived sources, not the original page.** Extensor indicis and extensor digiti minimi (digiti quinti proprius) are the extra extensors of the index and little finger; the ring finger has no muscle of its own and is served by extensor digitorum (SRC002, SRC011, search excerpts). L07 "นิ้วนางไม่มี" passes. Caveat for Film and for no one else: the little-finger extra tendon may send a slip to the ring finger in some people (SRC011 variant), and the slip is sometimes absent; this does not make "ring has none of its own" false and needs no spoken hedge. Do not draw the ring finger with a separate cord from the extra muscle.
- **Q1c, F013 "เล่นมาหลายปี นิ้วแยกกันได้ดีกว่าคนทั่วไป": pass.** Group difference between pianists and non-musicians (SRC007 excerpts: faster, more independent movement, less spill-over). The studies compared groups; "เล่นมาหลายปี" describes the people, not a method, so it does not give training advice. Guitar insert: shown, not claimed in speech (evidence is pianist-based). Keep it a visual of fingers playing only.
- **Q2, L10 "ระบบประสาทก็มีส่วน": acceptable, better than "สมองกับเส้นประสาท".** It matches F008/F012 (structure and nervous system both contribute, split debated: SRC005, SRC006) and avoids brain claims (F018 `uncertain`, brain-area names banned). "แต่ไม่ใช่แค่เส้นเอ็นนะ" is correct because "tendons only" is `unsupported`. Keep as drafted.

## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** Every dog owner has watched a dog run straight past a toy. Feelings in order: affectionate laughter at the overshoot (B02), a "whoa" when the whole painting changes (B03), the click of the 3-vs-2 mechanism and the myth falling (B04), relief when the nose wins (B05), a bright, satisfied payoff when only the ball colour changes and the dog runs straight to it (B06), a warm question to the viewer (B07). The picture never presents the game as proof: the dog business is dramatization (F011), the dog view is a labelled approximation (F012).
- **Story first.** A young dad-type owner in his early thirties on a Saturday-morning walk in a Bangkok public park (Lumpini/Benchakitti flavour, not named): mown lawn, a curving concrete path, a golden-shower tree (ราชพฤกษ์) in yellow bloom on one side and a flame tree (หางนกยูง) in red-orange bloom on the other, pale high-rises in haze above the far tree line. He is cheerful, a little proud of the shiny red ball he bought, and comically exasperated when the dog misses it. The dog is a big, soft, eager golden retriever: all heart, nose first, never stupid. The set's colours are chosen so the flip demonstrates the VO on its own: red ball and red flame-tree flowers sink toward their green surroundings, while the sky, his blue cap, the yellow tree and his mustard tee stay strong.
- **Medium.** Painted 2D, p5.brush, [painted 3D camera](../../../docs/film-visual-brief-workflow.md#painted-3d-camera) (project default, user 5 Nov 2026): the park is built in world metres (`PARK` space below) and seen through the kit yaw camera (`kit/ycam.ts`); every shot moves the camera inside the set; the owner is drawn in one of four body views squared to the lens (`squareToCam`); the dog in its kit views (`left`, `right`, `front`) chosen by its direction on screen. Background washes without outlines; owner, dog, balls and hero props get one thin ink line and one cel shade. Diagram marks (B04) are chalk-white brush line, drawn on. No SVG characters ([UT11](../../../docs/user-taste.md)).
- **The flip (signature moment).** The *same* painted frame, the *same* strokes and seeds, re-coloured through one dog-view colour map; nothing is repainted or moved by the flip (VD05). The sweep starts at the dog's head and spreads outward like water through wet paint.

- **Camera language.** Vertical 1080×1920, low and close to the dog, eye-level on the owner (0.6–3 m, [UT13](../../../docs/user-taste.md#ut-close-camera)); one deliberate wide (B03-SH02) to show the whole park in dog view. It returns four times, each with one change: normal miss (B02-SH02) → dog view of the same frame (B03-SH01) → nose find in normal colour (B05-SH01) → blue ball in dog view, straight run (B06-SH02).
- **Frame zones.** Captions bottom-anchored as in EDU-EP02 (series consistency): `captionStyle.bottomEdgeY: 1344`, at most two lines, band y 1176–1344. Tag `ภาพจำลอง (โดยประมาณ)` at the top (`labelTop` ≈ 200, as EP02) clear of the `@TamMaiLa` channel mark top-left.
- **Conceal / reveal.** The dog-view palette appears first at B03-SH01 frame 0 (after L03). The blue ball stays inside the closed sling bag until L10 (B06-SH01); no glimpse is used (CL02 is carried by the blue cap, the sky and the yellow tree). The dog never catches anything mid-air.

## Sound direction

Follows the script's "Sound intent" (R1.1). Library checked 2026-11-05 at `~/ReelsStore/library/sound/INDEX.md` (186 lines). Sound never carries a fact; dog vocals are dramatization. Editor sets levels and final picks at W4+.

- **Music family.** Bright acoustic-guitar bed, fingerpicked with light hand percussion, ~105 bpm, energy 3/5. Arc: enters on L01; bouncy for the throw and sprint (B02), dips under O1/O2; drops to a soft sustained pad from the flip (B03-SH01 frame 0); a curious plucked line in B04 with ~0.4 s near-silence before "หมาเห็นสี"; guitar returns, relieved, in B05; brightest lift in B06 on the run; settles to a warm tag under L11 and holds through the ~3 s end hold into the `B_loop` seam (last ~0.5 s free of speech; the bed tail must not retrigger at the loop). Candidates: `bed_curious-explainer-light_01` (marimba + plucked guitar, 95 bpm, loopable; partial fit), `bed_warm-pad-felt-third_01` (B03–B04 pad, partial). **NEEDS-GEN:** bright acoustic guitar park bed ~105 bpm, loopable, narration-safe (≥ 50 s).
- **Ambience per location.** Park, normal view: morning birdsong, light breeze in leaves, very faint distant city. Candidates `room_soi-morning-quiet_01` (faint birds; partial), `outdoor_dawn-street-empty_01` (far bird, too urban); **NEEDS-GEN:** park morning birdsong + leaf breeze loop 10–15 s. Dog view (B03, B04, B06-SH02): the same ambience low-passed and lowered ~6 dB (Editor processing, no new file).
- **SFX language.** Close, warm, playful foley; one glassy "shimmer" carries each flip; diagram pops are small and round. Library: `reveal_glass-shimmer-soft_01` (flip in; reversed by Editor for the flip back), `reveal_reverse-swell-airy_01` (alternative riser into the flip), `ui_pop_01` (each cone and label), `whoosh_air-swish-rising_01` (throws). **NEEDS-GEN:** rubber dog-ball squeak; ball thud/bounce on grass (2 small hits + roll); single happy bark (golden retriever); short puzzled whine; sniff-snuffle close; paws galloping on grass; grass skid; tail thumps on grass; chalk/marker cross-out scratch (not `comedy_record-scratch_01`, which reads as a freeze gag); canvas bag flap. Dog vocals from Stable Audio are a risk (R4).
- **Silence policy.** (1) Bed dips under O1 and O2. (2) ~0.4 s near-silence before "หมาเห็นสี" (L08). (3) No hard silence at the flips; the shimmer is the transition. (4) End hold: bed and birds only; nothing new starts in the last 0.5 s.
- **Not claimed.** No sound is presented as what a dog hears; the muffled ambience in dog view is a stylistic cue only.

## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood.** The viewer believes their height is a fixed number. Frame 0 is a boy measuring himself against a doorframe at night, and the pencil lands below the morning line. The film then answers three "แต่…" turns with three pictures: the see-through spine and a wet sponge (where the height went), the morning line coming back and the pads drinking water in bed (where it comes back), an astronaut in a cabin whose spine straightens with nothing pressing (what if it never pressed), and the cost of that (she winces mid-float). It ends on the boy asleep, then the doorframe in morning light. Feelings in order: "eh?" (B01), relief and a small worry (B02), a comic "อ้าว?" and calm (B03), wonder (B04), sympathy, then a warm smile (B05).
- **Story first** ([AGENTS.md#story-first](../../../AGENTS.md#story-first)). A Thai townhouse at night: a teak doorframe with pencil marks between a lamp-lit hallway and the boy's small bedroom, his bed visible through the doorway (CL02). He is about nine, a little sleepy, still in his school uniform with bare feet at bedtime; in the morning, messy hair and pale pyjamas. The diagrams are calm teaching pictures painted over his back: ivory bone blocks with water-blue pads between them, plainly a diagram, never anatomy. The second place is a space-station work cabin: white rack panels, blue-grey handrails, cloth stowage bags, a laptop on an arm, cables, a round window with the Earth, cool LED light that dims at the crew's night. The astronaut is an anonymous woman in her late thirties in ordinary station clothes (polo shirt, cargo trousers, socks), short hair and round glasses, no name, no flag, no agency patch. A crewmate in the same kind of clothes appears once, kind and quick. The rhyme that ties the two places: the boy marks his height on the doorframe; the astronaut marks hers on a strip of tape on the cabin wall. Down here the line drops at night; up there it only goes up.

Characters are 4-head kit rig cards squared to the lens (`squareToCam`). The spine diagrams are world-anchored overlays riding the figure's anchors (the EP08 chest-outline and EP09 `tendons.ts` pattern), drawn over the figure card. The sponge is a painted screen card. Tags, the L05 term label and captions are Remotion DOM. Backgrounds are washes without outlines; characters, the doorframe trims, bed edges, cabin handrails and hero props get one thin ink line and one cel shade ([DF-20261030-p5brush-large-fill-mottle](../../../docs/dev-findings.md#df-20261030-p5brush-large-fill-mottle)). ### Truth limits on picture (binding on every shot; FC-01, FC-03, FC-05, FC-06, A2, A3)

1. **Space spine (FC-03, hard).** On any astronaut shot there is no water, no drop, no glow, no plumping, no swelling, no sponge, no blue on the spine, and no picture or label that says "pads unchanged". The pads are not drawn at all in space: the space diagram is one plain smooth ivory column (no rings, bands or segment gaps that could read as discs; Boat N1) that only **straightens** (its arc length never changes, so nothing between the bones grows). The glowing pad (L05) and every plumping or squeezing pad are **Earth only** (B02, B03-SH03).
2. **Tags (FC-05).** "ภาพจำลอง" on every astronaut shot, B04-SH01 through B05-SH02, including the wince and L11. "ภาพประกอบ" on every spine diagram (B02-SH01–SH04, B03-SH03, B04-SH02) and the sponge card (B02-SH02). Both together on B04-SH02. None on the boy's doorframe and bedroom story shots. Placement and look: [VD14](#scene-decisions).
3. **Wince (FC-01, FC-06).** The wince happens in the cabin, mid-float, in the first days of the mission. There is no landing, capsule, return, impact, crowd or cheering anywhere; no spine diagram, tape ruler, height bar or marks in B05-SH01 or B05-SH02 (no mechanism and no link to the growth shown). No stinger, hit or riser on "แต่…".
4. **No day counter (A2).** No digits, day number, calendar or tally anywhere in the cabin. "The first days" is shown only by the cabin light cycling dim and bright in B04-SH03; the tape has tick marks without numbers. The wince shot is framed away from the tape so no mark count can be read as "day N".
5. **Cause-free text (A3).** If the logline or takeaway is reused for the cover or the description, it keeps growth and back pain as two separate facts: no "เพราะ", no "so", no "because" linking them.
6. **Scale honesty.** The doorframe gap is drawn true to scale (1.5 cm) and read in a close shot, never stretched; the tape marks total 2.5 cm ("สองสามเซน"). Only the diagrams exaggerate (pad thickness) and they carry "ภาพประกอบ". The astronaut is anonymous: no name tape, flag, agency logo or mission patch; the station is generic.

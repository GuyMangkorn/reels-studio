## Story core

- **Logline:** In 1977 a radio telescope listening for aliens printed out one strong, narrow signal on exactly the channel aliens were expected to use, then never heard it again. Nearly fifty years later, old Arecibo data point to a suspect hiding in that same channel: a cold hydrogen gas cloud.
- **Central question (hook):** a signal that made an astronomer write "Wow!": was it aliens?
- **Intention / obstacle:** scientists want to know what sent it. The obstacle: it never repeated, so there is nothing to re-test, and its features all look like a deliberate beacon.
- **Intended feeling:** a genuine "maybe it *was* aliens" build, a deflating silence, then the satisfying click that the clue pointing at aliens (the hydrogen channel) also points at nature, ending on an honest open door.
- **Learning takeaway:** hydrogen naturally emits radio near 1420 MHz, which is why SETI listened there and why nature can produce a Wow!-like signal on the "alien channel". The cloud explanation is the newest proposal, not a proven answer. Secondary: "6EQUJ5" is a strength reading, not a code.

## Truth and ending

- **Documented (research §1):** detection by Big Ear in 1977 during a SETI search; printout "6EQUJ5" circled with "Wow!"; strength values, not a message; near the 1420 MHz hydrogen line; narrow, strong, point-like; never detected again; no evidence it was aliens; 2024 Arecibo archive search found Wow!-like signals about 100 times weaker, in several places, from small cold hydrogen clouds in our galaxy.
- **Hypothesis (hedged in VO):** that the 1977 signal itself was such a cloud suddenly brightening about 100 times (WOW-C09, C10).
- **Kept open:** no specific cloud or trigger identified; preprints by one team, peer review unverified; aliens not ruled out, only unsupported (WOW-C07, C10, C12).
- **Ending chosen before clues:** reveal = "เมฆก๊าซไฮโดรเจน" as the leading new proposal, tied back to the 1420 MHz clue, then an explicit "ยังเป็นสมมติฐาน" and an open loop line. The script never says solved, proven, consensus or "not aliens".

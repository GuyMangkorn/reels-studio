## Story core and underlying truth

**Logline:** While worldwide fog traps a driver in a petrol-station supermarket, an approaching shape seems inhuman until light exposes an ordinary delivery worker struggling with an overloaded cart. The visitor brings news that leaves the occupants stranded.

**Central question:** What followed the narrator to the shop? **Intention / obstacle:** the narrator wants to refuel and continue home, then seeks immediate shelter and tries to understand the apparent pursuer; near-zero exterior visibility and incomplete views prevent a reliable interpretation. **Feeling:** dread → peak uncertainty → brief relief → unresolved confinement. **Learning takeaway (R3):** one short first-person perception insight, by user decision 2026-10-30 overriding the brief's no-takeaway constraint for EP1 only: in fog the eye gets incomplete shapes, the brain tends to fill the gap, and fear pulls the narrator's reading of it toward danger. Spoken once inside the story (S03-B02); no moral, card, slogan or safety summary.

**Truth:** The worker and loaded cart are one continuous physical assembly throughout. Stacked cartons and a helmet create the high apparent head; the worker's raincoat/body overlaps that stack from the concealed view; an umbrella handle supplies the apparent long reach; a jammed cart wheel scrapes. A slipping helmet is caught before falling. No real head detaches, limb elongates or nonhuman creature exists. The worker's report of a blocked road is fictional testimony, not a claim of a real incident. The fog's cause, toxicity and duration remain unknown.

**Ending (R6):** The worker is unequivocally human. The practical problem remains after the creature question is answered. The narrator's one reaction line (R3) turns relief into the stranded predicament over the disappearing forecourt lights. Then (R6, user decision 2026-10-31, superseding R5's "no supernatural addition / pure practical predicament" ending for EP1 only): after the last light goes out and a short silence, three knocks on the glass in the opening rhythm and a person-shaped silhouette in the fog behind the glass. The worker has just said (`VO-17`) that the customer who ordered the goods will come to collect them in person, so the most ordinary reading is the customer; nothing in EP1 confirms or denies that, and nothing shown is supernatural. No dialogue, reveal, narrator reaction, creature sound, paranormal sting, EP2 synopsis, call to action or fog explanation. Hard cut.

**Truth for the closing figure (R6):** unidentified within EP1. Objectively it is a solid figure of ordinary human height and outline that knocks with a hand; it is not the worker (he is inside) and carries no cart, cartons, helmet or umbrella. EP1 asserts nothing beyond that. The Long version (`WFOG-LONG`) may later read it either way; this Short does not resolve it.

**Event:** The cart stops outside. The worker uses the umbrella handle to make three short taps on the glass while keeping the load braced. **Other occupant, whispered:**

**Event:** With cart stationary, helmet slides sideways from the top carton; the worker catches and returns it. Preserve the same helmet and hand throughout. The viewer sees the alarming movement but not a genuinely detached human head. The wheel and cardboard clues remain available.

**Narrator, whispered:**

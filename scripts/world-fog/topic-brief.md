# WFOG-BRIEF-R1 — The person outside

- Deliverable: `WFOG-EP01-SHORT`; see [delivery](delivery.md).
- Language: English technical material; Thai spoken narration/dialogue/captions.
- Scope: EP1 only, 60–90 seconds; first-person narrator is the protagonist. World-spanning opaque fog is fictional. Outside visibility falls near zero, while the shop interior remains readable; no biological blindness.

## Approved premise and progression

The protagonist drives to refuel and becomes stranded in a small supermarket at the petrol station. Just before entering, they glimpse a seemingly nonhuman, unusually tall shape with an off-angle head. It appears to follow them into the forecourt. Through fog/glass, a hunched form, an apparently long arm, scraping metal and knocks lead occupants and viewers to fear a creature. Characters have incomplete information; the camera must not establish a genuinely nonhuman body.

Plant fair clues: a squeaking wheel, breath, unstable stacked cargo. The apparent head tilts/falls and is caught. A normal voice asks for help before a light reveals an ordinary delivery worker in a raincoat, steadying stacked boxes on a cart with a jammed wheel. A helmet on the boxes looked like a head; an umbrella handle used to knock looked like a long arm. Show a concise matching-view reveal so each plant has a coherent physical cause. Do not turn this into a blind-person reveal: that earlier proposal was superseded.

Once inside, the worker reports a road collision blocking their return. End on the fog swallowing forecourt lights and an unresolved practical predicament. Any collision report is fictional dialogue; do not invent casualties. Preserve tension without adding a final monster, paranormal confirmation or implication that the worker is secretly nonhuman.

## User constraints

- No closing moral, narrated crisis lesson, explanatory safety card or mandatory educational payoff in EP1; leave room for EP2.
- Sensible crisis actions may be dramatized without becoming a tutorial. Verify safety implications; do not teach that every unknown atmospheric hazard is safe or every building always protects occupants.
- No scientific explanation of the worldwide fog is required or approved. Do not present the premise as a documented real event.
- User permits Blender and explicitly wants difficulty/gaps stated candidly. Film must assess the actual shot, not promise quality from package availability. No Blender scene modification or runtime render is implied by an untested brief.
- Keep the delivery worker, cart, umbrella and cargo physically consistent before/after the reveal. Do not rely on sound alone for clues.

# WFOG-LONG-BRIEF-R1 — The second knock

- Deliverable: `WFOG-EP01-LONG`; see [delivery](../delivery.md). Separate editorial version from `WFOG-EP01-SHORT`; the Short stays as delivered.
- Format: YouTube long-form, 16:9 1920×1080, 30 fps. Target **about 10 minutes**; longer is welcome when the story earns it (user, 2026-10-31). Narration-led; Thai spoken narration/dialogue/captions, English technical material.
- Genre: **fictional supernatural horror** in the WORLD FOG world. No true-story basis, no real-case attribution, no factual claims beyond ordinary physical plausibility. The worldwide fog stays unexplained.
- Rating: **pressure horror, no blood or wounds** (user choice): tension from rhythm, shadows, sound and the face reveal. Keep YouTube advertiser-friendly; no gore, self-harm or graphic injury, including at the torn-smile reveal (the mouth is an impossible shape, not a wound).

## Approved structure (user, 2026-10-31)

1. **Retell EP1 inside the Long**, fuller than the Short, so a viewer who never saw the Short can follow: refuelling, the tall off-angle shape in the fog, being stranded in the petrol-station minimart, the follower at the glass, the "head" that falls and is caught, the normal voice, the torch revealing an ordinary delivery worker (raincoat, stacked boxes, cart with a squeaking jammed wheel, helmet on the boxes), the road-collision report. Source: [Short script R5](../short/script.md). Keep its characters, objects and physical facts consistent; wording may change for long-form pacing.
2. A cold open teasing the face is allowed before the retelling.
3. **The second knock:** after the worker is inside, someone knocks. Through fog and glass the silhouette is the delivery worker, same raincoat, same cart, while the real worker stands inside.

## The demon (concept A + B, user-approved)

- **A — the imposter.** It imitates a person it has seen: body, clothes, voice. It cannot come in on its own; someone must open the door or invite it in. While disguised its face looks human.
- **B — the light rule.** Light on its face lets it come closer. The torch that solved EP1 becomes a danger, yet light is the only way to see in the fog.
- **The smile.** When the protagonist recognizes the imposter, it gives up the disguise: the mouth slowly tears back to the ears in a smile, the eyes become empty black hollows. User requirement: this exposure-smile is the key reveal.
- Inspired by a torn-smile image, but **original**: do not use the film *Smile*'s mechanic (a curse passed on by witnessing trauma), names, scenes or rules.

## Story requirements

- Fair clues the viewer can solve before the protagonist: the real worker's cart wheel squeaks; the imposter's cart is silent. Plant the squeak in the EP1 retelling. Clues must also be visible, not sound-only.
- Middle act: power/light failure, knocks around the shop, and suspicion turning inward: is the worker inside the real one? Rules applied consistently: no invitation → no entry; light on the face → it gets closer.
- Ending: resolve the episode's main threat, then **hang an EP2 hook**. Seed concept C for EP2: the people from the road collision the worker reported; knocks from more than one direction, suggesting there is more than one. Do not confirm or explain C.
- Thrilling, follow-along narration: escalating stakes, chapter-like turns roughly every 1–2 minutes, no closing moral or lesson.
- Narrator: first-person woman protagonist (same as Short). Delivery worker male. Voices default to Gemini TTS per project defaults; the imposter's voice is the worker's voice, slightly wrong.

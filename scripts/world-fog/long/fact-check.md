## Originality versus *Smile*

- *Smile* (2022) / *Smile 2* (2024), as generally known: a curse/entity passed on by witnessing a traumatic death, a deadline, hallucination ambiguity, victims seeing people grin; the entity appears as familiar people. R1 has no curse, no transmission by witnessing, no countdown, no clinical setting, no hallucination ambiguity, no names, lines or scenes from the films.
- Overlap is limited to two broad images: a too-wide smile and impersonation. R1's smile is an exposure moment with black hollow eyes (not the films' staring grin) bound to explicit door and light rules. Pass.
- Wider genre awareness (not a fault): people trapped in a shop by an unexplained mist with an older woman voicing a folk belief resembles Stephen King's *The Mist* in setup only; Aunt is a sensible elder, not a zealot, and the threat mechanics differ. Inspecting a visitor at the door for a tell is a common doppelganger trope. No change requested.

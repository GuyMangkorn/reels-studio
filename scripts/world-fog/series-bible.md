## SB1 — Series question and ending

- **Series question:** In a fog where something can wear the face and voice of anyone you would open the door for, can she get home, and how will the person behind her own front door know it is really her?
- **Final episode answers:** she gets home, and her mother lets in the real one by the rules the series has taught (only a real person can come closer in the dark; only a real person carries damage, weight and breath; a copy knows only what it saw and heard); the copy stays outside a door nobody opens.
- **Stays unexplained:** what the things are, where they come from, what the fog is, why the power failed, and whether the fog ever lifts.

## SB3 — The light rule: options compared

Where the user's memory likely comes from: in Long C04-B02 the figure **stops where the light left it** (`ตรงที่แสงทิ้งมันไว้ ไม่ขยับเลยสักก้าว`). Light makes it come; losing the light makes it stop.

| | **A — recorded (DR04)** | **B — user's memory** | **C — A + "it changes only in the dark" (recommended)** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rule | Light on its face → it may come closer. Dark → only sideways or away. | Light on it → it freezes. Dark → it moves freely. | A unchanged, plus: it can put on a new face only where no light touches it. Corollary the characters work out: **anyone who comes closer in total darkness is a real person.** |
| Example beat | C05-B02: lit face at the side window drifts from an arm's length to the glass; `เอาไฟลง!` | Torch on the pumps figure, it stands still; beam slips, a second later it is at the glass. | EP2: the worker's face lit at the side window presses close; torch off; three knocks; torch on: a different face, the customer from the order photo. (The user's beat: hit by the torch → back into the dark → returns as the customer.) |
| Tension | A true dilemma: the only way to see is the thing that brings it. | The torch becomes a weapon; tension moves to batteries and blinking. Less dilemma. | A's dilemma doubled: light = it comes, but you see who it is; dark = it stays away, but it may be someone else when you look again. |
| Hook / rewatch | Strong (`ไฟฉายที่ช่วยเรา… กลายเป็นสิ่งที่พามันเข้ามา`). | Familiar "statue" game (Weeping Angels / SCP-173 / red-light-green-light): **originality risk**. | Every blackout becomes a possible swap; on rewatch, EP1 Long C04-B01 → C04-B02 (outline at the glass, full worker copy at the pumps, change never on camera) already shows the rule. |
| Long C04–C07 | Keeps all. | **Breaks** C00 second lesson, C04-B02 (walks only while lit), C05-B02, C06-B02 (asks for light), C06-B04 (low beam), C07-B01 (lunge along the beam, torch off freezes it), C07-B03, C08 last line: ≈ 10 beats. | Keeps all spoken text; consistent with DR01's "never assembled or changed on camera". |
| Cheat risk | Low (set up C04-B02 before C05-B02 names it; C00 plants the bend). | Low if replanted, but the already-written Long contradicts it. | Low: the change rule is already observable in C04-B01/B02; EP2 states it only after showing it once more. The dark-approach corollary is a deduction from DR04, not a new power. |
| Cost | none | Long R3 → R4 rewrite of C00, C04–C08, full Boat recheck, Film/Mark rework of the Long plan. | **P01** (optional): add ledger row `DR06` and one never-broken note to the Long; no spoken or shown change; Boat recheck of the ledger rows only. |

Also considered: **C-alt** (light on face = closer, light on body = freeze). Rejected: darkness already stops it, so it adds aiming fuss but no new choice, and it conflicts with C07-B01 (the low beam lights its legs and it still bends into the light).

**Recommendation: C.** It keeps the written Long intact, turns the user's shapeshift idea into a fair rule, and gives the series its key test (who can walk in the dark).

## SB4 — Transformation rules

| ID | Rule | Already planted in EP1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `T01` | **Needs sight and sound.** A body and face need the person seen; a voice needs the person heard. With only a voice it can call from the dark but has no face to show. | DR01; C07-B02 narrator's voice only after C06-B03/B04 |
| `T02` | **Changes only unlit** (rule C). Never on camera, never in a beam. Dropping the disguise when recognized (the smile, DR05) is the one exception: it is a removal, not a new face. | C04-B01 → C04-B02 |
| `T03` | **Copies the person as it last saw them.** Outline whole; never damage, wear, weight (DR01/DR02); never anything that changed after it saw them (a tied-back hair, a torn sleeve). | DR01, CL08 |
| `T04` | **Copies do not work.** Its cart has no squeak, its phone never lights, its torch never shines, its breath never fogs glass; its face wipes a smear through condensation instead of misting it. | DR02; C07-B03 smear; C06-B02 it asks *us* for light |
| `T05` | **One face at a time per imposter.** Several faces at once means several of them. | C08 three directions |
| `T06` | **Speech: repeat, then free** (DR01); free speech holds only what it has seen and heard: it cannot know what happened where it could not see (inside the dark shop, behind a door). | CL10; C07-B02 she covered her mouth in the dark |
| `T07` | **Recognition said aloud strips it** (DR05); it never enters uninvited (DR03); copying does not harm the original (the real customer is found alive in EP3). | C06-B04 → C07-B01 |

**Telling it from a real person (fair tests the characters learn):** walks up in total darkness (T02 corollary); breath blooms on the glass vs a wiped smear (T04); damage, wet, weight, squeak (T03/DR02); out-of-date details (T03); a question about something that happened unseen (T06).

**The user's idea placed:** EP2. Torch hits the worker copy at the side window → it presses close → torch off → it backs into the dark (allowed) → lit again it wears the customer, whom it saw and heard at the collision (`T01`).

## SB6 — Episode map

Recurring frame (series twist, planted from EP2): every new EP opens on a few seconds of a **porch light, a wooden front door with a small glass pane, her breath blooming on it**, never named. EP4 reveals it: the whole story has been told by the narrator to her mother, through her own front door. EP1 Long C00 `คืนนั้น ฉันได้เรียนรู้สองอย่าง` reads both ways (no change needed).

**`WFOG-EP02` — ลูกค้า (The customer), Long.** Continues from C08 in the dark: a woman's voice at the front (crash on the main road), a man's voice at the back door. Premise: two knockers, one door they dare open. The man says he is the customer (`ผมมารับของครับ`); the worker's order app has a voice note in the same voice, and the man repeats it word for word. Torch on the worker copy at the side window, off, on again: the customer's face (user's beat; T02 shown, then named by Aunt). The narrator realizes the woman reached the front glass while every light was off: `มันเข้ามาใกล้ในความมืดไม่ได้… แต่เธอเดินมาถึงกระจกตอนที่มืดสนิท`. Her breath blooms on the glass; the customer's face leaves a smear; he is spotless after a crash. They open the front gap for her in the dark; the narrator names the customer copy aloud, it smiles and withdraws. The woman explains the standing people: the copies stood in the empty cars' headlights; the real people walked away in the dark. Plants for later: the narrator's phone gets one bar, her mother's voice message plays on speaker (`ถึงบ้านแล้วเคาะสามครั้งนะลูก แม่เปิดไฟหน้าบ้านรอไว้`) where things outside can hear it (T01); the narrator ties her hair back (T03). Hook, first 2 s: `คืนนั้น มีคนขอเข้ามาสองคน… เราเปิดให้ได้แค่คนเดียว`. Last line (the woman): `ตอนเดินผ่านตู้จ่าย… มีคนนั่งอยู่ในรถคันนั้น หน้าเหมือนพี่เลย`. Carries: the narrator's copy in her car (her only way home), the mother's message, the real customer missing.

**`WFOG-EP03` — ทางกลับบ้าน (The way home), Long.** Premise: reach the car through total darkness and drive out. The worker leads with his squeaking cart (the squeak becomes the guide; a copy cannot make it, T04). In the car sits her copy with **loose hair**; she says it aloud (`ผมเธอยังปล่อยอยู่… เธอไม่ใช่ฉัน`) and gets the smile at arm's length in the dark car (peak). Aunt and the shop worker stay behind the bolted shop door (Aunt: `ประตูนี้ ไม่มีใครเปิด`). On the main road the headlights light the standing people, who turn and walk toward the car; headlights off, they stop; she drives blind while the worker walks ahead with the squeak. A "rescuer" waves a torch that never lights and asks them to shine theirs (T04). They find the real customer hiding in his wrecked car, lights off, sprained wrist and soaked: damaged, so real (T07: copying did not harm him); the worker hands over the order (`VO-17` payoff) and stays with him. New rule/clue: T03 out-of-date copies; T04 copies do not work. Hook, first 2 s: porch-light frame, then `มันนั่งรอฉันอยู่ในรถของฉันเอง`. Last line, at her street, seeing someone at her door under the porch light: `ไฟหน้าบ้านที่แม่เปิดรอฉันไว้… กำลังส่องหน้ามันอยู่`. Carries: two of her at one door; the mother inside.

**`WFOG-EP04` — เคาะสามครั้ง (Three knocks), Long, finale.** Premise: her copy reached home first because the porch light her mother left on lit its face all the way to the door (DR04, planted in EP2's message). Both knock three times, the family code the copy heard on speaker. Reveal: the porch-light openings of EP2–EP4 are this doorstep; she has been telling the whole night to her mother through the door. Mother's tests, each learned in the series: (1) a question about something no one outside could see: what did you do in the dark shop when it spoke in your voice (C07-B02: she covered her mouth); the copy, speaking freely, guesses; (2) mother switches off the porch light: only the real one can step to the door in the dark; the copy stays where the light left it; (3) her breath blooms on the pane; its face only smears it. The door opens a gap in the dark for the one who came closer; shut, bolted; nobody invites the other. It knocks three times in the dark, then tries the mother's voice, then stops. Explains exactly: the full rule set as the characters understand it (DR01–DR05, T01–T07), what the standing collision people were, where the customer was, why the copy got home first. Explains nothing about what the things are or the fog. Hook, first 2 s: `ข้างนอกประตูบ้านฉัน… มีฉันยืนอยู่สองคน`. Last line, pairing with the Short's first line `มันตามฉันมา` (series-scale loop, not a seamless video loop): `มันตามฉันมาถึงบ้าน… แต่คราวนี้ ไม่มีใครเปิดประตูให้มัน`. Hard cut; threat resolved, fog stays.

Motifs:

- **Three knocks:** the worker's umbrella taps (EP1) → copied rhythm → the family code → in EP4 the knock proves nothing (`เคาะสามครั้ง ใคร ๆ ก็เคาะได้`).

- **Smear on the glass:** its face wipes condensation (C07-B03); a real person's breath fogs it. Final test in EP4.
- Sound intent (English, for later script packs; no claim): knocks stay bare knuckles, dry, no stinger on the knock itself; the squeak is the only warm sound in the series; total silence on every face swap blackout.

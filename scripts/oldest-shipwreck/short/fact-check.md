## Visual and sound conditions for Film (knowledge conveyed by visuals)

- **B03 label:** "ภาพจำลอง" must be visible and legible on every frame where the ship, the sea surface or the storm appears, including any dissolve in or out. It must sit outside the caption band. Storm, wind and thunder sound starts and ends inside the labelled span.
- **B03 ship:** the vessel type and rig are unknown because no hull survives, and "about 10 m" is inferred from the cargo (F006). Keep the ship a generic small silhouette. Do not add details that read as evidence.
- **Seabed (B01–B09):** the site is a rocky slope that is partly sand and partly rock (F014), not flat open sand. There must be no hull outline. Anchors, if shown, are loose and apart (F007).
- **Jar form (B04, B07):** ovoid, flat base, two handles, an optional raised clay belt at the widest part, and two parallel incised lines with a zigzag on the hero jar (F004).
- **B06:** the pyramid silhouette is fine, because the attributed VO names Giza. Show no legible text and no invented publication.
- **Sound:** no real recordings are planned. The underwater ambience and diver bubbles are present-day and acceptable. The stinger and riser are not factual.

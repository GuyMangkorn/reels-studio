## วิธีผลิตและเหตุผลที่มาร์คเลือก
- **Chosen: 2D scene, 2D characters, 2D props and 2D whole-picture movement.** 3D is unnecessary here: all acting, research diagrams and the emotional reveal are front-facing; a camera never needs to orbit the room. The approved main baseline has a 2D room/sofa and rigs, while no CATBOX props or integrated Three.js stage exist in the inspected main source. Adding 3D would add integration work without helping the evidence read more clearly.
- **Feasibility:** existing room/sofa and rigs are source-visible; new props and picture-stage movement are requirements, not verified implementations. Complexity: medium overall because shared props and movement must be registered; individual scene compositions are simple 2D staging.
- **Shared dependencies:** ROOM-2D, HUMAN-2D, CAT-2D, BOX-OLD, STUDY-PROPS, PICTURE-MOVE. Future scene timeline work waits for the shared asset/movement PR described in `index.md`.
- **Fallback:** if smooth picture-stage translation exposes edges or clamps, use a pre-padded full 2D room image with a smaller deterministic move; keep hand/actor/card movement inside the shot. Every shot still has a visible subject action. Never replace with a static hold except the explicitly held final ending after its animated lead-in.
- **Validation request:** block out at composition start/mid/end and inspect Short + Long crops; for box, toy and cards verify actor contact and foreground order. Verify Mochi is at least 480px tall in every Short shot where he appears; if the owner is also a foreground lead, he is also at least 480px tall. Do not use shadow, glow or transparent padding in the measurement.

## Shot direction

### SH01 — Hook through an empty frame

P01: an empty paper outline on the floor; a small schematic marker waits outside it. P02: the marker steps into the outline. It is a position marker in a diagram, not a cat, study participant, or evidence of an animal imagining an invisible box. Keep the “ภาพอธิบาย” status; no Mochi as study data.

### SH02 — Stimulus types and separate units

Assemble square / inward-facing Kanizsa / outward-control stimuli in sequence; keep “ชนิดสิ่งเร้า — ไม่ใช่ผังทดลอง.” Put 30 completers and 9 cats selecting at least once in separate labeled quantities; do not show a cat crowd.

### SH03 — Pairwise results

Compare inward Kanizsa versus outward-control, then square versus outward-control as a separate pair. Keep “ไม่พบความต่าง ≠ เท่ากัน.” No invented bars, effect sizes or tallies as test results.

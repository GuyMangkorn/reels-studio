## Intent and chosen approach

The audience should watch a question change, rather than watch the owner read twelve sets of cards. Let flat paper rotate into an illusion, let quantities separate by unit, let walls actually hide an object, let a toy cause pursuit, and let an evidence token change lanes. Return to the real fictional room for clues, action and payoff. A metaphor never becomes an explanation merely because it is animated.

**VD00:** use original layered **2D SVG explanatory stages** and the existing calibrated 2D room. They support planar paper comparisons, categorical diagrams and symbolic locations with clear evidence status. Pure crop/angle changes cannot substitute for subject/action changes below. Full 3D is permitted but adds no necessary information to these chosen actions; an orbit would falsely suggest unseen measured apparatus. A real spatial shot would need a new shared stage, guaranteed dependencies and a separate integration proof. Do not install Three just for variety.

R4's full-stage research panels and actor-exclusion rule are superseded by R5. Research diagrams remain schematic, clearly labeled and bounded to the reviewed claim; they appear as upper-third overlays while the fictional home action continues underneath. A symbolic shelter is still a diagram, not a filmed reconstruction of a specific building. Home actors retain shared physical proportions; separate schematic icons do not depict a population of Mochis.

## Shot direction

### SH01 — The wall changes the function

Reveal an occluder wall, then bring in “กล่องช่วยแมวได้แค่ไหน?” beside a silhouette. Do not code “ช่วย” as universal benefit, emotion or cure.

### SH02 — 2014 shelter result

Use symbolic groups labeled 10 with hiding / 9 without, total 19, and 14 days. Show the day-3/day-4 CSS result categorically. No invented daily score curve, animal posture, emotional face or building detail.

### SH03 — 2019 results stay separate

Cut to its own 2019 study card. Show faster CSS decline and separate outcome cards for weight loss, adoption and length of stay with “ไม่พบความต่างอย่างมีนัยสำคัญ.” Do not collapse non-significance to zero/equality.

### SH04 — Evidence does not transfer to tape

Compare a real wall with a flat outline. End with the studied hide-place conclusion and keep the frame visibly outside that evidence lane.

## Shared assets and acceptance

Use cream/blue study overlays with amber qualification over the room scene. Shelter-context, outcome-tracks and occlusion-compare are integrated shared modes. Keep the cat and owner visible as the study/result cards change.

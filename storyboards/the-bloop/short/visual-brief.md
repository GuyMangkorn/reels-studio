## Visual intent

**Audience knowledge.** The viewer hears the real sound first and sees only a listening device in the dark: the sound has no owner. The map and diagrams then build the case in layers (who listened, where from, how far, by what channel) while the monster exists only as a visibly drawn rumour. The ice replaces the rumour on screen in `B07`, and the last explanatory image (`B09`) is ice and the hydrophone. `B10` is a user-chosen tease: a sharp, solid creature under the "จินตนาการ" label swallows the camera.

**Two picture languages, one surface.** The world (deep water, the dream creature, the iceberg mass and, from R2, the solid `B10` creature) is painted watercolour; ink boils except on the `B10` creature, whose ink is frozen. Everything the viewer must read precisely (map, arrows, diagrams, sound rings, the crack light) is SVG drawn over the canvas in the same camera. A paper grain over both ([VD01](#vd01)) keeps them on one surface.

**Colour / value key ([VD02](#vd02)).**
- Deep water: blue-black to indigo (`#0b1622`, `#132538`, `#1d3550`), value 10–25 %. No surface light in the deep plates.
- The one warm light is **the sound**: amber rings and channel glow (`#f2b25c`, glow opacity ≤ 0.3).
- Ice: cold cyan-white (`#cfe9f2`, `#8cc3d6`), introduced only from `B05` as colour, from `B07` as form.
- Dream creature: grey-violet ink (`#6f6a86`) on paler, paper-showing water; it pales toward `#cfe0e8` in `B05`.
- Diagrams: the same deep-water wash, cream line work (`#efe6d2`), amber sound, cyan ice.
- The caption zone ([CAP-ZONE](#cap-zone)) stays mid-dark: nothing brighter than value 45 % behind captions.

**Camera language.** Slow pushes and drifts in the water plates (the viewer is a listener leaning in). Diagrams use slow, motivated pans (south in `B05`, south-east into the Scotia Sea in `B07` per Mark's `BLOOP-SHEETS-R2` SH09 and the user's MK-O2 answer, along the channel in `B06`/`B08`). One violent move only: the `B10` rush, capped at camera scale 3 ([VD11](#vd11)). Cuts and in-canvas dissolves; no shake.

**Conceal / reveal constraints (from the script and fact-check).**
- No creature in `B01`–`B03`, never beside "เสียงจริง เร่ง 16 เท่า", absent in `B08`–`B09`.
- Antarctica and ice forms not shown before `B05` (direction and cold colour only); not named before `B07`; the word "น้ำแข็ง" not on screen before `B07`.
- No origin point pinned on the map before `B05`; the `B05` arrow is broad and exits the map, never ending on the Pole, a pin or coordinates.
- No coordinates, depth numbers, Hz or time-axis numbers, iceberg names, dates on maps, NOAA logo or page screenshots.

### Projection contract

- **World plates:** 2D screen space; one `Camera` `{s, fx, fy, dx, dy}` per plate from `src/painted/camera.tsx` drives the canvas (`applyCamera`) and every in-camera SVG (`CameraLayer`). No other transform is applied to in-camera SVG (no duplicate scale). Screen-space items (fact labels, captions, chrome, Grain, Grade, marine snow) ignore it.
- **Map space:** equirectangular, central meridian 200° E (−160°), one shared `mapXY(lon, lat) → world px` owned by the BLOOP map module; dots, wavefronts, arrow and tags all go through it. Starting proposal: `B02` view ≈ lat +30…−38 inside y 380–1020 and lon 145° E…100° W across 1080 px; `B07` pans the view south until the Antarctic coast sits above the caption zone. Mark sets exact crops.
- **Section space:** schematic: x = south → north (unitless), y = surface → seafloor; surface y ≈ 300, channel band centre y ≈ 700 (band ≈ 120 px), seafloor below the caption zone. Not to scale and never labelled with numbers.
- **Panel space:** `PATTERN` lines in panel-local coordinates (0..1 time, 0..1 pitch, unlabelled), placed by the plate.

### Composition, depth, light, clocks, resources

- **Composition:** canvas below, SVG `Overlay` above, Remotion text above that. SVG never needs to pass behind a painted mark except the `B05` ghost (painted, under the SVG map, by design). `B10` (R2): creature and hydrophone are both painted in one canvas; the hydrophone is painted after the creature, so it stays in front with no copy (R1's depth seam is closed). The `B10` black fade is a screen-space layer above the canvas and below the fact label and caption (a painter-alpha drain lightens washes; measured in the spike).
- **Blend:** `CameraLayer` defaults to `screen`; rings and glows use `screen`; map land and tags use `normal` (the `B10` creature is painted from R2).
- **Light:** painted light is painted; SVG glows are capped at 0.3. No runtime lights.
- **Clocks:** plate-local `tl` for all picture motion (pure functions of `tl`, `hash()` for per-element randomness; `still(id)` per wash, `boil(id)` per ink group). Audio, envelope and captions on the composition clock. Crack fissures and ring arrivals keyed to frames Mark writes from the narration read.
- **Resources:** fonts Noto Sans Thai (existing). Map path and envelope JSON are static imports (no per-frame allocation). SVG filters (`feTurbulence`) keyed by `useId`, not per frame.
- **Rendering:** `--gl=angle` for stills and videos (skill). Dissolve/drain frames vary by ≤ 3 levels; compare with PSNR, not bytes (DF-20261030-render-run-noise).

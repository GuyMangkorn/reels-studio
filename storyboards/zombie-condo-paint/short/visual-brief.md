## Visual intent

- **Audience knowledge and mood (unchanged):** the viewer is addressed as "you" (คุณ). On screen, "you" is embodied by one recurring character ([VD-ZC-CH](#vd-zc-ch--you-character)). The viewer spots the danger a beat before the VO says it (hook). After that, they watch "you" inside one sealed unit where every tip becomes a physical action. The feeling runs jolt → urgency → small "aha" → grim control → isolation → unease. Only the corridor (B01–B02) and the city (B08) are outward views. Everything else turns inward and quiet.
- **R3 pacing (unchanged):** the extra time per language is breathing room on states the story already has (the turn landing, the freeze, the dark rooms, him *not* opening the fridge, the city before the VO, the knock silence) plus picture for the three restored lines (spoken container list, freezer upper bound, smoke and fire). No new set, character, prop or claim.
- **Look ([`VD-ZCP-LOOK`](#vd-zcp-look--painted-treatment-on-the-world-fog-base)):** the WORLD FOG painted world moved indoors: opaque decal-style figures with one thin boiling ink line; **R2: cream condo interiors** (user) instead of the grey haze, kept dark-toned for the horror read (dark floors and wall bases, black caps, Grade vignette, the infected as the darkest or most violent shape); one warm light per scene; Grade + Grain screen layers. The horror comes from value and concealment (the darkest shape, the black cap, the rage face appearing out of the silhouette), not from rendering detail. The survival tips stay grounded and plausible. Blood within [`VD-ZC-GORE`](#vd-zc-gore--blood-ceiling).
- **R2.5 light and body:** every shot now has one key direction, with two-tone cel shading on figures and props and hard cast shadows on floors and walls ([`VD-ZCP-LIGHT`](#vd-zcp-light--cel-light-and-cast-shadows-r25)); the rooms get an accent colour each, larger props with their own shadows and a value step from the dark door end to the bright balcony end ([`VD-ZCP-ROOM`](#vd-zcp-room--what-makes-the-rooms-attractive-r25)); "you" and the infected are about 6 heads tall ([`BODY-ZCP-6H`](#body-zcp-6h--zcpaint-6-head-body-r25)). The palette column below is the lit tone; the cel and cast-shadow tones derive from it.
- **Interior device:** the dollhouse **section cutaway** ([`VD-ZC-CUT`](#vd-zc-cut--section-cutaway)), painted as an architectural cutaway with gentle one-point depth. Inserts and reactions are painted one-point perspective views.

  - The barricade is a light cabinet that can obviously be moved (`CL02`).
  - No weapons anywhere, including improvised ones held by "you".

## `VD-ZCP-LIGHT` — cel light and cast shadows (R2.5)

**User (30 Oct 2026, after pass 5):** “Cel shade 2 โทน + เงาตกกระทบ”: every shot has a key-light direction; characters and furniture get one hard-edged shadow tone; cast shadows fall on floors and walls; the light comes from windows and lamps; the paint stays clean, with no blotches. Pass 5 had no key anywhere (every surface one value at `LIT_DAY` 0.8), which is the main reason the rooms looked flat ([`VD-ZCP-ROOM`](#vd-zcp-room--what-makes-the-rooms-attractive-r25)).

## Intent and continuity
Audience before → after: relief (he is just a person) → the road is blocked, his vehicle is stuck behind it, nobody can drive away → over the last warm lights she says what she feared was just a person, but the fog stays and so do they; the last light goes out in silence; the problem stays. Visible: a plain two-shot conversation, the narrator turning to the entrance, the empty window view with the canopy lamps fading under her line. Concealed: the fog's cause, safety and duration; anything at the crash. Not shown: crash imagery, casualties, sirens, a moral, a question card, an EP2 tag, a creature, a final sting; under the closing line no shape, shadow or silhouette in the fog and no recall of the pre-reveal outline (PD12).

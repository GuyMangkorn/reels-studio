## Intent and continuity
Audience before → after: "it is at the door, maybe it has not seen us" → peak uncertainty: its "head" slid off and was caught by a hand, then put back; the narrator doubts what she saw. Visible: the slide, the catch at shoulder height, the return, the shape steadying. Concealed: that the "head" is a helmet (no colour, no face, no stack/worker separation). Not shown: a detached human head, a bone crack, a creature sound.

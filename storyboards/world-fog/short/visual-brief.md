## Visual intent

**Audience knowledge.** The viewer shares the narrator's limited view: one tall, lopsided dark shape walking toward the only lit place in a world going grey. Every clue (cargo wobble, wheel scrape, thin reach, sliding "head", tired breath) is shown fairly but only as silhouette. The light reveal answers the creature question with the same geometry, then the story turns from fear to being stranded.

**Mood arc.** Dread (S01–S02-B02) → peak uncertainty (S02-B03–B04) → interrupted fear (S03-B01) → brief relief (S03-B02) → unresolved confinement (S04).

**Color and value key** (VD16). Outside: cool grey-green fog (`#b9c2bf` family from the spike), values compressing toward one mid-light grey as fog rises. The one warm light is the sodium canopy (amber halos). It backlights the shape, and losing it is the ending. Inside: flat cool-white fluorescent, desaturated but readable midtones; the shop is the readable pocket. The shape is the darkest value in every pre-reveal exterior frame. **Colour is withheld until the reveal**: the helmet's red, the raincoat's navy and the cartons' kraft brown first appear in the flashlight beam (S03-B02). The flashlight is the second warm source and the only one aimed at the outside.

**Camera language.** Mostly eye-level, observational, slow drift or push on every shot (production rule). One fixed vantage dominates: the shop glass seen from inside (`CAM-GLASS-OUT`). Its repetition is the story (confinement, and the matched reveal), so it returns with a new action each time and alternates with interior reaction shots. No handheld shake, snap zoom or whip. Hard cuts throughout; no in-canvas dissolves are needed.

**Conceal/reveal constraints** (from script handoff items 2–4, CL01–CL05, PD01). Before `S03-B01`: no face, no readable person/boxes separation, only the shop-side front view of the assembly. After `S03-B02`: uninterrupted ordinary identity. No creature model, anatomy deformation, object swap, teleport or reveal-only prop.

### Projection contract

- **Named spaces.** `WORLD-SHOP`: metres, origin at the floor under the centre of the glass front, +x right as seen from inside, +y up, +z outward into the forecourt (negative z is inside the shop). `PLATE`: composition pixels, top-left origin, 1080×1920. `CAMERA`: the painted `Camera {s, fx, fy, dx, dy}` (`src/painted/camera.tsx`), a picture-plane push/drift applied to the whole plate and to the Overlay.
- **Projection.** Authored pinhole on one ground plane, implemented in 2D (no Three.js). Each named camera setup records eye height `h_eye`, distance to the glass `d0`, horizon `y_h` and `P0` (px/m at the glass plane). An object at depth z projects with `k(z) = P0 · d0 / (d0 + z)` px/m and ground line `y_h + h_eye · k(z)`. One helper per episode plate module owns this mapping for actors, assembly, shadows and contact; no shot rescales an actor by hand.
- **Starting values (estimated, Mark may refine and record).** `CAM-GLASS-OUT`: `h_eye` 1.45 m, `d0` 3.0 m, `y_h` about 700, `P0` 447. `CAM-LOW-SHELF`: `h_eye` 0.6 m, `d0` 2.2 m, `y_h` about 900. S01 setups are separate (car interior and forecourt wide) and keep the same 447 px/m calibration for the narrator.
- **Safe regions (this deliverable).** Caption-safe box x 120–888, y 288–1248; caption band about y 1000–1248. **Clue-safe area: y 300–950** for helmet, upper cartons, tap contact and faces. The wheel clue reads either from `CAM-LOW-SHELF`, at a depth where its ground line is above y 950, or in a caption-free gap; Mark records which. Lead ≥ 480 px whenever visible: the assembly at the glass is about 1.9 m × `k(z)`; crouched people (about 1.0 m) need a camera scale ≥ about 1.1 or a tighter framing.

### Composition, depth, light, clocks

- **Composition (VD01, VD07).** One canvas per composition (`PaintedStage`); plates paint world marks inside the camera, screen marks after it. The `Overlay` of a `PaintedShot` gets `camera` and `opacity`; foreground SVG architecture and glows use `CameraLayer` so they move with the push. Overlays always sit above the whole canvas, so nothing painted can be in front of them.
- **Depth order per window plate** (back to front): fog sky → far forecourt/pumps (fading with FOG-STATE) → canopy and lamps → high fog banks → `WFOG-ASSEMBLY` with ground shadow → low ground banks → glass tint/condensation → [interior, painted or Overlay per VD07] → screen-space glow/caption layers.
- **Fog clock (VD06).** `FOG-STATE` is a function of story time, not of shot-local time, so cuts keep continuity: FS0 lane and forecourt readable (S01 start); FS1 forecourt closing, lamps bright (S01 end, after the car is parked); FS2 parking area gone, pumps ghosted, lamps as halos, only about the first 2 m beyond the glass readable as silhouette (S02-B01 through S04-B01); FS3 lamps gone (S04-B02). The flashlight lights about 1.5 m of fog and the assembly; beyond that, fog brightens into a flat grey without detail.
- **Silhouette → lit (VD04).** Per-part colour lerp (`mixColor` pattern) on a full-alpha plate keeps frames byte-identical; alpha cross-fades of whole drawings fall under DF-20261029-painted-timeline-dissolve (≤3-level variance) and are not needed.
- **Determinism.** Every mark is a function of the frame; `still(id)` per wash and `boil(id)` per ink group; no `brush.wash()` after textured fills (build-notes finding); fixed seed bases for fills whose count changes (blinks). Render with `--gl=angle`.
- **Loading.** Fonts (Noto Sans Thai) for captions, marker and phone text wait through Remotion's lifecycle; p5.brush plates allocate no per-frame textures beyond the canvas.

### Spikes and acceptance criteria

- **SP01 — Assembly read and match (PD01; VD04, VD05, VD10, VD12).** One window plate, 4 key states: approach with wheel glimpse, tap 3 at contact, helmet caught, reveal at `light` 1. Accept when: (a) a reviewer who has not read the script describes the silhouette frames as one tall figure, not "a person with boxes" (coordinator or user); (b) the silhouette outline of the match frame and of the first reveal frame are the same geometry (only colours differ; mask difference ≈ 0 apart from the beam halo); (c) the helmet catch is at or below shoulder height and the hand touches the helmet; (d) the umbrella tip touches the glass plane on each tap frame; (e) assembly ≥ 480 px tall; (f) helmet, tap contact and face sit inside y 300–950; (g) a full-alpha frame renders byte-identical twice with `--gl=angle`.
- **SP02 — Fog visibility ramp (PD02; VD06, VD08, VD14).** FS0, FS1, FS2 and FS3 stills plus a flashlight-on still. Accept when: the lane and parked car read at FS0–FS1; at FS2 the four lamps read as halos and nothing beyond about 2 m has readable shape; in the beam the worker's face and the helmet's red read while the background stays flat grey; at FS3 no lamp is visible and the interior is unchanged.
- **SP03 — Painted interior in front of fogged glass (VD07).** `CAM-LOW-SHELF` with narrator and occupant crouched, fog through the panes. Accept when no fog pickup shows in interior fills on a 540 px sheet and a full-resolution crop; else apply the VD07 fallback.

Each spike: qa-sheet viewed, caption-zone overlay on a frame with a two-line cue, lead height measured, determinism cmp.

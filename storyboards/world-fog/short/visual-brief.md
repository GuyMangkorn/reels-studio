## Visual intent

**Audience knowledge.** The viewer shares the narrator's limited view: one tall, lopsided dark shape walking toward the only lit place in a world going grey. Every clue (cargo wobble, wheel scrape, thin reach, sliding "head", tired breath) is shown fairly but only as silhouette. The light reveal answers the creature question with the same geometry, then the story turns from fear to being stranded.

**Mood arc.** Dread (S01–S02-B02) → peak uncertainty (S02-B03–B04) → interrupted fear (S03-B01) → brief relief (S03-B02) → unresolved confinement (S04).

**Color and value key** (VD16). Outside: cool grey-green fog (`#b9c2bf` family from the spike), values compressing toward one mid-light grey as fog rises. The one warm light is the sodium canopy (amber halos). It backlights the shape, and losing it is the ending. Inside: ~~flat cool-white fluorescent~~ (R4: replaced by [VD18](#interior-character-look-vd18): overhead tube key with falloff, cool fog rim from the glass, the approved window shots' dark slate floor), desaturated but readable midtones; the shop is the readable pocket. The shape is the darkest value in every pre-reveal exterior frame. **Colour is withheld until the reveal**: the helmet's red, the raincoat's navy and the cartons' kraft brown first appear in the flashlight beam (S03-B02). The flashlight is the second warm source and the only one aimed at the outside.

**Camera language.** Mostly eye-level, observational, slow drift or push on every shot (production rule). One fixed vantage dominates: the shop glass seen from inside (`CAM-GLASS-OUT`). Its repetition is the story (confinement, and the matched reveal), so it returns with a new action each time and alternates with interior reaction shots. No handheld shake, snap zoom or whip. Hard cuts throughout; no in-canvas dissolves are needed.

**Conceal/reveal constraints** (from script handoff items 2–4, CL01–CL05, PD01). Before `S03-B01`: no face, no readable person/boxes separation, only the shop-side front view of the assembly. After `S03-B02`: uninterrupted ordinary identity. No creature model, anatomy deformation, object swap, teleport or reveal-only prop.

## Sound direction

Library checked: `/Users/mangkornkatawong/Documents/ChatGPT/sound-library/INDEX.md` (read 29 Oct 2026; no file in it is human-auditioned yet). Duties per [sound roles](../../../docs/sound-generation-workflow.md#role-responsibilities); generation, mixing and sound QA are Editor's.

- **Music (one family): dark ambient drone + sparse piano, free tempo (no drums), narration-safe.** Candidate `tension_drone-sparse-piano_01` (31.85 s loop; needs audition, loop-seam check and per-file license note). Energy: *bed* S01–S02-B01 → gentle *lift* S02-B02 → *hold* S02-B03 with a **drop to silence after the third tap** → subjective *rise* S02-B04 (optional `reveal_riser-tension_01` ending dead on the "head" peak, never on a creature cue) → near-silence before the plea → quiet *release* S03-B02 (same family thinned, piano out) → almost none in S04-B01 → **faint unresolved tone** S04-B02 (candidate `bed_dark-drone-sub_01`, ambience bed) → **hard stop** on the last frame. No outro chord, no stinger.
- **Ambience per location.** S01 car interior: engine idle, decelerating to a stop (gap). Forecourt exterior (heard from the car door and through glass, muffled): damp hush with a faint lamp hum, no wind debris or sirens (gap). Shop interior: fridge compressor plus fluorescent hum, steady, with no creaks, drips or flicker events (gap; `room_dark-empty-building_01` and `electric_light-flicker-buzz_01` imply events the story does not have).
- **SFX language: physical, close and sourced on screen.** Every meaningful sound has a visible source (script). Wheel squeak plus scrape (one jammed wheel, the same sound before and after the reveal); three short umbrella-tip taps on glass; cardboard slide plus a small hard-shell helmet catch; human exertion breath (natural, not processed); flashlight click; phone notification (`ui_notification-ding_01`, restrained). Shop glass door opening for the entry is optional (no chime unless Mark plants one).
- **Fog-lens illusion (VD17) is silent.** No wobble, whoosh, pitch-bend or processed breath tracks the distortion; the drone bed carries it, and the torch click plus the light are what lift it.
- **Excluded.** Zombie/creature breath or groans, bone crack, wood-door pounding, glass break, sirens, car alarm, `stinger_horror-screech_01`, `stinger_twist-reveal_01`, `stinger_outro-resolve_01`, whooshes on cuts.
- **Silence policy.** Silence is a clue tool. About 1 s of music drop after tap 3; one held breath (about 0.5–1 s, ambience only) before `มีใครอยู่ไหมครับ?`; S04-B02 has no voice, only shop hum and the faint tone, then a hard cut with the audio.
- **Voice note for Editor (not a Film decision).** R2: the narrator is a woman (`th-TH-PremwadeeNeural`) and the worker is the only male voice (`th-TH-NiwatNeural`), so the S04-B01 exchange separates by voice. Narrator, shop worker and occupant are three female speakers; the shop worker and occupant voices are unassigned (delivery record) and need distinction from the narrator and each other. The worker in S03-B01 is muffled by glass, never comically processed. The two female lines (shop worker, occupant) also need distinction (VD03).
- **Generation gaps** (for Mark's `Sound` lines and Editor; ID ideas only, not requests to generate now): `vehicle_car-engine-decel-stop_01` (sfx, 4–6 s, S01); `outdoor_fog-forecourt-hush_01` (ambience loop, 10–15 s); `room_shop-fridge-hum_01` (ambience loop, 10–15 s, no events); `foley_cart-wheel-squeak-scrape_01` (sfx, 3–6 s, loopable, jammed caster on wet concrete); `foley_umbrella-tap-glass_01` (sfx, three separate one-shots or one 3-tap file, about 0.15 s each, sync to tip contact frames); `foley_carton-slide-helmet-catch_01` (sfx, 1.5–2.5 s); `foley_flashlight-click_01` (sfx, under 0.3 s). ### Projection contract

- **Named spaces.** `WORLD-SHOP`: metres, origin at the floor under the centre of the glass front, +x right as seen from inside, +y up, +z outward into the forecourt (negative z is inside the shop). `PLATE`: composition pixels, top-left origin, 1080×1920. `CAMERA`: the painted `Camera {s, fx, fy, dx, dy}` (`src/painted/camera.tsx`), a picture-plane push/drift applied to the whole plate and to the Overlay.
- **Projection.** Authored pinhole on one ground plane, implemented in 2D (no Three.js). Each named camera setup records eye height `h_eye`, distance to the glass `d0`, horizon `y_h` and `P0` (px/m at the glass plane). ### Composition, depth, light, clocks

- **Composition (VD01, VD07).** One canvas per composition (`PaintedStage`); plates paint world marks inside the camera, screen marks after it. ### Fog-lens illusion (VD17)

**Technique (decided).** Per-vertex warp of the assembly polygons in the plate code (FW-A) plus an SVG fog veil on the outline (FW-B). Rejected: (1) an SVG `feDisplacementMap` over the canvas: the canvas is one element under every Overlay, so a filter would bend the whole plate (fog, puddles, lamp hazes) unless masked, the rim and veil overlays computed from the polygons would separate from a pixel-warped silhouette, and an animated `feTurbulence` on the WebGL canvas under `--gl=angle` is untested; (2) a stretched shadow projected on the fog: a second dark shape is a new visual entity, and the lamps behind the shape throw its shadow toward the glass onto the ground, not into the air; (3) a stronger bloom halo: the rim is already at `GLOW_PEAK` 0.3; (4) uniform breathing scale: it reads as the object growing (anatomy), not as the medium. Breathing survives only as the slow change of the top stretch.

**Field (FW-A), amount `A` = 1.** PLATE px at camera scale 1, before the camera, for a polygon point (x, y). `yG` = projected ground line of the assembly's current `S`, `H` = projected height of 1.95 m at the stack plane, `xc` = projected x of `S` + 0.1 m, `t` = G / 30 (story clock). Height weight `h = (yG − y) / H`, `w = A · smoothstep(0.30, 0.75, h) · smoothstep(15, 70, distance to the current umbrella tip)`; `smoothstep` is the cubic ease. Then
`dx = 14 · (0.65 · sin(2π·y/230 + 1.3t + 0.7) + 0.35 · sin(2π·y/90 − 2.1t + 2.1)) − 0.035 · h² · (x − xc)` and
`dy = −0.03 · (1 + 0.25 · sin(2π·t/3.4)) · h² · H + 5 · sin(2π·x/230 + 0.9t)`; the point moves by `(w·dx, w·dy)`.
Resample every polygon edge to ≤ 10 px before warping; warp outline and inner parts with the same field (contacts inside the mass hold because the field is continuous); the rim and the wet-ground reflection take the warped parts; the shadow pool and drag mark do not. At `A` = 0 the code returns the parts unchanged (no resampling), which is what makes the lit frames honest.

| Quantity | Assembly plane (m, stop z 0.62) | PLATE px, s 1 (k 370 px/m) | Screen px at the match push (s 1.30) |

| Ripple wavelengths | 0.62 and 0.24 | 230 and 90 | 300 and 117 |

| Weight ramp | zero below about 0.59 m, full above about 1.46 m | — | — |

## Findings

Workaround: the viaduct is a distant sky card hung on the tilt at 62 deg elevation (`VIADUCT_ELEV`), pillars end behind the roofline, train 3 cars = 1606 px at f 950 crossing in f 40-115 (36 px/f). **Angle fix (user: "train angle looks odd", 3 Nov 2026):** the first card drew a side-on elevation (car 112 px tall, level pillars) under a 38 deg pitch. Now faces are foreshortened for a ~60 deg view from below (car 76 px, fascia 40 px, darker deck soffit below it), and every vertical edge (car ends, window posts, pillars) leans to the zenith vanishing point `vy = hy - f/tan(pitch)`, the same convergence the storefront shows; the track edges stay level (parallel to the image plane). The soft wash under the train is a hard band (the wash blob read as smoke). Caption-zone band 360:560 p95 0.925 -> 0.811 (the lighter, shorter card lowers the sky). Stills `out/eg/bts/before.png`, `after.png`. The pitch is a real rotation (`Cam.pitch`), ground is not painted in that shot (`ground:false`); wires and the post are therefore absent in the tilt. Film: the "deck about 11 m" is not a measurement in this shot; captions (high band, bottom y 560) cover the roofline, not the train (y about 190-300).

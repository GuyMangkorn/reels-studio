# Render rules

**Requirement** for every role that renders video or stills (Boss, Editor, Film, coordinator). Moved out of [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#shared-agent-conventions) on 5 Nov 2026 so Preaw and Boat do not load them; the text is unchanged.

<a id="render-by-scene"></a>
- **Render by scene, keep the scenes (Requirement):** every video render goes through `scripts/render.mjs`, which renders one timeline scene at a time and keeps each in `$REELS_STORE/cache/segments/<CompositionId>/` ([scripts/segments.mjs](../scripts/segments.mjs)). The same segments serve review and delivery: a review clip (`--range`, `scripts/storyboard-page/clips.mjs`) renders the scenes it touches into the cache, the user's final approval pins those segments (`scripts/pin.mjs`), and the delivery render and every re-edit reuse each scene whose inputs did not change, so after an edit only the touched scenes are painted. A re-edit that would change a pinned scene (a caption, a cue, shared code) stops the render: `--repaint=<shot>` renders it again and returns it to the user's Final tab for a new approval, or `--keep-pinned` ships the approved picture unchanged, with the reason recorded. **Default:** for painted deliverables one shot is one timeline scene, so approval, pinning and re-edits each touch exactly one shot; a scene that must hold several shots (a continuous camera move, a dissolve chain) is recorded in the scene sheet and reviewed as one unit. Never replace this with a whole-timeline `npx remotion render` for a deliverable or a re-edit; a whole-timeline render is for a frame-range check with `--no-cache` only, recorded with the reason. Measured in [DF-20261031-render-speed](dev-findings/DF-20261031-render-speed.md); workflow in [shot-by-shot review](sequence-workflow.md#painted-shot-by-shot).
<a id="render-stage"></a>
- **Render stage before every video render (Requirement; user, 7 Nov 2026):** a deliverable's video renders run in three stages, in order, and the renderer names the stage before each render in one line of its reply: `Render stage: <draft clips | final cut | delivery | range check> · <shots or frames> · gate: <task.md line>`.
  1. **Draft clips**, one shot ±1 s each (`scripts/storyboard-page/clips.mjs --draft`; a non-painted deliverable renders each scene with `render.mjs --range`), once the storyboard and the [voice lock](delivery-workflow.md#voice-lock) have passed (W5).
  2. **Final cut**, the whole video once (`clips.mjs --full`), only when every shot's draft passed (task.md W5.3 at N/N; W6).
  3. **Delivery**, `render.mjs <CompositionId>` with no review flag, only when every scene is pinned (W7, `pin.mjs pin-rows`).

  A draft and a final paint the same picture since 6 Nov 2026, so the stages differ in scope (one shot against the whole video), not in grade; matching pictures are no reason to skip a stage. A request that does not name a stage ("render", "ทำคลิป") means the next stage in task.md. Skipping a stage needs the user's choice: their words that say so ("ข้าม draft", "render ทั้งคลิปเลย"), or else one structured question giving the time of both options from the latest `out/logs/render-*.jsonl`. The scripts enforce stages 2 and 3: `clips.mjs --full` stops while a shot has no draft clip, and a delivery render stops while a scene is unpinned. `--skip-draft="<reason>"` and `--unreviewed="<reason>"` override them only after that choice, with the user's words and date as the reason. A whole draft reel (`render.mjs --draft`) is rendered only when the user asks to watch the cut in one piece. Evidence: the user, 7 Nov 2026, reported renders that skipped the draft clips and went straight to a full final, so every fix waited on a long render.
<a id="review-renders"></a>
- **Review renders reuse the scene cache (Requirement):** a video rendered for the user to look at is rendered at full size through the scene cache, so a later delivery reuses it: per shot with `scripts/storyboard-page/clips.mjs` (`--draft` for the blocking draft) for the [storyboard review page](mark-storyboard-workflow.md#user-storyboard-review-page), or with `scripts/render.mjs <CompositionId> --range=<from>-<to>` covering the frames under review plus about one second either side. `--preview` (half size, never reused for delivery) is only for a quick look the user asked for, and the reply says so. Stills for frame checks (`--stills`, `scripts/qa-sheet.sh`, caption-zone and lead-height measurements) stay full size.
<a id="stills-one-chrome"></a>
- **Stills on one Chrome (Requirement):** QA and review stills are rendered with `scripts/render.mjs <CompositionId> --stills --frames=<a,b,...>` or by piping `ID FRAME OUTPUT` lines into `node scripts/stills.mjs <bundleDir|entry.ts>` ([scripts/stills.mjs](../scripts/stills.mjs)), never with `npx remotion still` in a per-frame loop (each call bundles, starts Node and launches Chrome). A script calling `@remotion/renderer` opens one browser and passes it as `puppeteerInstance`; Remotion ignores `browserInstance` and launches a Chrome per call. Measured in [DF-20261031-render-speed](dev-findings/DF-20261031-render-speed.md#stills-and-browser-reuse-31-oct-2026-claudep5-brush-rendering-optimize-fd68e1).

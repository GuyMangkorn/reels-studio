# User taste

**Status:** Requirement to read before painting a look frame, thumbnail or plate, and before a [viewer check](sequence-workflow.md#viewer-check) (user, 3 Nov 2026). The entries are Reference: the user's repeated review notes, each with its evidence. A brief or scene sheet may override an entry for one shot with a recorded reason.

Why this file exists: in WDG-EP01-SHORT one look frame went through seven painted rounds, and KSO-EP01-SHORT took three Draft fix rounds on arms and mouths. Most of those notes had been given before on another episode. An agent checks its picture against this list before the user sees it.

**Adding an entry.** When the user gives the same kind of note on a second episode, the coordinator adds it here with both episodes as evidence. One-episode notes stay in that episode's records.

## Reading

<a id="ut-read"></a>
- **UT01 — The place reads at a glance.** A viewer knows where we are and whether we are inside or outside within the first second, without the narration. Evidence: WDG LF-TENT r2–r4 ("cannot tell it is a tent, or inside/outside", "make it read as inside", 2 Nov 2026).
- **UT02 — The hero object reads.** The object a shot is about is large, lit and detailed enough to be named. Evidence: WDG S01-B02-SH02 ("more detail on the tin; it is the hero of the scene"); KSO S01-B01-SH01 extreme close-up the user could not read (1 Nov 2026).

## Frame and light

<a id="ut-frame"></a>
- **UT03 — Not too much black.** Dark scenes keep the subject and set readable; a large empty black area is a fault, not mood. The picture fills the 9:16 frame and does not sit high with black below. Evidence: WDG LF-TENT r4 ("the picture sits too high, the black part is too much"), r6 ("black only 45%").
- **UT04 — Light behaves as the brief says.** A light source named in the brief lights its surroundings and leaks where it should, with a clean colour, not a muddy pool. Evidence: WDG S01-B02-SH01 ("lantern light must leak through, per the brief"), LF-TENT r6 ("the light beside the tent must be fixed").
- **UT05 — Era and place look right.** A scene set in another era or culture shows it in the picture, not only in the narration. Evidence: WDG S03-B01-SH01 ("change the look so it reads as the atmosphere of that era").

## Bodies and faces

<a id="ut-body"></a>
- **UT06 — Arms are anatomically right in depth.** Arms that point toward or away from the camera keep believable foreshortening; elbows sit at the waist, upper arms angle out from the torso, hands grip what they hold. Use the [pose sheet](character-proportion-principles.md#cp-p-pose-sheet) and [depth arms](character-proportion-principles.md#cp-p-arm-depth). Evidence: HANAKO B03-SH01 sample ("wrong anatomy, perspective of the depth arm", 31 Oct 2026); KSO Draft rounds 3–5 (elbows, arms at the sides, glass grip, fist, 1 Nov 2026).
- **UT07 — Faces return to rest cleanly.** An expression change ends on the kit's plain face with no leftover lines. Evidence: KSO S03-B01-SH01 (grin closing, line under the nose, Draft rounds 4–5).
- **UT08 — Female bodies carry clear cues** (waist, hips, bust). Evidence: char_base lineup (29 Oct 2026).

## Horror and monsters

<a id="ut-horror"></a>
- **UT09 — Dread over jump scares.** Imply the ghost (a look, a sound, a shadow); avoid a heavy jump scare. Evidence: HANAKO ("not too much jumpscare", Hanako never shown as a body, 31 Oct 2026); KSO ("implication only", 1 Nov 2026).
- **UT10 — A monster design starts from the user's reference.** Ask for or find a reference before designing a creature the user names; do not invent its look. Evidence: WDG untildawn wendigo (reference supplied after SB-R2, 2 Nov 2026).

## Medium

- **UT11 — Painted, not flat SVG,** for pictures the user sees. Evidence: BLOOP ("paint, not SVG", 30 Oct 2026); [project defaults](delivery-workflow.md#project-defaults).

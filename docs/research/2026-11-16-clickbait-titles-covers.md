# Clickbait titles and covers that keep the promise

Research note, 16 Nov 2026, for the user request "ปรับแต่ง skill ทำปกและทำ title ให้ clickbait หน่อย … ลองหาตัวอย่างใน internet". Feeds [cover-principles](../cover-principles.md) CV02, CV04, CV07, CV11 and the [cover-design skill](../../.agents/skills/cover-design/SKILL.md). Builds on the [7 Nov cover note](2026-11-07-cover-thumbnail-principles.md).

## Summary

- Push the click as hard as the video can pay: stakes, "you", danger, a yes/no question, a true extreme. What fails is the promise the video does not keep ("clicktrap"), not a strong hook ("legitbait").
- On this channel the two biggest Shorts put **the viewer (or a person) in physical danger and ask whether they survive**. Topic-label titles (a name plus a description) are the weakest.
- Title and cover work as a pair: the title asks, the cover shows the danger moment; neither repeats the other.

## 1. Channel evidence (edu, Studio 18 Oct – 14 Nov 2026)

Source: [analytics snapshot](../../channels/edu/analytics/2026-11-15-data.json), public views at pull and Studio "stayed to watch". Observational; topics differ, so this is a pattern, not a test.

| Views | Stayed % | Title | Shape |
| ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| 32,841 | 78.4 | ถ้าคุณตัวเล็กเท่ามด… โลกจะอันตรายแค่ไหน? | you + transformation + danger question |
| 24,861 | 76.7 | ลิฟต์ขาด กระโดดตอนท้ายรอดมั้ย? 🛗 | disaster + survival yes/no |
| 6,847 | 70.5 | จ้องตาแมวด้วยความรัก… ทำไมมันหนี? | everyday action + contradiction |
| 5,916 | 61.8 | ถ้าหมัดตัวเท่าคน จะกระโดดข้ามตึกได้มั้ย? | scale-up + yes/no |
| 4,643 | 58.6 | ถ้าตุ๊กแกจับเต็มแรง ห้อยคนได้ทั้งตัว | extreme claim |
| 1,027 | 37.7 | เวนดิโก ปีศาจแห่งความหิว | topic label |
| 1,003 | 47.5 | เรืออับปางที่เก่าที่สุดในโลก… ไม่เหลือเรือสักชิ้น | superlative + twist, no stakes for the viewer |
| 325 | 32.1 | เมืองที่ไม่รอคุณ | vague, no concrete noun |

Pattern: a concrete situation the viewer can picture themself in, a risk to a body, and a question with a yes/no answer beat abstract or named-topic titles. Plain "ทำไม…" explainers sit in the middle (1.6–1.9k).

## 2. External sources

- **Veritasium, "Clickbait is Unreasonably Effective" (2021).** Retitling "Strange Applications of the Magnus Effect" to "Backspin Basketball Flies Off Dam", with a matching thumbnail, gave a second, steady rise in views; "These Are The Asteroids To Worry About" with a new thumbnail passed 10M. A title should reach people who do not know the topic's name (Collatz: name it by the mystery, not the term). In his poll "The Biggest Myth in Education" beat "You Are NOT a Visual Learner" by a wide margin. He splits clickbait into accurate "legitbait" and misleading "clicktrap". Sources: [veritasium.com](https://www.veritasium.com/videos/2021/8/17/we-need-to-talk-about-clickbait), [GIGAZINE summary](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20210830-clickbait-effective).
- **MrBeast production memo (via commentary).** Title, thumbnail, first sentence and first shot match; "World's Largest Bouncy Castle" is the memo's mismatch warning (thumbnail castle ≠ video castle). Titles are a concrete extreme situation: "I Spent 50 Hours Buried Alive", "Last To Leave The Circle Wins $1,000,000". Source: [Zack Chewning](https://zackchewning.substack.com/p/content-lessons-from-the-1-youtubers).
- **vidIQ, 10 viral title types.** Contradictory curiosity, tangible timeframe, insider authority, negativity, regret, personal victory, cliffhanger question, broad relatability, buzzwords, sensational promise. Boundary test: would a viewer who watched to the end agree the promise was kept. Source: [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/types-of-youtube-titles-that-go-insanely-viral).
- **Shorts title guides (vendor blogs, weak evidence).** Keep it short so the overlay does not cut it; generic teasers ("This will shock you") no longer work, specific ones do. Sources: [OverseerOS](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/best-youtube-shorts-title-formulas), [ClipSpeed](https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/how-to-write-viral-titles-descriptions-shorts.html).

## 3. Evidence quality

Veritasium's cases are first-hand but on long videos. The memo and the vidIQ list are secondary. The channel table is our own data but not a controlled test. No source was found for Thai audiences specifically. Treat the formulas as defaults to try and log the angle per episode so the channel data can confirm or drop them.

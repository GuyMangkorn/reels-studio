# Short-form editing principles for James

**Status:** Project defaults for editorial choices, subject to the supplied brief/boards. The [production requirements](production-requirements.md) and [current visual rules](boss-asset-workflow.md#latest-production-rules) remain mandatory. Primary-source observations and their limits live in [short-form editing research](short-form-editing-research.md); the guidance here is the project's editorial synthesis, not a platform ranking formula.

Use these principles with [James's workflow](james-editing-workflow.md). Keep stable decision IDs such as `ED-SH03-01` in edit notes. Each decision states the audience purpose, shot/beat, technique, event frames and review evidence. A technique is useful when the resulting image/sound makes the intended information or emotion clearer.

## Hook, progression and payoff

- **ED01 — Make the opening understandable.** Put the visual question, surprising action, conflict or useful result into the project's first-two-second hook window. Start the Thai subtitle at frame 0 as required. An opening must be truthful to the eventual payoff; Film/Mark own changes to the treatment, Preaw/Boat to story/claims.
- **ED02 — Advance information.** For each beat, identify what changes in audience knowledge, observation or emotion. Use action, interaction, comparison or a purposeful hold. A new crop or effect alone is not a new story beat. Mark resolves repeated or unclear visual intent.
- **ED03 — Cut at meaningful changes.** Prefer cuts on action, reaction, gaze, evidence, information or a deliberate tension release. Preserve a readable setup and consequence. Choose shot duration from actual action, Thai delivery, subtitle reading and reveal tests; avoid universal cuts-per-second prescriptions.
- **ED04 — Protect suspense and explanation.** Author cue, conceal, reveal, reaction and comprehension-hold frames. Review with and without sound. Rapid montage must not leak the answer early or remove the evidence needed to understand the payoff. Keep the required closing hold; shorter is not automatically clearer.

## Picture, motion and transitions

- **ED05 — Give motion a job.** The project requires intentional motion every shot; use restrained frame-driven movement that directs attention while preserving contact and caption readability. Punch-ins can emphasize a real detail or reaction. Use shared camera/effect APIs; ask Boss for missing behavior instead of stacking transforms to hide defects.
- **ED06 — Choose a transition for the relationship.** A hard cut is the default when it clearly connects beats. Use match cuts, cutaways or motivated wipes for a specific spatial, causal or time relationship. J/L-style sound bridges may connect scenes when sound perspective and chronology remain clear. Check actual runtime support before authoring overlaps; preserve clue/reveal timing.
- **ED07 — Orient the viewer.** Preserve screen direction, eyelines, object identity and entry/exit contact, or make an intentional change understandable. Track foreground occlusion and both-format focal points across cuts. Compare `F-1/F/F+1` at risky events; continuous playback establishes flow beyond sampled stills.
- **ED08 — Keep direction consistent.** Use Film's palette/value/light and camera language and Mark's shot targets. Match adjacent shots before adding stylized grading, shake, speed ramps, blur or trend effects. Apply those only where licensed, supported and helpful to the beat; test flashes under current accessibility guidance in the research.

## Thai captions and mobile composition

- **ED09 — Caption meaning and speech accurately.** Use the project's Thai font/style and source text; review vowel/tone marks, natural phrase boundaries, line breaks, contrast and synchronization at output size. Keep critical facts, qualifications and reveals intact. Correct automatic transcription by listening. Emphasize a few useful terms rather than animating every word by default.
- **ED10 — Reserve space for the whole experience.** Test the actor/action, evidence, subtitle and channel label together in Short and Long. Keep essential content away from current platform controls using current destination previews where available; fixed percentages from an ad template are not universal organic safe zones. Label missing platform-overlay checks unverified. Captions cannot substitute for a hidden or unreadable action.

## Sound and finishing

- **ED11 — Let narration lead comprehension.** Check Thai VO at ordinary mobile listening volume and through the actual delivery mix; lower or shape competing music/SFX where words are masked. Use purposeful impacts, ambience and silence tied to events, including offscreen cues. Check clipping, cut clicks, tails, overlaps and continuity. Do not claim a universal platform LUFS target without an applicable delivery specification and measurement.
- **ED12 — Track rights by destination.** Record source and commercial/export permissions for every sound/music asset. A platform music library or trend does not establish reuse rights on another platform or in a downloaded export. Missing rights require a licensed replacement, not an assumed permission.
- **ED13 — End with the promised payoff.** Preserve the production hold and required fade/loop variants at publishable boundaries. A loop must make the last-to-first relationship readable and preserve story truth, audio and caption timing; do not apply ending effects at every internal scene cut.

## Review and learning

- **ED14 — Show the edit before embellishment.** Review the continuous blockout with Mark and Film before polish. Use frame-specific feedback and visible acceptance targets, route shared faults to Boss, and retain the agreed script/brief/board revision. This reduces interpretation through actual pictures rather than longer prose alone.
- **ED15 — Diagnose analytics in context.** When actual analytics are provided, use platform-specific definitions: feed choice, engaged views, watch time/percentage, retention shape, shares/saves and the clip's objective as available. Distinguish rewatching from confusion and traffic/audience/duration differences from an editing effect. Form a hypothesis tied to a beat, change one meaningful editorial variable when practical, and record uncertainty. No technique here guarantees reach or retention.

Pre-delivery evidence covers the hook, action/reveal/hold, captions/UI, sound-on/muted comprehension, contacts, cuts, continuous playback, seek determinism, both formats and publishable endings. Detailed technical coverage remains in [shared QA](boss-asset-workflow.md#visual-and-runtime-qa-before-push); readiness/review ownership remains in [James's workflow](james-editing-workflow.md).

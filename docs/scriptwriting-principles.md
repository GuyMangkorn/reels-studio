# Scriptwriting principles for mystery, knowledge and twist Reels

Researched and synthesized on 26 October 2026 for Preaw and Boat. The required workflow and handoff files are in [editorial-workflow.md](editorial-workflow.md). The principles below are selective creative guidance, not a guarantee of views or a requirement that every story use the same structure.

<a id="หลักจากแหล่งต้นทาง"></a>
## Principles from primary sources

<a id="hook-และ-mini-story"></a>
### Hook and mini-story

The conversation between YouTube Shorts Product Lead Todd Sherman and Jenny Hoyos emphasizes immediate attention and a compact mini-story. The one-second recommendation is creator experience reported in the article, not a channel requirement or an explanation of the algorithm.

Application: open with an anomaly or question that the story can resolve, and make the ending deliver the hook's promise. Follow the channel timing targets linked from AGENTS.md.

Source: [YouTube Shorts deep dive — YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-shorts-deep-dive/), 28 January 2025.

<a id="ความต้องการและอุปสรรค"></a>
### Intention and obstacle

Aaron Sorkin teaches starting with someone who wants something and faces a meaningful obstacle. This drives a story beyond attractive dialogue without progression.

Application: let characters observe, investigate, test or decide, and let those actions affect events. The intention can be finding an answer; every story does not need danger.

Source: [Intention & Obstacle — Aaron Sorkin / MasterClass](https://www.masterclass.com/classes/aaron-sorkin-teaches-screenwriting/chapters/intention-obstacle-11ba8c15-7856-490d-85bb-eb0601e02c55), instructor preview inspected directly.

<a id="twist-ที่ย้อนเข้าใจได้"></a>
### A twist that rewards looking back

Writing Excuses' author-hosts explain twists that change how earlier information is understood through expectations, misdirection and already-visible clues. Their exercise reasons backward from the reveal, then removes details to identify what its logic requires.

Application: choose the underlying truth and ending first, then plant clues with two coherent readings. Surprise should not depend on a decisive rule invented at the ending.

Source: [Twists: How to Make the Unexpected Feel Inevitable — Writing Excuses](https://writingexcuses.com/21-36-twists-how-to-make-the-unexpected-feel-inevitable/).

<a id="ตรวจถ้อยคำจริงกับหลักฐาน"></a>
### Check actual wording against evidence

Brooke Borel's Knight Science Journalism at MIT handbook recommends sourcing every fact, checking line by line, assessing evidence quality and wording uncertainty when sources disagree. Writing, editing and verification are separate responsibilities, with the level of detail adapted to the assignment.

Application: Boat's research and post-writing fact-check are separate passes with claim/source IDs and a named script revision. A possible mechanism does not establish that it caused a particular real case.

Source: [The Fact-Checking Process — KSJ Handbook](https://ksjhandbook.org/fact-checking-science-journalism-how-to-make-sure-your-stories-are-true/the-fact-checking-process/).

## Guidance for this channel

Resolve [delivery selection](delivery-workflow.md) before writing. Give each selected version its own narrative progression: a focused standalone Short and a developed Long, using the targets in [production requirements](production-requirements.md#outputs-and-story-timing). The experimental Short structure below does not set Long chapter lengths or automatically create a Short per chapter.

- Start with a central question and a learning takeaway that fit the available time. Adjust through spoken reads/animatics rather than a fixed sentence count.
- Connect beats causally: a clue prompts a test, whose result changes the hypothesis. Avoid unrelated strange events in sequence.
- Give knowledge a plot function: observation, testing, decisions or understanding the reveal. If removing it leaves the story unchanged, reconsider whether it is merely appended.
- A supernatural story need not always end with a scientific explanation. Preserve uncertainty in unresolved cases. Fiction may remain mysterious without presenting invented material as real evidence.
- Manage differences between audience and character knowledge while keeping real-case hooks/narration faithful to evidence.
- Reread to test the twist: clues remain present, causes still connect and the reveal changes the meaning of earlier information.
- Write Thai for listening. Remove repeated explanation, leave pauses for information and emotion, and test audio before locking timing.

<a id="edu-short-retention"></a>
### Edu Short retention (Default; user, 8 Nov 2026)

Evidence: three posted edu Shorts ([EP05 retrospective](../scripts/edu-series/ep05/lessons-learned.md)). EP05 "ถ้าคุณตัวเล็กเท่ามด": 24k views, 20.2 % swiped away, ~91 % average watched. EP02 and EP03: 1.3k–2.5k views, 34–37 % swiped away, 60–66 % watched. Three data points, so these are defaults to test, not proven causes; record each new episode's numbers in [channel performance](channel-performance.md).

- **ER01 — Hook (Preaw).** Line 1 carries the title's promise and speaks to "คุณ". Within about 5 s comes one concrete claim that contradicts something the viewer is sure of, with stakes the picture can show (EP05 L02 "ตกจากตึกสิบชั้น… คุณจะแทบไม่เป็นอะไรเลย" over the drop). By about 7 s, answer the objection a sharp viewer would raise (EP05 L03 "สมมติว่าหดทั้งตัว… รวมทั้งน้ำหนักด้วย"). Avoid a hook whose answer the viewer thinks they already know (EP02 "หัวหอมไม่ได้ทำให้คุณร้องไห้…"). In ideation, Preaw states each concept's counter-intuitive claim in one line.
- **ER02 — Levels (Preaw).** Pay the hook by about 30 % of the runtime with a comparison the body knows (EP05 "ราว ๆ กระโดดลงจากเก้าอี้"), and open the next problem in the same breath ("แต่…"). Then a new problem about every 10 s. The character stays on screen and meets each problem before the narrator names its cause (show, then tell). The mechanism is one everyday line after the character has felt it, never a block of terms with the character off screen (EP02 L06 "ในเซลล์ มีเอนไซม์ กับสารที่มีกำมะถัน…" sits on its sharpest retention drop, ~15 s).
- **ER03 — One rule that flips (Preaw, Boat).** One spoken rule explains every beat and returns as a refrain (EP05 "ตัวยิ่งเล็ก…"); the twist turns the same rule from a cost into an advantage. Plant the twist's objects early as a burden or background (EP05 crumb CL01, ant CL02). Put the best moment last and let it land: a closed ending with a ~2 s hold when the reversal is the payoff. Boat keeps the hook strong and true with narrow hedges ("แทบ", "ราว ๆ", "บางชนิด") instead of turning the whole claim into a maybe. The last line is the flip or the payoff, never a call to action or a tip (EP03 ended on "ครั้งหน้าที่ซื้อของเล่น ลองถามตัวเองว่า…"; verify with its curve after 10 Nov).
- **ER04 — A friend telling it (Preaw).** The character reacts with small vocal sounds (squeak, "huh?", sigh), and the narrator talks like a friend (particles "นะ", "หรอก"; EP05 R1.4 user rewrites), not a textbook.
- **ER05 — Audience level (Preaw writes, Boat checks).** Write for a high-school graduate (12th grade) from any field; the hook (first ~7 s) is middle-school level (grades 7–9) and carries no technical term. Explain the thing before its name: an everyday example first, the term after it or not at all. A term that stays gets one everyday comparison in the same breath, and a new term comes at most about once per 15 s. Test: a friend from another field hears it over lunch and can retell it in one sentence; a line the user finds confusing fails. A simplification must stay true (Boat, [fact-check](editorial-workflow.md#fact-check--boat)).
- **ER06 — Open-question map (Preaw).** The beat list carries an open-question map: each question the viewer is waiting on, the line that opens it and the line that answers it, with times. No stretch longer than about 5 s without an open question; an explanation longer than about 6 s keeps the character on screen and in trouble, or is split into a question and its answer. Evidence: EP02's steepest drop (12.6–15.6 s) sits inside a 17 s term block with no open question; EP05's longest gap is about 3 s ([deep dive](../scripts/edu-series/ep05/retention-deep-dive.md#api-pull)).
- **ER07 — Only what has an answer (Preaw, Boat).** A beat whose honest answer is "evidence unclear" or "results differ" is cut, not narrated; accuracy is kept by leaving it out, not by hedging on screen. One well-supported answer beats a list of tips. Boat recommends the cut when a qualification would empty the beat. Evidence: EP02's tips list starts a second drop at 30–32 s ("ผลยังไม่ตรงกัน", "หลักฐานยังไม่ชัด").

<a id="loop-friendly-writing"></a>
## Loop-friendly writing

For a deliverable whose style profile asks for a seamless loop (`C_seamless` ending), design the last line and the first line as a pair: the closing beat should raise or answer the hook so that the video can start again without feeling restarted (a closing question the opening line answers, an ending that is the cause the opening shows, or an opening that makes sense as a continuation of the final words). Keep the final ~0.5 s free of speech for the blend window, avoid a goodbye or call to action that breaks the circle, and make sure the last visual state can plausibly return to the opening frame. A loop does not replace the payoff: the reveal still lands before the seam. Whether looping raises replays or watch time for this channel is untested.

## Experimental structure for roughly 45 seconds

This timing split is an experiment, not a Reels/Shorts requirement or a formula for every story:

| Approximate time | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 seconds | Anomaly and central question |
| 2–10 | Context, intention and first clue |
| 10–25 | Investigation/test, obstacle or hypothesis-changing evidence |
| 25–35 | Apply the main knowledge and approach the answer |
| 35–42 | Reveal / twist connected to earlier clues |
| 42–45 | Consequence and time to absorb the ending |

Adapt to the script, actual audio and channel rules linked from AGENTS.md. This structure has not demonstrated performance for this channel.

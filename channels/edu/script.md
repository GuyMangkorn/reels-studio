# Edu channel: script rules

**Status:** Default for every deliverable of the [edu channel](channel.md) (user, 8 Nov 2026). Preaw reads this before pitching or writing, and Boat before a fact-check or clarity pass. The shared craft (hooks, causality, fair twists, loops) stays in [scriptwriting principles](../../docs/scriptwriting-principles.md); this file holds only what the channel's own numbers taught. Moved here unchanged from `docs/scriptwriting-principles.md#edu-short-retention` (11 Nov 2026); the IDs ER01–ER10 are stable.

## Edu Short retention

Evidence: three posted edu Shorts ([EP05 retrospective](../../scripts/edu-series/ep05/lessons-learned.md)). EP05 "ถ้าคุณตัวเล็กเท่ามด": 24k views, 20.2 % swiped away, ~91 % average watched. EP02 and EP03: 1.3k–2.5k views, 34–37 % swiped away, 60–66 % watched. Three data points, so these are defaults to test, not proven causes; record each new episode's numbers in [channel performance](performance.md).

- **ER01 — Hook (Preaw).** This is the default hook shape (HK01 in the [hook catalogue](../../docs/research/2026-11-16-shorts-hook-types.md#2-hook-catalogue)). The user picks each script's hook at the [hook pick](../../docs/delivery-workflow.md#hook-pick); a picked hook of another shape replaces this rule for that deliverable. Line 1 carries the title's promise and speaks to "คุณ". Within about 5 s comes one concrete claim that contradicts something the viewer is sure of, with stakes the picture can show (EP05 L02 "ตกจากตึกสิบชั้น… คุณจะแทบไม่เป็นอะไรเลย" over the drop). By about 7 s, answer the objection a sharp viewer would raise (EP05 L03 "สมมติว่าหดทั้งตัว… รวมทั้งน้ำหนักด้วย"). Avoid a hook whose answer the viewer thinks they already know (EP02 "หัวหอมไม่ได้ทำให้คุณร้องไห้…"). In ideation, Preaw states each concept's counter-intuitive claim in one line.
- **ER02 — Levels (Preaw).** Pay the hook by about 30 % of the runtime with a comparison the body knows (EP05 "ราว ๆ กระโดดลงจากเก้าอี้"), and open the next problem in the same breath ("แต่…"). Then a new problem about every 10 s. The character stays on screen and meets each problem before the narrator names its cause (show, then tell). The mechanism is one everyday line after the character has felt it, never a block of terms with the character off screen (EP02 L06 "ในเซลล์ มีเอนไซม์ กับสารที่มีกำมะถัน…" sits on its sharpest retention drop, ~15 s).
- **ER03 — One rule that flips (Preaw, Boat).** One spoken rule explains every beat and returns as a refrain (EP05 "ตัวยิ่งเล็ก…"); the twist turns the same rule from a cost into an advantage. Plant the twist's objects early as a burden or background (EP05 crumb CL01, ant CL02). Put the best moment last and let it land: a closed ending with a ~2 s hold when the reversal is the payoff. Boat keeps the hook strong and true with narrow hedges ("แทบ", "ราว ๆ", "บางชนิด") instead of turning the whole claim into a maybe. The last line is the flip or the payoff, never a call to action or a tip (EP03 ended on "ครั้งหน้าที่ซื้อของเล่น ลองถามตัวเองว่า…"; verify with its curve after 10 Nov).
- **ER04 — A friend telling it (Preaw).** The character reacts with small vocal sounds (squeak, "huh?", sigh), and the narrator talks like a friend (particles "นะ", "หรอก"; EP05 R1.4 user rewrites), not a textbook.
- **ER05 — Audience level (Preaw writes, Boat checks).** Write for a high-school graduate (12th grade) from any field; the hook (first ~7 s) is middle-school level (grades 7–9) and carries no technical term. Explain the thing before its name: an everyday example first, the term after it or not at all. A term that stays gets one everyday comparison in the same breath, and a new term comes at most about once per 15 s. Test: a friend from another field hears it over lunch and can retell it in one sentence; a line the user finds confusing fails. A simplification must stay true (Boat, [fact-check](../../docs/editorial-workflow.md#fact-check--boat)).
- **ER06 — Open-question map (Preaw).** The beat list carries an open-question map: each question the viewer is waiting on, the line that opens it and the line that answers it, with times. No stretch longer than about 5 s without an open question. Each answer stays within [ER09](#er09)'s 10 s; a character on screen does not extend it (user, 9 Nov 2026). The map records each answer's length. Evidence: EP02's steepest drop (12.6–15.6 s) sits inside a 17 s term block with no open question; EP05's longest gap is about 3 s ([deep dive](../../scripts/edu-series/ep05/retention-deep-dive.md#api-pull)).
- **ER07 — Only what has an answer (Preaw, Boat).** A beat whose honest answer is "evidence unclear" or "results differ" is cut, not narrated; accuracy is kept by leaving it out, not by hedging on screen. One well-supported answer beats a list of tips. Boat recommends the cut when a qualification would empty the beat. Evidence: EP02's tips list starts a second drop at 30–32 s ("ผลยังไม่ตรงกัน", "หลักฐานยังไม่ชัด").
- **ER08 — Pitch check (Preaw; ideation).** Each edu concept file in `scripts/edu-series/concepts/` answers four lines: (1) **Hook** breaks a certainty rather than a truism, is understood in about 3 s with no prior knowledge and no technical term (ER01, ER05); (2) **Who** can picture themselves in it: everyone, or only a niche such as dog owners; (3) **Beats** are a chain of problems, each opening the next with "แต่…", with no mechanism-only beat and no tips list (ER06, ER07); (4) **Ending** is a flip or payoff, not a tip or a call to action (ER03). A lesson that steers a round ("why X worked") cites [channel performance](performance.md) numbers; without them it is labelled a guess and does not steer. Evidence: C1's pitch already held EP02's term block (beats 2–3) and tips montage (beat 4); R2 was steered by an unmeasured "Why EP03 worked" ([concepts](../../scripts/edu-series/concepts.md)).
- <a id="er09"></a>**ER09 — Short answers (Preaw writes, Boat respects; user, 9 Nov 2026).** Each problem gets one cause, explained in at most about 10 s of speech: one or two lines, counted from the first explaining line to the line that opens the next problem or the payoff. An analogy and a term-naming line count inside the 10 s. Further mechanism steps are cut, not chained; when an answer truly needs two causes, split it into two problems with a "แต่…" between them. The open-question map (ER06) records each answer's length, and a script with an answer over 10 s goes back to Preaw before Boat review. Evidence: posted EP05 answers run 3–6 s (L05 3.8 s, L12 3.6 s, L15 5.5 s); later scripts chained 13–18 s: EP09 L06–L09 ~17.7 s, EP12 L02–L05 ~18 s, EP13 L03–L06 ~13 s, EP08 L07–L09 12.9 s (passed under ER06's former on-screen exception); EP02's 17 s term block holds its steepest drop.
- <a id="er10"></a>**ER10 — Hedges in spoken lines (Preaw, Boat; user, 9 Nov 2026).** A hedge is a narrow word inside the claim ("แทบ", "ราว ๆ", "มัก", "บางชนิด", "อาจ"), at most one per line. Narration carries no source, method or evidence-quality phrase ("ตามแบบจำลอง…", "มักอธิบายกันว่า…", "ข้อมูลส่วนใหญ่มาจาก…"); a claim that needs one is reworded to what is solid, or cut (ER07). A simplification whose direction is true passes; detail the next beat does not need is left out. Evidence: EP13 L10, EP09 L09, EP08 L12 and EP11 L12 carry qualifiers kept for accuracy that lengthen the line without changing what the viewer learns.

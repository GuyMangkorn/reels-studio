# Edu channel: script rules

**Status:** Default for every deliverable of the [edu channel](channel.md). Preaw reads this before pitching or writing, and Boat before research or a fact-check. Rewritten 18 Nov 2026 on the user's review of EP20, EP22 and EP26: "บทคือหนังสือที่ผมไม่อยากอ่าน… ไม่มีมุก สาระเยอะ… อยากได้เล่าเรื่องสนุก น่าติดตาม สาระแทรกได้ เรื่องเดียวก็พอ". The earlier ER01–ER11 measured structure (hook time, question spacing, answer length, hedges) and never asked for a laugh, so a script could pass every rule and stay dull ([what went wrong](#what-went-wrong)). IDs ER01–ER13 are stable; ER06 is retired. Shared craft stays in [scriptwriting principles](../../docs/scriptwriting-principles.md).

<a id="fun-bar"></a>
## The bar: a story a friend tells

The episode is one funny story, told by a friend who cannot wait to tell it. The viewer leaves with one fact they repeat to someone else. When two things compete, the order is **fun, then story, then fact count**. Truth is not traded: a line stays true by being a bold true line, never by being hedged until it is safe.

**Bar test (Preaw before handoff; Boat before any other finding).** Read the Thai aloud once and mark every place a listener would laugh or say "จริงดิ". A 50–60 s Short has at least five marks and no stretch over 10 s without one. A script under the bar goes back to Preaw, whatever else it passes.

<a id="edu-short-retention"></a>
## Rules

- <a id="er11"></a>**ER11 — One story with a victim (Preaw).** The episode follows one character who wants something and keeps getting it worse. Pick the source in this order: (1) a true story that is already wild, told with attitude; (2) an absurd what-if that happens to a character (EP05 "ถ้าคุณตัวเล็กเท่ามด": the best numbers on the channel). The character has a name or a clear type and a voice; "คุณ" works only when "คุณ" suffers on screen as EP05's TINY did. A real historical person may star: the events are what sources report, and comic dialogue is plainly played as a joke. Invented material is never presented as a real event, number or result, and no story is invented about a living person. An on-screen label ("เรื่องสมมุติ", "แต่งเติมจากเรื่องจริง") carries the honesty; narration does not stop to say "ลองนึกภาพ…".
- <a id="er12"></a>**ER12 — Bits (Preaw).** A **bit** is one gag: setup, then punchline. The script lands a bit about every 8 s, from at least three of these kinds:
  1. **Character line**: the victim reacts out loud ("พี่… ไหนี่มันฉี่ใช่ไหม").
  2. **Deadpan aside**: the narrator comments dryly ("ซึ่งก็ไม่มีใครถามความเห็นเขา").
  3. **Today comparison**: the old or strange thing set beside the viewer's life ("เหมือนเด็กฝึกงานวันแรกโดนใช้ไปล้างห้องน้ำ แต่ที่นี่เอาห้องน้ำมาล้างผ้า").
  4. **Exaggeration or rule of three**: two normal items, then the turn.
  5. **Visual gag**: one line for Film to paint (EP05's crumb bigger than the head).
  6. **Callback**: an earlier bit returns, bigger.
  7. **Irony**: the viewer knows what a character does not.

  The narration table carries a **Bit** column naming the kind and the gag for each line, or `-`, so a dry stretch is visible on the page.
- **ER04 — The narrator gossips (Preaw).** The narrator is a friend telling gossip: takes sides, teases the victim, is amazed, uses particles ("นะ", "หรอก", "เนี่ย"). Characters speak two or three short lines in their own voices ([Gemini TTS](../../.agents/skills/gemini-tts/SKILL.md) acts them). A line that only reports an action ("คุณล้างเสื้อแล้วเอาไปผึ่ง") is rewritten with an attitude or moved to the picture.
- **ER02 — Escalation (Preaw).** Every beat leaves the victim worse off or the situation stranger than the beat before, joined by "แต่…" or "แล้วก็…". The character meets each problem on screen before the narrator explains it (show, then tell). Curiosity comes from wanting to know what happens to this person next.
- **ER03 — Punchline ending (Preaw, Boat).** The last line is the biggest laugh or the flip, and the best ending is a callback or an irony the story planted early (EP05 crumb CL01, ant CL02). Hold about 2 s after it. A tip, a moral, a summary or a call to action is replaced by the punchline.
- **ER01 — Hook (Preaw).** The user picks each script's hook at the [hook pick](../../docs/delivery-workflow.md#hook-pick); the picked hook replaces this default shape (HK01 in the [hook catalogue](../../docs/research/2026-11-16-shorts-hook-types.md#2-hook-catalogue)). Default: within about 5 s, one concrete claim that contradicts something the viewer is sure of, with stakes the picture can show (EP05 L02 "ตกจากตึกสิบชั้น… คุณจะแทบไม่เป็นอะไรเลย"). The story then pays what the hook promised: a hook about a poison taster is followed by a poison taster in trouble.
- **ER07 — Wow facts as punchlines (Preaw, Boat).** One to three facts, each one a line that makes a listener say "จริงดิ". A fact sits where a punchline would: at the turn of a beat, after the character has run into it. A fact that is true and unsurprising is left out; one great fact beats five fair ones. Boat's research packet opens with a **จริงดิ list**: the three to five wildest well-supported things about the topic, each with its source and confidence, so Preaw starts from the best material.
- <a id="er10"></a>**ER10 — Bold true lines (Preaw, Boat).** A fact line is the boldest wording the evidence supports ("ช่างซักผ้าโรมันซักผ้าด้วยฉี่"), with at most one narrow hedge inside the claim ("แทบ", "ราว ๆ", "บางชนิด"). Sources, methods, conditions and scholarly disputes go to the fact-check file and the video description. When the bold wording is false, find another bold true line.
- <a id="er09"></a>**ER05 / ER09 — Plain and short (Preaw).** Write for a listener from any field; the hook has no technical term, and a term that stays gets an everyday comparison in the same breath. Each explanation is one cause in at most about 10 s of speech; further mechanism steps are left out. Evidence: EP05's answers run 3–6 s; EP02's 17 s term block holds its steepest drop.
- <a id="er13"></a>**ER13 — Boat protects the fun (Boat).** A joke, an exaggeration no listener would take literally and a character's speech are `not-factual`. A finding on a bit or a bold line arrives with a replacement that is true and as punchy; when no true line carries the bit, Boat says so and Preaw writes a new bit. Clarity notes keep the joke's wording wherever it is already plain.
- **ER08 — Pitch check (Preaw; ideation).** Each new concept file in `scripts/edu-series/concepts/` answers six lines: (1) **Hook**: the certainty it breaks, understood in about 3 s; (2) **Victim**: who, and what they want; (3) **Three bits**: one line each, the funniest moments of the story; (4) **Ending**: the punchline; (5) **Wow fact**: the one or two "จริงดิ" lines; (6) **Category**: the ID from the [category index](categories.md). A concept without three bits is not pitched. A lesson that steers a round cites [channel performance](performance.md) numbers, or is labelled a guess. Concept files written before 18 Nov 2026 keep their old lines.
- **ER06 — retired (18 Nov 2026).** The open-question map made scripts a quiz: EP22 asks five narrator questions in 42 s, EP20 four in 44 s. After the hook, the narrator asks at most one question; ER02 carries curiosity.

<a id="what-went-wrong"></a>
## What went wrong (review, 18 Nov 2026)

- **Structure was measured, fun was not.** EP05's rules were read off its retention curve (hook time, payoff at 30 %, answer length). What made it work was a small character getting wrecked by dew, wind and a breadcrumb, and no rule kept that.
- **Quiz rhythm.** The open-question map plus "a new problem every 10 s" produced question, answer, question, answer (EP20, EP22).
- **Accuracy by deletion.** Hedge and cut rules let every caveat thin a line or remove it. The wildest claims are the ones with caveats, so they went first (EP22 L16's notes list what the line avoids saying).
- **Filler story.** ER11's first form (17 Nov) asked for 70 % invented hypothetical story. EP26 R1 became a plain invented shift carrying one hedged fact, narrated to "คุณ" step by step with "ลองนึกภาพ…".

Evidence for this rewrite is the user's review, not numbers. Record each episode written to it in [channel performance](performance.md) and compare with EP20 and EP22.

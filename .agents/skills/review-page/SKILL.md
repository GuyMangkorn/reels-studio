# Storyboard review page

Merged on 15 Nov 2026 from the Film section of `docs/mark-storyboard-workflow.md` and the coordinator section of `docs/coordinator.md`; the rules are unchanged. Film writes the rows; the coordinator builds, publishes and reads the page.

## Rows and page content (Film)


**Default:** after Film marks a scene-sheet revision `ready-for-dev`, the coordinator publishes the deliverable's review page so the user can check the plan before plates are rendered. **One page per deliverable** (user, 1 Nov 2026): the same URL carries every scene-sheet revision, draft clip and final clip from the first storyboard pass to the last final; the artifact keeps earlier versions. For painted deliverables the page drives the [shot-by-shot review](../../../docs/sequence-workflow.md#painted-shot-by-shot).

- **First pass — Requirement (user, 31 Oct 2026):** the user sees a storyboard right after the script, before the [voice lock](../../../docs/delivery-workflow.md#voice-lock), to judge camera and VO only. Film's pre-lock staging pass also writes `review-rows.json` with `"untimed": true` and no `start`/`end`: per shot the full Thai VO and a `seen` cell that leads with the camera (shot size, angle, movement) and then what is seen; no times or lengths appear. Film reports `review page: pending`; the coordinator builds and publishes it the same way, at the URL that later timed revisions reuse. Film later adds the look frames to the same rows file (`looks`, `look` per beat; [W3](../../../docs/sequence-workflow.md#painted-shot-by-shot)) and the coordinator republishes. In a legacy Light deliverable, Film writes these rows from the brief's shot list. The first pass is not a gate: the voice lock and later stages continue while it is open, and its `change` notes enter the next scene-sheet revision.

- **Form:** a private HTML review page written for the user in Thai. Agent-facing sheets stay in English and own the shot direction.
- **Build — Requirement (31 Oct 2026):** the layout is fixed in `scripts/storyboard-page/template.html` (taken from the WORLD FOG SS-R2 page). With each `ready-for-dev` revision Film writes the rows in Thai as `review-rows.json` beside the index (schema and example: `scripts/storyboard-page/example.json`) and reports `review page: pending`. The coordinator runs `python3 -I scripts/storyboard-page/build.py <review-rows.json>` and publishes the `index.html` it prints, with its `pv/` files and `capabilities: {"db": {}}`, to the same artifact URL for each later revision of that deliverable. A layout change goes into the template, never into one page; a hand-written page does not count as the review page.
- **Content:** one table, one row per shot, with beat-name group rows. Columns: shot ID with Thai start time and length · short Thai title with the full Thai VO · what is seen (one or two plain sentences, camera folded in) with any brief deviation or open item flagged, and the sound in a few words · the tab's clip (Draft and Final tabs only) · review. The storyboard tab is text only. Keep cells short enough to scan; the scene sheet keeps the detail. The page carries no painted storyboard art per shot. Each beat header shows the **look frame** of its location (Film, [W2](../../../docs/sequence-workflow.md#painted-shot-by-shot)): `looks` at the top level of `review-rows.json` and `look` on each beat; beats at the same location share one look frame. A shot without a capture may show a plain SVG schematic (frame, horizon, figure boxes, caption band) drawn from the scene sheet, labelled as a schematic.
- **Not a gate:** the page adds no approval gate to `ready-for-dev` unless the delivery record asks for one. Boss and Editor may start blockout while review is open; changes from review follow the normal revision rules. **Default:** Boss paints finished-grade plates for a shot only after its Draft verdict is `ok`, and the coordinator sends fixes as one batched revision of 3–5 shots, so a look note costs one fix pass instead of one per shot.

## Publishing and reading the page (coordinator)

- **Claude Code:** use `Artifact` to publish and `ArtifactData` to read the stored verdicts. **Codex:** build the same bundle and use `preview.html` over local HTTP for a preview. For a preview review, the user can send the result from the copy-all button along with the current revision. Have the Claude Code coordinator publish or update the canonical artifact and read its stored verdicts when the Codex session lacks those tools. Record the built bundle as pending publication until the artifact is published.
- **Tabs:** three tabs over the same shot rows: **สตอรีบอร์ด** (storyboard), **Draft** and **Final**, each with its own pass/change verdict per shot. A status bar and the tab labels count the shots passed per tab (for example สตอรีบอร์ด 13/13 · Draft 5/13 · Final 0/13), live from the verdicts; each row shows three dots for its passed tabs and a 🔒 when `pinned`.
- **Clips:** the Draft and Final tabs each show one cut of the whole video (`node scripts/storyboard-page/clips.mjs <ID> <review-rows.json> --full [--draft]`; the draft at half size), recorded as `draftCut` / `finalCut` with each shot's `clips.draft` / `clips.final` (`src`, `in`, `out`, `cut`) pointing into it; "▶ ช็อตนี้ในคลิปเต็ม" plays the shot inside the cut and "▶ ดูทั้งคลิป" the whole cut ([render stage](../../../docs/render-rules.md#render-stage)). A single shot can also carry its own clip, a 360×640 proxy with about 1 s of each neighbour (`--shots=<ids> [--draft]`): "▶ ช็อตนี้" loops the shot's own frames and "▶ รอยต่อ" plays the whole clip. A shot without a clip for the tab reads `ยังไม่มี` and has no verdict buttons. The coordinator republishes the page when clips arrive or the revision changes.
- **Final cut:** the Final tab shows one clip of the whole video (`clips.mjs <ID> <review-rows.json> --full`, a 540×960 proxy in `finalCut`), and each row has a button that plays its shot inside that cut. A shot's final clip is keyed by its scene-cache keys, so a re-cut after a fix asks again only for the shots whose scenes changed ([W6](../../../docs/sequence-workflow.md#painted-shot-by-shot)).
- **Feedback:** each shot has a pass/change verdict and a free-text note per tab, stored in the artifact's `db` capability (`reviews/<shot ID>@<board|draft|final>`, fields `shot`, `stage`, `status`, `note`, `rev`, `hash`, `clip`, `updatedAt` and, after a re-answer, `prev`; open questions use `reviews/<Q ID>`) so the coordinator reads them back with `ArtifactData`. **Answers carry over (user, 1 Nov 2026):** `build.py` stamps each shot, question and clip with a content hash and a verdict holds while the hash of what it judged is unchanged, so republishing a revision keeps the user's answers on untouched shots. The storyboard hash covers the shot's text (title, VO, seen, sound, flag) and leaves out timing, so a retime after the voice lock keeps them; a Draft or Final verdict counts only for the clip file it was given on, so a re-rendered clip asks again. A changed item shows a `เปลี่ยน` badge with its old answer and asks again; Film may add a short Thai `changed` line on a shot to say what changed, and `previousVerdicts` is no longer needed. The coordinator reads a verdict as current when its `hash` matches the item's hash in the built page. A copy-all button covers viewers without `db`. User-owned open questions from the index appear as their own items.
- <a id="review-rounds"></a>**Review rounds — Requirement (user, 3 Nov 2026; daily cap removed by the user, 8 Nov 2026):** no limit on rounds per day. The coordinator republishes the page once per round, with every shot that is ready for a tab, not after each fix. The user marks only the shots to change; when the user says the round is done, the coordinator asks with the structured-question tool whether the unmarked shots on that tab pass, and on yes records them as `pass` with the note `default (round <date>)`. All change notes from one round go to each owner as one revision ([AI01](../../../docs/delivery-workflow.md#agent-invocation)). Evidence: `KSO-EP01-SHORT` reached storyboard page v7.
- **Recording — Requirement (user, 31 Oct 2026):** record the page URL in the same turn you publish it, in the deliverable's `delivery.md` (URL, scene-sheet revision, artifact version, and that verdicts live in its `db` under `reviews/<shot ID>@<stage>` with `rev`) and in the episode `handoff.md`, so a new or cleared session can find, read and republish the page without chat history. Republishing a later revision to the same URL updates both records. Also add the page URL and its scene-sheet revision to the index items table. The coordinator relays `change` notes to Film as a new revision request and records each verdict as user feedback with its date; a full set of storyboard `ok` verdicts is recorded as user approval of that revision only. A Final `ok` is the user's approval of that shot's picture for delivery: the coordinator pins it (`scripts/pin.mjs`) and records the pin in `delivery.md`.

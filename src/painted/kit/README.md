catalog image (`out/kit-catalog/KIT-CATALOG.png`, regenerate with `node scripts/painted-kit/catalog.mjs --out=out/kit-catalog`,
view with `scripts/qa-sheet.sh`). Source of the catalog: [catalog.tsx](catalog.tsx).

Template cast (look here after designing; [story first, kit second](../../../AGENTS.md#story-first))
- Designed a person who matches a template? Use `PEOPLE` in `people.ts` (see `PEOPLE_INFO` for age, gender, hair) and view `KIT-PEOPLE-LINEUP` / `KIT-PERSON-<id>`; copy the entry into the episode's cast and change only `outfit` and props. Add a new character to `PEOPLE` only for a reusable look.
- Face look: every `PEOPLE` head carries a `look` (eye `almond|lidded|hooded|round`, nose `hook|ell|nostrils|broad|button`, brow `arch|tapered|thick`, mouth `line|lip|full|thin`), chosen to match the person (user-approved 2 Nov 2026, sheet `KIT-FACE-CAST`). Keep the look when copying a person; a new person picks parts that fit their age and features. Every `PEOPLE` entry must have a `look` (`tests/people.test.ts`); the bare round-eye face stays only for episode casts that predate it (WFOG, ZCPAINT, KSO). The profile view does not use `look` yet.
- Mouth polish (7 Nov 2026, the new default for every figure; arms and legs unchanged: the user kept the two-part limbs). In profile the jaw follows `open` (`jawOpenOf(face)`: the chin and the lower part of the mouth drop and swing back; the mouth shapes stay the plain profile line/fill, **no drawn lips in profile**: the user rejected shaped (cupid's-bow) profile lips, 7 Nov 2026), and the turned 3/4 head drops its chin the same way; `FaceState.chew` 0..1 swells the near cheek. Motion `eat-chew` (1.5 chews a second, 2 s loop, `talk` open up to 0.4, `chew` cheek, a tiny head dip; no hand-to-mouth bite): sheets `KIT-MOUTH-STRIPS`, `KIT-CHEW-STRIPS`.
- Turned-head jaw lock (EDU-EP07, 8 Nov 2026): opt-in `FaceState.jaw` 0..1 scales `jawOpenOf`; 0 keeps the chin fixed on the turned 3/4 head while the talk mouth still opens inside the face. Unset = the old behaviour for every other episode.
- Talking and turning: face controls (`FaceState`, `talk` mouth with `open` from `mouthAt()`), head `front|left|right`. Example: composition `KIT-PEOPLE-TALK`. Head cock (roll, user 4 Nov 2026): `FigPose.headCock` in degrees, positive tips the top of the head toward screen right; the whole head (skull, hair, face, ears, hood-up hood, hat) rotates about the neck top (0.051 m above the chin on the head's centre line) while the neck and body stay, in the front, front three-quarter, left, right and back views; absent or 0 draws exactly as before; readable about -15..15 with no gap at the neck join; the `head`, `headTop` and `chin` anchors follow. It eases like any number: a motion's `body.headCock` adds to the pose's, or key it with `track` (`zcpaint/pose.ts`). Tilt plus eyes aside (`face.look`) reads puzzled. Comparison of the three cheap head tricks (head in profile on a front body, whole-body front three-quarter, head cock): `HEADTRICKS-YOU`, clip `HEADTRICKS-COCK` (`spike/HeadTricks.tsx`). No 3/4 view yet (front, left, right and back only; a 3/4 shot cuts between views).
- `CastDef.headScale` / `armScale` size children; `Outfit.skirtHem` sets a short skirt.

Recurring cast
- A character is a `CastDef = {height, head: HeadStyle, outfit: Outfit, seed}`; the episode owns its entries (`worldfog/cast.ts` `CAST`) and calls `drawFigure(p, def, ...)`.
- A recurring character keeps the same head and body in every video. Per video only `outfit` (and props) changes: `{...def, outfit: {...def.outfit, jacket: ...}}` (catalog cell "outfit swap").
- Changing a recurring head or body (`head`, `height`, proportions in `cast.ts`/`head.ts`) is a look change and needs user approval.
- Arms: `Arm {to, out?, via?, behind?}`. `via` is an authored elbow (no IK, no reach clamp); `behind: 'upper' | 'all'` paints the arm behind the torso, a shade darker (front view).
- Limit: each figure is a flat card at one depth (front, profile or back view). It turns between the six views only (front, back, left, right, plus the front diagonals `FigPose.quarter`, [`views.ts`](#)): no cross-fade (back diagonals and a camera-following quarter turn are opt-in: `backQuarter`, `quarterFollow`); a shot that turns cuts between views. Back view: `drawFigure(p, def, g, unit, {view: 'back', face, ...poseArms('P01', def, g, unit)}, t)` (arms from a front pose are mirrored); no face; sit, crouch/squat, kneel, floorSit and run hold their pose from behind (KIT-VIEW-AUDIT VA01, 13 Nov 2026), lie and bend do not. Hood: `hoodUp: true` in any view; swap it on one frame for a hood pull (the pulling motion itself is not in `MOTIONS` yet).
- Props in the rig today: torch, phone, umbrella. New props (bottle, glass, bucket) are not in the kit yet.

<a id="env-rules"></a>
Sets and atmosphere (ENV-P01, ENV-P02; user, 3 Nov 2026)
- **ENV-P01 — Promote on the second use (Requirement).** An atmosphere layer or set tool (sky, falling particles, fog, halo, water) moves into the kit when a second episode needs it ([TL01](../../../docs/contributing.md#tools-and-document-improvements)). A finished set (room, door, street, cabinet) is never promoted: it stays in its episode and gets a `PLATES` entry when its episode is delivered. Exception: a test set in `test-sets.ts` (TESTSET-*) lives in the kit for camera and view tests only and is never painted in a delivered shot (user, 4 Nov 2026).
- **ENV-P02 — Name the kit layers used (Default).** The set is designed from the story first ([story first, kit second](../../../AGENTS.md#story-first)); a kit layer that matches the design is used and named next to the casting line in the scene sheet, e.g. `kit: ENV-SKY, ENV-FALL:snow`. A layer the design needs and the kit lacks is written new with no further justification. `PLATES` is reference only, never a default.

## Hand macro

`new: hand-macro` (EDU-EP09 N1, VD05; UT14 exception at macro scale only: five separate fingers; body-scale rig hands stay mittens). A 3D skeletal hand posed by joint angles in the shot's units and projected joint by joint through the shot's `YCam`; `src/painted/kit/hands.ts`.

```ts
const hand = macroHand('test-fold-middle', {side: -1, base: 1000, tone: MACRO_TONES.boy, at: [0.3, 8.6], plane: 0, elbow: [0.6, 3.0, 30], lift: {index: 4.0}});
const anchors = paintHandMacro(p, cam, {...hand, tremble: {finger: 'ring', amp: 0.08, hz: 10}}); // anchors per joint, wrist, palm normal
```

- **Units.** Everything is in world units: lengths are cm x `size` x `unit` (`unit` 1 = set-cm, 0.01 = a metre set). BOY `size 1` has a 7.2 cm middle finger (index 6.48, ring 6.72, little 4.7; phalanx split 0.28 / 0.45 / 0.27 on index, middle and ring, 0.32 / 0.35 / 0.34 on the little finger: L4, user pick 9 Nov 2026 after L3, +20 % on the old 6.0 / 5.4 / 5.6 cm, per-finger `SPLIT` table in `hands.ts`) on a palm 6.5 cm long (wrist centre to middle MCP; finger/palm 1.11), about 6.5 cm wide, wrist 4.8 cm (forearm 4.8 cm across at the wrist), finger widths 1.4-1.7 cm with a slight splay, thumb 6.4 cm from its CMC joint (free part about 3.8 cm, thumb/middle finger 0.63) lying along the index with the tip about 1.5 cm past the index MCP, little tip near the ring's DIP crease, ELDER `size 1.25`, pianist `size 1.2`. HAND-CLOSE axes: fingers point -z, thumb of the left hand +x (the right hand mirrors; `side` -1 left, +1 right). For a metre set use `unit 0.01` and `yaw 180` (`hands-macro-specs.ts` `dining()` is the EP09 mapping). Give the camera `near` in the same units for a very small set (`near: 0.003` at `unit 0.01`).
- **Pose.** `HandMacro`: `wrist` (centre), `yaw` (fingers toward +x), `pitch` (fingers up), `roll`; `fingers.{index,middle,ring,little}` = `{mcp, pip, dip, abd?}` and `thumb` = `{cmc, cmcAbd, mcp, ip}`, relative joint angles in degrees, + flexes toward the palm, - extends (lift). Animate with numeric angles (`blendHand(a, b, u)`, eased by the caller), never by switching a pose id mid-rise. `liftFinger(h, finger, rise)` solves the first joint so the tip rises `rise` world units (the brief's lifts: `TP_LIFTS`).
- **Named poses (`HAND_POSES`, via `macroHand`).** `flat`, `test-fold-middle` (TP: pitch 4, index/ring/little PIP/DIP 8/6 with MCP solved so each tip rests on the plane, middle folded MCP 95 / PIP 110 / DIP 20 so only its knuckle shows at the palm edge), `hand-lifted-dangle` (palm down, fingers hanging 15/25/10; caller raises the wrist), `ring-strain` (TP + strain; add `tremble: {finger: 'ring', amp: 0.08, hz: 10}`). Lifts come from `macroHand(..., {lift})`. Not built: `piano-press-<finger>` (the pianist's pose needs its own wrist placement, B04 W5), `cross-middle-over-index` (EP10); use `order` for crossings.
- **Draw order.** Shadows on `plane` (light `shadowDir`, default the HAND-CLOSE lamp), forearm and `sleeve`, fingers folded under the palm (from above; a joint of the middle or tip phalanx beneath the palm plane counts as under from its first frame, FX07), the back of the hand (shaded edge band, lit rim and a lit hypothenar and thenar crown, FX09), then the other finger groups far to near; `order` overrides the sort (back to front). Fingers are sorted as groups, not phalanges.
- **Look.** Crisp opaque fills (`crisp`), one ink line round each finger, one cel shade on the side away from the light, a short nail where the back of the finger faces the camera, two crease strokes per PIP and DIP (`strain` 0..1 straightens them). `skinAlpha` 0.28 draws the hand on a translucent layer for the diagram (joints overlap a little, so the knuckles read slightly darker; draw cords above).
- **Anchors (`HandAnchors`).** Per finger `mcp`/`pip`/`dip`/`tip` (the thumb: cmc, mcp, ip, tip) as `{w: world, p: picture | null}`, `pad`, `dorsal`, `tipRadius`, `gap` (lowest point above `plane`); `wrist`, `palm` (+ `normal`), `elbow`, `forward`, `toThumb`. `macroHandTarget(cam, hand)` is the picture point for a rig `Arm.to`, so the rig mitten sits under the macro hand (draw the macro after the figure).
- **Seeds.** `base` .. `base + 230` per hand (give each hand its own range of 240).
- **Check stills.** `node scripts/painted-kit/hand-macro.mjs --out=out/hand-macro [--only=POV-flat,TRY-tp]` renders every SP1 pose at CAM-POV and CAM-TRY, the SP2 see-through spike, the SP3 rig-wrist stills and the straight top-down PLAN-flat / PLAN-tp check (judge the hand there first; a camera that looks along the fingers foreshortens them, CAM-POV shows the middle finger at about 0.7 of the palm); tile with `scripts/qa-sheet.sh`.
